১. সারসংক্ষেপ ও মৌলিক ACCOUNTING INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
এই টপিকটি Year-end adjustment entries and closing transfer entries to Trading & P&L Accounts নির্দেশ করে।
বাণিজ্যিক Bookkeeping-এ পাস করা প্রতিটি Journal Entry-কে অবশ্যই Unbreakable
Double-Entry Invariant মেনে চলতে হবে:
              TOTAL DEBIT AMOUNT (₹) = TOTAL CREDIT AMOUNT (₹)

২. DOUBLE-ENTRY DEBIT / CREDIT LEDGER FLOW DIAGRAM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   [ Source Document ] ----> [ Identify Accounts ] ----> [ Apply Rules ]
   (Invoice/Receipt)         (Dr A/c & Cr A/c)           (Golden / Modern)
                                                                 |
                                                                 v
   [ Balance Sheet ] <---- [ Trial Balance ] <---- [ Journal Entry ]
   (P&L & BS Total)        (Total Dr = Cr)         (Particulars & Narration)

৩. ধাপে ধাপে TALLYPRIME SOFTWARE NAVIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Gateway of Tally -> Vouchers -> Select Voucher Type (F4-F9)
  * Double Entry Mode (Ctrl+H) -> Enter Dr/Cr Ledgers and Amounts
  * Narration Field -> Type 'Being...' narration text
  * Save Entry -> Ctrl+A to accept voucher
