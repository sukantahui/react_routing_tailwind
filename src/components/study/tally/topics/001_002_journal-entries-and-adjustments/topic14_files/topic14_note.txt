1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This topic presents a real-world commercial accounting capstone simulation.
Students practice analyzing 10+ standard business transactions, framing 
5-column double-entry journal entries, and mapping them to TallyPrime 
electronic voucher keys (F4, F5, F6, F7, F8, F9).

2. KEY TRANSACTION WORKFLOW & VOUCHER MAPPINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Capital Contribution (Compound Entry):
   - Cash A/c Dr ₹6,00,000, Furniture A/c Dr ₹50,000 to Capital A/c ₹6,50,000.
   - Tally Voucher: Receipt Voucher (F6).

B. Credit Inventory Purchase:
   - Purchases A/c Dr ₹1,20,000 to Apex Electronics A/c ₹1,20,000.
   - Tally Voucher: Purchase Voucher (F9).

C. Credit Goods Sales:
   - Sharma Traders A/c Dr ₹95,000 to Sales Account ₹95,000.
   - Tally Voucher: Sales Voucher (F8).

D. Return Outward (Debit Note):
   - Apex Electronics A/c Dr ₹15,000 to Return Outward A/c ₹15,000.
   - Tally Voucher: Journal Voucher (F7) / Debit Note (Alt+F5).

E. Return Inward (Credit Note):
   - Return Inward A/c Dr ₹10,000 to Sharma Traders A/c ₹10,000.
   - Tally Voucher: Journal Voucher (F7) / Credit Note (Alt+F6).

F. Banking Contra Deposit:
   - Bank Account Dr ₹3,00,000 to Cash Account ₹3,00,000.
   - Tally Voucher: Contra Voucher (F4).

G. Operating Expense Payment:
   - Office Rent A/c Dr ₹25,000 to Bank Account ₹25,000.
   - Tally Voucher: Payment Voucher (F5).

3. TALLYPRIME SHORTCUT KEY SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- F4: Contra Voucher (Cash <-> Bank transfers)
- F5: Payment Voucher (Expenses & Creditor payouts)
- F6: Receipt Voucher (Capital inflows & Debtor collections)
- F7: Journal Voucher (Non-cash adjustments, Debit/Credit Notes)
- F8: Sales Voucher (Billing sales turnover)
- F9: Purchase Voucher (Inventory purchases)
- Ctrl + A: Save and Accept Voucher instantly
