1. BOOKKEEPING (PRIMARY STAGE)
   - Scope: Mechanical and routine recording of daily transactions.
   - Activities: Identifying financial events, preparing source vouchers, journal entry recording.
   - Performed By: Junior Bookkeeper, Data Entry Operator, Billing Clerk.
   - Output: Journal Books, Cash Book, Purchase Daybook, Sales Daybook.

2. ACCOUNTING (SECONDARY & ANALYTICAL STAGE)
   - Scope: Analyzing, summarizing, auditing, tax reporting, and management decision making.
   - Activities: Ledger posting, Trial Balance, Trading & P&L, Balance Sheet, Ratio analysis.
   - Performed By: Professional Senior Accountant, Auditor, Chartered Accountant.
   - Output: Final Accounts, MIS Reports, GST/TDS returns, Cash Flow Statements.

3. THE COMPLETE 6-STEP ACCOUNTING LIFECYCLE
   Step 1: Financial Transaction occurs & Source Document generated (Invoice, Receipt, Voucher).
   Step 2: Journalizing the transaction using Double-Entry Rules (Debit = Credit).
   Step 3: Posting Journal entries into individual Ledger Accounts.
   Step 4: Preparing the Trial Balance to mathematically verify Debit and Credit equality.
   Step 5: Year-end Adjustments & Final Accounts (Trading, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet).
   Step 6: Closing Entries, Ratio Analysis, Audit trail verification & Tax Filing.

4. TALLYPRIME AUTOMATION PARADIGM
   - In manual accounting: Every single step (Journal -> Ledger -> Trial Balance -> P&L) requires manual calculation.
   - In TallyPrime: The operator performs Bookkeeping (Voucher entry in F5/F6/F8/F9), and Tally automatically updates Ledgers, Trial Balance, P&L, and Balance Sheet in real time.
