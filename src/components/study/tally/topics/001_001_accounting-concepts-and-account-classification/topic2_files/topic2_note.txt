1. BUSINESS ENTITY CONCEPT
   - Principle: The business enterprise is a distinct legal and accounting entity separate from its owner/proprietor.
   - Consequence: Capital invested by the owner is treated as an internal liability for the business. Personal expenses paid with business funds are recorded as "Drawings", not business expenses.

2. GOING CONCERN CONCEPT
   - Principle: The enterprise is assumed to continue operating indefinitely into the foreseeable future without any intention or necessity of liquidation.
   - Consequence: Fixed assets are recorded at Historical Cost minus Depreciation on the Balance Sheet, rather than at current liquidation or forced-sale value.

3. MONEY MEASUREMENT CONCEPT
   - Principle: Only events, transactions, and claims that can be expressed and quantified in terms of money (currency units) are recorded in the books of accounts.
   - Consequence: Vital qualitative factors like managerial skill, employee satisfaction, brand reputation, or strikes cannot be recorded in financial statements.

4. ACCOUNTING PERIOD CONCEPT
   - Principle: The continuous operational lifespan of a business is segmented into discrete, standardized time intervals (usually 12 months) to measure performance and prepare financial statements.
   - In India: Standard Financial Year runs from 1st April to 31st March.

   - Company Creation Screen: Set "Financial Year Beginning From" (01-Apr-202X) and "Books Beginning From".
   - Separate Ledgers: Owner's investment created under "Capital Account", personal withdrawals created as "Drawings" under "Capital Account".
