1. THE 5 MODERN ACCOUNT CATEGORIES
   - Category 1: ASSETS (Cash, Bank, Debtors, Stock, Machinery, Buildings)
   - Category 2: EXPENSES (Rent, Salaries, Electricity, Freight, Depreciation)
   - Category 3: CAPITAL / EQUITY (Owner Capital, Partner Funds, Retained Earnings)
   - Category 4: LIABILITIES (Creditors, Bank Loans, Taxes Payable, Overdraft)
   - Category 5: INCOMES / REVENUES (Sales, Interest Earned, Discount Received)

2. DEBIT & CREDIT POLARITY MATRIX
   +--------------------+------------------+------------------+------------------+
   | Account Category   | Normal Balance   | When INCREASING  | When DECREASING  |
   +--------------------+------------------+------------------+------------------+
   | ASSETS             | Debit (Dr)       | DEBIT (+)        | CREDIT (-)       |
   | EXPENSES & LOSSES  | Debit (Dr)       | DEBIT (+)        | CREDIT (-)       |
   | CAPITAL / EQUITY   | Credit (Cr)      | CREDIT (+)       | DEBIT (-)        |
   | LIABILITIES        | Credit (Cr)      | CREDIT (+)       | DEBIT (-)        |
   | INCOMES & REVENUES | Credit (Cr)      | CREDIT (+)       | DEBIT (-)        |
   +--------------------+------------------+------------------+------------------+

3. MNEMONIC CODES FOR INSTANT RECALL
   - DEAL (Debit to Increase): Drawings, Expenses, Assets.
   - CLEAR (Credit to Increase): Capital, Liabilities, Equity, Incomes, Revenues.

4. WHY MODERN CLASSIFICATION POWERS COMPUTERIZED ERPs
   In software engines like TallyPrime, database transactions evaluate algebraic signs:
   Assets & Expenses carry (+) Debit balances.
   Capital, Liabilities & Revenues carry (+) Credit balances.
   Debit Total minus Credit Total MUST ALWAYS equal ZERO in every saved voucher.
