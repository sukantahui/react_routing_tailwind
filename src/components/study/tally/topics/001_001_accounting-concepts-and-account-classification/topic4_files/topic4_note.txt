1. REVENUE VS INCOME
   - Revenue: Gross inflow of money/receivables resulting from the ordinary operating activities (Gross Sales of Goods, Service Fees).
   - Net Income / Profit = Total Revenue - Total Operating Expenses.

2. CAPITAL EXPENDITURE VS REVENUE EXPENDITURE
   +-----------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
   | Feature               | Capital Expenditure (CapEx)      | Revenue Expenditure (OpEx)       |
   +-----------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+
   | Purpose               | Acquires/improves Fixed Assets   | Maintains routine daily business |
   | Benefit Period        | Multiple accounting years        | Current accounting year (<12 mos)|
   | Financial Statement   | Recorded on Balance Sheet (Asset)| Debited to Trading or P&L Account|
   | Example               | Buying Delivery Van (₹8,00,000)  | Petrol/Diesel for Van (₹5,000)   |
   +-----------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------+

3. PURCHASES VS PURCHASE OF FIXED ASSETS
   - Purchases: Buying trading merchandise intended exclusively for resale or raw materials for manufacture (Voucher: F9 Purchase).
   - Fixed Asset Purchase: Buying machinery or furniture for office operational use, NOT for resale (Voucher: F7 Journal or F9 / F5).

4. SALES VS GAINS
   - Sales: Total revenue from selling trading goods or delivering commercial services (Voucher: F8 Sales).
   - Gain: Incidental or non-operational monetary profit (e.g. Selling old obsolete machinery above book value).

5. LOSSES VS EXPENSES
   - Expense: Cost incurred deliberately to generate revenue (e.g. Salaries, Shop Rent, Advertising).
   - Loss: Reduction in financial value with zero return benefit (e.g. Goods destroyed by fire, Bad Debts, Theft).
