1. THE 4-STEP TRANSACTION ANALYSIS ALGORITHM
   Step 1: IDENTIFY THE AFFECTED LEDGERS
           Examine the source voucher (bill, receipt, cheque, invoice) and identify at least two accounts involved.
   Step 2: CLASSIFY THE ACCOUNTS
           Determine each account's nature under Traditional (Personal/Real/Nominal) or Modern (Asset/Expense/Capital/Liability/Income).
   Step 3: DETERMINE INCREASE / DECREASE & POLARITY
           Apply the Golden Rules or Modern Polarity (+Dr / +Cr) to decide which ledger is DEBITED and which is CREDITED.
   Step 4: VERIFY MATHEMATICAL EQUALITY & PASS VOUCHER
           Confirm Total Debit = Total Credit, add clear narration, and save in TallyPrime with Ctrl+A.

2. WORKED PRACTICAL CASE STUDIES
   Case 1: "Paid ₹12,000 office electricity bill by HDFC NetBanking"
           - Accounts: Electricity Expense A/c & HDFC Bank A/c
           - Nature: Electricity = Nominal (Expense) | Bank = Personal (Giver)
           - Direction: Expense + (Dr) | Bank Asset - (Cr)
           - Entry: Dr Electricity Expense ₹12,000 | Cr HDFC Bank ₹12,000
           - Tally Voucher: F5 Payment

   Case 2: "Received ₹40,000 NEFT from customer Sen Traders against Invoice #102"
           - Accounts: SBI Bank A/c & Sen Traders A/c (Sundry Debtor)
           - Nature: SBI Bank = Asset/Real (+) | Sen Traders = Personal (Giver)
           - Direction: Bank Asset + (Dr) | Debtor Asset - (Cr)
           - Entry: Dr SBI Bank ₹40,000 | Cr Sen Traders ₹40,000
           - Tally Voucher: F6 Receipt

3. TALLYPRIME VOUCHER MAPPING RULES
   - F4 Contra   : Cash deposit to bank / Cash withdrawal from bank / Inter-bank transfer.
   - F5 Payment  : Any outward payment of money (Cash or Bank).
   - F6 Receipt  : Any inward receipt of money (Cash or Bank).
   - F7 Journal  : Non-cash adjustments, credit asset purchase, depreciation, bad debts.
   - F8 Sales    : Sales of goods/services on cash or credit.
   - F9 Purchase : Purchase of trading goods on cash or credit.
