1. GOLDEN RULE 1: PERSONAL ACCOUNTS (Individuals & Corporate Entities)
   - DEBIT THE RECEIVER (The person/entity receiving value or benefit).
   - CREDIT THE GIVER   (The person/entity delivering value or benefit).
   - Practical Example: Paid ₹10,000 to Supplier Sen & Co.
     Debit: Sen & Co. A/c (Receiver) | Credit: Bank A/c (Giver).

2. GOLDEN RULE 2: REAL ACCOUNTS (Properties & Assets)
   - DEBIT WHAT COMES IN (Asset entering the business).
   - CREDIT WHAT GOES OUT (Asset leaving the business).
   - Practical Example: Bought Office Computer for ₹35,000 Cash.
     Debit: Computer A/c (Comes In) | Credit: Cash A/c (Goes Out).

3. GOLDEN RULE 3: NOMINAL ACCOUNTS (Incomes, Expenses, Losses & Gains)
   - DEBIT ALL EXPENSES & LOSSES (Costs incurred to operate).
   - CREDIT ALL INCOMES & GAINS   (Revenues & earnings received).
   - Practical Example: Paid Shop Electricity Bill ₹4,500 Cash.
     Debit: Electricity Expense A/c (Expense) | Credit: Cash A/c (Goes Out).

4. SUMMARY MATRIX
   +--------------------+----------------------------+----------------------------+
   | Account Class      | Debit Criterion            | Credit Criterion           |
   +--------------------+----------------------------+----------------------------+
   | Real (Assets)      | What COMES IN              | What GOES OUT              |
   | Personal (Entities)| The RECEIVER               | The GIVER                  |
   | Nominal (P&L)      | All EXPENSES & LOSSES      | All INCOMES & GAINS        |
   +--------------------+----------------------------+----------------------------+

5. TALLYPRIME CONFIGURATION
   - Press F12 in Voucher Entry Screen > Enable "Use Cr/Dr instead of To/By during voucher entry".
   - Verify every transaction ensures Total Debit equals Total Credit before pressing Ctrl+A.
