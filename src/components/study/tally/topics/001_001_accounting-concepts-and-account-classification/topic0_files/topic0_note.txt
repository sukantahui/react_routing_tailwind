1. THE CORE ACCOUNTING EQUATION
   Assets = Capital + Liabilities
   - Assets: Properties & claims owned (Cash, Bank, Debtors, Stock, Machinery)
   - Capital: Owner's equity invested into business
   - Liabilities: External amounts owed (Creditors, Bank Loans, Overdraft)

2. TRADITIONAL CLASSIFICATION OF ACCOUNTS & GOLDEN RULES
   +--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
   | Account Category   | Golden Rule - DEBIT            | Golden Rule - CREDIT           |
   +--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
   | REAL ACCOUNTS      | Debit what COMES IN            | Credit what GOES OUT           |
   | (Assets & Property)| (e.g. Cash recd, Fixed Asset)  | (e.g. Cash paid, Asset sold)   |
   +--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
   | PERSONAL ACCOUNTS  | Debit the RECEIVER             | Credit the GIVER               |
   | (Individuals/Firms)| (e.g. Goods sold to customer)  | (e.g. Cash paid by supplier)   |
   +--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
   | NOMINAL ACCOUNTS   | Debit all EXPENSES & LOSSES    | Credit all INCOMES & GAINS     |
   | (Incomes/Expenses) | (e.g. Rent, Salary, Freight)   | (e.g. Sales, Interest received)|
   +--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+

3. MODERN CLASSIFICATION & DEBIT-CREDIT POLARITY MATRIX
   +--------------------+------------------+------------------+------------------+
   | Account Category   | Normal Balance   | Debit Effect     | Credit Effect    |
   +--------------------+------------------+------------------+------------------+
   | ASSETS             | Debit (Dr)       | INCREASES (+)    | DECREASES (-)    |
   | EXPENSES & LOSSES  | Debit (Dr)       | INCREASES (+)    | DECREASES (-)    |
   | CAPITAL / EQUITY   | Credit (Cr)      | DECREASES (-)    | INCREASES (+)    |
   | LIABILITIES        | Credit (Cr)      | DECREASES (-)    | INCREASES (+)    |
   | INCOMES & GAINS    | Credit (Cr)      | DECREASES (-)    | INCREASES (+)    |
   +--------------------+------------------+------------------+------------------+

4. SOURCE DOCUMENTS TO FINANCIAL STATEMENTS PIPELINE
   [Source Invoice/Receipt] -> [Journal Book Entry] -> [Ledger Account Posting]
   -> [Trial Balance Agreement] -> [Trading & P&L Account] -> [Balance Sheet]

5. TALLYPRIME MAPPING GUIDE
   - Real Assets          -> Fixed Assets / Current Assets / Cash-in-hand / Bank
   - Personal Parties     -> Sundry Debtors (Customers) / Sundry Creditors (Suppliers)
   - Nominal Expenses     -> Direct Expenses (Trading) / Indirect Expenses (P&L)
   - Nominal Incomes      -> Sales Accounts / Direct Incomes / Indirect Incomes
