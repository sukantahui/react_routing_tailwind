SEGMENT 4: MODULE 2 - TOPIC 10: STAR TOPOLOGY (CENTRAL SWITCH, FAULT ISOLATION)

1. ARCHITECTURE OF STAR TOPOLOGY:
   - Every peripheral device (computer, printer, server) is connected directly to a central connecting device (usually a Network Switch or Hub) via a dedicated point-to-point cable.
   - Resembles a star radiating outwards from the center.
   - Universal Cabling: Twisted Pair cable (Cat-5e/Cat-6 UTP) terminated with RJ-45 modular connectors.

2. HOW DATA TRAVELS IN A STAR TOPOLOGY:
   - When Node A sends data to Node B:
     * Node A transmits the frame along its dedicated cable to the central switch.
     * The switch reads the destination MAC address.
     * The switch delivers the frame directly to Node B's dedicated cable.
     * All other workstations (Nodes C, D, E) remain unaffected and free to conduct independent communications.

3. ADVANTAGES & DISADVANTAGES (CBSE BOARD FAVORITE):
   - Major Advantages:
     * Robust Fault Isolation: If one workstation's cable breaks, becomes loose, or shorts out, ONLY that single workstation loses connectivity. The rest of the network operates with zero interruption.
     * Easy Installation & Maintenance: New nodes can be added or removed dynamically simply by plugging/unplugging an RJ-45 cable into the central switch without disrupting active traffic.
     * Zero Collisions & High Performance: With a full-duplex switch, every node enjoys dedicated line-rate bandwidth with zero collisions.
     * Simple Troubleshooting: The switch's port LEDs immediately pinpoint which cable or computer has failed.
   - Major Disadvantages:
     * Central Point of Failure: If the central switch/hub hardware or its power supply fails, the ENTIRE network collapses.
     * Higher Cable Consumption: Requires significantly more total cable length than a bus topology, because an individual dedicated cable run must extend from the central switch to every single computer.

4. CBSE 5-MARK CASE STUDY APPLICATION:
   - In Section D case studies, when asked to suggest a topology for connecting computers inside a building or computer lab, ALWAYS recommend "Star Topology"!
   - Justification: Excellent fault isolation (single cable fault does not bring down the network) and high scalability for adding new computers.
