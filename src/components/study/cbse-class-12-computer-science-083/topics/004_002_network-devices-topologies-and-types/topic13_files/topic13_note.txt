1. CLASSIFICATION BY GEOGRAPHICAL SPREAD:
   Networks are categorized based on the geographic distance they span:
   1. PAN (Personal Area Network):
      - Coverage Range: Up to 10 meters (within the reach of an individual person).
      - Primary Technologies: Bluetooth (IEEE 802.15.1), Infrared (IrDA), USB, Wi-Fi Direct.
      - Examples: Connecting a smartphone to wireless earbuds, smartwatch to phone, laptop to wireless mouse, mobile hotspot.
   2. LAN (Local Area Network):
      - Coverage Range: Up to 1 kilometer (single room, office floor, school building, or small campus).
      - Primary Technologies: Ethernet (802.3 over Cat-6 UTP/Fiber), Wi-Fi (802.11).
      - Ownership: Privately owned and managed by a single organization (school, hospital, office).
      - Key Characteristics: Highest data transmission speed (100 Mbps to 10 Gbps) and lowest error rate.
   3. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network):
      - Coverage Range: Up to 50 kilometers (spans an entire city or municipal town).
      - Primary Technologies: Cable Television networks (CATV), Municipal Optical Fiber rings, WiMAX.
      - Ownership: Owned by a consortium of users, municipal government, or a single telecom network provider.
      - Examples: Citywide Cable TV broadband, municipal traffic camera networks, interconnected bank branches across a metropolis.
   4. WAN (Wide Area Network):
      - Coverage Range: Spans entire states, nations, continents, or the entire planet Earth.
      - Primary Technologies: Subsea optical fiber cables, Geostationary satellites, telecom leased lines.
      - Ownership: Multiple public telecom operators, ISPs, and international consortia.
      - Prime Example: The global INTERNET.
      - Key Characteristics: Highest propagation latency and highest error rate due to vast distances.
