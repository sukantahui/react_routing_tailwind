1. SUMMARY OF MODULE 1 CURRICULUM:
   - Evolution of Networking: ARPANET (1969, US DoD), NSFNET (mid-1980s, academic research), Interspace, and the modern Internet.
   - Data Communication Components: Sender, Receiver, Message, Medium, Protocol (the rule set governing exchange).
   - Measuring Capacity: Bandwidth (Hertz vs bps), Transfer Rates (bps, Kbps, Mbps, Gbps, Tbps).
     * Calculation rule: 1 Byte = 8 bits. Divide file size in bits by transfer rate in bps to get transfer time.
   - Transmission Modes:
     * Simplex: Unidirectional flow only (Keyboard to CPU, TV broadcast).
     * Half-Duplex: Both directions, but only one at a time (Walkie-talkie).
     * Full-Duplex: Both directions simultaneously (Telephone, modern Ethernet switch).
   - Guided Transmission Media:
     * Twisted Pair Cable: UTP (cheap, flexible, standard LANs) vs STP (shielded against extreme EMI, industrial). Segment limit: 100 meters. Connector: RJ-45.
     * Coaxial Cable: 4 concentric layers (core, dielectric, shield, jacket). Baseband (50 Ohm, 10BASE2/10BASE5) vs Broadband (75 Ohm, CATV). Terminator: 50 Ohm BNC.
     * Optical Fiber: Total Internal Reflection (n_core > n_cladding, theta_i > theta_c). Single-mode (laser, long distance) vs Multi-mode (LED, campus LANs). 100% EMI immune.
   - Unguided Transmission Media:
     * Radio Waves: 3 kHz to 1 GHz, omnidirectional, penetrates walls, AM/FM/Cellular/Wi-Fi.
     * Microwaves: 1 GHz to 300 GHz, unidirectional focused beam, line of sight, repeater towers every 40-50 km due to Earth curvature.
     * Infrared: 300 GHz to 400 THz, short range (< 10m), stopped by solid walls (high security), TV remotes, IrDA.
     * Satellite Communication: GEO at 35,786 km, 24-hr orbit, Uplink (higher freq) != Downlink (lower freq) to prevent feedback, transponder, latency ~250-500ms, VSAT.
   - Case Study Decision Guidelines:
     * <= 100m within building -> Cat-6 UTP Cable.
     * 100m - 5km inter-building -> Optical Fiber Cable.
     * Hilly terrain / river / busy highway -> Terrestrial Microwave.
     * Remote island / offshore / rural desert -> Satellite (VSAT).
