2. CBSE 5-MARK CASE STUDY DECISION MATRIX:
   Rule 1 (Indoor & Under 100m):
     * Distance: <= 100 meters inside same building.
     * Recommendation: Unshielded Twisted Pair (Cat-6 UTP with RJ-45).
     * Justification: Most economical, flexible, standard Ethernet speed.
   Rule 2 (Inter-Building Campus 100m to 5 km):
     * Distance: Between 100m and 5 km across campus grounds.
     * Recommendation: Optical Fiber Cable (Multi-Mode or Single-Mode).
     * Justification: High bandwidth, immune to lightning/ground loops between buildings, low loss.
   Rule 3 (Hilly Terrain / Busy Roadways 5 km to 30 km):
     * Distance: Up to 30 km where digging trenches or laying cables is prohibited.
     * Recommendation: Terrestrial Microwave Link.
     * Justification: Unidirectional line of sight bridges obstacles without cabling permissions.
   Rule 4 (Remote / Desert / Island / Offshore > 100 km):
     * Distance: Remote isolated branches without telecom infrastructure.
     * Recommendation: Satellite Communication (VSAT).
     * Justification: Ubiquitous coverage independent of terrestrial physical cabling.
   Rule 5 (In-Room Device Control < 5m):
     * Distance: Within single room without leaking outside.
     * Recommendation: Infrared Transmission.
     * Justification: Contained within room by walls, zero interference, high security.
