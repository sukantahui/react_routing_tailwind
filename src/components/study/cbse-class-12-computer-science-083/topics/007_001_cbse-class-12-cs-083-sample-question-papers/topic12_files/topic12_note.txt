2. KEY EXAMINATION POINTS:
  (1) CBSE follows strictly positive step-marking: you earn marks for every correct syntactic construct even if the final result is flawed.
  (2) In SQL: Keywords can be upper or lower case; column aliases do not require AS; semicolons at the end of queries are expected.
  (3) In Python: Indentation MUST be visibly consistent; function definitions must include def keyword and arguments.
  (4) Underline final output values and enclose dry-run trace tables in neat pencil borders.
