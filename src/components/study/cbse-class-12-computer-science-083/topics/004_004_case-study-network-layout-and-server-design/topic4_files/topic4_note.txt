TOPIC 4: REPEATER PLACEMENT RULES (>70-100 METERS THRESHOLD)

================================================================================
1. THE 100-METER REPEATER RULE
================================================================================
Standard Rule:
Under the international TIA/EIA-568 standard, the maximum certified cable length
for Category 5e / 6 Unshielded Twisted Pair (UTP) is 100 METERS.

Why Signals Attenuate:
- As digital electrical square pulses travel through copper wires, electrical
  resistance and capacitance cause ATTENUATION (loss of signal amplitude) and
  dispersion.
- Past 100 meters, pulses degrade below the threshold voltage required by NICs,
  resulting in packet corruption, CRC failures, and connection drops.

Function of a Repeater:
- A Repeater operates at the PHYSICAL LAYER (Layer 1).
- It receives weak, attenuated digital signals, cleans out background noise,
  reshapes the pulses into sharp square waves, and retransmits fresh bits.

================================================================================
2. CBSE BOARD MARKING SCHEME DECISION MATRIX
================================================================================
Distance on Link           Repeater Recommendation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Distance <= 70 meters      NO Repeater needed (Signals travel reliably).
Distance 70 - 100 meters   Borderline (Usually NO repeater needed, but accepted if reasoned).
Distance > 100 meters      MANDATORY REPEATER REQUIRED (Marks awarded for naming link).

EXCEPTIONS TO NOTE IN EXAMS:
- Fiber Optic Cable: If the school chooses Fiber Optic, NO repeater is needed
  even for distances of 500m - 2km, because fiber has virtually zero attenuation.
