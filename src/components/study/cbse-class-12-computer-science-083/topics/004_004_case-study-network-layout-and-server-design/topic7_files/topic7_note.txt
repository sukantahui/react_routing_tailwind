1. GEOGRAPHIC SCENARIOS & RECOMMENDED TECHNOLOGIES
================================================================================
Scenario 1: HILLY / MOUNTAINOUS REGION (e.g. Shimla, Darjeeling, Ooty)
- Recommended Technology: Radio Wave / Microwave (Line-of-Sight) or Satellite Link (VSAT).
- Justification: Laying physical cables across rugged, steep terrain is cost-prohibitive,
  technically difficult, and highly vulnerable to landslides and physical severance.
  Unguided wireless signals overcome terrain obstacles effortlessly.

Scenario 2: CITY-TO-CITY WITHIN SAME METROPOLITAN AREA (5 - 50 km)
- Network Classification: MAN (Metropolitan Area Network).
- Recommended Technology: Metro Ethernet Leased Line / High-speed Fiber Optic / Microwave.
- Justification: Provides high, guaranteed symmetric bandwidth with minimal latency.

Scenario 3: INTER-STATE / INTERCONTINENTAL DISTANT BRANCHES (1,000+ km)
- Network Classification: WAN (Wide Area Network).
- Recommended Technology: Satellite Communication (VSAT) or Transoceanic Leased Lines / VPN.
- Justification: Spans vast oceans and state borders through public carrier infrastructures.

Scenario 4: SECURE REMOTE EMPLOYEE ACCESS
- Recommended Technology: VPN (Virtual Private Network).
- Justification: Creates an encrypted tunnel over public internet, protecting internal data.

2. GOLDEN ANSWER TEMPLATES FOR CBSE EXAM
================================================================================
Question: "Suggest a communication technology to link the Head Office to a branch
office in a hilly region. Give reasons."
Answer: "Radio Wave / Microwave transmission (or Satellite communication).
Reason: Laying physical cables across rugged mountainous terrain is economically
prohibitive and vulnerable to environmental disruptions (landslides). Wireless
transmission travels through the air, bypassing all terrain obstacles."

Question: "What hardware and software should be recommended for video conferencing?"
Answer: "Hardware: High-definition webcams, microphones, and display screens.
Protocol / Software: VoIP / SIP / H.323 protocols, and video conferencing software
such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet."
