MASTER TEACHER SUKANTA HUI'S GOLDEN RULES FOR 100% MARKS IN AGGREGATES & JOINS:
1. Never put aggregate functions (SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX, COUNT) in the WHERE clause; always use HAVING.
2. In queries with GROUP BY, ensure every non-aggregated column in the SELECT list appears in GROUP BY.
3. To join N tables, you MUST include at least (N - 1) join conditions in the WHERE clause.
4. When qualifying columns in multi-table queries, use concise table aliases (e.g. E.DeptId = D.DeptId).
5. In Cartesian Products: Degree adds (Columns), Cardinality multiplies (Rows).
