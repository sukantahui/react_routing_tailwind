1. FIVE CORE ENTERPRISE DOMAINS:
   A) Telecommunications: Call Detail Records (CDR) processing and live subscriber rating.
   B) Banking & Finance: Double-entry accounting ledgers and ATM/UPI transaction concurrency.
   C) Indian Railways (IRCTC): Real-time seat inventory reservation and anti-double booking locks.
   D) Healthcare Systems: Electronic Health Records (EHR) and patient clinical histories.
   E) Educational Institutions: Student Information Systems (SIS) and CBSE board marks compilation.

2. SUMMARY DOMAIN MATRIX:
   Telecom -> CDR Processing (Caller, Receiver, Duration, TowerID)
   Banking -> Account Ledger (AccountNo, TxnType, Amount, Balance)
   Railways -> Seat Inventory (PNR, TrainNo, TravelDate, BerthNo)
   Healthcare -> EHR Records (PatientID, Diagnosis, Medication)
   Education -> Student Master (StudentID, RollNo, Theory, Practical)
