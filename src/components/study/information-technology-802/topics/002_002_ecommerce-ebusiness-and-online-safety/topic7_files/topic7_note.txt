1. WHAT IS A WEBINAR?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Webinar (a blend of 'Web' + 'Seminar') is an interactive, real-time online seminar, 
lecture, workshop, or multimedia presentation delivered over the Internet using video 
conferencing and collaboration technologies.

Key Characteristic: Two-Way Bidirectional Interactivity.
Unlike one-way television broadcasts or static recorded videos, webinars enable live, 
synchronous communication between the instructor/presenter and participants.

2. CORE INTERACTIVE FEATURES OF WEBINAR PLATFORMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Real-Time Audio & Video Streaming:
   - High-definition live video feeds of the speaker, with WebRTC browser protocols 
     and noise suppression.
2. Screen Sharing & Application Streaming:
   - Presenters broadcast slides, software IDEs (e.g. NetBeans, Eclipse, VS Code), 
     or browser web apps live to all attendee monitors.
3. Interactive Digital Whiteboards:
   - Live collaborative canvases allowing instructors to sketch circuit diagrams, 
     ER diagrams, and write mathematical formulas in real time.
4. Dedicated Q&A Pod & Moderated Chat:
   - Structured question queues with upvoting and marked answers, preventing student 
     doubts from disappearing in rapid chat streams.
5. 'Raise Hand' Virtual Queuing:
   - Attendees signal for speaking permissions to ask questions via live voice audio.
6. Live Audience Polling & Instant Quizzes:
   - Instant multiple-choice questions displayed on screen to evaluate class comprehension 
     with real-time percentage feedback graphs.
7. Breakout Rooms for Group Collaboration:
   - Splitting attendees into small virtual rooms for team problem solving before reconvening.
8. Cloud Session Recording:
   - Automated cloud archival allowing absent students to review the complete lesson asynchronously.

3. SYNCHRONOUS VS ASYNCHRONOUS E-LEARNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PARAMETER                SYNCHRONOUS (LIVE WEBINARS)       ASYNCHRONOUS (PRE-RECORDED MOOCs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Interaction Timing       Real-time live scheduled meetings Self-paced, on-demand at student's choice
Doubt Resolution         Instant live clarification        Delayed forum / email responses
Attendance Schedule      Fixed scheduled time slot         Flexible 24x7 anytime viewing
Engagement Dynamic       High social presence & live polls Self-motivated, independent learning
Key Examples             Zoom, Google Meet, MS Teams, Webex Coursera, edX, SWAYAM, YouTube lectures

4. BENGALI SUMMARY / শিক্ষক নির্দেশিকা
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ওয়েবিনার (Webinar) হলো ইন্টারনেটের মাধ্যমে আয়োজিত লাইভ সেমিনার বা ভার্চুয়াল ক্লাস। 
রেকর্ডেড ভিডিওর তুলনায় এর বড় সুবিধা হলো শিক্ষকের সাথে সরাসরি কথা বলা, স্ক্রিন শেয়ার 
দেখা এবং তৎক্ষণাৎ প্রশ্নের উত্তর (Question Clearance) পেয়ে যাওয়া।

পরিচালিত হয়।
