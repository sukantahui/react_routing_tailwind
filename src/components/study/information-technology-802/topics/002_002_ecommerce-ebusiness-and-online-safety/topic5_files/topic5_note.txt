Threat Surfaces:
- Unsecured public Wi-Fi sniffing.
- Phishing & lookalike fake URLs.
- Hardware/Software Keyloggers.
- Social Engineering / Vishing phone calls.

2. THE 7 GOLDEN PRECAUTIONS FOR SAFE DIGITAL TRANSACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Verify HTTPS & SSL Padlock:
   - Always inspect the address bar for `https://` and a closed padlock icon. Never enter 
     banking passwords or card details on unencrypted `http://` pages.

2. Never Transact on Public or Unsecured Wi-Fi:
   - Free Wi-Fi in railway stations, airports, or cafes lacks encryption. Hackers can sniff 
     data packets or deploy "Evil Twin" fake hotspots. Always use cellular mobile data or trusted private VPNs.

3. Protect Secret Credentials (OTP, CVV, ATM PIN, MPIN):
   - Never share your 3-digit CVV, 4/6-digit ATM PIN, or dynamic OTP with anyone. 
   - Remember: Legitimate bank employees, RBI officials, or police NEVER ask for OTPs or PINs.

4. Utilize Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):
   - Always ensure 2FA is active. A password alone is vulnerable to breaches; requiring an OTP 
     or biometric scan stops unauthorized logins even if passwords leak.

5. Use Virtual On-Screen Keyboards on Shared Computers:
   - When using shared or cybercafe PCs, click characters on an on-screen keyboard to defeat 
     keystroke-logging malware.

6. Always Explicitly 'Log Out' & Avoid Saving Credentials on Shared Devices:
   - Never check "Remember Me" on public computers. Explicitly log out to invalidate the server session token.

7. Enable Real-Time SMS/Email Bank Transaction Alerts:
   - Immediate notifications ensure unauthorized debits are caught within minutes. Report fraud 
     immediately to Bank Helpline and National Cyber Crime Portal (1930 / cybercrime.gov.in).
