1. WHAT IS E-COMMERCE? (ELECTRONIC COMMERCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E-Commerce (Electronic Commerce) refers to the buying and selling of goods, products, 
and services, as well as the transmitting of funds and data, over an electronic network, 
primarily the Internet.

In simple terms, whenever a commercial transaction occurs between a buyer and a seller 
using a digital device (Computer, Smartphone, Tablet) and the Internet, it is E-Commerce.

Key Models of E-Commerce:
1. B2C (Business-to-Consumer): Online retailers selling to end consumers (e.g. Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa).
2. B2B (Business-to-Business): Wholesale trade between commercial enterprises (e.g. IndiaMART, Udaan).
3. C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer): Individuals selling pre-owned goods to other individuals (e.g. OLX, Quikr, eBay).
4. C2B (Consumer-to-Business): Freelancers/creators providing services to businesses (e.g. Upwork, Shutterstock).

2. TRADITIONAL COMMERCE VS E-COMMERCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PARAMETER                TRADITIONAL COMMERCE              E-COMMERCE (ONLINE SHOPPING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Storefront               Physical brick-and-mortar shop    Virtual web portal / Mobile app
Operating Hours          Fixed (e.g., 10 AM to 8 PM)      24 Hours x 7 Days x 365 Days
Geographical Reach       Local neighborhood / City limits  Global, national, boundary-free
Product Inspection       Physical touch and feel possible  High-res photos, videos, specifications
Commute & Travel         Customer travels & parks vehicle  Delivered directly to customer doorstep
Price Comparison         Time-consuming store hopping      Instant cross-portal price comparison
Payment Options          Primarily cash / physical POS     UPI, Net Banking, Cards, EMI, Wallets, CoD
Order Tracking           No milestone tracking             Real-time GPS parcel milestone updates
Operational Overhead     High showroom rent & staff salary Lower overheads passing savings to buyers

3. KEY CUSTOMER ADVANTAGES OF ONLINE SHOPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 24x7 Round-the-Clock Convenience:
   - Consumers are not bound by shop opening/closing hours. Orders can be placed at midnight, 
     early morning, or during public holidays from anywhere (home, office, or while traveling).

2. Vast Assortment & Product Variety:
   - Physical shops have limited shelf space; online platforms aggregate millions of Stock 
     Keeping Units (SKUs) across diverse brands, categories, colors, and technical configurations.

3. Effortless Price Comparison & Best Deals:
   - Consumers can compare prices across multiple merchants within seconds. Built-in promotional 
     discounts, seasonal festival sales (Diwali/Puja sales), and cashback incentives provide substantial savings.

4. Customer Reviews & Transparent Social Proof:
   - Verified buyer ratings, authentic video reviews, and Q&A forums help consumers assess product 
     quality, durability, and seller reliability before purchasing.

5. Doorstep Delivery & Real-Time Tracking:
   - Goods are delivered directly to the buyer's home or office address. An Air Waybill (AWB) number 
     provides transparent live updates on package dispatch, transit hubs, and expected delivery date.

6. Easy Returns & Reverse Logistics:
   - If a product is defective, damaged, or of incorrect size, automated return pickup from home 
     and direct bank/wallet refunds simplify customer redressal.
