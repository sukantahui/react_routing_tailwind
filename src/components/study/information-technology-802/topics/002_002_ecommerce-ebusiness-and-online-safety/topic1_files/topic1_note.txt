1. POPULAR E-COMMERCE PORTALS & SPECIALIZATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Amazon (amazon.in):
   - Global multi-category retail powerhouse founded by Jeff Bezos.
   - Products: Electronics, books, apparel, groceries (Amazon Fresh), cloud services (AWS).
   - Core Features: Prime 1-day delivery, A-to-Z Guarantee, Customer Reviews, 1-Click Ordering.

2. Flipkart (flipkart.com):
   - Indian e-commerce giant founded by Sachin Bansal and Binny Bansal (acquired by Walmart).
   - Products: Mobile phones, electronics, fashion, home appliances.
   - Core Features: Big Billion Days, SuperCoins, Flipkart Assured quality badge.

3. Snapdeal (snapdeal.com):
   - Indian e-commerce marketplace founded by Kunal Bahl & Rohit Bansal.
   - Focus: Value-for-money, affordable consumer products tailored for Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

4. Nykaa (nykaa.com):
   - Specialized Indian beauty, cosmetics, skincare, and fashion portal founded by Falguni Nayar.
   - Key USP: 100% authentic, curated beauty products sourced directly from manufacturers.

5. Infibeam (infibeam.com / CCAvenue):
   - Pioneer in Indian digital retail, later diversified into digital payment gateway infrastructure.

2. POPULAR E-LEARNING & MOOC PORTALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Coursera (coursera.org):
   - Founded by Stanford University professors Daphne Koller & Andrew Ng.
   - Offers Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs), university degrees, and professional certifications.

2. edX (edx.org):
   - Founded by Harvard University and MIT (Massachusetts Institute of Technology).
   - Delivers rigorous university courses in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, and Engineering.

3. SWAYAM (swayam.gov.in) & NPTEL:
   - Flagship digital education portals developed by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Govt of India.
   - Delivers free online college courses with credit transfer recognition across Indian universities.

4. Khan Academy (khanacademy.org):
   - Non-profit platform offering free K-12 interactive STEM learning, math practice, and tutorials.

3. COMPARISON MATRIX: E-COMMERCE VS E-LEARNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PARAMETER                E-COMMERCE PORTALS               E-LEARNING PORTALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Deliverable      Physical / Digital consumer goods Digital knowledge, videos, certificates
Core Technology          Shopping Cart, Inventory, 3PL    LMS (Learning Management System), Video CDN
Fulfillment Channel      Road/Air Couriers to Doorstep    Instant Web Streaming & PDF downloads
Revenue Model            Product Margin, Seller Commission Freemium, Course Subscription, Certification Fee
Return Mechanism         Doorstep pickup & physical refund 7 to 14 days digital fee refund policy
Key Examples             Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, Snapdeal Coursera, edX, SWAYAM, NPTEL, Udemy

4. BENGALI SUMMARY / শিক্ষক নির্দেশিকা
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ই-কমার্স পোর্টাল (যেমন Amazon, Flipkart, Snapdeal, Nykaa) মূলত বাণিজ্যিক পণ্য সামগ্রী 
কেনার জন্য ব্যবহৃত হয় যেখানে কুরিয়ারের মাধ্যমে জিনিস বাড়িতে পৌঁছায়। 
অপরদিকে ই-লার্নিং পোর্টাল (যেমন Coursera, edX, SWAYAM, NPTEL) শিক্ষামূলক কোর্স, ভিডিও 
লেকচার ও সার্টিফিকেট অর্জনের জন্য ব্যবহৃত হয়। উভয় প্ল্যাটফর্মেই ডিজিটাল পেমেন্ট গেটওয়ে 
ও ইউজার লগইন ব্যবস্থা থাকে।
