2. SUBJECTIVE BOARD QUESTIONS WITH MODEL ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[QUESTION 1 - 2 MARKS]
Q: Differentiate between E-Commerce and E-Business with one practical example of each.
Model Answer:
- E-Commerce refers specifically to the commercial buying and selling of goods, products, 
  and services over the Internet (e.g., buying a laptop on amazon.in).
- E-Business is the broader concept encompassing all electronic business processes, including 
  E-Commerce plus Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Customer Relationship Management (CRM), 
  Supply Chain Management (SCM), and internal employee intranets (e.g., a manufacturing enterprise 
  using SAP ERP and Salesforce CRM to manage production, vendor bidding, and staff).

[QUESTION 2 - 2 MARKS]
Q: What is a Virtual Shopping Cart? State two primary functions performed by it.
Model Answer:
- Definition: A Virtual Shopping Cart is an interactive software module on an e-commerce 
  website that temporarily stores and organizes items selected by a customer before final checkout.
- Two Primary Functions:
  1. Session State Persistence: Retains selected products and quantities as the user browses multiple catalog pages.
  2. Dynamic Calculation: Computes live subtotals, promotional coupon discounts, statutory GST taxes, and shipping fees.

[QUESTION 3 - 3 MARKS]
Q: Explain the three major operational risks that an E-Business enterprise faces.
Model Answer:
1. Violation of Customer Privacy: Unauthorized harvesting, tracking, or selling of confidential 
   user data without consent, leading to severe regulatory penalties under the DPDP Act 2023.
2. Server Downtime during Peak-Hour Traffic Spikes: Sudden festive traffic surges (e.g. flash sales) 
   overwhelming server CPU/RAM and crashing databases (HTTP 503 errors), causing immense revenue loss.
3. Hacker Penetration: Malicious cyber attacks such as SQL Injection (stealing customer data), 
   DDoS floods, and Ransomware encrypting transaction records.

[QUESTION 4 - 3 MARKS]
Q: State any three critical precautions that a consumer must observe while performing digital financial transactions.
Model Answer:
1. Verify HTTPS & Padlock: Ensure the URL starts with `https://` with a closed padlock icon indicating TLS encryption.
2. Avoid Public Wi-Fi: Never conduct net banking or enter card details over open, unsecured public Wi-Fi hotspots.
3. Protect Secret Credentials: Never disclose OTPs, CVV codes, or ATM/UPI PINs to anyone, as authentic banks NEVER ask for them.

[QUESTION 5 - 5 MARKS]
Q: (a) What is a Webinar? Explain its two hallmark characteristics.
   (b) How does Synchronous online learning differ from Asynchronous E-Learning?
Model Answer:
(a) Webinar Definition & Characteristics:
- A Webinar (Web + Seminar) is an interactive, real-time multimedia presentation or lecture delivered over the Internet.
- Characteristic 1: Two-Way Bidirectional Interactivity (audio, video, live chat, live polls, and dedicated Q&A pods).
- Characteristic 2: Real-time Screen Sharing and collaborative digital whiteboards.
(b) Synchronous vs Asynchronous Learning:
- Synchronous Learning occurs live in real time at scheduled intervals with active teacher-student interaction (e.g., Zoom, Google Meet). Complex doubts are cleared instantly.
- Asynchronous Learning is self-paced where learners access pre-recorded video lectures and readings at their convenience (e.g., Coursera, edX, SWAYAM). Doubt resolution is delayed via forums.
