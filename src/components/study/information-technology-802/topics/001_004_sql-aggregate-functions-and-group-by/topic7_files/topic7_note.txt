1. CORE CONCEPT SUMMARY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[1] Multi-Column Grouping divides rows by unique combinations of two or more columns.
[2] Syntax: GROUP BY col1, col2 (e.g. GROUP BY Department, Stream).
[3] Subgroups are created for each discrete (col1, col2) pair.
[4] Useful in enterprise business analytics, payroll matrices, and departmental breakdowns.

2. PRACTICAL SQL BENCHMARK SCRIPT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
-- Multi-Column Grouping
USE SchoolDB;

-- Group by City AND Stream
SELECT 
  City,
  Stream,
  COUNT(*) AS StudentCount,
  AVG(TotalMarks) AS AvgMarks
FROM Student
GROUP BY City, Stream
ORDER BY City ASC, Stream ASC;
