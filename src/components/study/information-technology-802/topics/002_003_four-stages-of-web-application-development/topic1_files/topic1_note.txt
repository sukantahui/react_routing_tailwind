1. WHAT IS REQUIREMENT DEFINITION / ANALYSIS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage 1 is the discovery and scoping phase. It determines 'WHAT' the web application 
must do, who will use it, under what constraints it will operate, and whether the 
project is viable to undertake.

2. CORE ACTIVITIES IN STAGE 1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Fact-Finding & Requirement Elicitation:
   - Stakeholder Interviews: One-on-one sessions with managers, cashiers, and users.
   - Questionnaires & Surveys: Collecting structured inputs from distributed users.
   - Document Analysis: Examining existing paper bill books, receipts, and ledger registers.
   - On-Site Observation: Watching current manual bottlenecks and recording timings.

2. Feasibility Analysis:
   - Technical Feasibility: Do we have the hardware, database servers, and coding skills?
   - Economic Feasibility (Cost-Benefit): Will business savings exceed development costs?
   - Operational Feasibility: Will employees willingly adopt the new web portal?
   - Schedule Feasibility: Can we launch before the required business deadline?

3. Functional vs Non-Functional Requirements:
   - Functional Requirements: Specific system behaviors and features (e.g. Barcode scanning, 
     calculating 18% GST, generating printable PDF tax invoices, searching customer records).
   - Non-Functional Requirements: System quality attributes (e.g. Page load under 1.5 seconds, 
     handling 5,000 concurrent users, 99.9% uptime, AES-256 password encryption).

4. Scope Definition & Boundary Management:
   - Explicitly documenting what is INCLUDED and what is EXCLUDED to prevent 'Scope Creep'.

3. THE PRIMARY DELIVERABLE: THE SRS DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Software Requirements Specification (SRS) is the formal contract and technical 
blueprint produced at the end of Stage 1.

Key Sections of an Authentic SRS (e.g. Online Billing System):
1. Introduction & Project Scope.
2. User Personas & Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Cashier, Customer, Auditor).
3. Detailed Functional Requirements & Use Cases.
4. Non-Functional Criteria (Performance, Security, Concurrency, Availability).
5. External Software & Hardware Dependencies (MySQL 8.0, Java 17, Apache Tomcat).
6. Business Rules & Operational Constraints.

4. BENGALI SUMMARY / শিক্ষক নির্দেশিকা
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Requirement Definition বা প্রথম ধাপে মূলত ক্লায়েন্টের চাহিদা বোঝা হয়। এর প্রধান কাজ হলো:
- ইন্টারভিউ ও প্রশ্নপত্রের মাধ্যমে প্রয়োজনীয় তথ্য সংগ্রহ করা।
- সম্ভাব্যতা যাচাই (Technical ও Economic Feasibility) করা।
- সফটওয়্যারের কাজের পরিধি (Scope) নির্দিষ্ট করা যাতে স্কোপ ক্রিপ না ঘটে।
- সমস্ত তথ্য দিয়ে একটি আনুষ্ঠানিক SRS (Software Requirements Specification) দলিল তৈরি করা।
