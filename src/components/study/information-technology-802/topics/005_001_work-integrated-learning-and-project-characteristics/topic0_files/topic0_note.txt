TOPIC 0: What is Work-Integrated Learning (WIL)?
================================================================================

1. DEFINITION OF WORK-INTEGRATED LEARNING (WIL):
------------------------------------------------
- Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) is an educational model that intentionally integrates academic curriculum and theoretical concepts with authentic, hands-on workplace or industry experience.
- It provides a structured bridge between classroom learning and practical professional execution.

2. CORE PILLARS OF WIL:
-----------------------
1. Academic Grounding: Theoretical knowledge acquired in schools/classrooms (e.g. Java OOP, MySQL RDBMS, Web Architecture).
2. Authentic Workplace Practice: Practical application on real client requirements, school management systems, or industry internships.
3. Structured Reflection & Evaluation: Students reflect on workplace challenges, receiving feedback from both academic teachers and industry mentors.

3. FORMS OF WIL IN CBSE IT 802:
-------------------------------
- Industry Internships / Apprenticeships (Summer training).
- Live Client Projects (Developing a Fee Collection system or Library Database).
- Capstone School Projects (Building a multi-table Java Swing application for the school administration).
- Field Visits and Industry Expert Workshops.

4. THE TRIPARTITE WIL RELATIONSHIP:
-----------------------------------
- **Student**: Gains employability, technical competence, teamwork, and professional ethics.
- **Educational Institute**: Keeps curriculum relevant, updated, and aligned with market demands.
- **Industry Partner**: Accesses motivated junior talent and contributes to workforce readiness.
