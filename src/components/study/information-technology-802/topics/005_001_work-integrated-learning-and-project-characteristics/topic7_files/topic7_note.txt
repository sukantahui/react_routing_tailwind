1. MODULE 005_001 MASTER REVISION CHECKLIST:
--------------------------------------------
1. WIL Definition: Educational strategy combining academic classroom studies with practical workplace experience.
2. Tripartite Partnership: Students + Educational Institutions + Industry Employers.
3. Industry Benefits: Fresh talent pipeline, reduced recruitment & onboarding costs.
4. Student Benefits: High employability, practical portfolio, teamwork, and professional ethics.
5. Project Definition: A temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result.
6. Core Project Characteristics: Definite start/end, defined scope, finite resources, distinct boundaries.
7. What is NOT a Project: Perpetual duration, infinite funding, boundary-less requests (Scope Creep).
8. Triple Constraint (Iron Triangle): Interdependence of Scope, Time, and Cost in determining Quality.
9. 6 SDLC Phases: Conception -> Requirement Definition (SRS) -> Design (ER/UI) -> Implementation (Code) -> Testing -> Deployment.
10. School Capstone Projects: School Library, Fee Management, and Report Card systems linking Java GUI to MySQL.
