TOPIC 3: Core Characteristics of a Project

1. THE 4 ESSENTIAL CHARACTERISTICS OF A PROJECT:
------------------------------------------------
1. **Definite Beginning and End (Time-Bound)**:
   - Starts at a specific calendar date and concludes upon deliverable sign-off or target milestone achievement.
   - It is never perpetual or infinite.

2. **Defined Scope and Extent**:
   - Outlines exactly what features, reports, database tables, and user interfaces must be built.
   - Clearly documents both in-scope requirements and out-of-scope exclusions.

3. **Finite Resources (Budget, Team, Infrastructure)**:
   - Constrained by limited financial capital (e.g. ₹50,000 budget), developer headcount (e.g. 3 student developers), and equipment limits.

4. **Distinct Boundaries**:
   - Establishes a transparent boundary around project responsibilities to prevent "scope creep" (uncontrolled expansion of project scope).

2. THE PROJECT MANAGEMENT TRIPLE CONSTRAINT (THE IRON TRIANGLE):
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every project is governed by the interdependence of three variables:
- **Scope**: What work will be done?
- **Time**: How long will it take?
- **Cost**: What budget/resources are allocated?
*Quality* sits at the center, constrained by balance across these three pillars.

3. CBSE BOARD EXAMINATION REVISION SUMMARY:
-------------------------------------------
- When asked to list 3-4 characteristics of a project, write:
  1. Temporary nature with a definite beginning and end.
  2. Creates a unique product, service, or result.
  3. Governed by a defined scope and distinct boundaries.
  4. Executed using finite financial, human, and hardware resources.
