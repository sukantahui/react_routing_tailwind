1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVES OF WIL IN IT VOCATIONAL EDUCATION:
--------------------------------------------------------
1. **Bridging the Academic-Industry Gap**: Converting abstract algorithmic knowledge into commercial software products.
2. **Developing Core Technical Competencies**: Hands-on mastery in database modeling, backend logic, debugging, and UI design.
3. **Nurturing Professional Soft Skills**: Team collaboration, client communication, sprint planning, and deadline management.
4. **Instilling Professional Workplace Ethics**: Code confidentiality, software licensing, data privacy, and intellectual property respect.

2. MULTI-STAKEHOLDER BENEFITS MATRIX:
-------------------------------------
A. Benefits for Students:
   - High graduate employability and market competitiveness.
   - Creation of an authentic project portfolio.
   - Clear understanding of IT industry career paths.
   - Real-world problem-solving confidence.

B. Benefits for Educational Institutions (Schools):
   - Curriculum stays cutting-edge and aligned with industry standards.
   - Stronger school reputation and placement records.
   - Collaborative research and teacher development partnerships with IT firms.

C. Benefits for Industry Partners:
   - Early access to a screened, motivated talent pipeline.
   - Lower onboarding time and reduced long-term hiring expenses.
   - Cost-effective solutions for non-critical internal software tools.

3. CBSE BOARD EXAM QUICK POINTS:
--------------------------------
- Question Pattern: "State any two benefits of Work-Integrated Learning for IT students."
- Ideal Response:
  1. Equips students with practical industry experience and hands-on coding competence.
  2. Enhances professional soft skills such as teamwork, communication, and client problem-solving.
