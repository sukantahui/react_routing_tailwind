5. SENIOR ARCHITECTURAL PITFALLS & BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- PITFALL: Race-Around Condition in level-triggered JK flip-flops when clock pulse width
  t_p is greater than propagation delay t_pd (Output toggles continuously).
  CURE: Use Master-Slave JK flip-flop or edge-triggered flip-flop architectures.
- PITFALL: Static-1 and Static-0 Hazards in combinational logic caused by unequal gate delays.
  CURE: Add redundant consensus terms in K-Map groupings.
- BEST PRACTICE: Always synchronize asynchronous external inputs using a 2-stage D flip-flop
  synchronizer before feeding into sequential state machines.
- BEST PRACTICE: Use active-low chip enables (G1, G2A, G2B) for cascading multi-chip decoders.

6. TEACHER'S MASTER SUMMARY - SIR SUKANTA HUI

"In digital electronics, never treat gates as instantaneous abstract logic symbols.
Every physical gate takes 2 to 10 nanoseconds to propagate charge through silicon.
When you design counters, decoders, or state machines, always draw the timing diagram!
Timing is the difference between working hardware and a glitchy prototype!"
