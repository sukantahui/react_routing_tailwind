--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This topic provides an exhaustive theoretical and practical deep-dive into:

Understanding hardware number systems, binary codes, and register representations

and low-level firmware engineering.

2. FORMAL THEORETICAL FOUNDATIONS & MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In digital hardware architectures:
- Representation of signed numbers: Sign-Magnitude, 1's Complement, and 2's Complement.
- Range of n-bit signed integers in 2's Complement: [-2^(n-1) to +2^(n-1) - 1].
- Overflow Condition: Occurs when adding two numbers of the same sign produces a result
  with the opposite sign, or when Carry-In (C_in) to MSB != Carry-Out (C_out) from MSB (V = C_n XOR C_(n-1)).
- Binary Coded Decimal (BCD): 8421 weighted binary representation where each decimal
  digit is encoded in 4 bits; valid range 0000 to 1001 (0 to 9); values 1010 to 1111 are illegal.
- Gray Code (Reflected Binary): Unit-distance code where adjacent numbers differ by
  only 1 bit, eliminating multi-bit switching glitches in optical shaft encoders.

3. CONCRETE STEP-BY-STEP WORKED EXAMPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Example: 8-Bit Arithmetic & 2's Complement Representation
Decimal Value: -25
1. Positive binary (25): (00011001)_2
2. 1's Complement (invert bits): 11100110
3. 2's Complement (add 1): 11100111 = (-25)_10

Arithmetic Check (45 - 25 = 20):
  +45: 00101101
  -25: 11100111
  Sum: 1 00010100 (End carry discarded) → Result: 00010100 = (20)_10. Verified!

5. SENIOR ARCHITECTURAL PITFALLS & BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- PITFALL: Confusing 1's complement with 2's complement; 1's complement possesses
  two representations of zero (+0 and -0), complicating ALU zero-detector logic.
- PITFALL: Ignoring bit-width expansion during signed addition (e.g. sign-extending 8-bit to 16-bit).
- BEST PRACTICE: Always use 2's complement for integer representation in CPU architectures.
- BEST PRACTICE: Use hardware XOR gate between C_in and C_out of MSB to generate the ALU Overflow (V) flag.
