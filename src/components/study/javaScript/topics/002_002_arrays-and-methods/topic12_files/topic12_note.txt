3. EXCEPTIONS, CORNER CASES, COERCIONS & QUIRKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[!] RangeError: Invalid typed array length: Allocating buffer sizes exceeding available system memory.
[!] Byte Alignment Offset Errors: Creating a `Uint32Array` on an unaligned byte offset (must be a multiple of 4 bytes).
[!] Non-Resizable Fixed Buffers: Standard ArrayBuffers cannot be resized; use `SharedArrayBuffer` or copy to a larger buffer.
