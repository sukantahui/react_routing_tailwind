MASTER SUMMARY OF SENTENCE CLASSIFICATION BY COMMUNICATIVE PURPOSE:

1. DECLARATIVE (ASSERTIVE):
   - Purpose: Statements of fact, belief, or opinion.
   - Types: Affirmative ("Sun rises in the east") & Negative ("He is not here").
   - Terminal mark: Period (.)

2. INTERROGATIVE:
   - Yes/No: Formed by Subject-Auxiliary Inversion (Aux + Sub + Verb).
   - Wh- Questions: Wh-word + Aux + Sub + Verb.
   - Question Tags: Statement + Tag Pronoun + Auxiliary (Polarity Inversion).

3. IMPERATIVE:
   - Purpose: Commands, requests, directions, invitations, warnings.
   - Subject: Implied/Understood 2nd person "(You)".
   - Verb Form: Base verb (V1) bare infinitive.

4. EXCLAMATORY:
   - Purpose: Spontaneous outpouring of intense emotion, wonder, shock.
   - Formulas: "What a + [Adj] + [Noun] + [S] + [V]!" or "How + [Adj/Adv] + [S] + [V]!"
   - Terminal mark: Exclamation mark (!)

5. OPTATIVE:
   - Purpose: Wishes, blessings, prayers, solemn hopes.
   - Formulas: "May + [Sub] + [V1]!" or Elliptical ("Long live the King!").

BENGALI CHEAT SHEET (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ):
- Assertive: সাধারণ বিবৃতিমূলক বাক্য (.)
- Interrogative: প্রশ্নবোধক বাক্য (?)
- Imperative: আদেশ, উপদেশ বা অনুরোধমূলক বাক্য (.)
- Exclamatory: আবেগ বা বিস্ময়সূচক বাক্য (!)
- Optative: প্রার্থনা বা শুভকামনামূলক বাক্য (!)
