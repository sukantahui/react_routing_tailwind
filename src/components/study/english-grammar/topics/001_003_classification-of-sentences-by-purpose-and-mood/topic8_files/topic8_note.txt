1. THE 5 COMMUNICATIVE SENTENCE ARCHITECTURES (MASTER MATRIX)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+---+---------------+----------------------+--------------------+---------------+
| # | Sentence Type | Core Function        | Syntactic Formula  | Terminal Mark |
+---+---------------+----------------------+--------------------+---------------+
| 1 | Assertive     | States facts/opinion | S + V + O/C/A      | Period (.)    |
| 2 | Interrogative | Seeks info / confirm | Aux + S + V ... ?  | Question (?)  |
| 3 | Imperative    | Commands / Requests  | Base V1 (You)      | Period / (!)  |
| 4 | Exclamatory   | Spontaneous emotion  | What a / How + S+V | Exclamation(!)|
| 5 | Optative      | Prayers / Blessings  | May + S + V1       | Exclamation(!)|
+---+---------------+----------------------+--------------------+---------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. QUESTION TAG SYNTACTIC INVARIANTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* POLARITY PRINCIPLE: Positive Statement <===> Negative Tag; Negative Statement <===> Positive Tag.
* SPECIAL TAG CATALOG:
  - "I am right"                ===> "aren't I?"
  - "I am not late"             ===> "am I?"
  - "Let's proceed"             ===> "shall we?"
  - "Let us work" (permission)  ===> "will you?"
  - "Close the door"            ===> "will you? / won't you?"
  - "Don't touch"               ===> "will you?"
  - "He seldom studies"         ===> "does he?" (Semi-negative -> Positive tag)
  - "Everybody was present"     ===> "weren't they?" (Indefinite person -> 'they')
  - "Nothing happened"          ===> "did it?" (Inanimate entity -> 'it')
  - "There is hope"             ===> "isn't there?" (Existential 'there' retained)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MASTER TRANSFORMATION CHEATSHEET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* AFFIRMATIVE <===> NEGATIVE:
  - "Man is mortal"          <===> "Man is NOT immortal."
  - "Only Swadeep came"      <===> "NONE BUT Swadeep came."
  - "He is too weak to walk" <===> "He is SO weak THAT he CANNOT walk."
  - "As soon as he came..."  <===> "NO SOONER DID he come THAN..."

* ASSERTIVE <===> INTERROGATIVE (RHETORICAL):
  - "Everyone loves freedom" <===> "WHO DOES NOT love freedom?"
  - "Nobody can bear this"   <===> "WHO CAN bear this?"

* EXCLAMATORY <===> ASSERTIVE:
  - "What a pity it is!"     <===> "It is a GREAT pity."
  - "How fast he ran!"       <===> "He ran VERY fast."

* IMPERATIVE <===> ASSERTIVE:
  - "Please help me"         <===> "You are REQUESTED to help me."
  - "Shut the door"          <===> "You are ORDERED to shut the door."

* OPTATIVE <===> ASSERTIVE:
  - "May you prosper!"       <===> "I PRAY / WISH THAT you may prosper."
