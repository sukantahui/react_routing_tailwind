1. CORE DEFINITION & COMMUNICATIVE ESSENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An Optative sentence expresses a prayer, blessing, benediction, heartfelt wish, 
or solemn curse/imprecation.

* Structural Hallmark: Starts with the modal auxiliary 'May' or employs an 
  elliptical subjunctive root, terminating with an Exclamation Mark (!).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE TWO PRIMARY SYNTACTIC FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORMULA 1: EXPLICIT 'MAY' CONSTRUCTION
  [ MAY ] + [ SUBJECT ] + [ BASE VERB (V1) ] + [ COMPLEMENT / OBJECT ] !
  - "May God shower blessings upon your family!"
  - "May you achieve the highest honor in your career!"
  - "May peace reign across our nation!"

FORMULA 2: ELLIPTICAL SUBJUNCTIVE ROOT (UNDERSTOOD 'MAY')
  Used in traditional greetings, royal proclamations, and solemn prayers:
  - "Long live the Republic of India!" (= [May] the Republic live long!)
  - "God save the President!"         (= [May] God save the President!)
  - "Wish you a speedy recovery!"      (= [I] wish you a speedy recovery!)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. OPTATIVE VS INTERROGATIVE 'MAY' CLARIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Feature           | Optative Sentence          | Interrogative Sentence      |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Purpose           | Blessing, Wish, or Prayer  | Seeking Permission / Inquiry|
| Final Punctuation | Exclamation Mark (!)       | Question Mark (?)           |
| Exemplar          | "May you prosper in life!" | "May I enter the hall?"     |
| Meaning           | "I pray that you prosper." | "Do I have permission?"     |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INDIRECT NARRATION OF OPTATIVE SENTENCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Direct:   The mentor said to Swadeep, "May you master React and Node.js!"
Indirect: The mentor PRAYED / WISHED THAT Swadeep MIGHT master React and Node.js.

* Key Transformations:
  1. Reporting verb becomes 'prayed' or 'wished'.
  2. Conjunction 'that' connects the clauses.
  3. 'May' backshifts to past modal 'might'.
  4. Punctuation converts to a standard Period (.).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & EXAM TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INFINITIVE WITH 'TO' TRAP:
   * INCORRECT: *May God to protect you!
   * CORRECT:    May God protect you! (Modals require bare infinitive).

2. SUBJUNCTIVE S-ENDING TRAP:
   * INCORRECT: *Long lives the President!
   * CORRECT:    Long LIVE the President! (Base form subjunctive).
