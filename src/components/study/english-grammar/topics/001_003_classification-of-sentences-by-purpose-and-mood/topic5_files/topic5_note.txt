1. WHAT IS AN OPTATIVE SENTENCE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: A sentence that articulates an earnest prayer, pious wish, 
  blessing, or solemn invocation.
- Structural Formula:
  [ MAY ] + [ SUBJECT ] + [ BASE VERB (V1) ] ... [ ! / . ]
- Examples:
  * "May you achieve the highest distinction in your academic career!"
  * "May God grant you health, wisdom, and peace!"

2. ELLIPTICAL OPTATIVE FORMULAS (WITHOUT SURFACE 'MAY')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In traditional and literary prose, 'May' is often understood:
  * "Long live the Republic!" (=[May] the Republic live long).
  * "God save the nation!"    (=[May] God save the nation).

3. REPORTED SPEECH (NARRATION) RULE FOR OPTATIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Direct:   The mentor said to Swadeep, "May you prosper in life!"
Indirect: The mentor PRAYED / WISHED that Swadeep MIGHT prosper in life.
