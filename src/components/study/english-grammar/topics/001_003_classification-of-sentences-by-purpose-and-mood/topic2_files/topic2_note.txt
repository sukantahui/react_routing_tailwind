1. THE POLARITY DYNAMICS OF QUESTION TAGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Positive Statement -> Negative Tag:
  "Swadeep is a software engineer, [isn't he]?"
- Negative Statement -> Positive Tag:
  "Tuhina does not skip classes, [does she]?"

2. HIGH-FREQUENCY EXCEPTION REPOSITORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. "I am" -> aren't I? (e.g. "I am late, aren't I?").
2. "Let's" -> shall we? (e.g. "Let's begin the exam, shall we?").
3. Imperatives -> will you? / won't you? (e.g. "Close the door, will you?").
4. Semi-Negative Words (hardly, seldom, rarely, barely, scarcely, neither, few, little)
   -> REQUIRE POSITIVE TAGS:
   * "She seldom speaks in public, [does she]?" ✔️
   * "There is little water in the tank, [is there]?" ✔️
5. Indefinite Pronouns (Everyone, Somebody, Nobody) -> Plural Tag Pronoun 'THEY':
   * "Nobody came, [did they]?" ✔️
   * "Everyone was thrilled, [weren't they]?" ✔️
6. Introductory 'There' -> Repeats 'there':
   * "There is a server error, [isn't there]?" ✔️
