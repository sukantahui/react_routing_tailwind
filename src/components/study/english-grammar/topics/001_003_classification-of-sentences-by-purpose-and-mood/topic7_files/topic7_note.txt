1. THE GOLDEN INVARIANT OF TRANSFORMATION:
   - Form changes; Meaning, Truth Value, and Propositional Essence REMAIN CONSTANT.
   - Never flip polarity without adding an opposing semantic modifier (antonym/negative operator).

2. KEY PATTERNS & TRANSFORMATION FORMULAS:
   a. Affirmative to Negative:
      - Always + Positive  -->  Never + Antonym
        (e.g., "She is always honest" --> "She is never dishonest")
      - Only / Alone (Person) --> None but
        (e.g., "Only God can save us" --> "None but God can save us")
      - Too... to  -->  So... that + cannot / could not
        (e.g., "He is too old to run" --> "He is so old that he cannot run")

   b. Assertive to Interrogative (Rhetorical Questions):
      - Positive Statement --> Negative Question
        ("He is a true patriot" --> "Is he not a true patriot?")
      - Negative Statement --> Positive Question
        ("Nobody can touch the sky" --> "Who can touch the sky?")
      - Universal Subject ("Everyone / All") --> "Who does not...?"
        ("Everyone wants peace" --> "Who does not want peace?")

   c. Exclamatory to Assertive:
      - "What a + [Adj] + [Noun]!" --> "It is a very [Adj] [Noun]."
      - "How + [Adj] + [Sub] + [Verb]!" --> "[Sub] + [Verb] + very/extremely [Adj]."
      - "O that / If only I were a child again!" --> "I strongly wish that I were a child again."

   d. Optative to Assertive:
      - "May God bless you!" --> "I pray that God may bless you."
      - "Long live the King!" --> "We wish that the King may live long."

3. BENGALI SUMMARY (সারসংক্ষেপ):
   বাক্য রূপান্তরের প্রধান নীতি হল: কাঠামোর পরিবর্তন ঘটলেও মূল অর্থের কোনো পরিবর্তন হবে না।
   - Positive বাক্যকে Negative করতে বিপরীত শব্দ (Antonym) ব্যবহার করুন।
   - জোর দেওয়ার জন্য বা অলংকারিক প্রশ্নে রূপান্তরের ক্ষেত্রে Polarity পরিবর্তন আবশ্যক।
