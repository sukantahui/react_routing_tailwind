1. YES/NO QUESTIONS (SUBJECT-AUXILIARY INVERSION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Formula: [ AUXILIARY VERB ] + [ SUBJECT ] + [ MAIN VERB ] ... ?
- Examples:
  * "He is working." -> "Is he working?"
  * "They have solved it." -> "Have they solved it?"
  * "Swadeep codes in Python." -> "Does Swadeep code in Python?" (Dummy 'Does')
  * "They attended yesterday." -> "Did they attend yesterday?" (Dummy 'Did')

2. WH- INFORMATION QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Wh- Word as OBJECT:
   - Formula: [ Wh- Word ] + [ Auxiliary ] + [ Subject ] + [ Main Verb ] ?
   - Example: "What did you buy?" / "Where does she live?" / "Why were they late?"

B. Wh- Word as SUBJECT (NO INVERSION!):
   - Formula: [ Wh- Subject ] + [ Finite Verb ] + [ Object ] ?
   - Example: "Who wrote this code?" (Never "Who did write this code?").

3. EMBEDDED / INDIRECT QUESTIONS (NO INVERSION!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In noun clauses following verbs like wonder, ask, know:
  * Wrong: "I do not know where does he live." ❌
  * Right: "I do not know where he lives." ✔️

4. BENGALI LEARNER PEDAGOGICAL TRAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Bengali: "তুমি কেন সেখানে গেলে?" (পদক্রম পরিবর্তন হয় না).
* English Error: "Why you went there?" ❌
* English Correction: "Why did you go there?" ✔️ (Inversion Mandatory!)
