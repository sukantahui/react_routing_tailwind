1. CORE STRUCTURAL CLASSIFICATION OF QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Interrogative sentences seek information, confirmation, or performative interaction.
They fall into 4 primary syntactic architectures:

A. YES/NO QUESTIONS (POLAR QUESTIONS)
   * Function: Seeking binary confirmation (True/False, Yes/No).
   * Formula 1 (With Auxiliary/Modal/Copula Be):
     [ AUXILIARY / MODAL / COPULAR BE ] + [ SUBJECT ] + [ MAIN VERB ] + ... ?
     - "Are you studying?" | "Has Swadeep submitted the module?" | "Can you solve this?"
   * Formula 2 (Simple Present / Simple Past - No Auxiliary):
     [ DO / DOES / DID ] + [ SUBJECT ] + [ BASE VERB (V1) ] + ... ?
     - "Do they live in Barrackpore?" | "Did Debangshu attend the seminar?"

B. WH- QUESTIONS (INFORMATION QUESTIONS)
   * Function: Inquiring about specific entities, places, times, reasons, or methods.
   * Type 1: Wh- as OBJECT / ADVERBIAL (Mandatory Inversion):
     [ WH- WORD ] + [ AUXILIARY / DO-OPERATOR ] + [ SUBJECT ] + [ BASE VERB ] + ... ?
     - "Where do you practice coding?" | "Why did the system crash?"
   * Type 2: Wh- as SUBJECT (NO Inversion, NO Dummy Do):
     [ WH- WORD (Subject) ] + [ FINITE VERB ] + [ OBJECT / COMPLEMENT ] + ... ?
     - "Who developed this algorithm?" (NOT *Who did develop...?)

C. ALTERNATIVE QUESTIONS
   * Offers closed choices using 'or': "Will you take tea or coffee?"

D. RHETORICAL QUESTIONS
   * Formulated as questions but functioning as powerful emphatic assertions:
   * "Who does not desire success?" (= Everyone desires success).

2. WH- OPERATOR CATALOG & INTERROGATIVE FUNCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+------------+--------------------+---------------------------------------------+
| Wh- Word   | Target Inquiry     | Structural Exemplar                         |
+------------+--------------------+---------------------------------------------+
| Who        | Subject (Person)   | "Who won the top scholarship?"              |
| Whom       | Object (Person)    | "Whom did you consult at the desk?"         |
| Whose      | Possessor / Owner  | "Whose code passed the unit test?"          |
| What       | Entity / Activity  | "What caused the rendering error?"          |
| Which      | Choice (Restricted)| "Which of these two algorithms is optimal?" |
| Where      | Location / Place   | "Where is the Barrackpore campus situated?" |
| When       | Time / Temporal    | "When does the evening batch commence?"     |
| Why        | Reason / Cause     | "Why did the client request revisions?"     |
| How        | Manner / Quality   | "How do you optimize bundle size in Vite?"  |
| How many   | Countable Quantity | "How many students enrolled today?"         |
| How much   | Uncountable Amount | "How much memory does the cache consume?"   |
| How often  | Frequency          | "How often do you deploy updates?"          |
| How long   | Duration / Length  | "How long did the diagnostic exam last?"    |

3. EMBEDDED & INDIRECT QUESTIONS (THE NO-INVERSION RULE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a question is embedded inside a polite introductory matrix clause:
- Matrix: "Could you please tell me..." / "Do you know..." / "I wonder..."
- RULE: The subordinate clause REVERTS TO DECLARATIVE ORDER (Subject + Verb).

INCORRECT: *Could you tell me where is the railway station?
CORRECT:    Could you tell me where the railway station is?

INCORRECT: *Do you know what time does the train arrive?
CORRECT:    Do you know what time the train arrives?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & EXAM TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MISSING AUXILIARY TRAP:
   * Bengali: "তুমি কাল কেন এলে না?" (No inversion concept in Bengali syntax).
   * INCORRECT: *Why you did not come yesterday?
   * CORRECT:    Why didn't you come yesterday? / Why did you not come yesterday?

2. DOUBLE PAST TENSE TRAP:
   * INCORRECT: *Did you finished the work?
   * CORRECT:    Did you finish the work? ('did' absorbs the tense; verb stays V1).

3. WH- SUBJECT VS OBJECT TRAP:
   * Who called you? (Subject question: 'Who' performed the call -> No 'did').
   * Who did you call? (Object question: 'You' called 'whom' -> Requires 'did').
