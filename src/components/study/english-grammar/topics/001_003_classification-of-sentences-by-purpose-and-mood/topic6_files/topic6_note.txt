1. SPECIAL QUESTION TAG INVARIANTS DISSECTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Pronoun 'THEY' vs 'IT':
   - Personal Indefinites (Everyone, Somebody, Nobody) -> THEY.
     * "Nobody came, did [they]?"
   - Non-Personal Indefinites (Everything, Something, Nothing) -> IT.
     * "Nothing happened, did [it]?"
     * "Everything is fine, isn't [it]?"

2. 'LET US' VS 'LET HIM / LET THEM':
   - "Let us (Let's) examine the code, [shall we]?" (Joint Proposal)
   - "Let him leave the room, [will you]?" (Permission / Order to listener)

3. IMPATIENT IMPERATIVES:
   - "Shut up, [can't you]?" (Expressing annoyance)
   - "Have a seat, [won't you]?" (Polite invitation)

2. TONE & INTONATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Rising Pitch (↗): Genuine Question / Verification needed.
- Falling Pitch (↘): Confirmation / Speaker is confident of the truth.
