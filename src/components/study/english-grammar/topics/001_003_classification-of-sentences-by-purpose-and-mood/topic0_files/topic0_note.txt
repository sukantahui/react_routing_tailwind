1. THE FIVE COMMUNICATIVE SENTENCE CATEGORIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ASSERTIVE / DECLARATIVE (Making Statements / Facts):
   - Affirmative: "Swadeep studies computer science."
   - Negative:    "Tuhina does not neglect her revisions."
   - Punctuation: Period (.)

2. INTERROGATIVE (Asking Questions):
   - Wh- Questions (Information): "Where is the coaching center located?"
   - Yes/No Questions (Inversion): "Did you complete the assignment?"
   - Question Tags (Confirmation): "You are coming, aren't you?"
   - Rhetorical Questions (Effect): "Who does not desire happiness?"
   - Punctuation: Question Mark (?)

3. IMPERATIVE (Commands, Requests, Advice, Proposals):
   - Command / Order: "Stand up immediately!"
   - Polite Request:  "Please pass the dictionary."
   - Advice:          "Always double-check your calculations."
   - Proposal / Sug.: "Let us analyze the problem together."
   - Subject: Implied second-person '[You]'.
   - Punctuation: Period (.) or Exclamation Mark (!).

4. EXCLAMATORY (Sudden Emotion / Wonder / Grief / Joy):
   - "What a spectacular goal!"
   - "How gracefully she recited the poem!"
   - Punctuation: Exclamation Mark (!).

5. OPTATIVE (Wishes, Prayers, Blessings, Curses):
   - "May God bless you with prosperity!"
   - "Long live the Republic!" (Implied 'May').
   - Punctuation: Exclamation Mark (!) or Period (.).

2. MASTER QUESTION TAG MATRIX (POLARITY SHIFT RULES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+--------------------+----------------------+-------------------+--------------+
| Statement Type     | Main Sentence        | Correct Tag       | Rule Logic   |
+--------------------+----------------------+-------------------+--------------+
| Positive Statement | "He is ready."       | ", isn't he?"     | Pos -> Neg   |
| Negative Statement | "He is not ready."   | ", is he?"        | Neg -> Pos   |
| 'I am' Special     | "I am correct."      | ", aren't I?"     | Amn't is null|
| 'Let's' Proposal   | "Let's begin."       | ", shall we?"     | Fixed tag    |
| Imperative Request | "Help me."           | ", will you?"     | Request tag  |
| Negative Imperative| "Don't move."        | ", will you?"     | Pos tag      |
| Negative Pronouns  | "Nobody called."     | ", did they?"     | Plural they  |
| Inanimate Pronouns | "Nothing happened."  | ", did it?"       | Singular it  |
| Semi-Negatives     | "She seldom speaks." | ", does she?"     | Positive tag |
| Introductory There | "There is time."     | ", isn't there?"  | Echo 'there' |
+--------------------+----------------------+-------------------+--------------+

3. BENGALI LEARNER PEDAGOGICAL BRIDGES (বাংলা মাধ্যম শিক্ষার্থীদের জন্য)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Trap 1: Bengali Question Tag Translation
  - Bengali colloquial: "তুমি যাবে, তাই তো?"
  - Common Mistake: "You will go, is it?" ❌
  - Correct English: "You will go, WON'T YOU?" ✔️

* Trap 2: Indirect Question Inversion Error
  - Incorrect: "He asked me where DID I live." ❌
  - Correct:   "He asked me where I LIVED." ✔️
  - Rule: Subordinate noun clauses in indirect questions follow Declarative
    word order (Subject + Verb), never Inverted order!
