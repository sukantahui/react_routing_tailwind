1. THE TWO STRUCTURAL TEMPLATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Template A: Using "WHAT (A/AN)" (For Noun Phrases):
  Formula: WHAT + (a/an) + [Adjective] + [Noun] + [Subject + Verb] !
  Example: "What a [magnificent cathedral] [it is]!"
  Plural:  "What [brilliant ideas] [they have]!" (No 'a/an' with plurals).

Template B: Using "HOW" (For Bare Adjectives or Adverbs):
  Formula: HOW + [Adjective / Adverb] + [Subject + Verb] !
  Example: "How [sweet] [the rose smells]!"
  Example: "How [flawlessly] [she programmed the system]!"

2. ASSERTIVE <-> EXCLAMATORY TRANSFORMATION FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Assertive:    "The view is very charming."
Exclamatory:  "How charming the view is!"

Assertive:    "It is a truly extraordinary achievement."
Exclamatory:  "What an extraordinary achievement it is!"

Assertive:    "I wish I were a bird."
Exclamatory:  "If only I were a bird!" / "O that I were a bird!"

3. PUNCTUATION LAW WITH INTERJECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Interjection + ! + Complete Assertive Sentence + .
  * "Hurrah! We have won the championship."
  * "Alas! The noble leader has passed away."
