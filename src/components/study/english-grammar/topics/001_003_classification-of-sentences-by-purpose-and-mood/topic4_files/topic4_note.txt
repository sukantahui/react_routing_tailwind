1. CORE DEFINITION & FUNCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An Exclamatory sentence gives voice to sudden, intense emotion (amazement, joy, 
grief, disgust, admiration, or alarm). It always concludes with an Exclamation Mark (!).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE TWO MASTER ARCHITECTURAL PATTERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPLATE A: 'WHAT (A / AN)' + NOUN PHRASE
* Used when qualifying a noun head (with or without an adjective).
  1. Singular Countable Nouns (Take 'a/an'):
     Formula: [ WHAT A / AN ] + [ ADJECTIVE ] + [ NOUN ] + [ S ] + [ V ] !
     - "What a spectacular presentation Swadeep delivered!"
  2. Plural Countable Nouns (NO article):
     Formula: [ WHAT ] + [ ADJECTIVE ] + [ PLURAL NOUN ] + [ S ] + [ V ] !
     - "What magnificent monuments we visited in Barrackpore!"
  3. Uncountable Mass Nouns (NO article):
     Formula: [ WHAT ] + [ ADJECTIVE ] + [ UNCOUNTABLE NOUN ] + [ S ] + [ V ] !
     - "What delightful music they played!" (NOT *What a delightful music!)

TEMPLATE B: 'HOW' + ADJECTIVE / ADVERB
* Used when directly qualifying a bare adjective or adverb without a nominal head.
  Formula: [ HOW ] + [ ADJECTIVE / ADVERB ] + [ S ] + [ V ] !
  - "How elegantly Debangshu structured the algorithm!"
  - "How profound the mentor's insights are!"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EXCLAMATORY <===> ASSERTIVE TRANSFORMATION MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Rule: Replace 'How' with 'very / extremely'; replace 'What a' with 'a great / wonderful / terrible'.
  - Exclamatory: "What a terrible storm it was!"
  - Assertive:   "It was a very terrible storm."

  - Exclamatory: "How fast time flies!"
  - Assertive:   "Time flies very fast."

  - Exclamatory: "If only I could see him once!"
  - Assertive:   "I earnestly wish I could see him once."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & EXAM TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. UNCOUNTABLE ARTICLE TRAP:
   * Bengali: "কী চমৎকার আবহাওয়া!"
   * INCORRECT: *What a pleasant weather!
   * CORRECT:    What pleasant weather! (Weather is uncountable).

2. INVERSION TRAP:
   * INCORRECT: *How fast did he run! (Inversion is for questions only).
   * CORRECT:    How fast he ran! (Subject precedes Verb).
