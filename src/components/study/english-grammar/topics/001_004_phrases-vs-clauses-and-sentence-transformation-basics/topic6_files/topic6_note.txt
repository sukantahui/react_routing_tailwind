1. THE POLARITY INVERSION PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Rhetorical Question is an assertion in the disguise of an inquiry.

* RULE 1: Affirmative Assertive  ===>  Negative Interrogative (Contracted)
  "Everyone loves freedom."      ===>  "WHO DOES NOT love freedom?"
  "Knowledge is power."          ===>  "ISN'T knowledge power?"

* RULE 2: Negative Assertive     ===>  Positive Interrogative
  "Nobody can touch the stars."  ===>  "WHO CAN touch the stars?"
  "We can never forget him."     ===>  "CAN WE EVER forget him?"

2. MASTER RHETORICAL MAPPING MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+---+----------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| # | Assertive Pattern                | Interrogative Rhetorical Equivalent   |
+---+----------------------------------+---------------------------------------+
| 1 | Everybody / Everyone             | Who does not + V1 ... ?               |
| 2 | Nobody / No one / None           | Who + Finite Verb ... ?               |
| 3 | Never                            | When can ... / Can ... ever ... ?     |
| 4 | Nowhere                          | Where ... ?                           |
| 5 | There is no use in ...           | What is the use of ... ?              |
| 6 | It does not matter if ...        | What though ... ?                     |
| 7 | A is nothing but B               | What is A but B ... ?                 |
| 8 | Universal Truth (Positive)       | Isn't / Is not + S + Comp ... ?       |
+---+----------------------------------+---------------------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & EXAM TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 'WHO DOES NOT' EQUIVALENCE:
   * Bengali: "কে না দেশকে ভালোবাসে?" (= সকলেই দেশকে ভালোবাসে)।
   * English: "Who does not love the country?" (= Everyone loves the country).

2. POLARITY LOSS TRAP:
   * INCORRECT: Assertive: "He is honest." -> Interrogative: *"Is he honest?"
     (This is a neutral question asking for info, NOT a rhetorical transformation).
   * CORRECT:    "ISN'T he honest?" (Asserts that he IS honest).
