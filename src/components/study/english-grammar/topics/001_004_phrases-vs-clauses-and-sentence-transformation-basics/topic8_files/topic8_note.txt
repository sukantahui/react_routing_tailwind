1. BARRACKPORE TRANSFORMATION CLINIC: HIGH-FREQUENCY DIAGNOSTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE 1: THE TEMPORAL CORRELATIVE ALIGNMENT
- Attempt: *No sooner had he arrived WHEN the bell rang.
- Diagnosis: 'No sooner' strictly pairs with 'THAN'.
- Correct:  "NO SOONER had he arrived THAN the bell rang."
- Parallel: "HARDLY / SCARCELY had he arrived WHEN the bell rang."

CASE 2: THE TENSE HARMONY AUDIT
- Attempt: *He was so tired that he CANNOT walk.
- Diagnosis: Matrix verb 'was' is past tense -> Subordinate modal must be 'COULD NOT'.
- Correct:  "He WAS so tired that he COULD NOT walk."

CASE 3: THE SUPERLATIVE 'ONE OF THE' CONVERSION
- Superlative: "Tagore was ONE OF THE GREATEST poets."
- Comparative: "Tagore was GREATER THAN MOST OTHER poets."
- Positive:    "VERY FEW poets were AS GREAT AS Tagore."
