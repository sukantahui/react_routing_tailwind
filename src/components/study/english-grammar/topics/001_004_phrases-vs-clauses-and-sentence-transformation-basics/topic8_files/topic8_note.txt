CRITICAL STEP-BY-STEP WORKED TRANSFORMATION DRILLS:

DRILL 1: THE DUMMY OPERATOR IN NEGATIVE INVERSION
- Statement: "No sooner did the inspector entered..." ❌ ERROR!
- Rule: 'Did' takes base verb (V1) 'enter'.
- Correction: "No sooner did the inspector enter than..." ✅

DRILL 2: DOUBLE NEGATIVE TRAPS WITH 'TOO'
- Statement: "He is too wise not to see through the deception."
- Breakdown: 'too... not to' = positive capacity.
- Transformed: "He is so wise that he will certainly see through the deception." ✅

DRILL 3: HARDLY / SCARCELY VS NO SOONER
- "Hardly had the meeting started WHEN the power failed." (Pairs with WHEN)
- "No sooner had the meeting started THAN the power failed." (Pairs with THAN)
* NEVER MIX 'Hardly... than' or 'No sooner... when'.

DRILL 4: INTERROGATIVE RHETORICAL EQUIVALENCE
- Assertive: "Knowledge is true power."
- Interrogative: "Is knowledge not true power?"

BENGALI SUMMARY (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ):
- 'Did'-এর পরে সবসময় Verb-এর Present/Base Form (V1) বসে।
- 'No sooner'-এর সাথে সর্বদা 'than' এবং 'Hardly/Scarcely'-র সাথে 'when' বসে।
