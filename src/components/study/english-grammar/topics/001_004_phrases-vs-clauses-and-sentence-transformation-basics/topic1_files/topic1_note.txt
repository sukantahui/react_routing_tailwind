1. DEFINITION OF A PHRASE:
   - A cluster of two or more words functioning as a single syntactic unit.
   - Crucial Invariant: A phrase NEVER contains a Subject + Finite Verb pair.

2. THE 5 STRUCTURAL & FUNCTIONAL PHRASE TYPES:
   a. Noun Phrase (NP):
      - Head: Noun or Pronoun
      - Modifiers: Determiners, Pre-adjectives, Post-prepositional phrases
      - Role: Subject, Direct Object, Indirect Object, Complement
      - Example: [The courageous young student from Barrackpore] scored top marks.

   b. Verb Phrase (VP):
      - Consists of Lexical Main Verb + Auxiliaries / Modals
      - Example: She [has been studying] since dawn.

   c. Prepositional Phrase (PP):
      - Structure: Preposition + Object of Preposition (Noun Phrase)
      - Dual Function:
        * Adjectival PP (modifies Noun): The book [on the table] is mine.
        * Adverbial PP (modifies Verb): He ran [into the auditorium].

   d. Adjective Phrase (AdjP):
      - Modifies a Noun or Pronoun
      - Example: A man [of immense integrity and honour] led the movement.

   e. Adverb Phrase (AdvP):
      - Modifies a Verb, Adjective, or another Adverb
      - Example: She spoke [in an exceptionally polite manner] / [very softly].

3. BENGALI NOTES (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ):
   - Noun Phrase: বিশেষ্যধর্মী শব্দগুচ্ছ (Subject বা Object হিসেবে ব্যবহৃত)।
   - Prepositional Phrase: Preposition দিয়ে শুরু শব্দগুচ্ছ; এটি বিশেষ্যকে বিশেষিত করলে Adjective Phrase এবং ক্রিয়াকে বিশেষিত করলে Adverb Phrase হয়।
   - কোনো Phrase-এ কখনোই Finite Verb থাকে না।
