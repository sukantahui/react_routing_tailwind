1. THE SUPREME LAW OF TRANSFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Transformation is the process of changing the grammatical architecture of a 
 sentence WITHOUT changing its underlying meaning, tone, or temporal truth."

* CONVERSION:   Alters meaning ("He is honest" -> "He is not honest" [FALSE]).
* TRANSFORMATION: Preserves meaning ("He is honest" -> "He is NOT dishonest" [TRUE]).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MASTER TRANSFORMATION VECTORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. DEGREE OF COMPARISON INTERCHANGE:
   - Superlative: "Iron is the most useful metal."
   - Comparative: "Iron is MORE useful THAN any other metal."
   - Positive:    "NO other metal is AS useful AS iron."

B. PHRASE <===> CLAUSE EXPANSION & REDUCTION:
   - Phrase: "ON HEARING the whistle, the train started."
   - Clause: "AS SOON AS the whistle was blown, the train started."

C. SIMPLE <===> COMPOUND <===> COMPLEX SYNTHESIS:
   - Simple:   "BESIDES BEING intelligent, Swadeep is hardworking."
   - Compound: "Swadeep is NOT ONLY intelligent BUT ALSO hardworking."
   - Complex:  "Although Swadeep is hardworking, he remains humble."
