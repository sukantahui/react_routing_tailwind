1. THE GOLDEN INVARIANT:
   "Transformation of a sentence means changing its grammatical form without altering its sense or meaning in the slightest degree."
   - P. C. Wren & H. Martin

2. TRANSFORMATION VS CONVERSION (DON'T CONFUSE THEM):
   - Conversion: Changes meaning (Affirmative: "I agree" -> Negative: "I do not agree"). [Meaning Lost]
   - Transformation: Retains meaning (Affirmative: "I agree" -> Negative: "I do not disagree"). [Meaning Preserved]

3. CORE TRANSFORMATION MECHANISMS:
   a. Antonym Pairing: Subject + Verb + not + [Antonym]
      ("He is honest" --> "He is not dishonest")
   b. Double Negation / Litotes:
      ("She loves music" --> "She is not without love for music" / "She never fails to enjoy music")
   c. Polarity Flip in Rhetorical Interrogatives:
      ("No one can defeat him" --> "Who can defeat him?")
   d. Correlative Re-packaging:
      ("As soon as..." --> "No sooner had... than...")
      ("Too... to..." --> "So... that... cannot...")

4. BENGALI SUMMARY (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ):
   - ব্যাকরণে রূপান্তর (Transformation)-এর অর্থ হল বাক্যের বহিরাবরণ পরিবর্তন করা, কিন্তু অন্তরের অর্থকে অপরিবর্তিত রাখা।
   - পরীক্ষায় 'Do as Directed'-এ অর্থ পরিবর্তন হলে শূন্য নম্বর দেওয়া হয়।
