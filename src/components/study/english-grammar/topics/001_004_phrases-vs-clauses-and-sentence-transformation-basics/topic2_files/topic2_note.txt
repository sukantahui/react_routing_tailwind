Teacher & Author: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax, Barrackpore)

1. THE CLAUSE DEFINITION:
   - A clause is a syntactic unit consisting of a Subject and a Finite Verb (Predication Core).
   - Formula: Clause = [Subject] + [Finite Verb (+ Objects / Complements / Adverbials)]

2. FINITE VERB VS NON-FINITE VERB (THE CRUCIAL TEST):
   - Finite Verb: Changes form according to Tense, Person, and Number (e.g. goes, went, was writing).
   - Non-Finite Verb: Does NOT show tense or person (Infinitives: to write; Gerunds/Participles: writing, written).
   * RULE: A clause MUST have at least ONE Finite Verb.

3. COMPARATIVE TABLE: PHRASE VS CLAUSE
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Feature             Phrase                     Clause
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Subject-Verb pair   NO                         YES (Mandatory)
   Finite Verb         NO                         YES
   Complete Thought    No (fragments of meaning)  Yes (if Independent)
   Example             "in the morning"           "when morning arrived"
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. BENGALI SUMMARY (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ):
   - Clause (বাক্যাংশ/উপবাক্য) হল এমন শব্দসমষ্টি যার মধ্যে নিজস্ব Subject এবং সমাপিকা ক্রিয়া (Finite Verb) থাকে।
   - Non-finite verb (to + verb, verb+ing) কখনো একা Clause গঠন করতে পারে না।
