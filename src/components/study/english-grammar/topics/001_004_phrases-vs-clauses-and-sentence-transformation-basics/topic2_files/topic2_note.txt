1. THE CLAUSE DEFINITION & CORE NEXUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Clause is a syntactic construction consisting of a SUBJECT paired with a FINITE VERB:

                 [ CLAUSE NUCLEUS ]
                     /        \
          [ SUBJECT NP ]    [ FINITE VERB PREDICATE ]
                 |                   |
              Swadeep            developed (the software)

* FINITE VERB: Inflected for Tense (Past/Present), Person (1st/2nd/3rd), and Number.
* NON-FINITE VERB: Infinitives (to do), Gerunds (doing), Participles (done/doing).
  Non-finite verbs CANNOT anchor a clause alone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PHRASE VS CLAUSE COMPARATIVE MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+---+--------------------+------------------------+-----------------------------+
| # | Feature            | Phrase                 | Clause                      |
+---+--------------------+------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 1 | Subject + Verb     | NO                     | YES (Mandatory)             |
| 2 | Finite Verb        | Absent                 | Present                     |
| 3 | Completeness       | Partial meaning        | Complete or dependent thought|
| 4 | Exemplar           | "in the morning"       | "when the morning arrived"  |
| 5 | Participial Variant| "Seeing the mentor"    | "When he saw the mentor"    |
+---+--------------------+------------------------+-----------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CLAUSE COUNTING RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Number of Clauses in a sentence  ===  Number of Finite Verbs.

Example: "The student [1: who won the award] [2: is studying] at Barrackpore."
- Finite Verb 1: 'won' (Past tense) -> Clause 1
- Finite Verb 2: 'is studying' (Present continuous) -> Clause 2
- Total: 2 Clauses (Complex Sentence).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & EXAM TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FINITE VS NON-FINITE BENGALI PARALLELS:
   * সমাপিকা ক্রিয়া (Finite Verb): "পড়ল", "শিখছে", "যাবে" -> Clause তৈরি করে।
   * অসমাপিকা ক্রিয়া (Non-finite Verb): "পড়ে", "শিখতে", "দেখে" -> Phrase তৈরি করে।
   * "পড়ে [Non-finite] সে বাড়ি গেল [Finite]" -> "Having read, he went home" (1 Clause).
   * "যখন সে পড়ল [Finite], সে বাড়ি গেল [Finite]" -> "When he read, he went home" (2 Clauses).
