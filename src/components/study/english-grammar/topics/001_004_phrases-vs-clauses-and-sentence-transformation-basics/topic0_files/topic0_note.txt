2. COMMON PHRASE FAMILIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Noun Phrase:        "The diligent student from Barrackpore"
2. Adjective Phrase:   "A leader of great wisdom and integrity"
3. Adverb Phrase:      "He answered with immense confidence"
4. Prepositional Phr.: "On the study table in the library"
5. Participial Phrase: "Having completed the code review"
6. Infinitive Phrase:  "To master advanced English syntax"

3. MASTER TRANSFORMATION CATALOG (DO AS DIRECTED RULES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. AFFIRMATIVE <-> NEGATIVE (Without meaning shift):
   - By Antonym:        "Man is mortal" <-> "Man is not immortal"
   - By 'No sooner':    "As soon as the bell rang, they entered" <->
                        "No sooner had the bell rung than they entered"
   - By 'None but':     "Only Swadeep can solve it" <-> "None but Swadeep..."
   - By 'There is no':  "Every rose has a thorn" <-> "There is no rose..."
   - By 'Too... to':    "She is too weak to walk" <->
                        "She is so weak that she cannot walk"

2. ASSERTIVE <-> INTERROGATIVE:
   - Universal positive:"Everyone loves flowers" <-> "Who does not love flowers?"
   - Universal negative:"Nobody can deny truth" <-> "Who can deny truth?"
   - Rhetorical shift:  "Can an elephant fly?" <-> "An elephant cannot fly."

3. EXCLAMATORY <-> ASSERTIVE:
   - "What a glorious sunset!" <-> "It is a very glorious sunset."
   - "How fast the time flies!" <-> "The time flies very fast."
   - "O that I had wings!" <-> "I wish that I had wings."
