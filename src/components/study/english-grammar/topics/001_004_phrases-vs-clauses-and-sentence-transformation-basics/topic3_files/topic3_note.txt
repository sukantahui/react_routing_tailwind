MODULE 001_004 | TOPIC 3: INDEPENDENT VS SUBORDINATE CLAUSES
========================================================================
Teacher & Author: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax, Barrackpore)

1. INDEPENDENT (MAIN / PRINCIPAL) CLAUSE:
   - Contains a Subject and a Finite Verb.
   - Expresses a complete, stand-alone thought.
   - Can form a complete simple sentence by itself.
   - Example: [The train departed on time.]

2. SUBORDINATE (DEPENDENT) CLAUSE:
   - Contains a Subject and a Finite Verb.
   - Introduced by a Subordinating Conjunction (because, although, when, since, if, unless, while) or Relative Pronoun (who, which, that).
   - CANNOT stand alone; depends on the Main Clause for complete meaning.
   - Example: [Although the weather was stormy], the train departed on time.

3. THE 3 SUBORDINATE CLAUSE FAMILIES:
   a. Noun Clause: Acts as Subject/Object ("I know [that he is honest]").
   b. Adjective (Relative) Clause: Modifies a Noun ("The boy [who won] is my friend").
   c. Adverb Clause: Modifies Verb/Adjective ("Wait here [until I return]").

4. COORDINATE CLAUSES (FANBOYS):
   - Two independent clauses joined by For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So form a COMPOUND Sentence.

5. BENGALI SUMMARY (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ):
   - Principal Clause: স্বাধীন উপবাক্য, যা কোনো সাহায্য ছাড়া একা সম্পূর্ণ অর্থ প্রকাশ করতে পারে।
   - Subordinate Clause: পরাধীন উপবাক্য, যা সম্পূর্ণ অর্থের জন্য Principal Clause-এর উপর নির্ভরশীল।
