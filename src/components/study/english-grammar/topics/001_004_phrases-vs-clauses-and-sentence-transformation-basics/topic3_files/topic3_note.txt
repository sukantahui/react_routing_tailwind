1. CORE SYNTACTIC DICHOTOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. INDEPENDENT (MAIN) CLAUSE:
   - Contains: Subject + Finite Verb.
   - Capability: Expresses a complete standalone thought.
   - Punctuation: Can be terminated with a Period (.), Question Mark (?), or (!).
   - Exemplar: "Swadeep mastered React state architecture."

B. SUBORDINATE (DEPENDENT) CLAUSE:
   - Contains: Subordinating Conjunction/Relative Pronoun + Subject + Finite Verb.
   - Capability: Incomplete standalone meaning; grammatically dependent on Main Clause.
   - Exemplar: "...BECAUSE he practiced deliberately every morning."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE THREE FUNCTIONAL CLASSES OF SUBORDINATE CLAUSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+---+----------------------+--------------------+-------------------------------+
| # | Subordinate Category | Core Syntactic Role| Structural Exemplar           |
+---+----------------------+--------------------+-------------------------------+
| 1 | Noun Clause          | Subject / Object   | "I know THAT HE IS HONEST."   |
| 2 | Adjective Clause     | Modifies Noun      | "The man WHO WON THE PRIZE..."|
| 3 | Adverb Clause        | Modifies Verb/Adj  | "We left WHEN THE BELL RANG." |
+---+----------------------+--------------------+-------------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE CLASSIFICATION TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Time:        when, whenever, before, after, since, as soon as, until, while
2. Reason:      because, since, as, seeing that
3. Condition:   if, unless, provided that, in case
4. Purpose:     so that, in order that, lest (+ should)
5. Result:      so + adj + that, such + noun + that
6. Concession:  although, though, even if, whereas, while
7. Comparison:  as...as, than
8. Manner:      as, as if, as though

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & EXAM TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SENTENCE FRAGMENT TRAP:
   * Bengali: "যেহেতু বৃষ্টি হচ্ছিল।" (Conversational fragment).
   * INCORRECT: *Because it was raining. (Punctuated alone as a sentence).
   * CORRECT:    Because it was raining, the match was called off.

2. COMMA SPLICE TRAP:
   * INCORRECT: *The class ended, the students left.
   * CORRECT:    The class ended, AND the students left. / The class ended; the students left.
