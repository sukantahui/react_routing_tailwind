2. THE 5 CORE GRAMMATICAL INVARIANTS ESTABLISHED IN SEGMENT 1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CLAUSE LAW:
   Every clause requires exactly ONE Subject NP + ONE Finite Verb VP nexus.

2. THE COPULAR SVC LAW:
   Sense and state linking verbs take Subject Complement Adjectives (NOT adverbs).
   - "The coffee smells rich." (SVC, not *richly).

3. THE DATIVE SHIFT LAW:
   SVOO (IO + DO) transforms to SVOA (DO + TO/FOR + IO).
   - "He gave me a book" <===> "He gave a book TO me".
   - Latinate exception: 'explain, describe, suggest' FORBID SVOO (*explain me).

4. THE POLARITY LAW OF QUESTION TAGS:
   - Positive Statement <===> Negative Tag ("I am ready, aren't I?").
   - Negative Statement <===> Positive Tag ("He rarely speaks, does he?").

5. THE SUPREME TRANSFORMATION LAW:
   Transform the outward syntactic casing, but preserve underlying semantic 
   meaning, tone, and tense with 100% fidelity.
