MASTER REVISION FOR SEGMENT 1: SENTENCE ARCHITECTURE & FOUNDATIONS

1. MODULE 001_001: WORDS & PARTS OF SPEECH
   - 8 Parts of Speech classified by FUNCTION, not static dictionary label.
   - Open Classes (Noun, Verb, Adj, Adv) vs Closed Classes (Pronoun, Prep, Conj, Interj).

2. MODULE 001_002: SENTENCE ANATOMY & CLAUSE PATTERNS
   - Subject + Predicate.
   - Direct Object (What/Whom) vs Indirect Object (To/For whom).
   - Subject Complements (Linking verbs) vs Object Complements (Complex-transitive verbs).
   - 7 Sentence Patterns: SV, SVO, SVOO, SVC, SVOC, SVA, SVOA.

3. MODULE 001_003: SENTENCE TYPES & COMMUNICATIVE MOODS
   - Declarative / Assertive (.)
   - Interrogative: Inversion, Wh- Questions, Question Tags (Polarity flip).
   - Imperative: Implied (You), Base V1.
   - Exclamatory: What a / How (!).
   - Optative: May + S + V1 (!).

4. MODULE 001_004: PHRASES, CLAUSES & TRANSFORMATION
   - Phrase: No Subject-Finite Verb nexus.
   - Clause: Subject + Finite Verb pair.
   - Transformation Golden Rule: Form alters; Meaning & Truth Value are 100% INVARIANT.
   - Key Formulas: 'None but', 'Too...to' -> 'So...that...cannot', 'No sooner had...than', 'Who does not...?', 'What a...' -> 'a very...'.
