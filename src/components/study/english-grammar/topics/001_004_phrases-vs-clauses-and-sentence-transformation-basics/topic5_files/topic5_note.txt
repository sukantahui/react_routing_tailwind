MODULE 001_004 | TOPIC 5: AFFIRMATIVE TO NEGATIVE TRANSFORMATION
========================================================================
Teacher & Author: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax, Barrackpore)

MASTER FORMULAS FOR AFFIRMATIVE ↔ NEGATIVE CONVERSION:

1. NOT + ANTONYM:
   - "He is diligent" --> "He is not lazy / indolent."
   - "I am hopeful" --> "I am not without hope / doubtful."

2. ONLY / ALONE:
   - For Persons: "Only Swadeep" --> "None but Swadeep"
   - For Inanimate Objects: "Only books" --> "Nothing but books"
   - For Age / Numbers: "He is only ten" --> "He is not more than ten"

3. EVERY / ALL:
   - "Every mother loves her child" --> "There is no mother but loves her child / without love for her child."

4. AS SOON AS ↔ NO SOONER... THAN:
   - "As soon as the bell rang..." --> "No sooner had the bell rung than..."
   * CRITICAL: Use 'than' (NOT 'then' or 'when').

5. TOO... TO ↔ SO... THAT... CANNOT/COULD NOT:
   - "He is too weak to walk" --> "He is so weak that he cannot walk."
   - "She was too proud to beg" --> "She was so proud that she could not beg."

6. MUST / HAVE TO ↔ CANNOT BUT:
   - "We must obey" --> "We cannot but obey / We cannot help obeying."

BENGALI SUMMARY (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ):
- Affirmative থেকে Negative করার সময় অর্থ বজায় রাখতে উপরের ৬টি সূত্র কণ্ঠস্থ রাখুন।
