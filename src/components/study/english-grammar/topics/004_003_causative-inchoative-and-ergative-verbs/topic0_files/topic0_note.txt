1. THE ANATOMY OF CAUSATIVE VERBS (প্রযোজক ক্রিয়া)
========================================================================
A Causative Verb denotes that the grammatical subject does not execute the action directly, but instead causes, compels, authorizes, facilitates, or persuades another agent to perform it.

While Bengali forms causatives morphologically by adding verbal suffixes (করা -> করানো, দেখা -> দেখানো, পড়া -> পড়ানো, খাওয়া -> খাওয়ানো), English utilizes analytical periphrastic auxiliaries (Make, Have, Let, Get, Help).

========================================================================
2. THE MASTER CAUSATIVE MATRIX & SYNTACTIC FORMULAS
========================================================================
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verb   Semantic Nuance       Active Voice Formula               Passive Voice Formula
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAKE   Force / Compulsion    Subj + MAKE + Person + V1 (Bare)   Person + BE MADE + TO + V1 (Mandatory 'to'!)
HAVE   Delegation / Service  Subj + HAVE + Person + V1 (Bare)   Subj + HAVE + Thing + V3 (Passive Causative)
LET    Permission / Allow    Subj + LET + Person + V1 (Bare)    Person + BE ALLOWED TO + V1
GET    Persuasion / Convince Subj + GET + Person + TO + V1      Subj + GET + Thing + V3 (Passive Causative)
HELP   Assistance / Aid      Subj + HELP + Person + (to) + V1   Person + BE HELPED + TO + V1
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. STEP-BY-STEP CAUSATIVE MECHANICS & EXAMPLES
========================================================================
A. CAUSATIVE "MAKE" (Force, Obligation, Authority):
   - Active: "The principal made the errant student apologize." [Bare infinitive: apologize]
   - Passive: "The errant student was made TO apologize by the principal." [CRITICAL: 'to' is mandatory!]
   - Involuntary Reaction: "Her hilarious joke made everyone laugh." [Bare infinitive]

B. CAUSATIVE "HAVE" (Hiring, Delegating, Professional Commissioning):
   - Active: "I had the electrician inspect the circuit breaker." [Bare infinitive]
   - Passive Causative: "I had the circuit breaker inspected by the electrician." [Thing + V3]

C. CAUSATIVE "GET" (Persuading, Convincing, Overcoming Reluctance):
   - Active: "She got her brother to wash the family car." [Mandatory to-infinitive!]
   - Passive Causative: "She got the car washed." [Thing + V3]

D. CAUSATIVE "LET" (Permitting, Giving Leave):
   - Active: "The invigilator let the candidates use scientific calculators." [Bare infinitive]
   - Passive Transformation: "Candidates were allowed to use scientific calculators." ['Let' is replaced by 'be allowed to']

E. CAUSATIVE "HELP" (Assisting):
   - Dual Compatibility: "He helped me solve the equation." (Bare) OR "He helped me to solve the equation." (To-infinitive) — both are 100% correct.

F. FULL-LEXICAL CAUSATIVES (Force, Compel, Require, Cause, Enable, Order):
   - These verbs strictly require 'Object + To-Infinitive' in active voice:
     * "The law forces motorists to wear helmets."
     * "The heavy monsoon caused the dam to overflow."
     * "Cloud computing enables developers to deploy globally."

4. ERGATIVE (LABILE / MIDDLE VOICE) VERBS
========================================================================
An Ergative Verb is a verb that can be used transitively (with an agent causing the action) and intransitively (where the affected patient acts as the grammatical subject) without any morphological change or passive auxiliary.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verb       Transitive (Agent-Driven)            Intransitive / Ergative (Middle Voice)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open       The guard opened the heavy gate.     The heavy gate opened quietly.
Break      The vandal broke the glass window.   The glass window broke into pieces.
Melt       The scorching sun melted the ice.    The ice melted under the heat.
Boil       The cook boiled the water in a pot.  The water boiled at 100°C.
Ring       The peon rang the brass bell.        The brass bell rang loudly.
Start      The engineer started the engine.     The engine started smoothly.
Drop       The player dropped the cricket ball. The price of oil dropped sharply.
Close      She closed the library door.         The library door closed automatically.
------------------------------------------------------------------------

SEMANTIC MIDDLE VOICE (QUASI-PASSIVE):
- Verbs active in form but passive in meaning:
  * "This smooth cotton fabric washes easily." (= can be washed easily)
  * "His latest novel reads well." (= is enjoyable to read)
  * "Ripe Alphonso mangoes sell fast." (= are sold quickly)
  * "Sugar tastes sweet." (= when it is tasted)

5. INCHOATIVE VERBS (VERBS OF BECOMING & STATE TRANSITION)
========================================================================
Inchoative Verbs express the beginning of an action, entering a new condition, or gradual change of state. They function as copular / linking verbs taking predicate adjectives.

CORE INCHOATIVE VERBS & THEIR COLLOCATIONS:
1. BECOME: "He became famous overnight." (General change)
2. GROW: "The sky grew dark." / "He grew weary." (Gradual change with time)
3. TURN: "The leaves turned red in autumn." / "The milk turned sour." (Change in appearance/taste)
4. GET: "It is getting late." / "He got angry." (Informal / rapid transition)
5. GO: "The meat went bad." / "He went bald / went blind." (Negative deterioration)
6. COME: "My dream came true." / "The knot came loose." (Realization / positive state)
7. FALL: "The child fell asleep." / "He fell ill." / "They fell silent." (Involuntary shift)

6. BENGALI PEDAGOGICAL SUMMARY & COMPARATIVE BRIDGES (বাংলা সারসংক্ষেপ):
========================================================================
১. প্রযোজক ক্রিয়া (Causative Verbs) ধারণা:
   - বাংলায় 'করা' থেকে 'করানো', 'দেখা' থেকে 'দেখানো' ইত্যাদিতে রূপান্তর হয়।
   - ইংরেজিতে এটি গঠন করতে 'Make', 'Have', 'Get', 'Let' ব্যবহার করা হয়।

২. অত্যন্ত গুরুত্বপূর্ণ পরীক্ষার নিয়ম (Exam Invariants):
   - Make (Active): "He made me write" (Bare Verb, 'to' নেই)।
   - Make (Passive): "I was made TO write" (Passive-এ 'to' বসবেই!)।
   - Get (Active): "I got him TO write" (ব্যক্তি থাকলে 'to' বসবে)।
   - Have / Get + বস্তু + V3: "I had my car repaired" / "I got my hair cut" (অন্যকে দিয়ে কাজ করিয়ে নেওয়া)।

৩. Ergative Verb (উভমুখী ক্রিয়া):
   - যখন কোনো ক্রিয়া রূপ না বদলে Transitive ও Intransitive উভয়ভাবেই বসে:
     "The peon rang the bell" (সকর্মক) বনাম "The bell rang" (অকর্মক)।
