1. FORMULA & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Affirmative: Subject + had + Past Participle (V3)
  - Formula: Subject + had + V3
  - Example: The train had departed before we reached the platform.

• Negative: Subject + had not (hadn't) + V3
  - Example: They had not completed the analysis before the audit started.

• Interrogative: Had + Subject + V3 ...?
  - Example: Had the patient taken the medicine before the fever escalated?

2. THE "PAST OF THE PAST" TIMELINE PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Past Perfect is never used in a temporal vacuum. It exists specifically to indicate that of two actions that occurred in the past, ONE OCCURRED EARLIER THAN THE OTHER:

Timeline Order:
[Past Action 1: EARLIER] ──────> [Past Action 2: LATER] ──────> [Present Moment (NOW)]
        ↓                                 ↓
   PAST PERFECT                      SIMPLE PAST
    (had + V3)                          (V2)

Classic Example:
"The patient HAD DIED before the doctor ARRIVED."
• Earlier Action (Happened first): Patient died -> HAD DIED.
• Later Action (Happened second): Doctor arrived -> ARRIVED.

3. THE FATAL ISOLATED PAST EVENT TRAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• ERROR TRAP: Using Past Perfect when there is only ONE isolated past event with no second reference point.
  ✗ Fatal Error: I had visited Barrackpore yesterday.
  ✓ Pure Grammar: I visited Barrackpore yesterday. (Simple Past V2).

  ✗ Fatal Error: In 1947, India had gained independence.
  ✓ Pure Grammar: In 1947, India gained independence. (Simple Past V2).

• RULE: If there is no second past event or past milestone, use SIMPLE PAST.

4. UNFULFILLED PAST WISHES & INTENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Past Perfect is also used with verbs like hope, expect, think, intend, want to express a past intention that was NOT realized:
- "I had hoped that Swadeep would win the tournament (but he did not)."
- "She had intended to study law in London (but financial constraints prevented it)."

5. BENGALI PEDAGOGICAL INSIGHTS (বাংলায় বিশেষ ব্যাখ্যা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• অতীতকালের দুটি ঘটনার মধ্যে যে কাজটি অপেক্ষাকৃত পূর্বে ঘটেছিল তার ক্ষেত্রে 'Past Perfect' (had + V3) এবং যেটি পরে ঘটেছিল তার ক্ষেত্রে 'Simple Past' (V2) ব্যবহৃত হয়।
• বিখ্যাত উদাহরণ: "ডাক্তার আসার পূর্বে রোগী মারা গেল" = The patient had died before the doctor arrived.
• সতর্কবার্তা: কোনো বাক্যে যদি একটিমাত্র অতীত ঘটনা থাকে, তবে কখনোই 'had + V3' হবে না; সর্বদা Simple Past (V2) হবে (যেমন: "আমি গতকাল গিয়েছিলাম" = I went yesterday; কখনোই I had gone yesterday নয়)।
