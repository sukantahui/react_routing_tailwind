MODULE 004.006 - TOPIC 7 NOTE: CLASSROOM DIALOGUE & PAST INVARIANT DIAGNOSTICS CAPSTONE
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: THE 4 PAST TENSE ASPECTS
Aspect describes the internal temporal unfolding of an action in relation to the timeline:
1) Simple Past (V2):
   - Discrete, finished events at a specified past point.
   - Narrative sequencing of sequential events (A -> B -> C).
   - Negative / Interrogative: 'did + not + V1' / 'Did + S + V1?'.
2) Past Continuous (was/were + V1-ing):
   - Action in progress during a past timeframe.
   - Background setting for a sudden interrupt (When + V2 vs While + Past Cont).
   - Parallel ongoing events (While X was doing Y, Z was doing W).
3) Past Perfect (had + V3):
   - The Pluperfect / 'Past of the Past'.
   - Establishes priority: Action 1 (Earlier) = had + V3; Action 2 (Later) = V2.
   - Connectives: 'Before' (had + V3 before V2); 'After' (V2 after had + V3); 'By the time' (had + V3 by the time V2).
4) Past Perfect Continuous (had been + V1-ing):
   - Extended duration/accumulation leading up to a past cutoff milestone.
   - Explanation of visible past physical result / evidence.

2. INVERSION & CORRELATIVE PAIR INVARIANTS:
- "No sooner HAD + S + V3 ... THAN + S + V2" (or "No sooner DID + S + V1 ... THAN")
- "Hardly / Scarcely / Barely HAD + S + V3 ... WHEN + S + V2"
- Strict rules: 'No sooner' takes 'THAN' (comparative); 'Hardly/Scarcely' take 'WHEN'. Never mix with 'then' or 'when'.

3. PAST HABITUAL MODALS:
- 'Used to + V1': Past habits (discontinued) + Past states / conditions (stative verbs).
- 'Would + V1': Nostalgic repeated dynamic actions ONLY with established past context (Never stative verbs).
- 'Didn't use to' (Base form 'use', not 'used').

4. PAST ASPECT DECISION ALGORITHM:
Step 1: Is there a finished past time anchor (yesterday, in 2020, ago)?
        -> Must use a past tense; Present Perfect is strictly forbidden!
Step 2: Are there two past actions?
        -> Determine temporal sequence.
        -> Earlier action = had + V3 (Past Perfect).
        -> Later action = V2 (Simple Past).
Step 3: Is there a duration ('for 3 hours', 'since 9 AM') before that past moment?
        -> Use Past Perfect Continuous (had been + V-ing).
        -> Exception: If the verb is stative (know, own, believe), downgrade to Past Perfect Simple (had + V3).
Step 4: Is it a past habit?
        -> Action verb = used to / would.
        -> State / Condition verb = used to ONLY.

5. BENGALI SUMMARY (বাংলা সারসংক্ষেপ):
অতীতের চারটি রূপের সমন্বয়:
- সাধারণ অতীত (Simple Past): নির্দিষ্ট সময়ে সম্পন্ন কাজ (V2)।
- ঘটমান অতীত (Past Continuous): অতীতে কোনো একটি মুহূর্তে কাজটি চলছিল।
- পুরাঘটিত অতীত (Past Perfect): দুটি অতীত কাজের মধ্যে যেটি আগে ঘটেছিল (had + V3)।
- পুরাঘটিত ঘটমান অতীত (Past Perfect Continuous): অতীতের কোনো নির্দিষ্ট সময়ের পূর্ব পর্যন্ত কোনো কাজ দীর্ঘ সময় ধরে চলছিল (had been + V-ing)।
- 'No sooner...than' এবং 'Hardly...when' বাক্যের শুরুতে বসলে Auxiliary Verb কর্তার (Subject) আগে বসে।
