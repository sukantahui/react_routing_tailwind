1. THE THREE CORE CHRONOLOGICAL FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In English narrative prose and competitive examinations, three primary subordinating time connectives establish chronological sequencing between two past actions:

(A) THE 'BEFORE' FORMULA:
    Formula: [Earlier Action: Past Perfect] + BEFORE + [Later Action: Simple Past]
    • The clause PRECEDING 'before' takes 'had + V3'.
    • The clause FOLLOWING 'before' takes 'V2'.
    • Example: The train HAD DEPARTED before we REACHED the station.
    • Example: The patient HAD PASSED AWAY before the specialist ARRIVED.

(B) THE 'AFTER' FORMULA:
    Formula: [Later Action: Simple Past] + AFTER + [Earlier Action: Past Perfect]
    • The clause PRECEDING 'after' takes 'V2'.
    • The clause FOLLOWING 'after' takes 'had + V3'.
    • Example: We REACHED the cinema hall after the movie HAD COMMENCED.
    • Example: The doctor ARRIVED after the patient HAD DIED.

(C) THE 'BY THE TIME' FORMULA (PAST DEADLINE):
    Formula: BY THE TIME + [Later Action: Simple Past (V2)], [Earlier Action: Past Perfect (had + V3)]
    • 'By the time' clause ALWAYS takes Simple Past (V2).
    • The main clause ALWAYS takes Past Perfect (had + V3).
    • Example: By the time the fire engines ARRIVED, the neighbors HAD EXTINGUISHED the fire.
    • Example: By the time Swadeep SUBMITTED his project, the evaluation committee HAD CONCLUDED the session.

2. MNEMONIC RULE FOR BARRACKPORE STUDENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• "Before-এর পূর্বে Had + V3" (Before sits AFTER the Past Perfect).
• "After-এর পরে Had + V3" (After sits BEFORE the Past Perfect).
• "By the time-এর ক্লজে V2, মেইন ক্লজে Had + V3".

3. RAPID CONSECUTIVE SEQUENCE (AS SOON AS / NO GAP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When two actions occur immediately one after another in natural sequence without any perceptible time gap, BOTH clauses take the Simple Past:
✓ As soon as the teacher ENTERED the room, the students STOOD up.
✓ When he HEARD the explosion, he HIT the floor.

4. BENGALI PEDAGOGICAL INSIGHTS (বাংলায় বিশেষ ব্যাখ্যা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 'Before' (পূর্বে): যে কাজটি আগে হয়েছে তা Before-এর পূর্বে (Had + V3) বসে এবং যে কাজটি পরে হয়েছে তা Before-এর পরে (V2) বসে।
• 'After' (পরে): যে কাজটি পরে হয়েছে তা After-এর পূর্বে (V2) বসে এবং যে কাজটি আগে হয়েছে তা After-এর পরে (Had + V3) বসে।
• 'By the time': "যখন সময়মতো পৌঁছাল..." এই ক্লজটিতে Simple Past (V2) এবং তার আগেই সম্পন্ন হয়ে যাওয়া মূল ক্লজটিতে Past Perfect (Had + V3) বসে।
