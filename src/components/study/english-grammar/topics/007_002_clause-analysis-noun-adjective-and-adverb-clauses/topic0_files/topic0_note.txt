1. WHAT IS A CLAUSE? (SENTENCE vs CLAUSE vs PHRASE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Phrase (শব্দগুচ্ছ): A group of words without a finite verb that acts as a 
  single part of speech (e.g., "in the morning", "with great courage").
• Clause (বাক্যাংশ / উপবাক্য): A group of words containing its OWN SUBJECT and 
  its OWN FINITE VERB that forms part of a larger sentence.
• Sentence (পূর্ণাঙ্গ বাক্য): A complete syntactic unit expressing a full thought.

WHAT IS A FINITE VERB? (সমাপিকা ক্রিয়া কী?):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Finite Verb is a verb that is limited/governed by:
  1. The Tense of the sentence (Present / Past / Future).
  2. The Person (1st, 2nd, 3rd) and Number (Singular, Plural) of its Subject.
Examples: "He [writes] code" (Present), "They [wrote] code" (Past).
It can anchor and complete a clause independently.

VERB NOTATION REFERENCE (V1 to V5):
  • V1: Base Form / Present Plural (e.g., know, write, bring)
  • V2: Simple Past Form (e.g., knew, wrote, brought)
  • V3: Past Participle Form (e.g., known, written, brought)
  • V4: Present Participle / -ing Form (e.g., knowing, writing, bringing)
  • V5: Third-Person Singular Simple Present Form (Base + -s/-es/-ies: knows, writes, brings)
[Detailed Reference: Module 004_001: Verb Classification & Module 004_002: Conjugation Mechanics]

In contrast, Non-Finite Verbs (Infinitives like 'to write', Gerunds like 'writing', 
Participles like 'written') do NOT change with tense/person and CANNOT form a clause alone.
[Detailed Reference: Module 005_003: Non-Finite Verbs]

THE GOLDEN RULE OF CLAUSE COUNTING:
  Number of Clauses = Number of Finite Verbs
  [১টি Finite Verb = ১টি Clause; যতগুলি Finite Verb থাকবে, বাক্যে ঠিক ততগুলি Clause থাকবে।]

THREE MAIN CATEGORIES OF CLAUSES:
  1. Principal / Main / Independent Clause (প্রধান খণ্ডবাক্য):
     - Makes complete sense by itself and can stand alone as an independent sentence.
     - Example: "He bought a laptop [Principal Clause] because he needed it for coding."

  2. Subordinate / Dependent Clause (আশ্রিত / অধীনস্ত খণ্ডবাক্য):
     - Contains a subject and a finite verb, but depends on the Principal clause 
       to complete its meaning. Introduced by a Subordinating Conjunction or Relative word.
     - Example: "...because he needed it for coding."

  3. Coordinate Clause (সমমর্যাদাসম্পন্ন খণ্ডবাক্য):
     - Two or more independent clauses joined by Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS: 
       For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So).
     - Example: "[He studied hard], and [he topped the examination]."

2. SUBORDINATE CLAUSES: THE THREE FUNCTIONAL FAMILIES
================================================================================
Subordinate clauses are classified entirely by the job they perform in the sentence:

  +-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------+
  | Clause Type           | Syntactic Function          | Diagnostic Question   |
  +-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------+
  | 1. NOUN CLAUSE        | Performs the role of a Noun | What? Who? Whom?      |
  | 2. ADJECTIVE CLAUSE   | Modifies an Antecedent Noun | Which? What kind of?  |
  | 3. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE   | Modifies Verb/Adj/Adverb    | When? Where? Why? How?|
  +-----------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------+

3. NOUN CLAUSES: IN-DEPTH 5+ SYNTACTIC ROLES & THE "WHAT / IT" TEST
================================================================================
A Noun Clause acts as a nominal argument. 

DIAGNOSTIC TEST ("The 'IT / SOMETHING' Test"):
If you can substitute the entire subordinate clause with "IT" or "SOMETHING" 
and the sentence remains grammatically coherent, it is a NOUN CLAUSE.
  - "I know [that honesty is best]." -> "I know [SOMETHING]." (Valid -> Noun Clause)
  - "The boy [who came yesterday] is sick." -> "The boy [SOMETHING] is sick." (Invalid -> Adjective Clause)

THE 5 CORE SYNTACTIC ROLES OF NOUN CLAUSES:

1. SUBJECT OF A FINITE VERB (ভার্বের কর্তা হিসেবে):
   - Example 1: "[That he is an honest officer] IS known to the entire city."
     * Analysis: The whole bracketed clause is the subject of the linking verb 'is'.
     * বাংলা: তিনি যে একজন সৎ অফিসার তা পুরো শহরের জানা।
   - Example 2: "[What Swadeep explained] MADE complete sense to the mentor."
     * বাংলা: স্বদীপ যা ব্যাখ্যা করেছিল তা মেন্টরের কাছে পুরোপুরি অর্থপূর্ণ মনে হয়েছিল।
   - Example 3: "[How the burglar entered the locked vault] REMAINS an unsolved mystery."
     * বাংলা: চোরটি কীভাবে তালাবদ্ধ ভল্টে ঢুকেছিল তা এখনও এক অমীমাংসিত রহস্য।
   - Example 4: "[Whether she will accept the promotion] IS still uncertain."
     * বাংলা: তিনি পদোন্নতি গ্রহণ করবেন কিনা তা এখনও অনিশ্চিত।

2. OBJECT OF A TRANSITIVE VERB (সকর্মক ক্রিয়ার কর্ম হিসেবে):
   - Example 1: "Sukanta Sir taught [that practice makes a student perfect]."
     * Question: Taught what? -> Answer: [that practice makes a student perfect].
     * বাংলা: সুকান্ত স্যার শিখিয়েছিলেন যে নিয়মিত অনুশীলনই ছাত্রকে নিখুঁত করে।
   - Example 2: "I do not know [where he has parked his car]."
     * বাংলা: আমি জানি না সে কোথায় তার গাড়িটি পার্ক করেছে।
   - Example 3: "She asked [if I could assist her with React routing]."
     * বাংলা: তিনি জিজ্ঞাসা করলেন আমি তাঁকে রিঅ্যাক্ট রাউটিংয়ে সাহায্য করতে পারব কিনা।
   - Example 4: "We discovered [who had leaked the question paper]."
     * বাংলা: আমরা আবিষ্কার করলাম কে প্রশ্নপত্র ফাঁস করেছিল।

3. OBJECT OF A PREPOSITION (Preposition-এর অবজেক্ট হিসেবে):
   - Example 1: "Pay careful attention TO [what your mentor demonstrates]."
     * Analysis: The clause is governed by the preposition 'to'.
     * বাংলা: তোমার মেন্টর যা প্রদর্শন করেন তার প্রতি গভীর মনোযোগ দাও।
   - Example 2: "There is no substance IN [what the opposition claims]."
     * বাংলা: বিরোধীরা যা দাবি করছে তার মধ্যে কোনো সারবত্তা নেই।
   - Example 3: "Her success depends ON [how consistently she practices]."
     * বাংলা: তার সাফল্য নির্ভর করে সে কতটা ধারাবাহিকভাবে অনুশীলন করে তার ওপর।
   - Example 4: "He was punished FOR [what he had confessed in writing]."
     * বাংলা: সে লিখিতভাবে যা স্বীকার করেছিল তার জন্য তাকে শাস্তি দেওয়া হয়েছিল।

4. SUBJECT COMPLEMENT (Linking Verb-এর পর বসে Subject-এর অর্থ পূর্ণ করে):
   - Example 1: "The harsh reality is [that we lack sufficient computing hardware]."
     * Analysis: Follows the linking verb 'is' to complete the identity of 'reality'.
     * বাংলা: কঠিন বাস্তবতা হলো আমাদের পর্যাপ্ত কম্পিউটিং হার্ডওয়্যারের অভাব রয়েছে।
   - Example 2: "My greatest fear was [that the storm might destroy the crops]."
     * বাংলা: আমার সবচেয়ে বড় ভয় ছিল এই যে ঝড়টি হয়তো ফসল নষ্ট করে দেবে।
   - Example 3: "Life is [what we make of our opportunities]."
     * বাংলা: জীবন হলো আমরা আমাদের সুযোগগুলিকে যেভাবে রূপ দিই।
   - Example 4: "His explanation appears [that he was misled by rumors]."
     * বাংলা: তাঁর ব্যাখ্যাটি এমন মনে হয় যে তাঁকে গুজবের মাধ্যমে বিভ্রান্ত করা হয়েছিল।

5. IN APPOSITION TO A PRECEDING NOUN (Noun-এর পাশে বসে তার অভ্যন্তরীণ বিষয়বস্তু প্রকাশ করে):
   - Example 1: "The news [that the scientist won the Nobel Prize] delighted everyone."
     * Analysis: The clause explains the exact internal content of the noun 'news'.
     * বাংলা: বিজ্ঞানী যে নোবেল পুরস্কার জিতেছেন সেই খবরটি সকলকে আনন্দিত করেছিল।
   - Example 2: "The rumor [that the commercial bank has failed] is completely baseless."
     * বাংলা: বাণিজ্যিক ব্যাংকটি দেউলিয়া হয়ে গেছে বলে যে গুজব তা সম্পূর্ণ ভিত্তিহীন।
   - Example 3: "The belief [that hard work always yields results] sustains his efforts."
     * বাংলা: কঠোর পরিশ্রম সর্বদা ফল দেয়—এই বিশ্বাসটি তাঁর প্রচেষ্টাকে বাঁচিয়ে রাখে।
   - Example 4: "We cannot ignore the fact [that pollution is rising exponentially]."
     * বাংলা: দূষণ যে জ্যামিতিক হারে বাড়ছে এই সত্যকে আমরা উপেক্ষা করতে পারি না।

4. ADJECTIVE (RELATIVE) CLAUSES: DEFINING vs NON-DEFINING & PRONOUN LAWS
================================================================================
An Adjective Clause modifies an immediately preceding Noun or Pronoun called 
its ANTECEDENT (পূর্ববর্তী বিশেষ্য/সর্বনাম).

CONNECTIVES USED:
• Relative Pronouns: who, whom, whose (for persons); which (for things/animals); 
  that (for persons/things); as, but (negative relative).
• Relative Adverbs: where (place), when (time), why (reason), how (manner).

CRUCIAL COMPARISON: DEFINING vs NON-DEFINING RELATIVE CLAUSES

+------------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------+
| Dimension              | Defining (Restrictive) Clause   | Non-Defining (Parenthetical)    |
+------------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------+
| 1. Purpose             | Essential to identify which     | Extra supplementary information |
|                        | specific entity is meant.       | (can be dropped without loss).  |
| 2. Punctuation         | STRICTLY NO COMMAS.             | MUST BE ENCLOSED IN COMMAS (,). |
| 3. Use of 'THAT'       | Allowed and very common.        | STRICTLY FORBIDDEN!             |
| 4. Pronoun Omission    | Pronoun can be omitted if       | Pronoun can NEVER be omitted.   |
|    (Contact Clause)    | it acts as object of clause.    |                                 |
| 5. Core Example        | "The doctor who treated Swadeep | "Dr. Sen, who lives in Kolkata, |
|                        | is a cardiologist."             | is a renowned cardiologist."    |
| 6. Bengali Meaning     | (নির্দিষ্ট ডাক্তারকে চেনাচ্ছে)   | (ডা. সেন একজনই, বাড়তি তথ্য)    |
+------------------------+---------------------------------+---------------------------------+

SEMANTIC IMPACT OF COMMAS (EXAM FAVORITE TRAP):
• Sentence A: "My brother who lives in London is a software engineer."
  -> Meaning: I have multiple brothers; the specific one living in London is the engineer.
• Sentence B: "My brother, who lives in London, is a software engineer."
  -> Meaning: I have only one brother; by the way, he lives in London.

OMISSION OF RELATIVE PRONOUN (CONTACT CLAUSES):
• Allowed when Relative Pronoun is the OBJECT of the relative clause:
  - Full: "The programming book [that I bought yesterday] is brilliant."
  - Contact: "The programming book [I bought yesterday] is brilliant." (✓ CORRECT)
• FORBIDDEN when Relative Pronoun is the SUBJECT of the relative clause:
  - Full: "The student [who wrote this code] received full marks."
  - Contact: "The student [wrote this code] received full marks." (✗ WRONG! Ungrammatical!)

HOW TO DIFFERENTIATE NOUN CLAUSE IN APPOSITION vs ADJECTIVE CLAUSE (THE 'WHICH' TEST):
1. "The statement [that the witness gave] was recorded."
   -> Test: Can 'that' be replaced with 'which'? Yes -> "The statement [which the witness gave]..."
   -> VERDICT: ADJECTIVE CLAUSE (The witness gave the statement).
2. "The statement [that the accused was innocent] was accepted."
   -> Test: Can 'that' be replaced with 'which'? No! 'that' is pure conjunction.
   -> VERDICT: NOUN CLAUSE IN APPOSITION (Explains the fact inside the statement).

5. ADVERBIAL CLAUSES: THE EXHAUSTIVE 9-CLASS TAXONOMY
================================================================================
An Adverbial Clause modifies a Verb, Adjective, or Adverb in the Principal Clause.

1. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF TIME (সময়সূচক ক্লজ - Answers 'When?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: when, whenever, while, before, after, since, until, 
     as soon as, no sooner... than, hardly... when.
   • Example: "As soon as the teacher entered the classroom, the students stood up."
   • বাংলা: শিক্ষক শ্রেণীকক্ষে প্রবেশ করা মাত্রই ছাত্রছাত্রীরা উঠে দাঁড়াল।
   • Rule: Never use 'will' in subordinate time clauses: "I will call you when I [arrive] (NOT 'will arrive')."

2. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF PLACE (স্থানসূচক ক্লজ - Answers 'Where?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: where, wherever, whither (to where), whence (from where).
   • Example: "You may sit wherever you feel most comfortable."
   • বাংলা: তোমার যেখানে সবচেয়ে আরামদায়ক মনে হয় তুমি সেখানে বসতে পারো।

3. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF CAUSE / REASON (কারণসূচক ক্লজ - Answers 'Why?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: because, as, since, seeing that, for, in as much as.
   • Example: "Since it was raining heavily, the football match was postponed."
   • বাংলা: যেহেতু প্রবল বৃষ্টি হচ্ছিল, তাই ফুটবল ম্যাচটি স্থগিত করা হয়েছিল।

4. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF PURPOSE (উদ্দেশ্যসূচক ক্লজ - Answers 'With what aim?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: so that, in order that, that, lest (+ should + V1).
   • Example 1: "Swadeep studies systematically so that he may crack the exam."
   • Example 2: "Walk cautiously lest you should stumble on the rocky path."
   • বাংলা: সাবধানে হাঁটো যাতে পাথুরে পথে তুমি হোঁচট না খাও ('lest'-এর পর সর্বদা should বসে, not বসে না)।

5. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF RESULT / CONSEQUENCE (ফলাফলসূচক ক্লজ - Answers 'With what effect?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: so... that, such... that.
   • Example: "The coding problem was so intricate that nobody could solve it in time."
   • বাংলা: কোডিং সমস্যাটি এতটাই জটিল ছিল যে কেউ সময়মতো তা সমাধান করতে পারেনি।

6. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF CONDITION (শর্তমূলক ক্লজ - Answers 'On what condition?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: if, unless (if not), provided that, in case, as long as.
   • Example: "Unless you practice writing daily, fluency cannot be attained."
   • বাংলা: তুমি যদি প্রতিদিন লেখার অনুশীলন না করো, তবে সাবলীলতা অর্জন করা অসম্ভব।

7. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF CONCESSION / CONTRAST (স্বীকারোক্তিমূলক বা বৈসাদৃশ্য ক্লজ - Answers 'In spite of what?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: although, though, even though, even if, while, whereas.
   • Example: "Although he faced immense financial hurdles, he completed his master's degree."
   • বাংলা: যদিও তিনি প্রচণ্ড আর্থিক বাধার সম্মুখীন হয়েছিলেন, তবুও তিনি তাঁর মাস্টার্স ডিগ্রি সম্পন্ন করেন।

8. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF COMPARISON / DEGREE (তুলনামূলক ক্লজ - Answers 'To what extent?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: than, as... as, so... as, the... the.
   • Example 1: "She codes much faster than her peers do."
   • Example 2: "The higher you climb the mountain, the cooler the air becomes."
   • বাংলা: তুমি পাহাড়ের যত উঁচুতে উঠবে, বাতাস ততটাই শীতল হবে।

9. ADVERBIAL CLAUSE OF MANNER (রীতিসূচক বা প্রণালীসূচক ক্লজ - Answers 'In what way?'):
   • Trigger Conjunctions: as, as if, as though (subjunctive were).
   • Example: "He commanded the team as if he were the supreme general."
   • বাংলা: সে দলটিকে এমনভাবে আদেশ দিচ্ছিল যেন সে সর্বাধিনায়ক।

6. UNIVERSAL 4-STEP ALGORITHM TO PARSE ANY COMPLEX SENTENCE
================================================================================
Step 1: Locate and count all FINITE VERBS.
        If there are 3 finite verbs, there must be exactly 3 clauses.
Step 2: Identify all CLAUSE CONNECTIVES (Conjunctions / Relative words).
Step 3: Isolate the PRINCIPAL CLAUSE (the one that can stand alone).
Step 4: Identify the function of each SUBORDINATE CLAUSE:
        - Does it answer "What?" -> NOUN CLAUSE (Find role: Subj, Obj, Prep-Obj, Comp, Apposition).
        - Does it modify an antecedent noun? -> ADJECTIVE CLAUSE (Check Defining vs Non-Defining).
        - Does it answer When/Where/Why/How/Condition? -> ADVERBIAL CLAUSE (Categorize into 9 types).
