1. THE 10-POINT DIAGNOSTIC SCANNER PROTOCOL (পরীক্ষার নির্ভুল স্ক্যানিং পদ্ধতি)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When analyzing any sentence correction or error-spotting problem in competitive
exams (SSC, Banking, WBCS, CDS, UPSC, IELTS), scan sequentially using this
10-point checklist:

STEP 1: SUBJECT-VERB CONCORD (কর্তা ও ক্রিয়ার অন্বয়)
- Find the true grammatical head subject (ignore intervening prepositional phrases).
  * "The quality [of these apples] IS superior." (Subject = quality, singular).
- Proximity Rule: Either...or, Neither...nor, Not only...but also -> verb agrees with the CLOSER subject.
- Intervening Additives: As well as, along with, together with, with, in addition to -> verb agrees strictly with the FIRST subject.
- Indefinite Distributives: Each, every, either, neither, one of -> take SINGULAR verbs.

STEP 2: TENSE SEQUENCE & TEMPORAL ANCHORS (কাল ও সময়ের ধারাবাহিকতা)
- Past narrative must not mix present tenses without temporal shifts.
- Dual Past Actions with 'before/after/when':
  * Earlier past action = Past Perfect (had + V3).
  * Later past action = Simple Past (V2).
- Stative Verbs of cognition, emotion, perception, and possession NEVER take continuous (-ing) tenses.
- 'Since' Conjunction Rule: [Present Perfect] + since + [Simple Past] ("5 years have passed since I saw him").

STEP 3: PRONOUN CASE & ANTECEDENT HARMONY (সর্বনাম ও পূর্বপদের সামঞ্জস্য)
- Prepositions govern OBJECTIVE case: "Between you and me" (NOT 'you and I').
- Complement of linking 'be' verb in formal grammar: "It is I" / "If I were he".
- Distributive Antecedents: "Each student must submit his/her work" (NOT 'their' in strict prescriptive exams).
- Politeness Order: 2-3-1 for pleasant/neutral acts ("You, he, and I were selected"); 1-2-3 for confessions/blame ("I, you, and he made the mistake").

STEP 4: MODIFIERS & THE DANGLING PARTICIPLE TRAP (অসংলগ্ন বিশেষণ পদ)
- An introductory participial or modifier phrase must logically modify the SUBJECT immediately following the comma.
  * [X] "Walking in the garden, a snake bit him." (The snake was walking? ERROR!)
  * [✓] "Walking in the garden, HE was bitten by a snake." OR "While HE was walking..."

STEP 5: PARALLELISM & COORDINATION (সমান্তরাল বাক্যরীতি)
- Items in a series, comparisons, or connected by FANBOYS/correlatives must share identical grammatical forms.
  * [X] "She likes swimming, to jog, and books."
  * [✓] "She likes swimming, jogging, and reading."
- Correlative Balancers: 'Not only...but also', 'Either...or', 'Neither...nor' must precede identical parts of speech.

STEP 6: PREPOSITIONAL SUPERFLUITY & COLLOCATION (অনুপযুক্ত প্রিপজিশন)
- Transitive verbs taking DIRECT objects without prepositions:
  * discuss (NOT discuss about), describe (NOT describe about), investigate (NOT investigate into),
  * order (NOT order for), reach (NOT reach to), resemble (NOT resemble to), attack (NOT attack on),
  * enter (NOT enter into a room, though 'enter into an agreement' is figurative).
- Fixed Gerund Collocations: 'With a view to', 'look forward to', 'used to', 'addicted to' + V4 (-ing).

STEP 7: REDUNDANCY & PLEONASMS (দ্বিরুক্তি ও অনাবশ্যক শব্দবর্জন)
- Eliminating tautological repetition (see full catalog in Section 2).

STEP 8: CONDITIONAL CLAUSE INVARIANTS (শর্তযুক্ত বাক্যের অলঙ্ঘনীয় সূত্র)
- Zero Conditional: If + Present, Present (Facts).
- Type 1: If + Simple Present, will/can/may + V1. (Never use 'will' in the If-clause!).
- Type 2: If + Simple Past (were/V2), would/could/might + V1.
- Type 3: If + Past Perfect (had + V3), would have + V3. (Never 'would have' in the If-clause!).

STEP 9: ADVERB INVERSION & NEGATIVE FRONTING (নেতিবাচক ক্রিয়াপদের বিপরীত ক্রম)
- When negative or restrictive adverbs begin a sentence (Hardly, Scarcely, Seldom, Rarely, Barely, No sooner, Little, Under no circumstances):
  * Structure: [Negative Adverb] + [Auxiliary Verb] + [Subject] + [Main Verb]...
  * "No sooner HAD I ARRIVED than..."
  * "Hardly HAD SHE SPOKEN when..."

STEP 10: COMPARATIVES, SUPERLATIVES & EXCLUSION (তুলনামূলক ভুল)
- Double comparatives are illegal: "more better" -> "better".
- Self-exclusion with 'other' in comparative degree:
  * "He is taller than any other boy in the class" (Must include 'other'!).
- Latin Comparatives ending in -or (senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior, preferable):
  * Take 'TO', NEVER 'than' ("senior to me", NOT "senior than me").

2. THE TOP 15 PLEONASTIC REDUNDANCIES (পরীক্ষায় সর্বাধিক আসা দ্বিরুক্তি)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Incorrect Redundancy             Correct Standard English      Reasoning
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Repeat again                  Repeat / Say again            'Re-' already means again.
2. Return back                   Return / Go back              'Return' implies going back.
3. Revert back                   Revert / Reply                'Revert' means return/reply.
4. Reason because...             Reason why... / Reason is that 'Reason' already conveys cause.
5. Cope up with                  Cope with                     'Cope' means deal successfully.
6. Discuss about                 Discuss                       'Discuss' = talk about.
7. Enter into the room           Enter the room                'Enter' = come into.
8. Blend together                Blend / Mix                   'Blend' inherently combines.
9. Mutual agreement between both Agreement / Mutual agreement  'Mutual' already implies both.
10. Final conclusion             Conclusion                    A conclusion is inherently final.
11. Past history                 History                       History is inherently in the past.
12. Free gift                    Gift                          A gift is inherently free.
13. Unexpected surprise          Surprise                      A surprise is inherently unexpected.
14. Advance reservation          Reservation                   Reservations are always in advance.
15. Flee away                    Flee                          'Flee' = run away.

3. INDIAN ENGLISH (L1) INTERFERENCE TRAPS & CORRECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Subcontinental / Bengali Habit    Standard Formal English       Grammatical Explanation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"He passed out from college."    "He graduated from college."  'Pass out' = faint/lose consciousness.
"I am having a headache."        "I have a headache."          Sensory/bodily states are stative.
"He is my cousin brother."       "He is my cousin."            'Cousin' has no gender suffix.
"Do the needful."                "Please take necessary action." Archaic British colonial idiom.
"Let us prepone the meeting."    "Reschedule earlier / advance." 'Prepone' is informal Indian English.
"Sleep is coming." (ঘুম পাচ্ছে)  "I feel sleepy / I am drowsy." Direct L1 conceptual translation.
"Open/Close the fan."            "Turn on/off the fan."        Appliance switches require on/off.
"Cut the phone."                 "Hang up / disconnect."       Telephony requires 'hang up'.
"He gave a speech." (লেকচার দেওয়া) "He delivered a lecture."   High-register collocation.

4. BENGALI EXPLANATORY SUMMARY (বাংলা ব্যাকরণিক নির্দেশিকা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Spotting Errors হলো ইংরেজি ব্যাকরণের সমগ্র জ্ঞানের সম্মিলিত প্রয়োগ। 
- বাংলায় "আমার ঠান্ডা লাগছে" বা "তার জ্বর আসছে" মতো বাক্য ইংরেজিতে Stative Verb দিয়ে প্রকাশ করতে হয় ("I have a cold", "He has a fever")। continuous tense (is coming / is having) ব্যবহার করা বিরাট ভুল।
- Preposition-এর ক্ষেত্রে মনে রাখবেন: discuss, describe, investigate, resemble, order ইত্যাদি ক্রিয়ার পর কোনো preposition বসে না।
- 'With a view to' এবং 'Look forward to'-এর 'to' একটি preposition, তাই এদের পর Verb-এর সাথে '-ing' (Gerund) যোগ করা বাধ্যতামূলক।
- পরীক্ষায় কোনো বাক্যে 'Not only...but also' দেখলে সাথে সাথে উভয়ের পরের পদ লক্ষ্য করুন—একটি Noun হলে অন্যটিও Noun হতে হবে, একটি Verb হলে অন্যটিও Verb হতে হবে।
