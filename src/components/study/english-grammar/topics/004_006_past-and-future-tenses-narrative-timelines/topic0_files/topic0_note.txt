1. THE PAST TENSE SYSTEM: RETROSPECTIVE & NARRATIVE ASPECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. SIMPLE PAST TENSE (V2 Past Form)                                          |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  [A] Action completed at a definite point in the past:
      - "India won independence in 1947."
      - "She submitted her thesis yesterday."
  [B] Discontinued past habit / state ('Used to' / 'Would'):
      - "He played cricket during his college years." / "He USED TO play cricket."

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. PAST CONTINUOUS TENSE (was / were + V-ing)                                |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  [A] Action in progress at a specific past point:
      - "At 8 PM yesterday, I was watching the news."
  [B] Interrupted past action (Longer ongoing action interrupted by short event):
      - "I WAS COOKING dinner (longer action) WHEN the doorbell rang (V2 short)."
  [C] Parallel simultaneous past actions (Concurrently ongoing with 'WHILE'):
      - "WHILE Priya was studying, Rahul was playing the violin."

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. PAST PERFECT TENSE (had + V3 Past Participle) - "PAST OF THE PAST"       |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  When two actions happened in the past, the EARLIER action is in the PAST PERFECT, 
  and the SUBSEQUENT action is in the SIMPLE PAST:

  * THE 'BEFORE' FORMULA:
    PAST PERFECT (had + V3) + BEFORE + SIMPLE PAST (V2)
    - "The patient HAD DIED before the doctor ARRIVED."
    - "The train HAD LEFT before we REACHED the platform."

  * THE 'AFTER' FORMULA:
    SIMPLE PAST (V2) + AFTER + PAST PERFECT (had + V3)
    - "The doctor ARRIVED after the patient HAD DIED."
    - "We REACHED the platform after the train HAD LEFT."

  * UNFULFILLED PAST WISHES / HOPES:
    - "I HAD HOPED to see the Director (but I could not)."

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. PAST PERFECT CONTINUOUS TENSE (had + been + V-ing)                       |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  Emphasizes continuous duration of an action up to another past milestone:
  - "They HAD BEEN WAITING for three hours before the bus arrived."


2. THE FUTURE TENSE SYSTEM & THE 4 WAYS OF EXPRESSING FUTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| STRUCTURE         | PRIMARY COMMUNICATIVE ROLE | CANONICAL EXAMPLES          |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 1. WILL / SHALL   | Spontaneous decision /     | - "The phone is ringing;    |
|    + V1 (Base)    | Unplanned prediction       |    I WILL answer it."       |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 2. BE GOING TO    | Prior intention / Plan /   | - "I AM GOING TO buy a car."|
|    + V1 (Base)    | Prediction with evidence   | - "Look at the dark clouds; |
|                   |                            |    it IS GOING TO rain."    |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 3. PRESENT CONT.  | Fixed personal arrangement | - "I AM MEETING the client  |
|    (am/is/are+-ing| involving other people     |    tomorrow at 11 AM."      |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 4. SIMPLE PRESENT | Official schedule / public | - "The train LEAVES at      |
|    (V1 / V5)      | fixed timetable            |    6:30 PM this evening."   |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+

  * FUTURE CONTINUOUS (will be + V-ing):
    - Action in progress at a future moment: "This time tomorrow, I WILL BE FLYING."

  * FUTURE PERFECT (will have + V3):
    - Action completed BY a specific future deadline ('By + future marker'):
    - "By next year, I WILL HAVE COMPLETED my degree."
    - "By the time he arrives, we WILL HAVE PREPARED dinner."

  * FUTURE PERFECT CONTINUOUS (will have been + V-ing):
    - "By 2028, Dr. Roy WILL HAVE BEEN TEACHING for twenty years."


3. THE GRAND 12-TENSE REFERENCE MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------------+
| TENSE & ASPECT     | FORMULA                | ACTIVE CANONICAL EXAMPLE       |
+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------------+
| 1. Present Simple  | S + V1 / V5 (-s/-es)   | She writes code every day.     |
| 2. Present Cont.   | S + am/is/are + V-ing  | She is writing code right now. |
| 3. Present Perfect | S + have/has + V3      | She has written five modules.  |
| 4. Present Perf. C | S + have/has+been+V-ing| She has been writing since 8 AM|
+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------------+
| 5. Past Simple     | S + V2 (Past form)     | She wrote code yesterday.      |
| 6. Past Continuous | S + was/were + V-ing   | She was writing code at 9 PM.  |
| 7. Past Perfect    | S + had + V3           | She had written code before ...|
| 8. Past Perf. Cont | S + had+been + V-ing   | She had been writing for hours.|
+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------------+
| 9. Future Simple   | S + will/shall + V1    | She will write code tomorrow.  |
| 10. Future Cont.   | S + will be + V-ing    | She will be writing code then. |
| 11. Future Perfect | S + will have + V3     | She will have written by noon. |
| 12. Future Perf. C | S + will have+been+V-in| She will have been writing ... |
+--------------------+------------------------+--------------------------------+


4. HIGH-FREQUENCY COMPETITIVE EXAM TENSE TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRAP 1: Standalone Past Perfect without Secondary Reference
    - INCORRECT: I had met him two days ago.
    - CORRECT:   I MET him two days ago. (Simple Past)

  TRAP 2: Reversing 'Before' / 'After' Sequence
    - INCORRECT: The train left before we had reached the station.
    - CORRECT:   The train HAD LEFT before we REACHED the station.

  TRAP 3: False Irregular Past Inflections
    - INCORRECT: The news was broadcasted on television.
    - CORRECT:   The news was BROADCAST on television. (broadcast -> broadcast)

  TRAP 4: Missing Future Perfect after 'By the time'
    - INCORRECT: By next month, he will complete his training.
    - CORRECT:   By next month, he WILL HAVE COMPLETED his training.
