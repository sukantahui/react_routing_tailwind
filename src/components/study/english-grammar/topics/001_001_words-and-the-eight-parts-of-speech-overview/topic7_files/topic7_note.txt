1. SYSTEMATIC SENTENCE PARSING BLUEPRINT (বাক্য বিশ্লেষণের সার্বিক রূপরেখা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To accurately diagnose the part of speech of every token in a sentence:

STEP 1: Locate the Finite Verb (The Predicate Core).
        - Ask: What action/state is being predicated? What is the tense?
STEP 2: Identify the Subject (The Nominal Agent/Topic).
        - Ask: Who or what is performing the action / being described?
STEP 3: Identify Objects & Complements.
        - Direct Object (What/Whom?), Indirect Object (To whom/For whom?).
        - Subject Complement (after linking verb) or Object Complement.
STEP 4: Trace Modifiers.
        - Attributive Adjectives & Noun-adjuncts (modifying nouns).
        - Adverbs of Manner, Time, Place, Degree (modifying verbs/adj/adv).
STEP 5: Trace Connectors.
        - Prepositions (governing nominal objects).
        - Conjunctions (FANBOYS or Subordinating linking clauses).
        - Interjections (independent emotive markers).

2. MULTI-ROLE WORD DIAGNOSTIC BENCHMARK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TOKEN "UP":
  • Preposition: "He ran up the hill." (+ Nominal Object)
  • Adverb:      "Prices went up rapidly." (Modifies Verb 'went')
  • Adjective:   "Board the up train." (Modifies Noun 'train')
  • Noun:        "Life has its ups and downs." (Direct Object)
  • Verb:        "They decided to up the stakes." (Infinitive verb)

TOKEN "SINCE":
  • Preposition: "Since yesterday" (+ Nominal Object)
  • Conjunction: "Since it was late, we left." (+ Clause)
  • Adverb:      "I have not seen him since." (Stands alone at clause end)

TOKEN "YESTERDAY":
  • Adverb: "She arrived yesterday." (Answers 'When?')
  • Noun:   "Yesterday was historic." (Subject of linking verb 'was')
