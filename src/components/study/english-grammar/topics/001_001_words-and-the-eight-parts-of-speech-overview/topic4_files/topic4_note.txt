1. CORE DEFINITIONS & TAXONOMY (মূল সংজ্ঞা ও শ্রেণিবিভাগ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ADJECTIVES (Nominal Qualifiers)
   • Target: Modifies Nouns & Pronouns exclusively.
   • Positions:
     - Attributive: Placed directly before noun ("a brilliant student").
     - Predicative: Placed after linking verb ("the student is brilliant").
   • Royal Order of Cumulative Adjectives (OSASCOMP):
     [O]pinion -> [S]ize -> [A]ge -> [S]hape -> [C]olor -> [O]rigin -> [M]aterial -> [P]urpose
     Example: "A lovely small antique round wooden dining table."

2. ADVERBS (Action & Degree Modifiers)
   • Target: Modifies Verbs, Adjectives, other Adverbs, or whole clauses.
   • Types: Manner (how), Time (when), Place (where), Frequency (how often), Degree (how much).
   • Royal Order of Multiple Adverbs (MPT Rule):
     Manner -> Place -> Time
     Example: "Swadeep sang beautifully [M] at Barrackpore [P] yesterday [T]."

2. THE "-LY" DECEPTION & FLAT ADVERB MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Noun + "-ly" = ADJECTIVE:
  - Friend + ly  = Friendly   (e.g., "a friendly mentor")
  - Love + ly    = Lovely     (e.g., "a lovely morning")
  - Coward + ly  = Cowardly   (e.g., "a cowardly act")
  * ERROR: "He spoke friendly" [WRONG] -> "He spoke in a friendly manner" [CORRECT].

• Adjective + "-ly" = ADVERB:
  - Quick + ly = Quickly      (e.g., "he ran quickly")
  - Brave + ly = Bravely      (e.g., "they fought bravely")

• FLAT ADVERBS (Identical form for Adjective & Adverb):
  - Fast:  "a fast car" (Adj)  vs  "drives fast" (Adv; NEVER *"fastly"*)
  - Hard:  "hard work" (Adj)   vs  "works hard" (Adv) vs "hardly works" (Scarcely)
  - Late:  "late train" (Adj)  vs  "arrived late" (Adv) vs "lately" (Recently)

3. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGE & TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] TRAP 1: Using adverbs after sensory Linking Verbs.
    - "The flower smells sweetly" [WRONG] -> "The flower smells sweet" [CORRECT].
    - "I feel badly" [WRONG] -> "I feel bad" [CORRECT].

[X] TRAP 2: Confusing 'Hard' vs 'Hardly'.
    - "Swadeep worked hardly" [WRONG: means he did almost no work!]
    - "Swadeep worked hard" [CORRECT: means diligent effort].
