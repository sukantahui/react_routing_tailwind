1. THE MODIFYING DUO: ADJECTIVES VS ADVERBS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ADJECTIVE: Modifies Nouns & Pronouns.
  * Attributive: "A brilliant researcher." (Precedes noun)
  * Predicative: "The researcher is brilliant." (Follows linking verb)
  * Predicative-Only Invariants: afraid, asleep, awake, aware, alive, glad.

- ADVERB: Modifies Verbs, Adjectives, and other Adverbs.
  * Modifying Verb: "Swadeep coded flawlessly."
  * Modifying Adjective: "An exceptionally bright idea."
  * Modifying Adverb: "He spoke very eloquently."

2. THE ROYAL ADVERB SEQUENCE (M-P-T RULE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Formula: Manner (How) -> Place (Where) -> Time (When)
- Example: "Tuhina performed brilliantly [M] at the auditorium [P] yesterday [T]."

3. CRITICAL BENGALI LEARNER EXAM TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Trap 1: Noun + '-ly' vs Adjective + '-ly'
  - Noun + '-ly' = ADJECTIVE (Friendly, Lovely, Cowardly, Scholarly).
  - Adjective + '-ly' = ADVERB (Quickly, Gracefully, Beautifully).
  - Wrong: "He talked friendly." ❌
  - Right: "He talked in a friendly manner." ✔️

* Trap 2: Sensory Verbs & Complements
  - Wrong: "The rose smells sweetly." ❌
  - Right: "The rose smells sweet." ✔️ (Subject Complement Adjective!)

* Trap 3: Confusable Adverb Pairs
  - Hard (with effort) vs Hardly (almost not at all)
  - Late (after time) vs Lately (recently)
  - Fairly (+ pleasant) vs Rather (+ unpleasant / excess)
