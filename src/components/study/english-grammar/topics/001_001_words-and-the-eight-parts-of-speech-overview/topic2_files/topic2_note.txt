1. THE GOLDEN SYNTACTIC PRINCIPLE (ফর্ম বনাম ফাংশন)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In English, a word NEVER has a static, unchangeable Part of Speech in isolation.
"It is not what a word looks like, but what work it does in a sentence that 
 determines its Part of Speech." — Sukanta Hui

2. HIGH-FREQUENCY MULTI-FUNCTION WORD CATALOG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WORD: "WATER"
  • Noun:      "Drink clean water daily." (Object / Substance)
  • Verb:      "Water the saplings in the garden." (Action of irrigating)
  • Adjective: "Install a heavy-duty water pipe." (Noun-adjunct modifier)

WORD: "FAST"
  • Adjective: "This is a fast train to Howrah." (Modifies noun 'train')
  • Adverb:    "He ran fast to catch the bus." (Modifies verb 'ran'; NEVER 'fastly'!)
  • Noun:      "She broke her 24-hour fast." (Abstinence from food)
  • Verb:      "Devotees fast during religious festivals." (Action of fasting)

WORD: "ROUND"
  • Adjective:   "We sat around a round table." (Geometric shape)
  • Noun:        "The doctor made his morning round." (Inspection circuit)
  • Verb:        "The vehicles round the curve safely." (Action of passing)
  • Preposition: "The earth revolves round the sun." (Spatial relator)
  • Adverb:      "Come round to my office this evening." (Directional particle)

WORD: "BEFORE"
  • Preposition: "He submitted the report before 5 PM." (+ Nominal Object)
  • Adverb:      "I have never visited Barrackpore before." (Modifies verb; stands alone)
  • Conjunction: "Look before you leap." (+ Subordinate clause with subject & verb)

WORD: "BUT"
  • Conjunction: "He worked hard, but he failed." (FANBOYS contrast)
  • Preposition: "All but Debangshu arrived." (Means 'except')
  • Adverb:      "He is but a beginner." (Means 'only' / 'merely')

WORD: "WELL"
  • Noun:        "The village well has dried up." (Water source)
  • Adverb:      "Swadeep solved the algorithm well." (Modifies action 'solved')
  • Adjective:   "The patient is now completely well." (Subject complement: healthy)
  • Interjection:"Well! That was an unexpected outcome." (Emotive discourse marker)

3. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGE & TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] TRAP 1: Assuming words ending in '-ly' are always adverbs.
    - 'Friendly', 'lonely', 'lovely', 'brotherly', 'manly', 'cowardly' are ADJECTIVES!
    - "He behaved friendly" [INCORRECT] -> "He behaved in a friendly manner" [CORRECT].

[X] TRAP 2: Using 'fastly'.
    - "Fastly" DOES NOT EXIST in standard English. 'Fast' is both adjective and adverb.

[✓] METHOD OF IDENTIFICATION:
    1. Look at the word's neighbours (articles, prepositions, helping verbs).
    2. Ask: Does it name? (Noun) Does it act? (Verb) Does it describe? (Adj) 
       Does it specify manner/time/place? (Adv) Does it connect? (Prep / Conj).

4. ESSENTIAL REVISION CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[✓] Can demonstrate 'water', 'fast', 'round', 'before', 'but', and 'well' in 3+ roles.
[✓] Know how to distinguish Preposition (needs object) from Subordinating Conjunction (needs clause).
[✓] Recognize flat adverbs ('fast', 'hard', 'late') without creating artificial '-ly' words.
