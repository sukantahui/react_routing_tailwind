1. THE GOLDEN SYNTACTIC LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Never categorize a word in isolation. Look at the work it does in the sentence."
A single orthographic form can shift across 3 to 5 different parts of speech 
depending on its structural environment.

2. MULTI-FUNCTIONAL WORD SHOWCASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. WATER:
   - Noun:       "Drink fresh water daily." (Object Noun)
   - Verb:       "Water the saplings in the nursery." (Action Verb)
   - Adjective:  "Swadeep bought a water bottle." (Noun-Adjunct Modifier)

B. FAST:
   - Adjective:  "This is a fast train to Howrah." (Qualifying 'train')
   - Adverb:     "He ran fast to board the bus." (Modifying 'ran')
   - Noun:       "They observed a strict fast." (Object Noun)
   - Verb:       "Devotees fast during the celebration." (Action Verb)

C. ROUND:
   - Adjective:  "A large round conference table." (Qualifying 'table')
   - Noun:       "The doctor made the night round." (Circuit / Tour)
   - Verb:       "The bus rounds the corner." (Transitive Action)
   - Preposition:"The planets revolve round the sun." (Spatial Relator)
   - Adverb:     "Come round to my office tomorrow." (Particle Adverb)

D. BUT:
   - Conjunction:"Tuhina tried hard, but missed the deadline." (Coordinating)
   - Preposition:"All attended the meeting but Swadeep." (Meaning 'except')
   - Adverb:     "Life is but an empty dream." (Meaning 'only / merely')
   - Relative:   "There is no one but wishes him well." (Negative Relative)

3. EXAM TRAPS FOR BENGALI-MEDIUM STUDENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Trap 1: Assuming all words ending in '-ly' are adverbs.
  - "He speaks friendly" ❌ -> "He speaks in a friendly manner" ✔️
  - ('friendly', 'lovely', 'lonely', 'cowardly' are ADJECTIVES!)

* Trap 2: Inventing nonexistent forms like "fastly".
  - "He drove fastly" ❌ -> "He drove fast" ✔️
