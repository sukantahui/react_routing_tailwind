1. THE ARCHITECTURAL TRIAD (মৌলিক বাক্য ত্রয়ী)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. NOUNS (The Entities & Namers)
   • Proper (Barrackpore, Sukanta Hui) vs Common (student, city).
   • Concrete (laptop, river) vs Abstract (integrity, intelligence).
   • Countable (pen -> pens) vs Uncountable (water, information, advice).
   • Collective (team, committee, audience).

2. PRONOUNS (The Nominal Substitutes & Cohesion Keepers)
   • Personal: Subjective (I, he, they) vs Objective (me, him, them).
   • Possessive: Determiners (my, your, their) vs Standalone (mine, yours, theirs).
   • Relative: who, whom, whose, which, that.
   • Reflexive / Emphatic: myself, himself, themselves.
   • Demonstrative: this, that, these, those.
   • Distributive: each, either, neither (Always singular verbs!).

3. VERBS (The Temporal Engine & Predication Powerhouse)
   • Lexical (Main): Transitive (needs object), Intransitive (no object), Ditransitive (2 objects).
   • Linking (Copular): connects subject to predicate noun/adjective (is, seem, become, taste).
   • Auxiliaries: Primary (Be, Do, Have) + Modals (Can, May, Must, Shall, Will, etc.).
   • The 5 Conjugation Forms: V1 (Base), V2 (Past), V3 (Past Participle), V4 (-ing), V5 (-s/-es).

2. COMPARATIVE BENGALI BRIDGES & TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] TRAP 1: Zero Copula (Missing Verb Trap)
    - Bengali: "সে খুব চতুর" (No verb).
    - English requires Finite Verb: "He IS very clever." (Never *"He very clever"*).

[X] TRAP 2: Stative Verbs in Continuous Tense
    - Bengali: "আমি বুঝতে পারছি"
    - English: "I understand." (Never *"I am understanding"*).

[X] TRAP 3: Uncountable Noun Plurals
    - "Informations", "Sceneries", "Poetries", "Advices" are INVALID.
    - Correct: "Pieces of information", "Scenery", "Poems", "Words of advice".
