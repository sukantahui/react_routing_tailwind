1. CONCEPTUAL DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every word in the English language belongs to either an OPEN CLASS or a 
CLOSED CLASS based on lexical receptivity and syntactic function.

2. OPEN CLASSES (CONTENT / LEXICAL WORDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: Classes that freely admit new words (neologisms, loanwords, slang,
  scientific coinages) and evolve continuously.
- The 4 Open Classes:
  1. NOUNS: smartphone, algorithm, cybersecurity, webinar, vlog.
  2. LEXICAL VERBS: to google, to download, to troubleshoot, to livestream.
  3. ADJECTIVES: algorithmic, viral, user-friendly, photogenic.
  4. ADVERBS: cybernetically, seamlessly, flawlessly.

3. CLOSED CLASSES (STRUCTURE / FUNCTIONAL WORDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: Classes with a fixed, stable inventory of grammatical words that
  rarely or never accept new members.
- The Core Closed Classes:
  1. PRONOUNS: he, she, it, they, someone, whoever, each.
  2. PREPOSITIONS: in, on, at, through, despite, beneath.
  3. CONJUNCTIONS: and, but, or, because, although, since.
  4. DETERMINERS / ARTICLES: a, an, the, this, those, every.
  5. AUXILIARY VERBS: is, are, was, have, do, can, must, should.

4. COMPARATIVE MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Feature               | Open Classes          | Closed Classes        |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+
| Inventory Size        | Infinite / Growing    | Finite / Fixed (~300) |
| Semantic Weight       | Heavy lexical meaning | Grammatical glue      |
| Spoken Stress         | Typically stressed    | Typically unstressed  |
| Bengali Translation   | Direct lexical match  | Postposition/Inflect. |
+-----------------------+-----------------------+-----------------------+

5. BENGALI LEARNER PEDAGOGICAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* বাংলায় যেমন নতুন বিশেষ্য বা ক্রিয়াপদ সহজে যুক্ত হতে পারে কিন্তু অনুসর্গ বা
  যোজক (এবং, কিন্তু, অথচ) স্থির থাকে, ইংরেজিতেও Open Class পরিবর্তনশীল এবং 
  Closed Class স্থিতিশীল।
* ইংরেজি ভাষা দ্রুত আয়ত্ত করতে Closed Class (Preposition, Conjunction, Pronoun)
  সম্পূর্ণ মুখস্থ করে ফেলতে হবে, আর Open Class নিয়মিত রিডিং ও ভোকাবুলারির মাধ্যমে
  সমৃদ্ধ করতে হবে।
