1. CORE TAXONOMY & CLASSIFICATION (মূল বিভাজন ও শ্রেণীবিন্যাস)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The English lexicon is fundamentally divided into two great structural categories:

A. OPEN CLASSES (Content / Lexical Words)
   • Nouns: Entities, people, concepts, digital innovations (e.g. smartphone, prompt).
   • Lexical Verbs: Primary dynamic actions & states (e.g. to download, to stream).
   • Adjectives: Descriptive qualifiers & determinative modifiers (e.g. viral, resilient).
   • Adverbs: Modifiers of manner, degree, time, & position (e.g. seamlessly, globally).
   * Characteristic: Infinitely expandable; accepts neologisms, borrowings, & coinages.

B. CLOSED CLASSES (Structure / Function Words)
   • Pronouns: Nominal substitutes (e.g. he, they, someone, which).
   • Prepositions: Relators of space, time, cause, & logic (e.g. on, across, despite).
   • Conjunctions: Syntactic linkers & coordinators (e.g. and, because, although).
   • Determiners & Articles: Specificity & quantity markers (e.g. the, a, every, few).
   • Auxiliary Verbs & Modals: Aspect & modality markers (e.g. is, have, must, should).
   * Characteristic: Fixed inventory (~300-400 words); forms invariant syntactic rules.

2. COMPARATIVE ARCHITECTURE MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Property            Open Classes (Content)        Closed Classes (Structure)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Total Inventory     Infinite / Unbounded          Finite (~300 to 400 words)
Expansion Method    Derivation, Compounding, etc. Virtually static
Spoken Stress       Heavily stressed in rhythm    Normally unstressed / reduced
Semantic Function   Carries real-world meaning    Provides grammatical scaffolding
Exam Diagnostic     Vocabulary & Comprehension    Error Spotting, Concord, Prepositions

3. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGE & TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] COMMON ERROR: Treating closed classes as secondary or translating them literally.
    - Bengali: "আমি ঘরে আছি" (ঘরে = ঘর + এ বিভক্তি).
    - English requires an explicit preposition: "I am in the room." (Never "I room").

[X] COMMON ERROR: Attempting to invent adverbs by adding '-ly' to everything.
    - English has flat adverbs: 'fast', 'hard', 'late'.
    - "Fastly" is invalid English. 'Fast' functions as both Adjective & Adverb.
