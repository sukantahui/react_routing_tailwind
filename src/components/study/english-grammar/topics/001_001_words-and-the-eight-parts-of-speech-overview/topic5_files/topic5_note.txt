1. THE SYNTACTIC CONNECTORS & EMOTIVE MARKERS (সংযোজক ও আবেগ প্রকাশক পদ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PREPOSITIONS (The Spatial, Temporal & Causal Relators)
   • Role: Places a nominal entity into relationship with another word.
   • Mandatory Rule: Must be followed by a nominal object in Objective Case ("with him", "between you and me").
   • Classification: Simple (in, on, at), Compound (in front of, according to), Phrasal (by dint of).
   • Distinction:
     - Preposition: "He left after 5 PM." (Followed by Noun object)
     - Conjunction: "He left after the class ended." (Followed by full Clause)

2. CONJUNCTIONS (The Clause Synthesizers & Discourse Logic)
   • Coordinating (FANBOYS): For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So (Joins equals).
   • Subordinating: Although, because, since, unless, provided that (Joins dependent to main).
   • Correlative: Pairs requiring strict Syntactic Parallelism:
     - Either... or
     - Neither... nor
     - Not only... but also
     - No sooner... than (NEVER 'when'!)
     - Hardly / Scarcely... when (NEVER 'than'!)

3. INTERJECTIONS (The Emotive Exclamations)
   • Stand outside syntactic clause structure; express immediate human emotion.
   • Examples: Joy (Hurrah!, Bravo!), Grief (Alas!, Ah!), Pain (Ouch!), Attention (Hark!, Hush!).

2. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & HIGH-FREQUENCY TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] TRAP 1: "Despite of"
    - Bengali: "বৃষ্টি সত্ত্বেও"
    - English: "Despite heavy rain" OR "In spite of heavy rain" (NEVER *"Despite of"*).

[X] TRAP 2: Correlative Conjunction Mismatches
    - "No sooner had he arrived WHEN..." [WRONG] -> "No sooner had he arrived THAN..." [CORRECT].
    - "Hardly had we begun THAN..." [WRONG] -> "Hardly had we begun WHEN..." [CORRECT].

[X] TRAP 3: Beside vs Besides
    - Beside  = Next to ("Sit beside me").
    - Besides = In addition to ("Besides coding, he plays violin").

[X] TRAP 4: Double Negative with 'Unless'
    - "Unless you do not work hard" [WRONG: 'Unless' already means 'If not'].
    - "Unless you work hard" [CORRECT].
