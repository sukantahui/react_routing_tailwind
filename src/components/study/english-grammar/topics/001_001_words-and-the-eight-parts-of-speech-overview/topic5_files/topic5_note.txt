1. PREPOSITIONS (THE SYNTACTIC RELATORS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Function: Links nominals (Noun/Pronoun/Gerund) to other clause components.
- Case Requirement: Pronouns after prepositions MUST be in the Objective Case:
  * "Between you and me" ✔️ (Never "between you and I" ❌).
- State vs Motion:
  * In (Static location) vs Into (Dynamic interior motion).
  * On (Static surface) vs Onto (Dynamic upward/surface motion).

2. CONJUNCTIONS (THE SYNTACTIC BINDERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Coordinating (FANBOYS):
   - For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So.
   - Joins independent units of equal grammatical weight.
B. Subordinating:
   - because, although, since, unless, if, while, whereas.
   - Subordinates a dependent clause to a main matrix clause.
C. Correlative Pairs:
   - either... or, neither... nor, not only... but also, both... and.
   - Enforces strict PARALLELISM in structure.

3. INTERJECTIONS (THE EMOTIVE MARKERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Function: Spontaneous emotive bursts (Joy, Grief, Wonder, Disgust).
- Grammatically autonomous: Alas!, Hurrah!, Bravo!, Ouch!, Eureka!

4. BENGALI LEARNER PEDAGOGICAL COMPARISON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* বাংলা ভাষায় অনুসর্গ বা বিভক্তি (যেমন: 'ঘরে', 'টেবিলের ওপর') শব্দের পরে বসে;
  ইংরেজিতে Preposition সর্বদা শব্দের পূর্বে বসে (যেমন: 'in the room', 'on the table')।
* 'Before' / 'After' এর পর শুধু Noun থাকলে তা Preposition, কিন্তু পূর্ণাঙ্গ Clause 
  (Subject + Verb) থাকলে তা Conjunction।
