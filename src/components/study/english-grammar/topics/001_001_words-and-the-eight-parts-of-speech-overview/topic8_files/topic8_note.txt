1. MODULE 001_001 MASTER REVISION CAPSULE (সারসংক্ষেপ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE 8 PARTS OF SPEECH (Function dictates form):
  1. NOUN: Names entities, persons, places, concepts, states (Subject/Object/Comp).
  2. PRONOUN: Nominal substitute (Personal, Relative, Demonstrative, Reflexive, Distributive).
  3. VERB: Temporal engine & predication (Lexical, Linking, Auxiliary, Modal).
  4. ADJECTIVE: Nominal qualifier (Attributive vs Predicative; OSASCOMP order).
  5. ADVERB: Action & degree modifier (Manner, Time, Place, Frequency, Degree; MPT order).
  6. PREPOSITION: Spatial, temporal & causal relator (+ Objective case nominal).
  7. CONJUNCTION: Clause synthesizer (FANBOYS coordinators, Subordinators, Correlatives).
  8. INTERJECTION: Independent emotive markers (Joy, grief, surprise, praise).

• OPEN vs CLOSED CLASSES:
  - Open: Noun, Lexical Verb, Adjective, Adverb (Infinite, content-rich, coinages).
  - Closed: Pronoun, Preposition, Conjunction, Determiner, Auxiliary (~300 items).

• FORM vs FUNCTION TRUTHS:
  - 'Water' = Noun ("drink water"), Verb ("water plants"), Adjective ("water pipe").
  - 'Fast'  = Adjective ("fast car"), Adverb ("run fast"; NEVER *"fastly"*), Noun ("a fast").
  - 'Round' = Adjective ("round table"), Noun ("doctor's round"), Verb ("round the bend"), Preposition ("round the sun").
  - 'Before'= Preposition ("before noon"), Conjunction ("before you leap"), Adverb ("never seen before").

2. THE TOP 5 BENGALI LEARNER TRAPS ELIMINATED (৫টি প্রধান ফাঁদ নিরসন)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ZERO COPULA: "He ill" [WRONG] -> "He IS ill" [CORRECT].
2. STATIVE VERBS: "I am understanding" [WRONG] -> "I understand" [CORRECT].
3. FLAT ADVERBS: "He runs fastly" [WRONG] -> "He runs fast" [CORRECT].
4. PREPOSITION CASE: "Between you and I" [WRONG] -> "Between you and ME" [CORRECT].
5. CORRELATIVE MISMATCH: "No sooner had he come when..." [WRONG] -> "...THAN" [CORRECT].
