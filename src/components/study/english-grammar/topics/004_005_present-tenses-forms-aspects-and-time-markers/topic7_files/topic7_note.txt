TOPIC 7: THE FINISHED PAST TIME ANCHOR PROHIBITION (WHY 'YESTERDAY' + PRESENT PERFECT IS FATAL)

1. THE GOLDEN LAW OF TENSE SEPARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• The Present Perfect (have/has + V3) is a PRESENT tense with past relevance. Its temporal window extends right up to the split second of speech.
• A Finished Past Time Anchor (yesterday, ago, last night, in 1999) places the event inside a sealed, completely closed past timeframe that has ZERO temporal overlap with the present.
• THE IRON RULE: When a sentence specifies a finished past time anchor, the Present Perfect is STRICTLY ILLEGAL. The Simple Past (V2) is mandatory!

2. CATALOG OF FORBIDDEN FINISHED PAST TIME ANCHORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following time expressions CANNOT be paired with have/has + V3:
1. 'Yesterday' / 'The day before yesterday'
2. 'Ago' phrases: two hours ago, five days ago, three years ago
3. 'Last' phrases: last night, last week, last Monday, last year
4. Specific past calendar dates & years: in 1947, in 2020, on August 15th
5. Past life-stage & time clauses: when I was a child, during the pandemic
6. Interrogative 'WHEN': When did you arrive? (NOT: *When have you arrived?)

3. CRUCIAL COMPARATIVE ERROR MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ungrammatical Fatal Error (Exam Trap)   | Pure Standard English Correct Form
----------------------------------------+---------------------------------------
✗ I have seen him yesterday.            | ✓ I saw him yesterday.
✗ The train has arrived 10 minutes ago. | ✓ The train arrived 10 minutes ago.
✗ India has won freedom in 1947.        | ✓ India won freedom in 1947.
✗ When have you purchased this car?     | ✓ When did you purchase this car?
✗ We have visited Delhi last month.     | ✓ We visited Delhi last month.
✗ Shakespeare has written 37 plays.     | ✓ Shakespeare wrote 37 plays.

4. THE UNFINISHED VS FINISHED TIME WINDOW EXCEPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Certain time expressions (e.g. this morning, today, this afternoon, this year) depend on the SPEAKER'S EXACT TIME OF SPEECH:

Scenario A: It is 10:00 AM (Morning is ongoing / unfinished):
✓ "I have drunk two cups of tea this morning." (Present Perfect allowed).

Scenario B: It is 05:00 PM (Morning is finished / past history):
✓ "I drank two cups of tea this morning." (Simple Past mandatory).

5. BENGALI PEDAGOGICAL INSIGHTS (বাংলায় বিশেষ ব্যাখ্যা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• বাংলায় আমরা অনেক সময় বলি "আমি গতকাল তাকে দেখেছি" বা "১৯৪৭ সালে ভারত স্বাধীন হয়েছে"। বাংলা ক্রিয়াপদে এই পার্থক্য ধরা না পড়লেও ইংরেজিতে 'Yesterday', 'Ago', 'In 1947' থাকলে কোনোভাবেই 'have/has + V3' লেখা যাবে না।
• নিশ্চিত মনে রাখবেন: Yesterday, Ago, Last Night, In 1999 ইত্যাদি থাকলে চোখ বন্ধ করে Simple Past (V2) ব্যবহার করতে হবে (e.g. I saw him yesterday; কখনোই I have seen him yesterday নয়)।
• 'When' দিয়ে অতীত সময়ের প্রশ্ন করতে সর্বদা 'When did you...' ব্যবহার করবেন, কখনো 'When have you...' নয়।
