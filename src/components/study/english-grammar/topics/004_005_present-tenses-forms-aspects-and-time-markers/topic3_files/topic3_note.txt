1. STRUCTURAL MECHANICS & FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Affirmative: Subject + am / is / are + V-ing (Present Participle)
  - I + am + working
  - He / She / It / Singular Noun + is + working
  - We / You / They / Plural Noun + are + working

• Negative: Subject + am / is / are + not + V-ing
  - Example: Abhronila is not attending the lecture today.

• Interrogative: Am / Is / Are + Subject + V-ing ...?
  - Example: Are they developing the new algorithmic module?

2. THE 5 CORE SEMANTIC FUNCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(A) Action Happening at the Exact Moment of Speaking:
    - Verifiable real-time activity in progress right now.
    - Time Markers: now, right now, at present, at the moment, currently, Look!, Listen!, Hush!
    - Example: Listen! Someone is playing the violin in the next room.

(B) Temporary Situations (Ongoing Around Now):
    - The action is in progress in the broader timeframe, though not necessarily happening at the exact second of speaking.
    - Time Markers: these days, this week, this semester, currently, nowadays.
    - Example: Tuhina is preparing for the competitive civil services examination this year.
    - Example: I am reading an intriguing biography of Oppenheimer this week.

(C) Changing, Developing Situations & Macro Trends:
    - Dynamic evolution, growth, deterioration, or societal progression.
    - Frequent Verbs: get, become, change, increase, rise, fall, grow, improve, deteriorate.
    - Example: The computational power of AI models is increasing exponentially.
    - Example: Your English pronunciation is improving day by day.

(D) Definite Planned Personal Future Arrangements:
    - Personal commitments where time and place have already been fixed.
    - Time Markers: tonight, tomorrow evening, next Friday, this weekend.
    - Example: We are hosting a grammar seminar at Barrackpore next Sunday.
    - Example: My brother is arriving from London tomorrow at 10:00 AM.

(E) Habitual Irritation & Annoyance with 'ALWAYS' / 'CONSTANTLY':
    - Expresses emotional exasperation, impatience, or criticism toward an unreasonable habit.
    - Formula: Subject + is/are + ALWAYS / CONSTANTLY / CONTINUALLY + V-ing.
    - Neutral Simple Present: "He always loses his keys." (Objective statement of fact).
    - Annoyed Present Continuous: "He is always losing his keys!" (Speaker expresses intense frustration!).
    - Example: Why are you constantly checking your phone during class?

3. ORTHOGRAPHIC RULES FOR FORMING '-ING'
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Verbs ending in silent '-e': Drop '-e' and add '-ing'.
   - write -> writing, make -> making, come -> coming. (Exception: see -> seeing, agree -> agreeing).
2. One-syllable verbs ending in Consonant-Vowel-Consonant (CVC): Double the final consonant.
   - run -> running, sit -> sitting, stop -> stopping, swim -> swimming.
3. Two-syllable verbs with stress on the SECOND syllable: Double the final consonant.
   - be'gin -> beginning, re'fer -> referring, oc'cur -> occurring.
   - (If stress is on first syllable, do NOT double: 'visit -> visiting, 'open -> opening).
4. Verbs ending in '-ie': Change '-ie' to '-y' and add '-ing'.
   - die -> dying, lie -> lying, tie -> tying.

4. BENGALI PEDAGOGICAL INSIGHTS (বাংলায় বিশেষ ব্যাখ্যা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• বর্তমান মুহূর্তে কোনো কাজ চলছে বোঝাতে সাধু ভাষায় "করিতেছে" বা চলিত বাংলায় "করছে" (Present Continuous)।
• তবে বাংলায় "সে সর্বদা অভিযোগ করছে" বিরক্তি প্রকাশ করে; ইংরেজিতেও 'always' + Continuous ব্যবহার করে বিরক্তি বা ক্ষোভ বোঝানো হয় (e.g. He is always complaining!).
• ব্যক্তিগত ভবিষ্যৎ পরিকল্পনা যা ঠিক হয়ে গেছে, তার জন্য Present Continuous চমৎকার (e.g. আমি কাল দিল্লি যাচ্ছি = I am leaving for Delhi tomorrow)।
