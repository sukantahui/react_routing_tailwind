1. DIRECT SPEECH vs INDIRECT (REPORTED) SPEECH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Direct Speech: Quotes the exact words of the speaker enclosed in quotation marks.
  - Formula: [Speaker] + [Reporting Verb] , " [Reported Speech] . "
  - Example: Sukanta Sir said, "Grammar is structural thinking."

• Indirect (Reported) Speech: Reports the essence of what was spoken without exact quotes.
  - Formula: [Speaker] + [Reporting Verb] + THAT + [Subordinate Reported Clause]
  - Example: Sukanta Sir said THAT grammar was structural thinking.

Key Reporting Verb Rule:
  • SAY:  He said that he was ready. (No personal object needed)
  • TELL: He TOLD ME that he was ready. (Personal object mandatory; NEVER 'told to me')

2. THE LAW OF TENSE BACKSHIFT (WHEN REPORTING VERB IS IN PAST TENSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When the Reporting Verb is in the PAST TENSE (said, told, remarked, explained), 
all present and simple past tenses in the reported clause shift one step backward 
into past narrative time:

+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Direct Speech Tense        | Indirect Speech Backshifted Tense               |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Simple Present (V1)        | Simple Past (V2)                                |
| • "I write"                | • he wrote                                      |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Present Continuous (is/am) | Past Continuous (was/were V-ing)                |
| • "I am writing"           | • he was writing                                |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Present Perfect (have/has) | Past Perfect (had + V3)                         |
| • "I have written"         | • he had written                                |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Present Perfect Continuous | Past Perfect Continuous (had been V-ing)        |
| • "I have been writing"    | • he had been writing                           |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Simple Past (V2)           | Past Perfect (had + V3)                         |
| • "I wrote"                | • he had written                                |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Past Continuous (was/were) | Past Perfect Continuous (had been V-ing)        |
| • "I was writing"          | • he had been writing                           |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Past Perfect (had + V3)    | NO CHANGE (Remains Past Perfect)                |
| • "I had written"          | • he had written                                |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Past Perfect Continuous    | NO CHANGE (Remains Past Perfect Continuous)     |
| • "I had been writing"     | • he had been writing                           |
+----------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+

3. MODAL AUXILIARY BACKSHIFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  • Will  ==>  Would
  • Shall ==>  Should (Advice) / Would (Future in the past)
  • Can   ==>  Could
  • May   ==>  Might
  • Must  ==>  Had to (Situational obligation) / Must (Eternal moral rule)
  • Would / Should / Could / Might / Ought to ==> NO CHANGE

================================================================================
4. THE 4 INVARIABLE EXCEPTIONS: ZERO TENSE BACKSHIFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do NOT change the tense of the reported clause in these 4 scenarios:

1. Reporting Verb is in Present or Future Tense:
   - Direct:   The teacher SAYS, "The examination is postponed."
   - Indirect: The teacher says that the examination IS postponed. (NOT 'was')

2. Universal Scientific Truths & Geographical Laws:
   - Direct:   "Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius."
   - Indirect: He said that water BOILS at 100 degrees Celsius. (NOT 'boiled')
   - Direct:   "The earth rotates on its axis."
   - Indirect: She said that the earth ROTATES on its axis.

3. Habitual Actions / Daily Routines:
   - Direct:   He said, "I wake up at 5:00 AM every morning."
   - Indirect: He said that he WAKES up at 5:00 AM every morning.

4. Historical Events with Specific Past Dates:
   - Direct:   The guide said, "Rabindranath won the Nobel Prize in 1913."
   - Indirect: The guide said that Rabindranath WON the Nobel Prize in 1913.

5. TIME & PLACE ADVERBIAL SHIFTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+--------------------+---------------------+-----------------------------------+
| Direct Speech      | Indirect Speech     | Contextual Logic                  |
+--------------------+---------------------+-----------------------------------+
| Now                | Then                | Shift from immediate present      |
| Today              | That day            | Specific calendar reference       |
| Tonight            | That night          | Evening reference                 |
| Tomorrow           | The next day /      | Subsequent calendar day           |
|                    | The following day   |                                   |
| Yesterday          | The previous day /  | Preceding calendar day            |
|                    | The day before      |                                   |
| Last night         | The previous night  | Prior night                       |
| Ago                | Before              | Temporal distance                 |
| Here               | There               | Spatial distance from speaker     |
| This / These       | That / Those        | Remote demonstratives             |
| Hither / Hence     | Thither / Thence    | Archaic directional shifts        |
+--------------------+---------------------+-----------------------------------+
