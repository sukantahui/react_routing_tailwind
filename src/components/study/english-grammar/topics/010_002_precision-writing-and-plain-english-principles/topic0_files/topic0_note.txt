1. THE 7 PILLARS OF PLAIN ENGLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Plain English is not simplistic English; it is high-precision communication designed
to ensure the reader grasps the full meaning on the FIRST reading.

PILLAR 1: AUDIENCE-FIRST PERSPECTIVE
- Write for the reader's needs, cognitive load, and decision-making speed.

PILLAR 2: OPTIMAL SENTENCE LENGTH (THE 15-20 WORD SWEET SPOT)
- Average sentence length should hover between 15 and 20 words.
- Vary rhythm: Mix short 6-word punchy sentences with 22-word explanatory sentences.

PILLAR 3: ACTIVE VOICE DOMINANCE (80/20 RULE)
- At least 80% of sentences should use Active Voice (Subject + Active Verb + Object).
- Reserve Passive Voice strictly when the actor is unknown, irrelevant, or when scientific focus is on the object.

PILLAR 4: LEAN VOCABULARY OVER ARCHAIC JARGON
- Use common, vigorous words instead of Latinate pompous terms:
  * "use" instead of "utilize"
  * "help" instead of "facilitate"
  * "send" instead of "transmit"
  * "start" instead of "commence"

PILLAR 5: ACTIONABLE & DESCRIPTIVE HEADINGS
- Instead of vague headers ("Background Information"), use task-oriented headers ("How to Configure Network Security Settings").

PILLAR 6: BULLET POINT PARALLELISM
- All items in a list must share identical syntactic structure (all starting with base verbs, gerunds, or nouns).

PILLAR 7: POSITIVE & CONSTRUCTIVE FRAMING
- State what CAN be done rather than dwelling solely on prohibitions.
  * [Negative]: "You cannot receive the report unless you register."
  * [Positive]: "Register today to receive your free report."

2. TONE MODULATION SPECTRUM (পেশাদার স্বর নিয়ন্ত্রণ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scenario: A colleague misses a project deadline.

1. Aggressive / Accusatory (Toxic):
   - "You failed to submit the files on time and ruined our presentation."
2. Passive-Aggressive (Evasive):
   - "It would have been nice if some people cared about our deadline."
3. Neutral / Objective (Factual):
   - "The files were not received by the 5:00 PM deadline yesterday."
4. Diplomatic / Constructive (Professional Standard):
   - "We missed yesterday's submission window; could you share your current draft so we can help finalize it today?"
5. Executive / Authoritative (Decisive Leadership):
   - "To maintain our client launch schedule, we need the final draft submitted by 12:00 PM today without further delay."

3. THE FLESCH READING EASE & FLESCH-KINCAID GRADE LEVEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Formula:
Score = 206.835 - (1.015 x ASL) - (84.6 x ASW)
Where:
- ASL = Average Sentence Length (words / sentences)
- ASW = Average Syllables per Word (syllables / words)

Score Range     Readability Level       Target Audience
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
90 - 100        Very Easy               5th grade (children's books)
80 - 89         Easy                    6th grade (conversational English)
70 - 79         Fairly Easy             7th grade (popular newspapers)
60 - 69         Standard Plain English  8th - 9th grade (BBC, Reader's Digest, Tech Docs)
50 - 59         Fairly Difficult        High school
30 - 49         Difficult               Undergraduate / Academic journals
0  - 29         Very Confusing / Dense  Legal contracts / Bureaucratic fog

*GOAL*: Professional emails, technical reports, and business proposals should score 60 - 70!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE BLUF FRAMEWORK (BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Structure of High-Impact Business Communication:
1. THE BOTTOM LINE (1-2 sentences): State the exact decision, request, or recommendation immediately.
2. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE (2-3 bullet points): Present key data, facts, and quantified outcomes.
3. NEXT ACTIONS & DEADLINES: Specify who does what by when.

5. BENGALI EXPLANATORY SUMMARY (বাংলা ব্যাকরণিক ও রচনাশৈলীর বিশ্লেষণ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- বাংলা মাধ্যমে অনেকেই মনে করেন যে ইংরেজিতে যত বড় বড় শব্দ (যেমন: utilize, facilitate, effectuate) ও লম্বা বাক্য লেখা হবে, লেখা তত উন্নত হবে। কিন্তু আন্তর্জাতিক কর্মক্ষেত্রে এটি সম্পূর্ণরূপে ভুল।
- আধুনিক বিশ্বের সব আন্তর্জাতিক সংস্থা (Google, Microsoft, UN, BBC) Plain English নীতি অনুসরণ করে। এর মূল কথা হলো—সহজ শব্দ ও স্পষ্ট বাক্যে মনের ভাব প্রকাশ করা যাতে পাঠক কোনো দ্বিধা ছাড়াই প্রথমবারেই বুঝতে পারেন।
- ইমেইল ও যোগাযোগের ক্ষেত্রে Tone Modulation অত্যন্ত জরুরি। কাউকে সরাসরি দোষারোপ না করে (যেমন: "You made a mistake") সমস্যা সমাধানের দিকে আলোকপাত করুন (যেমন: "Let us review this figure together").
- বাক্যের গড় দৈর্ঘ্য ১৫ থেকে ২০ শব্দের মধ্যে রাখলে পড়ার ছন্দ ও বোধগম্যতা সর্বোচ্চ থাকে।
