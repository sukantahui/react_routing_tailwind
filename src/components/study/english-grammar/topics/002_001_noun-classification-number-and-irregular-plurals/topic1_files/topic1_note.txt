1. THE 5 TRADITIONAL NOUN CATEGORIES
================================================================================
A Noun is a naming word representing a person, place, thing, substance, 
collection, or abstract concept.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROPER NOUN (সংজ্ঞাবাচক বিশেষ্য):
   - Names a specific, unique individual, place, monument, or entity.
   - Orthographic Rule: ALWAYS capitalized regardless of sentence position.
   - Examples: Sukanta Hui, Barrackpore, The Himalayas, Sunday, July, Microsoft.
   - Shift: When used metaphorically with an article, it functions as a common noun:
     "He is the Newton of our modern physics laboratory."

2. COMMON NOUN (জাতিবাচক বিশেষ্য):
   - Names any member of a general class of persons, places, or things.
   - Can be singular or plural (boy/boys, city/cities, book/books).
   - Examples: teacher, engineer, river, mountain, laptop, university.

3. COLLECTIVE NOUN (সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্য):
   - Names a collection, assembly, or group of persons or things considered as a unit.
   - Examples:
     * A flock of birds / sheep
     * A herd of cattle / elephants
     * A fleet of ships / warships
     * A swarm of bees / locusts
     * A pride of lions
     * Committee, Jury, Orchestra, Parliament, Team, Crew.

4. MATERIAL NOUN (বস্তুবাচক বা উপাদানবাচক বিশেষ্য):
   - Names the raw substance, uncounted matter, or physical material from which 
     manufactured articles are made.
   - Uncountable in raw state: Gold, silver, iron, copper, cotton, wood, milk, cement.
   - Contrast: "Wood" (Material) vs "Table / Chair" (Common Noun).

5. ABSTRACT NOUN (গুণবাচক বা ভাববাচক বিশেষ্য):
   - Names a quality, state, action, emotion, or branch of learning that cannot 
     be experienced through the 5 physical senses (touch, taste, smell, sight, hearing).
   - Derived from:
     * Adjectives: Brave -> Bravery; Honest -> Honesty; Kind -> Kindness.
     * Verbs: Obey -> Obedience; Grow -> Growth; Choose -> Choice.
     * Common Nouns: Child -> Childhood; Friend -> Friendship; Leader -> Leadership.
   - Examples: Freedom, justice, wisdom, patience, integrity, geometry, music.

================================================================================
2. PEDAGOGICAL SUMMARY & BENGALI BRIDGES
================================================================================
- Proper Noun (নির্দিষ্ট নাম): সর্বদা Capital Letter দিয়ে শুরু হবে।
- Common Noun (সাধারণ জাতিবাচক): এক জাতীয় সকলকে বোঝায় (যেমন: 'Boy', 'River')।
- Collective Noun (সমষ্টিবাচক): একজাতীয় ব্যক্তি বা প্রাণীর দলকে এককভাবে বোঝায় ('Jury', 'Fleet')।
- Material Noun (উপাদানবাচক): যে মূল উপাদান দিয়ে জিনিস তৈরি হয় ('Gold', 'Wood')।
- Abstract Noun (গুণ বা ভাববাচক): যা ধরা বা ছোঁয়া যায় না, কেবল অনুভব করা যায় ('Honesty', 'Freedom')।
