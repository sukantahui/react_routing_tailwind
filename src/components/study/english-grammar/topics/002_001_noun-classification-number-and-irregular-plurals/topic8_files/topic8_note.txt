1. WHAT IS PLURALIA TANTUM?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Pluralia tantum" (Latin for "plural only", singular: "plurale tantum") denotes nouns that exist solely in the plural form and have no singular counterpart, or whose singular form possesses an entirely different semantic meaning.

They ALWAYS govern a PLURAL VERB and take PLURAL PRONOUNS ('they', 'them', 'their').

2. THE 4 SYSTEMATIC TAXONOMIES OF PLURALIA TANTUM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CATEGORY A: DUAL-PART INSTRUMENTS & TOOLS
• Nouns consisting of two equal, inseparable functional parts joined together:
  - Scissors: The scissors ARE sharp.
  - Shears: Pruning shears ARE kept in the shed.
  - Pincers: Pincers ARE used to extract nails.
  - Tongs: Fire tongs ARE made of iron.
  - Bellows: The blacksmith's bellows ARE working.
  - Tweezers: Tweezers ARE sterilized.
  - Pliers: Electrician's pliers ARE insulated.
  - Binoculars: Binoculars ARE essential for birdwatching.

  * THE "A PAIR OF" HEAD NOUN RULE:
    When quantified by "a pair of", the true subject head noun becomes "pair" (singular), governing a singular verb:
    - Scissors ARE on the desk.
    - A pair of scissors IS on the desk.
    - Two pairs of scissors ARE on the desk.

CATEGORY B: DUAL-LEGGED GARMENTS & EYEWEAR
• Articles of clothing and eyewear split into two symmetrical halves:
  - Trousers: His trousers ARE tailored.
  - Pants / Jeans / Shorts: Blue jeans ARE in fashion.
  - Pyjamas: Cotton pyjamas ARE comfortable.
  - Spectacles / Glasses: My spectacles ARE missing.
  - Goggles: Safety goggles ARE mandatory in the laboratory.

CATEGORY C: NOUNS SINGULAR IN FORM BUT INVARIABLY PLURAL IN CONCORD
• Words without '-s' that represent aggregated human or animal groups and ALWAYS take plural verbs:
  - Cattle: The cattle ARE grazing (NEVER *cattles).
  - Gentry: The local gentry WERE invited.
  - Poultry: Farm poultry ARE vaccinated.
  - Police: The police ARE investigating the crime (NEVER *polices).
  - People (humans): Many people WERE waiting (Note: 'peoples' with -s means distinct nations/ethnicities).
  - Peasantry: The peasantry WERE burdened with heavy taxes.
  - Clergy: The parish clergy WERE respected.
  - Vermin: Vermin ARE infesting the granary.

CATEGORY D: MISCELLANEOUS PLURALIA TANTUM NOUNS
• Assets: His total assets WERE liquidated.
• Alms: Alms WERE given to the poor.
• Riches: Riches HAVE wings (they fly away).
• Thanks: Heartfelt thanks WERE expressed.
• Surroundings: The natural surroundings ARE picturesque.
• Proceeds: The proceeds of the auction WERE donated.
• Remains: Mortal remains WERE interred.
• Outskirts: The city outskirts ARE developing rapidly.
• Tidings: Glad tidings HAVE arrived.
• Earnings: His monthly earnings ARE substantial.
• Wages:
  - Financial salary: His wages ARE paid every Friday.
  - Biblical/Proverbial retribution (Romans 6:23): "The wages of sin IS death."

3. BILINGUAL COMPARATIVE NOTES (ENGLISH ↔ বাংলা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• বাংলায় আমরা বলি "কাঁচিটা ধারালো" (একবচন) বা "পুলিশ আসছে" (একবচন ক্রিয়া)।
• কিন্তু ইংরেজিতে:
  1. Scissors, Trousers, Spectacles—এগুলো দুটো অংশ দিয়ে গঠিত, তাই ইংরেজিতে এগুলো সর্বদা Plural এবং এদের পরে Plural Verb ('are', 'were', 'have') বসে।
  2. এদের একবচন করতে হলে 'a pair of' যোগ করতে হয় ("a pair of scissors is")।
  3. Cattle, Police, Poultry, Gentry—এদের শেষে 's' না থাকলেও এরা জন্মগতভাবে Plural ("The police are", "The cattle are")।
