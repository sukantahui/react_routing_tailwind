1. COUNTABLE VS UNCOUNTABLE SYNTACTIC INVARIANTS
================================================================================
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURE               COUNTABLE NOUNS                 UNCOUNTABLE (MASS) NOUNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition            Discrete individual units       Continuous mass/substance/concept
Pluralization         Takes regular -s/-es (cars, books) NEVER takes plural '-s'
Articles              Takes 'a' / 'an' (a book, an apple) CANNOT take 'a/an' directly
Quantifiers           Many, Few, A few, Several       Much, Little, A little, A lot of
Partitive Units       Direct counting: "three chairs" Requires unit: "three pieces of furniture"
Verbal Concord        Singular OR Plural verb         ALWAYS takes a SINGULAR verb
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

================================================================================
2. PARTITIVE CLASSIFIERS (HOW TO COUNT THE UNCOUNTABLE)
================================================================================
To express quantity for uncountable nouns, use specific partitive classifiers:
- Information / Advice -> A piece of advice / two pieces of information
- Furniture / Equipment -> An item of furniture / pieces of equipment
- Bread -> A loaf of bread / two slices of bread
- Paper -> A sheet of paper / a ream of paper
- Soap -> A bar of soap / three cakes of soap
- Chocolate -> A bar of chocolate / a square of chocolate
- Luggage / Baggage -> A piece of luggage
- Rice / Sand -> A grain of rice / grains of sand

3. DUAL COUNTABLE & UNCOUNTABLE NOUNS (MEANING SHIFTS)
================================================================================
+---------+-----------------------------------+--------------------------------+
| NOUN    | UNCOUNTABLE SENSE (Mass Material) | COUNTABLE SENSE (Discrete Item)|
+---------+-----------------------------------+--------------------------------+
| Paper   | "We need more paper for printer." | "I read two morning papers."   |
| Glass   | "The window is made of glass."    | "He drank a glass of water."   |
| Hair    | "She has beautiful black hair."   | "There are two hairs on coat." |
| Iron    | "Iron is an industrial metal."    | "I bought an electric iron."   |
| Light   | "Sunlight gives us light."        | "Turn off all the lights."     |
| Room    | "Is there room in the car?"(space)| "The house has five rooms."    |
+---------+-----------------------------------+--------------------------------+

4. BENGALI PEDAGOGICAL SUMMARY (বাংলা সংক্ষেপ)
================================================================================
১. Countable Noun: যা আলাদাভাবে গোনা যায় (Book, Student, Apple)। এর একবচনে 'a/an' বসে এবং বহুবচনে 's/es' যুক্ত হয়।
২. Uncountable Noun: যা আলাদাভাবে গোনা যায় না (Water, Information, Furniture)।
   - এর সাথে কখনোই 's' বা 'es' যুক্ত করবেন না (যেমন: Informations, Furnitures ভুল)।
   - এর আগে সরাসরি 'a/an' বসে না ('an advice' ভুল -> 'a piece of advice' শুদ্ধ)।
   - এটি সর্বদা Singular Verb গ্রহণ করে ('The news IS true')।
