1. WHAT IS A COLLECTIVE NOUN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Collective Noun is the name given to a group of persons, animals, or things spoken of as a single combined whole.
Examples: Jury, Committee, Team, Army, Fleet, Crowd, Mob, Council, Board, Family, Audience, Parliament, Government, Class, Crew, Flock, Herd.

2. THE DUAL CONCORD RULE (UNITARY WHOLE VS NOUN OF MULTITUDE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The most crucial grammatical mechanic governing collective nouns is that their grammatical number (Singular vs Plural) depends strictly upon the INTENDED SENSE of unity or division in the context:

RULE A: UNITARY WHOLE (COLLECTIVE NOUN STRICTO SENSU)
• When the collection of individuals acts unanimously, collectively, or as one single body:
  - It governs a SINGULAR VERB.
  - It takes a SINGULAR PRONOUN ('it', 'its', 'which').
• Examples:
  1. The jury HAS reached its unanimous verdict.
  2. The committee HAS decided to adopt the new policy.
  3. The team IS celebrating its victory on the field.
  4. The family LIVES in a quiet suburban cottage.

RULE B: NOUN OF MULTITUDE (INDIVIDUALS ACTING SEPARATELY)
• When the individual members composing the collection are thought of distinctly, are divided in opinion, or are acting independently on their own:
  - It governs a PLURAL VERB.
  - It takes a PLURAL PRONOUN ('they', 'them', 'their', 'who').
• Examples:
  1. The jury WERE divided in their opinions.
  2. The committee ARE quarrelling among themselves over funding.
  3. The crew WERE taking their respective positions across the deck.
  4. The orchestra WERE tuning their individual instruments before the show.

3. PRONOUN-VERB HARMONY TRAP (THE CARDINAL RULE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEVER MIX SINGULAR VERBS WITH PLURAL PRONOUNS, OR VICE VERSA!

❌ INCORRECT (Mixed Discord): 
   "The committee has decided to submit their report."
   (Violation: Singular 'has' mixed with plural 'their').

✔ CORRECT (Pure Singular Harmony):
   "The committee has decided to submit its report."

❌ INCORRECT (Mixed Discord):
   "The team are taking its seats."
   (Violation: Plural 'are' mixed with singular 'its').

✔ CORRECT (Pure Plural Harmony):
   "The team are taking their seats."

4. CATALOG OF HIGH-FREQUENCY COLLECTIVE NOUN EXAMPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Noun         Unitary Singular (Acts as 1)                Noun of Multitude (Members separate)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Jury         The jury has delivered its verdict.         The jury were divided in their votes.
Committee    The committee has approved the budget.      The committee are arguing among themselves.
Crew         The rescue crew is well-trained.            The crew were leaping into their lifeboats.
Audience     The audience was silent in anticipation.    The audience were clapping their hands.
Family       The family owns a heritage home.            My family have different political views.
Government   The government has passed the bill.         The government are divided on tax reforms.
Board        The board of directors is convening today.  The board have cast their private ballots.

5. BILINGUAL COMPARATIVE NOTES (ENGLISH ↔ বাংলা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• বাংলা ভাষায় সমষ্টিবাচক বিশেষ্যের সাথে আমরা সাধারণত একবচনের ক্রিয়া প্রয়োগ করি (যেমন: "কমিটি সিদ্ধান্ত নিয়েছে", "জুরি রায় দিয়েছে")।
• কিন্তু ইংরেজিতে যখন কমিটির সদস্যরা পরস্পরের সাথে ঝগড়া করে বা ভিন্ন ভিন্ন মতামত দেয়, তখন ইংরেজি ব্যাকরণে তাকে 'Noun of Multitude' বলে এবং সেখানে বহুবচনের ক্রিয়া (Plural Verb) ও Pronoun ('their') বসানো বাধ্যতামূলক।
• উদাহরণ:
  - ঐক্যবদ্ধ সিদ্ধান্ত: The jury has given its verdict. (জুরি সর্বসম্মত রায় দিয়েছে)
  - মতবিরোধ: The jury were divided in their opinions. (জুরি সদস্যদের মধ্যে মতভেদ দেখা দিয়েছিল)
