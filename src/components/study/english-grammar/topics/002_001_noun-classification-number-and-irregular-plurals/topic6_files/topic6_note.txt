1. HISTORICAL VOWEL MUTATION PLURALS (ABLAUT / UMLAUT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Old English, a select group of core Germanic nouns formed their plurals not by suffixation, but by changing the root vowel:

• Man → Men
• Woman → Women (Pronounced: /ˈwɪm.ɪn/)
• Foot → Feet
• Tooth → Teeth
• Goose → Geese
• Mouse → Mice
• Louse → Lice

Note on Compound Nouns with '-man':
- Postman → Postmen
- Fisherman → Fishermen
- Statesman → Statesmen
- EXCEPTION (Not a compound of 'man', but national proper noun):
  - German → Germans (NOT *Germen)
  - Norman → Normans
  - Mussulman → Mussulmans
  - Brahman → Brahmans

2. GERMANIC '-en' SUFFIX PLURALS (OLD ENGLISH SURVIVALS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Ox → Oxen (NOT *oxes)
• Child → Children (Double plural: root vowel shift + '-r-' + '-en')
• Brother → 
  - Brothers (Sons of the same father/mother: biological siblings)
  - Brethren (Members of a religious society, guild, or philosophical community)

3. INVARIABLE ZERO-PLURALS (SAME FORM IN SINGULAR & PLURAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Certain nouns retain the EXACT same spelling and pronunciation whether singular or plural:

A. ANIMALS & FISH:
• Sheep → Sheep ("One sheep was lost", "Fifty sheep were grazing")
• Deer → Deer ("A majestic deer", "A herd of five deer")
• Swine → Swine
• Salmon → Salmon
• Trout → Trout
• Cod → Cod

B. TECHNOLOGICAL & MODERN NOUNS:
• Aircraft → Aircraft (NEVER *aircrafts)
• Spacecraft → Spacecraft
• Hovercraft → Hovercraft

C. LATIN INVARIABLE SPECIES / SERIES:
• Species → Species ("A rare species", "Multiple endangered species")
• Series → Series ("A TV series", "Three test series")
• Corps → Corps (Pronounced: singular /kɔː/, plural /kɔːz/)

4. SPECIAL DUAL NOUNS: FISH VS FISHES / CLOTH VS CLOTHES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FISH vs FISHES:
   - FISH: Used when referring to multiple fish considered as a collective quantity or of the same species ("He caught six fish").
   - FISHES: Used specifically in zoology/biology to refer to multiple DIFFERENT SPECIES of fish ("The diverse fishes of the Bay of Bengal").

2. CLOTH vs CLOTHS vs CLOTHES:
   - Cloth: The fabric/material (Uncountable).
   - Cloths: Pieces of fabric/rags used for wiping (Countable: "cleaning cloths").
   - Clothes: Garments worn on the human body (Pluralia Tantum: "stylish clothes").

5. BILINGUAL LEARNING BRIDGES (ENGLISH ↔ বাংলা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• German শব্দের সাথে 'man' থাকলেও এটি কোনো যৌগিক শব্দ নয়, তাই এর বহুবচন Germans (Germen নয়)।
• Sheep ও Deer-এর সাথে কখনোই 's' যুক্ত করবেন না। একবচনে "a sheep is", বহুবচনে "many sheep are"।
• Children শব্দের ভেতরে 's' নেই, কিন্তু এটি নিজেই বহুবচন, তাই কখনো *childrens বলবেন না।
• Brothers হলো আপন ভাইদের জন্য, আর Brethren হলো সমমনা সহকর্মী বা ধর্মীয় ভ্রাতৃমণ্ডলীর জন্য।
