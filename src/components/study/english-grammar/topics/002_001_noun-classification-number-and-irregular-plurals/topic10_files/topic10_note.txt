1. WHAT IS A COMPOUND NOUN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Compound Noun is a nominal unit formed by joining two or more words (hyphenated, spaced, or fused as a single solid word).
Examples: Son-in-law, Passer-by, Commander-in-chief, Step-son, Spoonful, Man-servant.

2. THE 4 CARDINAL RULES OF COMPOUND PLURALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RULE 1: THE PRINCIPAL HEAD NOUN RULE (MOST CRUCIAL)
• In most compound nouns, attach the plural marker '-s' strictly to the PRINCIPAL NOUN HEAD (the primary semantic anchor), NOT to the modifying preposition, adverb, or qualifying phrase:

  Singular                  Plural                        Head Noun
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Son-in-law                Sons-in-law                   Son
  Daughter-in-law           Daughters-in-law              Daughter
  Brother-in-law            Brothers-in-law               Brother
  Father-in-law             Fathers-in-law                Father
  Commander-in-chief        Commanders-in-chief           Commander
  Passer-by                 Passers-by                    Passer (NOT *passer-bies)
  Looker-on                 Lookers-on                    Looker
  Hanger-on                 Hangers-on                    Hanger
  Runner-up                 Runners-up                    Runner
  Coat-of-mail              Coats-of-mail                 Coat
  Governor-general          Governors-general             Governor
  Court-martial             Courts-martial                Court
  Knight-errant             Knights-errant                Knight

RULE 2: THE SECOND-ELEMENT HEAD NOUN RULE
• When the first element is an adjective, qualifying prefix, or subordinate noun, and the second element is the true grammatical head, pluralize the SECOND element:

  Singular                  Plural                        Head Noun
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Maid-servant              Maid-servants                 Servant
  Step-son                  Step-sons                     Son
  Step-daughter             Step-daughters                Daughter
  Book-case                 Book-cases                    Case
  Arm-chair                 Arm-chairs                    Chair
  Boy-friend                Boy-friends                   Friend
  Class-fellow              Class-fellows                 Fellow
  Postman                   Postmen                       Man

RULE 3: THE DOUBLE-PLURAL COMPOUND RULE
• When the compound consists of 'Man' or 'Woman' + a professional role / noun, BOTH elements mutate into the plural:

  Singular                  Plural
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Man-servant               Men-servants
  Woman-servant             Women-servants
  Man-doctor                Men-doctors
  Woman-doctor              Women-doctors
  Lord-justice              Lords-justices
  Knight-templar            Knights-templars

RULE 4: NOUNS ENDING IN '-ful' & IDIOMATIC PHRASE COMPOUNDS
• Nouns ending in '-ful' denote single units of volumetric capacity; append '-s' to the end:
  - Spoonful → Spoonfuls (NEVER *spoonsful)
  - Handful → Handfuls
  - Mouthful → Mouthfuls
  - Pocketful → Pocketfuls
  - Armful → Armfuls

• Idiomatic sentence-like compounds with no primary noun head add '-s' to the final element:
  - Merry-go-round → Merry-go-rounds
  - Forget-me-not (flower) → Forget-me-nots
  - Touch-me-not (plant) → Touch-me-nots
  - Castaway → Castaways
  - Good-for-nothing → Good-for-nothings

3. BILINGUAL COMPARATIVE NOTES (ENGLISH ↔ বাংলা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• বাংলা ভাষায় "জামাইরা" বা "সেনাপতিরা" বলি, কিন্তু ইংরেজিতে Son-in-law বা Commander-in-chief-এর মতো শব্দে '-s' শেষ শব্দে বসে না; মূল Noun Head-এর সাথে বসে (Sons-in-law, Commanders-in-chief)।
• মনে রাখবেন:
  1. *Son-in-laws লেখা মারাত্মক ব্যাকরণগত ভুল।
  2. Passer-by এর বহুবচন Passers-by (Passer-bies নয়)।
  3. Man-servant এর বহুবচনে দুটো অংশই পরিবর্তিত হয়ে Men-servants হয়।
  4. Spoonful এর বহুবচন Spoonfuls (Spoonsful নয়)।
