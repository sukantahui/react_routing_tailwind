1. THE 6 DEADLY STYLISTIC FLAWS OF ADVANCED WRITING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FLAW 1: NOMINALIZATION (THE "ZOMBIE NOUN" EPIDEMIC)
---------------------------------------------------
Nominalization (ক্রিয়াবাচক বিশেষ্য) occurs when a vigorous active verb is
murdered and morphed into a bloated abstract noun ending in -tion, -ment, -ance,
-ity, -ence.

Bloated Zombie Construction          Lean Active Verb Solution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Conduct an investigation into  ->  Investigate
• Reach an agreement regarding   ->  Agree on
• Make an assessment of          ->  Assess
• Effect a reduction in          ->  Reduce
• Take into consideration        ->  Consider
• Give an explanation of         ->  Explain
• Bring about an improvement in  ->  Improve
• Provide assistance to          ->  Assist / Help
• Perform an analysis of         ->  Analyze

FLAW 2: AMBIGUOUS & ORPHANED PRONOUN REFERENCES
------------------------------------------------
- Ambiguous Pronoun: "When Amit met Rahul, he looked worried."
  * Problem: Who is 'he'? Amit or Rahul?
  * Fix: "Amit looked worried when he met Rahul." OR "When Amit met Rahul, Rahul looked worried."
- Orphaned 'This' / 'That': "The inflation rose by 8%. This caused widespread protests."
  * Fix: Provide the noun summary tag: "This sharp economic spike caused widespread protests."

FLAW 3: PREPOSITIONAL PILE-UPS (PREPOSITIONAL CLUTTER)
------------------------------------------------------
Stringing 4+ prepositional phrases suffocates the sentence:
- [Bloated]: "The determination OF the policy FOR the regulation OF the supply OF water in the state..."
- [Lean]: "The state water regulation policy determines..."

FLAW 4: EVASIVE PASSIVE VOICE VS. LEGITIMATE PASSIVE
----------------------------------------------------
- Evasive / Bureaucratic Passive (Hides accountability):
  * "Mistakes were made." (By whom?) -> "We made mistakes."
  * "A decision was taken to terminate the project." -> "The board terminated the project."
- Legitimate Scientific / Historical Passive (Focus is rightly on the object):
  * "The COVID-19 vaccine was developed in record time." (Appropriate!)
  * "The ancient scroll was preserved under nitrogen." (Appropriate!)

FLAW 5: EXPLETIVE OPENERS & THROAT-CLEARING
--------------------------------------------
Empty dummy subjects ('There is', 'There are', 'It is important to remember that') delay the real meaning.
- [Bloated]: "There are many engineers who believe that AI will transform coding." (12 words)
- [Lean]: "Many engineers believe AI will transform coding." (7 words - 42% leaner!)
- [Throat-Clearing]: "It goes without saying that reading improves vocabulary." -> "Reading improves vocabulary."

FLAW 6: PARALYZING OVER-HEDGING
-------------------------------
Excessive caution undermines writer credibility:
- [Over-Hedged]: "It seems possible that we might perhaps consider potentially revising the draft."
- [Confident Balance]: "We should consider revising the draft." / "Preliminary evidence suggests revising the draft."

2. RICHARD LANHAM'S PARAMEDIC METHOD (6-STEP PROSE RESUSCITATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Circle the prepositions (of, in, about, for, on, at, to). Prune unneeded chains.
2. Circle the 'is/are/was/were' forms of 'to be'.
3. Find the real action: What is actually happening?
4. Make the real action into a dynamic, active verb.
5. Make the true doer (agent) the subject of the sentence.
6. Eliminate all expletive openers ("There are", "It is").

4. DEFENSIVE WRITING IN LEGAL, CORPORATE & ACADEMIC DRAFTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Defensive writing constructs unambiguous, legally resilient sentences that prevent
misinterpretation, scope creep, and dispute.

A. Modals of Obligation:
   - 'SHALL' = Mandatory legal obligation ("The vendor shall deliver the goods...").
   - 'WILL'  = Statement of future fact or intention ("The company will announce results tomorrow").
   - 'MAY'   = Discretionary permission ("The client may request an extension...").
   - 'MUST'  = Absolute imperative constraint ("The password must contain 8 characters").

B. Airtight Scope Specification Formula:
   [Agent] + [Shall/Must] + [V1 Action] + [Exact Deliverable/Parameter] + [Condition Trigger] + [Strict Deadline].
   - Example: "The contractor shall deliver the audited financial statement (v2.1) within five (5) business days following written notice from the auditor."

5. BENGALI EXPLANATORY SUMMARY (বাংলা ব্যাকরণিক ও রচনাশৈলীর বিশ্লেষণ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- বাংলা ভাষায় আমরা অনেক সময় ভাবগম্ভীর বোঝাতে ভারী ক্রিয়াবাচক বিশেষ্য ব্যবহার করি (যেমন: "তদন্ত পরিচালনা করা", "সিদ্ধান্তে উপনীত হওয়া")। কিন্তু ইংরেজি ভাষায় Zombie Nouns বা Nominalization রচনাশৈলীকে দুর্বল, ক্লান্তিকর ও ধীরগতির করে তোলে।
- ইংরেজিতে সরাসরি Active Verb ব্যবহার করাই সবচেয়ে আধুনিক ও শক্তিশালী লেখার কৌশল (যেমন: "conduct an investigation" না লিখে "investigate", "make a decision" না লিখে "decide")।
- অস্পষ্ট সর্বনাম (Ambiguous Pronoun) পরিহার করুন: বাক্যে একাধিক ব্যক্তি বা বস্তু থাকলে 'he', 'she', 'it' বা 'this' কার জন্য ব্যবহৃত হচ্ছে তা সরাসরি স্পষ্ট করুন।
- প্রিপজিশনাল বোঝা (Prepositional Clutter) ছেঁটে ফেলুন: "In the event of the occurrence of rain" না লিখে সংক্ষেপে "If it rains" লিখুন।
- আইন ও প্রাতিষ্ঠানিক রচনার ক্ষেত্রে Defensive Writing-এর নিয়ম মেনে চলা জরুরি, যাতে কোনো ফাঁকফোকর না থাকে।
