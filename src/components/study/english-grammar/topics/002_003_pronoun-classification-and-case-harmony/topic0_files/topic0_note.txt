1. THE NINE PRONOUN FAMILIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Personal Pronouns:     I, we, you, he, she, it, they (Subject/Object/Possessive).
2. Demonstrative Pron.:   This, that, these, those (Pointing to entities).
3. Relative Pronouns:     Who, whom, whose, which, that, what (Linking clauses).
4. Interrogative Pron.:   Who? Whom? Whose? Which? What? (Asking questions).
5. Reflexive Pronouns:    Myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves.
6. Emphatic Pronouns:     Added for dramatic emphasis ("I myself solved it").
7. Indefinite Pronouns:   Everyone, somebody, nobody, anyone, all, some, none.
8. Distributive Pron.:    Each, either, neither (Referring to one at a time).
9. Reciprocal Pronouns:   Each other (two), One another (more than two).

2. PERSONAL PRONOUN CASES & TRAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+--------------------+----------------------+----------------------------------+
| Pronoun Case       | Role in Sentence     | Critical Exam Invariants         |
+--------------------+----------------------+----------------------------------+
| Subjective (Nom.)  | Subject of verb      | "YOU, HE, AND I will attend"     |
| Objective (Acc.)   | Object of verb/prep. | "Between YOU AND ME" (NOT 'and I')|
|                    |                      | "Let HIM AND ME go"              |
| Possessive Det.    | Modifies noun        | "My, your, his, her, our, their" |
| Possessive Pronoun | Replaces noun phrase | "Mine, yours, his, hers, theirs" |
+--------------------+----------------------+----------------------------------+

3. THE 231 POLITENESS RULE VS THE 123 CONFESSION RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 231 RULE (Positive / Neutral Contexts):
  - Sequence: 2nd Person (You) -> 3rd Person (He/She/They) -> 1st Person (I)
  - Formula: "You, he, and I must collaborate on the project."

* 123 RULE (Admitting Mistakes, Guilt, Blame, or Plural Pronouns):
  - Sequence: 1st Person (I) -> 2nd Person (You) -> 3rd Person (He/She/They)
  - Formula: "I, you, and he are responsible for this calculation flaw."
  - Plural Sequence: "We, you, and they will participate."

4. RELATIVE PRONOUN INVARIANTS ('WHO' VS 'WHOM' VS 'THAT')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* WHO vs WHOM:
  - WHO  = Subject of the relative clause (Substitute 'He': 'Who wrote it? -> He wrote it').
  - WHOM = Object of the relative clause (Substitute 'Him': 'Whom did you see? -> Saw him').

* MANDATORY 'THAT' OVER 'WHICH'/'WHO':
  - After Superlatives: "He is the best scholar THAT I have known."
  - After Special Words: All, Same, Only, None, Any, Much, Little, Everything.
  - Formula: "All THAT glitters is not gold." | "This is the ONLY pen THAT works."

5. DEMONSTRATIVE COMPARISON INVARIANTS ('THAT OF' VS 'THOSE OF')
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Singular Comparison: "The climate of Barrackpore is like THAT OF Kolkata."
* Plural Comparison:   "The streets of Kolkata are wider than THOSE OF Naihati."
