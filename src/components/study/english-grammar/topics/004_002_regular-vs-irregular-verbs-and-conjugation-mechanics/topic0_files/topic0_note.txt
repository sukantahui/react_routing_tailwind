1. WEAK (REGULAR) VS STRONG (IRREGULAR) VERBAL MORPHOLOGY
========================================================================
A. WEAK / REGULAR VERBS:
   - Form their Past Tense (V2) and Past Participle (V3) by appending the dental suffix '-ed', '-d', or '-t' to the base form without internal vowel mutation.
   - Examples: Walk -> Walked -> Walked, Decide -> Decided -> Decided, Burn -> Burnt -> Burnt.
   - PHONETIC PRONUNCIATION OF '-ed':
     1. Voiceless Endings (/p/, /k/, /s/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/, /f/) -> Pronounced as /t/ (e.g. stopped /stɒpt/, laughed /lɑːft/, washed /wɒʃt/).
     2. Voiced Endings (vowels, /b/, /g/, /v/, /z/, /m/, /n/, /l/, /r/) -> Pronounced as /d/ (e.g. played /pleɪd/, robbed /rɒbd/, cleaned /kliːnd/).
     3. Dental Stops (/t/, /d/) -> Pronounced as separate syllable /ɪd/ (e.g. wanted /ˈwɒntɪd/, decided /dɪˈsaɪdɪd/, mended /ˈmendɪd/).

B. STRONG / IRREGULAR VERBS:
   - Form their Past (V2) and Past Participle (V3) through internal vowel mutation (Indo-European Ablaut) without dental suffixes.
   - Examples: Sing -> Sang -> Sung, Drive -> Drove -> Driven, Break -> Broke -> Broken.

2. THE MASTER 5 PRINCIPAL VERB FORMS (V1 TO V5)
========================================================================
Every English verb functions across 5 principal forms:
- V1: Base / Infinitive Form (go, write, see)
- V2: Simple Past Form (went, wrote, saw)
- V3: Past Participle Form (gone, written, seen) [Used with have/has/had and passives]
- V4: Present Participle / Gerund Form (going, writing, seeing) [V1 + ing]
- V5: 3rd Person Singular Simple Present Form (goes, writes, sees) [V1 + s/es]

5. VERBAL PARTICIPLES VS ATTRIBUTIVE ADJECTIVE VARIANTS
========================================================================
Certain strong verbs possess divergent forms when acting as verbal participles (after have/be) versus attributive adjectives (before nouns):
- Verbal Participle (V3): drunk ("He has drunk the milk.")
  Attributive Adjective: drunken ("A drunken brawl erupted.")
- Verbal Participle (V3): struck ("The ship was struck by lightning.")
  Attributive Adjective: stricken ("A poverty-stricken neighborhood.")
- Verbal Participle (V3): sunk ("The submarine has sunk.")
  Attributive Adjective: sunken ("Sunken treasure was recovered.")
- Verbal Participle (V3): melted ("The butter has melted.")
  Attributive Adjective: molten ("Molten lava poured down the mountain.")
- Verbal Participle (V3): shaved ("He has shaved his beard.")
  Attributive Adjective: shaven ("A clean-shaven young officer.")

6. BENGALI PEDAGOGICAL SUMMARY & BRIDGES (বাংলা সারসংক্ষেপ):
========================================================================
১. Transitivity Test (সকর্মক বনাম অকর্মক ক্রিয়ার পরীক্ষা):
   - যখন কর্তা নিজে অবস্থান পরিবর্তন করে বা বিশ্রাম নেয় (No Object), তখন Intransitive Verb বসে: Lie (lay, lain), Rise (rose, risen), Fall (fell, fallen)।
   - যখন কর্তা কোনো বাহ্যিক বস্তুর ওপর ক্রিয়া সম্পাদন করে (Object উপস্থিত), তখন Transitive Verb বসে: Lay (laid, laid), Raise (raised, raised), Fell (felled, felled)।

২. মারাত্মক ভুলগুলো এড়িয়ে চলুন:
   - "He laid in bed all morning" [ভুল] -> "He lay in bed all morning" [সঠিক]।
   - "The prisoner was hung" [ভুল] -> "The prisoner was hanged" [সঠিক - ফাঁসি দেওয়া]।
   - "The river has overflown" [ভুল] -> "The river has overflowed" [সঠিক - flow থেকে আসে]।
   - "He has casted his vote" [ভুল] -> "He has cast his vote" [সঠিক - cast invariable]।
