1. CORE TAXONOMY & DEFINITIONS (মূল সংজ্ঞা ও শ্রেণিবিভাগ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SIMPLE SUBJECT (একক মূল কর্তা / Head Noun):
   • The core noun or pronoun stripped of all modifiers that governs the verb.
   • Example: "The rapid [growth] of web applications fascinates everyone."
     -> Head noun = 'growth' (Singular) -> Verb = 'fascinates' (Singular).

2. COMPOUND SUBJECT (যৌথ উদ্দেশ্য / সমন্বিত কর্তা):
   • Two or more subjects joined by coordinating/correlative conjunctions.
   • Example: "Swadeep and Debangshu [Compound Subject] coded the backend."

2. THE 6 INVARIANT CONCORD LAWS FOR COMPOUND SUBJECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LAW 1: THE STANDARD 'AND' ADDITION (1 + 1 = 2 -> Plural)
       • "Swadeep and Tuhina ARE presenting today."

LAW 2: THE SINGLE UNIT / COMPOSITE EXCEPTION (Singular)
       • "Bread and butter IS a wholesome breakfast."
       • "Slow and steady WINS the race."
       • "Time and tide WAITS for no man."

LAW 3: THE SINGLE ARTICLE RULE (Same Person -> Singular)
       • "The novelist and poet HAS arrived." (1 person -> 1 article -> Singular)
       • "The novelist and THE poet HAVE arrived." (2 people -> 2 articles -> Plural)

LAW 4: THE RULE OF PROXIMITY (Either... or / Neither... nor)
       • Verb agrees with the NEAREST subject:
       • "Neither Swadeep nor his friends ARE coming."
       • "Neither his friends nor Swadeep IS coming."

LAW 5: THE INTERVENING PHRASE INVARIANT (As well as, Along with, Together with)
       • Verb agrees strictly with the FIRST subject:
       • "The mentor, along with his students, IS in the laboratory."

LAW 6: THE DISTRIBUTIVE 'EACH / EVERY' RULE (Always Singular)
       • "Every boy and every girl WAS given a certificate."

3. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & COMMON EXAM TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] TRAP 1: The False Proximity Error (Prepositional Object Trap)
    - "The quality of these apples are good" [WRONG!].
    - "The quality [S] of these apples [Prep Obj] IS good" [CORRECT!].

[X] TRAP 2: 'One of + Plural Noun'
    - "One of my friends are coming" [WRONG!].
    - "One [S] of my friends [Prep Obj] IS coming" [CORRECT!].

[X] TRAP 3: 'As well as' treated like 'And'
    - "Debangshu as well as his classmates are present" [WRONG!].
    - "Debangshu [S1] as well as his classmates IS present" [CORRECT!].
