1. SIMPLE PREDICATE (THE FINITE VERB GROUP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: Strictly the lexical verb plus all its modal and auxiliary 
  operators (without objects, complements, or modifiers).
- Examples:
  * "The researcher [arrived]." -> Simple Predicate: arrived.
  * "The researcher [has been working]." -> Simple Predicate: has been working.
  * "The researcher [could have been analyzing]." -> Simple Predicate: could have been analyzing.

2. COMPLETE PREDICATE (THE FULL ASSERTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: The finite verb group + Direct Object + Indirect Object + 
  Subject/Object Complement + Prepositional & Adverbial Modifiers.
- Example:
  "The researcher [has been analyzing the dataset meticulously in the laboratory]."
  * Complete Predicate = "has been analyzing the dataset meticulously in the laboratory"

3. COMPOUND PREDICATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: Two or more verbs sharing the EXACT SAME subject without repeating 
  the subject pronoun.
- Example: "Swadeep [designed the algorithm, wrote the code, and tested the output]."
  (Single Subject 'Swadeep' -> Three compound verbs).

4. BENGALI LEARNER PEDAGOGICAL NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* বাংলায় ক্রিয়াপদ সাধারণত বাক্যের শেষে বসে (যেমন: "তিনি কাজটি করেছেন")। 
  ইংরেজিতে Finite Verb Subject-এর ঠিক পরেই বসে (যেমন: "He has done the work")।
* বাক্যে Finite Verb শনাক্ত করার পরই Subject ও Predicate-এর বিভাজন সম্পূর্ণ হয়।
