1. DIRECT OBJECT (DO - THE ACCUSATIVE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Question Test: Verb + WHAT? / Verb + WHOM?
- Definition: The noun or pronoun that directly receives the action of the verb.
- Example: "Swadeep developed [a web application]." (Developed WHAT? -> an app).

2. INDIRECT OBJECT (IO - THE DATIVE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Question Test: Verb + TO WHOM? / FOR WHOM?
- Definition: The person or beneficiary who receives the Direct Object entity.
- Example: "Sukanta Sir taught [Tuhina] [English Grammar]."
  * Tuhina = Indirect Object (Person / Beneficiary).
  * English Grammar = Direct Object (Entity transferred).

3. DATIVE SHIFT FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Formula A (Pure SVOO):
  Subject + Verb + [Indirect Object] + [Direct Object]
  "I gave [him] [the key]." (No preposition needed!)

Formula B (SVO + Prepositional Phrase):
  Subject + Verb + [Direct Object] + TO/FOR + [Object]
  "I gave [the key] to [him]."

4. CRITICAL BENGALI EXAM TRAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Bengali Order: "আমি তাকে বইটি দিলাম" (IO + DO).
* English Error: Inserting 'to' before the person in SVOO:
  - "I gave to him the book" ❌ (Incorrect!)
  - "I gave him the book" ✔️ (Pure SVOO)
  - "I gave the book to him" ✔️ (SVO + Prep Phrase)
