1. CORE TAXONOMY & DIAGNOSTIC TESTS (কর্মের শ্রেণিবিভাগ ও চেনার উপায়)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DIRECT OBJECT (মুখ্য কর্ম - DO):
   • Definition: The noun/pronoun that directly receives the action of a transitive verb.
   • Diagnostic Question: Ask "VERB + WHAT? / WHOM?"
   • Example: "Swadeep wrote [What?] a compiler [Direct Object]."

2. INDIRECT OBJECT (গৌণ কর্ম - IO):
   • Definition: The beneficiary or recipient of the direct object.
   • Diagnostic Question: Ask "VERB + TO WHOM? / FOR WHOM?"
   • Example: "Swadeep gave [To whom?] Debangshu [IO] the documentation [DO]."
   • Invariant Rule: An Indirect Object CANNOT exist without a Direct Object.

2. THE TWO DITRANSITIVE SYNTACTIC PATTERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PATTERN A: S + V + IO + DO (No Preposition)
  • "Mentor Sukanta Sir taught [Swadeep: IO] [advanced grammar: DO]."
  • "Debangshu bought [Tuhina: IO] [a graphics tablet: DO]."

PATTERN B: S + V + DO + Preposition + Recipient (Prepositional Complement)
  • "Mentor Sukanta Sir taught [advanced grammar: DO] TO [Swadeep]."
  • "Debangshu bought [a graphics tablet: DO] FOR [Tuhina]."

PREPOSITION SELECTION RULES:
  • Use "TO" with Verbs of Transfer & Communication:
    (give, send, lend, tell, show, hand, pay, write, offer, pass, promise)
  • Use "FOR" with Verbs of Creation & Procurement:
    (buy, bake, cook, build, make, find, choose, order, prepare)

3. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] TRAP 1: Confusing Direct Object with Subject Complement
    • "Swadeep is a programmer" -> 'programmer' == Swadeep (Subject Complement after Linking Verb).
    • "Swadeep met a programmer" -> 'programmer' != Swadeep (Direct Object after Transitive Verb).

[X] TRAP 2: Using prepositions inside the SVOO pattern
    • "He gave to me a book" [WRONG!].
    • "He gave ME a book" [CORRECT: S + V + IO + DO] OR "He gave a book TO ME" [CORRECT].

[X] TRAP 3: Pronoun Case Error
    • "She gave he a book" [WRONG: Subjective case].
    • "She gave HIM a book" [CORRECT: Objective case].
