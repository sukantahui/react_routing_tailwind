1. LINKING (COPULAR) VERBS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: Verbs that do NOT express action, but connect the Subject to a 
  word/phrase that renames or describes it.
- Key Linking Verbs:
  * Pure State: be (is/am/are/was/were), seem, appear, remain.
  * Inchoative (Change of State): become, turn, grow, get.
  * Sensory Perception: look, sound, taste, smell, feel.

2. THE TWO FORMS OF SUBJECT COMPLEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Predicate Noun (Predicate Nominative):
   - Renames or classifies the subject ($Subject = Noun$).
   - Example: "Swadeep is [a software architect]." (Swadeep = architect).
B. Predicate Adjective:
   - Describes a quality or state of the subject ($Subject = Adjective$).
   - Example: "The mangoes taste [sweet]." (Mangoes = sweet).

3. THE MASTER EQUALITY TEST (DO VS SC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+--------------------+-------------------------+-------------------------+
| Criterion          | Direct Object (SVO)     | Subject Complement (SVC)|
+--------------------+-------------------------+-------------------------+
| Mathematical Proof | Subject != Object       | Subject == Complement   |
| Action Transfer    | Yes (Kinetic Action)    | No (Static State)       |
| Example            | "He kicked the ball."   | "He is a doctor."       |
| Passive Voice      | Possible ("The ball...")| IMPOSSIBLE              |
+--------------------+-------------------------+-------------------------+

4. CRITICAL SENSORY VERB ERROR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Wrong: "The flower smells sweetly." ❌
* Right: "The flower smells sweet." ✔️ (Subject Complement Adjective!)
