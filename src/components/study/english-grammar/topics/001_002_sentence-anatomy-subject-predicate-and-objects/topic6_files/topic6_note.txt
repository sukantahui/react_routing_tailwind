1. CORE CONCEPTS & THE MATHEMATICAL EQUALS SIGN (লিংকিং ভার্ব ও কমপ্লিমেন্ট)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Linking (Copular) verbs DO NOT express dynamic physical actions.
They serve as an EQUALS SIGN (=) connecting the Subject to its Complement:

[SUBJECT]  <===== LINKING VERB =====>  [SUBJECT COMPLEMENT]

A. PREDICATE NOUN / PRONOUN (Predicate Nominative):
   • Renames, classifies, or defines the subject.
   • Example: "Swadeep [S] IS [LV] a software engineer [Predicate Noun]."
     (Swadeep = Software Engineer).
   • Formal Pronoun Rule: "It is I." / "It was she who called." (Subjective case).

B. PREDICATE ADJECTIVE:
   • Ascribes a state, quality, or condition to the subject.
   • Example: "The algorithm [S] IS [LV] optimal [Predicate Adjective]."
     (Algorithm = Optimal).

2. MASTER CATALOG OF LINKING VERBS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. VERB 'BE': is, am, are, was, were, be, being, been.
2. VERBS OF PERCEPTION & SENSE: smell, taste, sound, feel, look, appear, seem.
   • "The rose smells sweet." (NOT *"sweetly"*!)
   • "The music sounded divine."
3. VERBS OF STATE & CONTINUATION: remain, stay, keep.
   • "The students remained silent." (NOT *"silently"*!)
4. VERBS OF CHANGE & RESULT: become, grow, turn, prove, go, come.
   • "The milk went sour." (went = became)
   • "His dream came true."

3. THE 'BE' SUBSTITUTION DIAGNOSTIC TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To test if a verb is a Linking Verb, substitute it with 'is/are/was':
  • "The soup tastes delicious"  -> "The soup IS delicious" (Logical -> Linking Verb!).
  • "The chef tasted the soup"   -> *"The chef IS the soup"* (Illogical -> Action Verb!).

4. BENGALI LEARNER BRIDGES & TRAPS (বাংলা মাধ্যমের সতর্কতা)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[X] TRAP 1: Using Adverbs of Manner after Sensory Linking Verbs
    - "The flower smells sweetly" [WRONG!].
    - "The flower smells sweet" [CORRECT: Adjective Subject Complement].

[X] TRAP 2: Using Adverbs after 'Remain' or 'Stay'
    - "He remained silently" [WRONG!].
    - "He remained silent" [CORRECT: Adjective].

[X] TRAP 3: Objective case after 'Be' in competitive exams
    - "It was him who did it" [Colloquial / Informal].
    - "It was HE who did it" [Strict Standard Formal English].

5. ESSENTIAL REVISION CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[✓] Master the formula: Subject = Subject Complement across a Linking Verb.
[✓] Apply the 'BE' substitution test to distinguish linking verbs from action verbs.
[✓] Never use an '-ly' adverb of manner as a subject complement.
[✓] Differentiate SVO (Subject != Object) from SVC (Subject == Complement).
