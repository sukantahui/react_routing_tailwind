1. DATIVE ALTERNATION ARCHITECTURE (দ্বিকর্মক রূপান্তর কাঠামো)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ditransitive verbs allow two interchangeable surface syntax structures:

STRUCTURE 1: S + V + IO (Recipient) + DO (Entity)
  • "Swadeep gave [Debangshu: IO] [a book: DO]."
  • "Tuhina bought [Abhronila: IO] [a laptop: DO]."

STRUCTURE 2: S + V + DO (Entity) + PREPOSITION + Recipient
  • "Swadeep gave [a book: DO] TO [Debangshu]."
  • "Tuhina bought [a laptop: DO] FOR [Abhronila]."

2. PREPOSITION GOVERNANCE: 'TO' vs 'FOR'
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. USE "TO" WITH VERBS OF TRANSFER & COMMUNICATION:
   • Verbs: give, hand, lend, offer, pass, pay, promise, read, sell, send, show, teach, tell, write.
   • Example: "He lent his notes TO Swadeep."

B. USE "FOR" WITH VERBS OF CREATION, PROCUREMENT & SERVICE:
   • Verbs: bake, build, buy, carve, choose, cook, fetch, find, get, knit, make, order, prepare, save.
   • Example: "She baked a cake FOR her mentor."

3. THE CRUCIAL LATINATE VERB INVARIANT (মারাত্মক ল্যাটিন ক্রিয়াপদ ফাঁদ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Certain verbs of Latin origin describing communication DO NOT allow the SVOO pattern!
They MANDATE the preposition "TO":

[X] "Please explain me this lesson" [FATAL BENGALI CALQUE ERROR!].
[✓] "Please explain this lesson TO ME."

[X] "He suggested me a great idea" [WRONG!].
[✓] "He suggested a great idea TO ME."

[X] "Describe me the incident" [WRONG!].
[✓] "Describe the incident TO ME."

* Verbs requiring "TO": explain, describe, suggest, recommend, introduce, announce, confess, mention.

4. SPECIAL DATIVE RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DOUBLE PRONOUN RULE:
   • When both objects are pronouns ('it' / 'them'), use Structure 2:
   • "He gave it to me." (Standard & required in formal English).

2. PRINCIPLE OF END-WEIGHT:
   • Place long/heavy descriptive phrases at the end using the prepositional pattern:
   • "Sukanta Sir gave the award TO the brilliant student from Barrackpore who scored top marks."

5. ESSENTIAL REVISION CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[✓] Distinguish 'to' verbs (transfer) from 'for' verbs (creation/service).
[✓] Never say *'explain me'* or *'suggest me'*; always use 'explain to me' / 'suggest to me'.
[✓] Apply the double pronoun rule ('give it to me') and End-Weight principle.
