1. WHAT IS AN OBJECT COMPLEMENT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Definition: A noun, pronoun, or adjective that immediately follows a Direct 
  Object to describe, qualify, or rename it.
- Fundamental Equation: [ DIRECT OBJECT == OBJECT COMPLEMENT ]
- Example: "They elected [Swadeep (DO)] [Captain (OC)]." (Swadeep == Captain).

2. COMPLEX-TRANSITIVE VERBS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Common Verbs taking SVOC:
  * Naming/Electing: appoint, elect, nominate, name, call, crown, declare.
  * Judging/Perceiving: find, consider, think, believe, judge.
  * Causing State: make, paint, keep, render, drive (mad).

3. SVOO VS SVOC: THE DEFINITIVE COMPARISON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+-------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Feature           | SVOO (Ditransitive)         | SVOC (Complex-Transitive)   |
+-------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| Entity Formula    | IO != DO (Two entities)     | DO == OC (One entity)       |
| Example           | "He gave [him] [a prize]."  | "He called [him] [a hero]." |
| Reality           | 'him' is NOT 'a prize'      | 'him' IS 'a hero'           |
| Passive Forms     | TWO possible passives       | ONE possible passive        |
+-------------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------+

4. PASSIVE VOICE TRANSFORMATION IN SVOC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Active:  "The committee elected [Debangshu] [President]." (SVOC)
Passive: "[Debangshu] was elected [President] by the committee." (SVC in passive!)
- Notice: The Object Complement becomes a Subject Complement in the passive!
