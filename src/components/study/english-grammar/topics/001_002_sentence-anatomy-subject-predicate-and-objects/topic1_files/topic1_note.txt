1. THE FUNDAMENTAL CLAUSE PARTITION (বাক্যের মৌলিক দ্বিখণ্ডন)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every standard English sentence consists of two fundamental syntactic halves:

A. THE COMPLETE SUBJECT (সম্পূর্ণ উদ্দেশ্য)
   • Answers: "Who or what is performing the action / being described?"
   • Components:
     - Head Noun / Pronoun (Simple Subject).
     - Determiners & Articles (a, the, this, their).
     - Modifying Adjectives (diligent, brilliant).
     - Prepositional Modifiers (from Barrackpore, in the laboratory).
     - Appositive Phrases (Sukanta Hui, the master mentor).
     - Relative Clauses (who solved the algorithm).

B. THE COMPLETE PREDICATE (সম্পূর্ণ বিধেয়)
   • Answers: "What does the subject do, experience, or what state is it in?"
   • Components:
     - Finite Verb & Auxiliaries (Simple Predicate / Verb Phrase).
     - Direct & Indirect Objects.
     - Subject & Object Complements.
     - Adverbial Modifiers of Manner, Time, Place, Reason, and Degree.

2. SPECIAL SYNTACTIC STRUCTURES & BENGALI COMPARISONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. IMPERATIVE SENTENCES (আদেশ ও অনুরোধ):
   • The Subject is covert / implied: "[You] submit your code now."
   • Bengali: "কোডটি জমা দাও" (এখানেও 'তুমি' উহ্য থাকে).

2. INVERTED STRUCTURES (উল্টানো বাক্যরীতি):
   • "Down the hill rolled [Predicate] the massive boulder [Subject]."
   • "Great is [Predicate] the power of truth [Subject]."

3. EXPLETIVE / DUMMY SUBJECTS ('There' and 'It'):
   • "There are forty students in the hall." -> Real subject = "forty students".
   • "It is essential to practice daily."    -> Real subject = "to practice daily".

4. BENGALI SOV vs ENGLISH SVO:
   • Bengali: আমি [S] ইংরেজি ব্যাকরণ [O] পড়ি [V].
   • English: I [S] study [V] English grammar [O].

3. SUKANTA SIR'S SENTENCE PARTITIONING RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[✓] Step 1: Scan for the primary Finite Verb phrase.
[✓] Step 2: Draw a vertical division immediately before that verb.
[✓] Step 3: Left side = Complete Subject; Verb + Right side = Complete Predicate.
[✓] Note: If the sentence starts with 'There' or is inverted, find the actual entity 
    performing or undergoing the verb to establish agreement.

4. ESSENTIAL REVISION CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[✓] Handle inversion and identify postponed subjects after dummy 'There' and 'It'.
