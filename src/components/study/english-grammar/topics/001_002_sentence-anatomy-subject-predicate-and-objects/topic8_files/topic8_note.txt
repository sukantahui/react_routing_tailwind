1. THE SEVEN FUNDAMENTAL CLAUSE PATTERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SV   (Subject + Intransitive Verb)
   - "The train arrived."
2. SVO  (Subject + Transitive Verb + Direct Object)
   - "Swadeep developed a web app."
3. SVOO (Subject + Ditransitive Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object)
   - "Sukanta Sir taught Tuhina English Grammar."
4. SVC  (Subject + Linking Verb + Subject Complement)
   - "Abhronila is a brilliant researcher."
5. SVOC (Subject + Complex-Transitive Verb + Direct Object + Object Complement)
   - "The committee elected Debangshu team leader."
6. SVA  (Subject + Intransitive Verb + Obligatory Adverbial)
   - "The coaching center is located in Barrackpore."
7. SVOA (Subject + Transitive Verb + Direct Object + Obligatory Adverbial)
   - "He placed the reference books on the study table."

2. SYNTACTIC SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every grammatical declarative clause in standard English maps to one of these
7 mathematical blueprints!
