1. THE GRAND 12-TENSE CROSS-MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
English structures time into 3 broad temporal zones (Present, Past, Future) 
intersected by 4 aspectual viewpoints (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous), 
producing the foundational 12-tense matrix:

+--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
| ASPECT             | PRESENT                        | PAST                           | FUTURE                         |
+--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
| 1. SIMPLE          | S + V1 / V5 (-s/-es)           | S + V2 (Past Form)             | S + will/shall + V1            |
|                    | "She codes daily."             | "She coded yesterday."         | "She will code tomorrow."      |
+--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
| 2. CONTINUOUS      | S + am/is/are + V-ing          | S + was/were + V-ing           | S + will be + V-ing            |
|                    | "She is coding right now."     | "She was coding at 9 PM."      | "She will be coding at 9 PM."  |
+--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
| 3. PERFECT         | S + have/has + V3              | S + had + V3                   | S + will have + V3             |
|                    | "She has coded five modules."  | "She had coded before lunch."  | "She will have coded by Friday"|
+--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+
| 4. PERFECT CONT.   | S + have/has + been + V-ing    | S + had + been + V-ing         | S + will have been + V-ing     |
|                    | "She has been coding since 8." | "She had been coding for hrs." | "She will have been coding..." |
+--------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+--------------------------------+


2. THE UNIVERSAL RULES OF SEQUENCE OF TENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Sequence of Tenses governs the relationship between the verb in the 
PRINCIPAL (Main) clause and the verb in the SUBORDINATE (Dependent) clause.

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| RULE 1: THE PAST TENSE DOMINANCE INVARIANT                                   |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  When the verb in the Principal Clause is in the PAST Tense, the verb in 
  the Subordinate Clause MUST also be in a PAST Tense.
  * Direct -> Indirect Examples:
      - "He says: 'I am unwell.'"    -> He says that he is unwell. (Present in main)
      - "He said: 'I am unwell.'"    -> He said that he WAS unwell. (Past in main)
      - "She thought that he WOULD come." (NOT 'will come')
      - "I knew that he HAD PASSED the examination." (NOT 'has passed')

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| EXCEPTION 1 TO RULE 1: UNIVERSAL TRUTHS & SCIENTIFIC LAWS                    |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  A past principal clause DOES NOT change the tense of a subordinate clause 
  if the subordinate clause states a UNIVERSAL TRUTH, SCIENTIFIC FACT, 
  MATHEMATICAL LAW, or HABITUAL FACT:
  * EXAMPLES:
      - "The teacher taught us that the earth REVOLVES round the sun." (NOT revolved)
      - "Newton discovered that gravity ATTRACTS all matter." (NOT attracted)
      - "He proved that honesty IS the best policy." (NOT was)

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| EXCEPTION 2 TO RULE 1: CLAUSES OF COMPARISON (THAN / AS...AS)                |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  When the subordinate clause is introduced by 'than' or 'as...as' expressing 
  comparison, it may take ANY tense required by the intended meaning:
  * EXAMPLES:
      - "He loved you better than he LOVES me now." (Past in main, Present in sub)
      - "He respected you more than he WILL EVER RESPECT him."

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| RULE 2: PRESENT OR FUTURE PRINCIPAL CLAUSE FREEDOM                           |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  When the verb in the Principal Clause is in the PRESENT or FUTURE Tense, 
  the verb in the Subordinate Clause can be in ANY Tense (Present, Past, Future) 
  depending on the sense:
  * EXAMPLES:
      - "He says that he IS happy." (Present)
      - "He says that he WAS happy yesterday." (Past)
      - "He says that he WILL BE happy tomorrow." (Future)

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| RULE 3: PURPOSE CLAUSES (SO THAT / IN ORDER THAT)                            |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  * If Principal Clause is in PRESENT/FUTURE -> Subordinate uses MAY + V1:
      - "We eat so that we MAY live."
  * If Principal Clause is in PAST           -> Subordinate uses MIGHT + V1:
      - "He worked hard so that he MIGHT qualify the test."


3. TIME VS TENSE DISCORD (A KEY LINGUISTIC DISTINCTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TENSE is a grammatical category of verb inflections. TIME is a universal, 
non-linguistic reality. The two do NOT have a 1-to-1 match in English:

  * PRESENT TENSE expressing FUTURE TIME:
      - "The college reopens tomorrow." (Simple Present -> Future Time)
      - "I am visiting Mumbai next week." (Present Continuous -> Future Time)

  * PRESENT TENSE expressing PAST TIME (Historic / Narrative Present):
      - "Babur now leads his troops into the valley of Panipat."
      - "In Hamlet, Shakespeare depicts the tragedy of hesitation."

  * PAST TENSE expressing PRESENT / UNREAL HYPOTHETICAL TIME:
      - "If I WERE you, I would accept the job." (Past Subjunctive -> Present Unreality)
      - "It is high time we STARTED studying." (Past Simple -> Immediate Present urgency)


4. CONDITIONAL SENTENCE TENSE CONCORD MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| TYPE              | IF-CLAUSE TENSE            | MAIN-CLAUSE TENSE           |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| ZERO CONDITIONAL  | Simple Present (V1/V5)     | Simple Present (V1/V5)      |
| (General Truth)   | "If you heat ice..."       | "...it melts."              |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 1ST CONDITIONAL   | Simple Present (V1/V5)     | Simple Future (will + V1)   |
| (Real Future)     | "If you study hard..."     | "...you will succeed."      |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 2ND CONDITIONAL   | Simple Past (V2 / were)    | Conditional (would + V1)    |
| (Unreal Present)  | "If I had a million dollars"| "...I would travel."        |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+
| 3RD CONDITIONAL   | Past Perfect (had + V3)    | Perfect Cond (would have+V3)|
| (Unreal Past)     | "If he had trained..."     | "...he would have won."     |
+-------------------+----------------------------+-----------------------------+


5. BENGALI GUIDANCE & PRACTICAL INVARIANTS (বাংলা বিশ্লেষণ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Sequence of Tenses-এর মূল নিয়ম:
    মূল ক্লজ (Principal clause) যদি Past Tense-এ থাকে, তবে আশ্রিত ক্লজটিও (Subordinate clause) 
    Past Tense-এ রূপান্তরিত হতে বাধ্য।
    - ভুল: He told me that he is going.
    - সঠিক: He told me that he WAS going.

  * চিরন্তন সত্যের ব্যতিক্রম:
    অতীতের কথা হলেও চিরন্তন সত্য বা বৈজ্ঞানিক তথ্যে Present Tense অপরিবর্তিত থাকে:
    - "Teacher said that light travels faster than sound."
