Module: 007_002_using-ai-with-excel-for-productivity

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Automated Analysis: Prompt: 'Analyze this table and show me key trends, top performers, and outliers' → Copilot Insight Cards

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Discovering deep business insights by asking conversational natural language questions: trend analysis, identifying statistical outliers, top-performer rankings, and executive summary cards

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Treat Copilot like your smartest business analyst. Ask it: 'Which products are growing the fastest?' or 'Show me my top 5 customers by profit'. In 2 seconds, it gives you the answer and builds a Pivot Table for you.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Asking Questions Outside Table Scope: Asking AI why revenue dropped without providing historical competitor or marketing data in the table. → Fix: Ensure all relevant analytical dimensions are present in the table.
- Accepting AI Summaries Without Spot-Checking: AI summarizing trends based on incomplete sample sets. → Fix: Click 'Show query' or inspect the generated Pivot Table to verify numbers.
- Confusing Sum with Average in Conversational Queries: Asking 'How did region East perform?' without specifying total sales vs average transaction size. → Fix: Be explicit: 'Compare total revenue and average order value across regions'.
