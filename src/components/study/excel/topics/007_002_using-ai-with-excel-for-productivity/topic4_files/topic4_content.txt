5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Asking Questions Outside Table Scope:
  Root Cause: Asking AI why revenue dropped without providing historical competitor or marketing data in the table.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure all relevant analytical dimensions are present in the table.
• Accepting AI Summaries Without Spot-Checking:
  Root Cause: AI summarizing trends based on incomplete sample sets.
  Diagnostic Fix: Click 'Show query' or inspect the generated Pivot Table to verify numbers.
• Confusing Sum with Average in Conversational Queries:
  Root Cause: Asking 'How did region East perform?' without specifying total sales vs average transaction size.
  Diagnostic Fix: Be explicit: 'Compare total revenue and average order value across regions'.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Open-Ended Discovery (Prompt: 'What are the most interesting trends in this data?'): Surfaces non-obvious statistical correlations.
• Top-N / Bottom-N (Prompt: 'Show top 5 products by margin %'): Instant ranking and contribution analysis.
• Insert to Grid (Click 'Add to a new sheet'): Generates permanent analytical tabs in 1 click.
