KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Enterprise Governance: SOC-2 / ISO-27001 Compliance | Zero Data Retention (ZDR) APIs | Verifying Calculations (Zero Blind Trust)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Establishing enterprise AI governance, ethics, and data privacy in corporate spreadsheets: Zero Data Retention (ZDR) enterprise agreements, preventing PII data leaks, and rigorous calculation verification

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Golden Rule of AI in Corporate Finance: NEVER blindly trust a number produced by AI! Always check the formula, verify the math, and ensure your company's confidential data is protected under enterprise privacy agreements.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Pasting Confidential Financial Data into Consumer Web Chatbots (Data Leak): Employees copying unreleased quarterly earnings into free consumer ChatGPT/Claude web interfaces (where data is used for model training). → Fix: Enforce strict use of Microsoft 365 Copilot Commercial Data Protection or Enterprise API endpoints.
- Blind Acceptance of AI Hallucinated Numbers: Submitting financial reports with AI-generated numbers without checking mathematical accuracy. → Fix: Cross-foot and verify all calculations against source accounting ledgers.
- Unsanitized PII Transmission: Sending personal medical records or bank account numbers to external API endpoints. → Fix: Deploy data masking functions before transmitting payloads.
