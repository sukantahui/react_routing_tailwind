5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Copilot Button Grayed Out / Disabled:
  Root Cause: Workbook is saved locally on C:\ drive or AutoSave is turned OFF.
  Diagnostic Fix: Save file to OneDrive or SharePoint and ensure AutoSave toggle is ON at top left.
• Trying to Use Copilot on .xls / .csv Files:
  Root Cause: Legacy `.xls` or flat `.csv` formats do not support modern M365 Copilot features.
  Diagnostic Fix: Save workbook as standard `.xlsx` or `.xlsm`.
• Multiple Unrelated Tables on Same Sheet:
  Root Cause: Confusing Copilot with 5 different disconnected tables on one tab.
  Diagnostic Fix: Maintain clean single-table architecture per worksheet tab.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Save to OneDrive (File > Save As > OneDrive / SharePoint): Enables cloud synchronization and Copilot activation.
• Turn AutoSave ON (Top left AutoSave toggle): Mandatory requirement for real-time AI grounding.
• Suggested Prompts (Click sparkler icon above prompt box): Discovers pre-built analysis templates.
