Topic: Practice: Corporate equipment lease vs purchase decision matrix
Module: 003_001_financial-functions-in-excel-basic-to-professional
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Lease vs Purchase Engine: PV of After-Tax Lease Payments vs PV of (Capex - TaxShields - ResidualValue)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Building a corporate capital allocation model evaluating Equipment Lease versus Outright Debt-Financed Purchase using after-tax discounted cash flows

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Lease vs Buy is a classic CFO decision. Always discount cash flows at the after-tax cost of debt [Kd * (1 - Tax)], because lease payments are fixed contractual liabilities just like bank debt.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Using WACC Instead of After-Tax Cost of Debt: Discounting lease cash flows at WACC instead of the after-tax cost of debt distorts lease valuation (lease payments are contractual debt-like obligations). → Fix: Discount lease vs buy cash flows strictly at: `After-Tax Cost of Debt = PreTaxCostOfDebt * (1 - TaxRate)`.
- Omitting Depreciation Tax Shields in Buy Option: Failing to credit the tax savings generated by depreciation overstates the net cost of purchasing. → Fix: Include `Depreciation * TaxRate` as a positive cash inflow in the Buy schedule.
- Ignoring Maintenance Cost Allocation: Assuming the lessor pays for maintenance under a capital/finance lease (where maintenance is the lessee's responsibility). → Fix: Clarify whether the contract is an Operating Lease (Lessor maintains) or Finance Lease (Lessee maintains).
