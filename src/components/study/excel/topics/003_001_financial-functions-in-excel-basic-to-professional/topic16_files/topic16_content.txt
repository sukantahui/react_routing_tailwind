1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Practice: Corporate equipment lease vs purchase decision matrix is a foundational pillar of corporate financial modeling, investment banking, and capital budgeting.
It equips financial modelers with the exact mathematical tools to value assets, evaluate debt structures, and appraise investments.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Lease vs Purchase Engine: PV of After-Tax Lease Payments vs PV of (Capex - TaxShields - ResidualValue)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. After-Tax Lease Cost Analysis:
   Lease payments are 100% tax-deductible operating expenses: `Net Cash Outflow = LeasePayment * (1 - CorporateTaxRate)`.

2. Debt-Financed Purchase Cost Analysis:
   Ownership involves loan debt service, maintenance opex, depreciation tax shields (`Depreciation * TaxRate`), interest tax shields (`Interest * TaxRate`), and terminal asset salvage value.

3. Net Advantage to Leasing (NAL):
   Calculates the difference in present value between leasing and buying discounted at the firm's after-tax cost of debt: `NAL = PV(Buy) - PV(Lease)`. If `NAL > 0`, Leasing is optimal.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Using WACC Instead of After-Tax Cost of Debt:
  Root Cause: Discounting lease cash flows at WACC instead of the after-tax cost of debt distorts lease valuation (lease payments are contractual debt-like obligations).
  Diagnostic Fix: Discount lease vs buy cash flows strictly at: `After-Tax Cost of Debt = PreTaxCostOfDebt * (1 - TaxRate)`.
• Omitting Depreciation Tax Shields in Buy Option:
  Root Cause: Failing to credit the tax savings generated by depreciation overstates the net cost of purchasing.
  Diagnostic Fix: Include `Depreciation * TaxRate` as a positive cash inflow in the Buy schedule.
• Ignoring Maintenance Cost Allocation:
  Root Cause: Assuming the lessor pays for maintenance under a capital/finance lease (where maintenance is the lessee's responsibility).
  Diagnostic Fix: Clarify whether the contract is an Operating Lease (Lessor maintains) or Finance Lease (Lessee maintains).

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• After-Tax Discount Rate (=PreTaxDebtRate * (1 - TaxRate)): The correct discount rate for debt-equivalent lease vs buy cash flows.
• Net Advantage to Leasing (=PV_Cost_Buy - PV_Cost_Lease): Positive NAL indicates leasing saves money; negative indicates buying is cheaper.
• Residual Salvage Credit (PV of estimated resale value): Credited against purchase cost at the end of the asset lifespan.
