1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Nominal vs Effective interest rates: NOMINAL and EFFECTIVE functions is a foundational pillar of corporate financial modeling, investment banking, and capital budgeting.
It equips financial modelers with the exact mathematical tools to value assets, evaluate debt structures, and appraise investments.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: =NOMINAL(effect_rate, npery) | =EFFECT(nominal_rate, npery)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Nominal Stated Rate (APR):
   The headline annual interest rate quoted by financial institutions without accounting for intra-year compounding (`APR = PeriodicRate * PeriodsPerYear`).

2. Effective Annual Rate (APY / EAR):
   The true economic rate earned or paid after factoring in compounding: `EAR = (1 + NominalRate/n)^n - 1`.

3. The Compounding Frequency Penalty:
   The more frequently interest compounds (Monthly > Quarterly > Annual), the higher the effective borrower cost and investor yield.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Confusing npery with Total Loan Years:
  Root Cause: Entering total years (e.g. 5) instead of compounding periods per year (12) produces distorted rate conversions.
  Diagnostic Fix: Remember: npery is periods PER YEAR (e.g. 12 for monthly, 4 for quarterly, 365 for daily).
• Passing Percentages as Whole Numbers:
  Root Cause: Entering 8 instead of 8% (or 0.08) causes Excel to treat the rate as 800% interest.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always enter interest rates with % sign (`8.5%`) or decimal fraction (`0.085`).
• Ignoring Compounding in Loan Comparisons:
  Root Cause: Choosing a loan with 9.8% monthly compounding over 10.0% annual compounding without realizing the 9.8% loan actually costs 10.25% APY.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always convert all competing debt offers to EFFECT() before comparing.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• True Annual Cost (APY) (=EFFECT(StatedRate, 12)): Calculates true annual borrower cost with monthly compounding.
• Convert to Stated Rate (APR) (=NOMINAL(EffectiveRate, 12)): Converts effective annual yield back to contractual stated rate.
• Daily Compounding Impact (=EFFECT(Rate, 365)): Reveals the true cost of credit cards and daily liquidity lines.
