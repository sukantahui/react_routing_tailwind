1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Loan payment computation with PMT, PPMT (Principal), and IPMT (Interest) is a foundational pillar of corporate financial modeling, investment banking, and capital budgeting.
It equips financial modelers with the exact mathematical tools to value assets, evaluate debt structures, and appraise investments.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: =PMT(rate, nper, pv, [fv], [type]) | =PPMT(rate, per, nper, pv) | =IPMT(rate, per, nper, pv)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. The Equal Monthly Installment (PMT):
   Calculates the fixed periodic payment required to fully amortize a loan balance over nper periods at a constant rate.

2. Interest Component Decomposition (IPMT):
   Computes the exact interest charge for specific period 'per' based on the remaining outstanding principal (`IPMT = RemainingBalance * Rate`).

3. Principal Component Decomposition (PPMT):
   Calculates the exact equity principal paid down in period 'per' (`PPMT = PMT - IPMT`).

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Period Index Out of Bounds (#NUM!):
  Root Cause: Passing a period argument (per) less than 1 or greater than nper to PPMT/IPMT returns #NUM!.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure 1 <= per <= nper in amortization row formulas.
• Forgetting Rate Frequency Division:
  Root Cause: Passing annual interest rate directly without dividing by 12 inflates monthly PMT payments by 10x.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always pass `AnnualRate / 12` into monthly PMT formulas.
• Summing IPMT + PPMT Discrepancy:
  Root Cause: Rounding errors occurring when individual payments are formatted as rounded integers.
  Diagnostic Fix: Verify that `=ROUND(PPMT + IPMT, 2)` exactly equals `=ROUND(PMT, 2)`.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Monthly EMI Formula (=PMT(Rate/12, Years*12, -LoanAmount)): Standard banking formula for equal monthly loan installments.
• Verification Identity (PMT = PPMT + IPMT): In any loan period, principal + interest must equal total payment exactly.
• Dynamic Month Index (PPMT(Rate/12, ROW(A1), Years*12, -Loan)): ROW(A1) provides a dynamic incrementing period counter for amortization tables.
