1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Asset depreciation modeling: SLN (Straight-line), DB (Declining balance), DDB (Double-declining), and SYD is a foundational pillar of corporate financial modeling, investment banking, and capital budgeting.
It equips financial modelers with the exact mathematical tools to value assets, evaluate debt structures, and appraise investments.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: =SLN(cost, salvage, life) | =DB(cost, salvage, life, period, [month]) | =DDB(cost, salvage, life, period, [factor]) | =SYD(cost, salvage, life, period)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Straight-Line Depreciation (SLN):
   Allocates equal annual depreciation expense across the asset's useful life: `SLN = (Cost - Salvage) / Life`.

2. Accelerated Double Declining Balance (DDB):
   Accelerates tax deductions by depreciating at double the straight-line rate (factor=2), automatically stopping when book value hits salvage value.

3. Sum-of-Years' Digits (SYD):
   Applies a declining fractional multiplier based on the sum of digits of the asset's lifespan.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• DDB Depreciation Exceeding Salvage Value:
  Root Cause: In older Excel versions, DDB could depreciate below salvage value in edge cases.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `=VDB(Cost, Salvage, Life, StartPeriod, EndPeriod, Factor, FALSE)` to ensure flawless salvage boundary adherence.
• Mismatching Depreciation Periods with Life:
  Root Cause: Passing life in years (5) but period in months (36) causes formula breakdown.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure period and life use identical time units (all years or all months).
• Ignoring Year 1 Partial Month Proration:
  Root Cause: Assets purchased mid-year require partial first-year depreciation.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use the optional `[month]` argument in DB() or use VDB() for exact fractional periods.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Equal Annual Expense (=SLN(Cost, Salvage, Life)): The standard straight-line depreciation formula for book accounting.
• Accelerated Tax Shield (=DDB(Cost, Salvage, Life, Period)): Maximizes upfront depreciation to reduce corporate tax liabilities in early years.
• Variable Depreciation (VDB) (=VDB(Cost, Salvage, Life, Start, End)): The most flexible depreciation function, handling partial years and automatic SLN crossover.
