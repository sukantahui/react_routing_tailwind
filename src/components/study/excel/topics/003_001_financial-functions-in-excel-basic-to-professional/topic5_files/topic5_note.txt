Topic: Real-world irregular cash flow valuation with XNPV
Module: 003_001_financial-functions-in-excel-basic-to-professional
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
=XNPV(discount_rate, cash_flows_range, dates_range)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Discounted cash flow valuation for real-world projects with irregular, non-annual transaction dates using the precision XNPV engine

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Contrast this carefully: In standard NPV, Year 0 is OUTSIDE the formula. In XNPV, Year 0 is INSIDE the formula! Knowing this difference is what separates financial pros from amateurs.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Adding Initial Outflow Separately in XNPV (Double Deduction): Applying the standard NPV rule to XNPV (`=XNPV(...) + CF0`) double-deducts the initial capex. → Fix: In XNPV, include the initial cash flow (CF0) and its date directly inside the formula ranges.
- Unsorted Chronological Dates (#NUM!): If the first date in dates_range is not the earliest date, XNPV returns a #NUM! error. → Fix: Sort cash flow schedules in strict ascending chronological date order.
- Text Date Entry in Dates Range: Dates entered as text strings ('27/08/2026') trigger a #VALUE! error. → Fix: Ensure the dates range contains genuine Excel serial date numbers.
