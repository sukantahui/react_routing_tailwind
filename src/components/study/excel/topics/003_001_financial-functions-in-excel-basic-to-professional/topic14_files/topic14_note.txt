KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Financial Modeling Standards: Blue (Hardcoded Inputs) | Black (Formulas) | Green (Cross-Sheet Links) | Red (External Links)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Implementing Wall Street financial modeling standards: universal color coding, dynamic input blocks, error audit flags, and FAST standard architecture

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Professional models are judged by their clarity, not just their math. Blue font for inputs, Black for formulas, Green for links. Never bury hardcoded numbers inside formulas!

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Hardcoding Constants Inside Formulas: Writing `=Revenue * 0.25` instead of `=Revenue * Assumption!$C$4` hides assumptions from auditors. → Fix: Place all growth rates, tax rates, and parameters in dedicated Assumption Blocks.
- Unchecked Balance Sheet Discrepancy: Submitting a financial model where Total Assets do not equal Total Liabilities + Equity. → Fix: Add high-visibility Audit Flag headers: `=IF(Assets=Liab+Equity, "✓ BALANCED", "⚠️ ERROR")`.
- Inconsistent Row Formula Logic: Changing the formula structure halfway across a 10-year timeline row creates hidden maintenance bugs. → Fix: Ensure every row uses a consistent, unbroken formula logic from Year 1 to Year N.
