KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
=PRICE(settlement, maturity, rate, yld, redemption, frequency, [basis]) | =YIELD(settlement, maturity, rate, pr, redemption, frequency, [basis]) | =DURATION(...) | =MDURATION(...)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Institutional bond mathematics: pricing fixed-rate debentures (PRICE), calculating Yield to Maturity (YIELD), and measuring interest rate risk sensitivity via Macaulay and Modified DURATION

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Modified Duration is the ultimate bond risk metric. If a bond has an MDURATION of 6.5, a 1% rise in interest rates will cause its market price to drop by approximately 6.5%. Institutional treasurers live by this rule.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Pricing Stated per 100 Par vs Total Value: Forgetting that Excel's PRICE and YIELD functions output values per ₹100 face value, not total currency. -> Fix: Multiply output by `(FaceValue / 100)` to calculate total trade settlement value.
- Settlement Date >= Maturity Date (#NUM!): Settlement date must be strictly earlier than maturity date. -> Fix: Ensure settlement date precedes maturity date.
- Frequency Parameter Invalid Code: Passing invalid frequency integers (only 1 = Annual, 2 = Semi-Annual, 4 = Quarterly are supported). -> Fix: Use frequency code 2 for standard semi-annual corporate and sovereign bonds.
