1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Bond valuation and yield calculation: YIELD, PRICE, DURATION, and MDURATION is a foundational pillar of corporate financial modeling, investment banking, and capital budgeting.
It equips financial modelers with the exact mathematical tools to value assets, evaluate debt structures, and appraise investments.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: =PRICE(settlement, maturity, rate, yld, redemption, frequency, [basis]) | =YIELD(settlement, maturity, rate, pr, redemption, frequency, [basis]) | =DURATION(...) | =MDURATION(...)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Inverse Price-Yield Relationship:
   When market yields rise above coupon rate, bond trades at a Discount (`PRICE < 100`); when yields fall below coupon rate, bond trades at a Premium (`PRICE > 100`).

2. Yield to Maturity (YTM) Solver:
   YIELD() calculates the total annualized rate of return expected on a bond held until maturity based on current market price.

3. Duration & Interest Rate Sensitivity:
   Macaulay DURATION measures weighted average cash flow maturity; Modified Duration (MDURATION) estimates the exact % change in bond price for a 1% shift in interest rates (`%ΔPrice ≈ -MDuration * ΔYield`).

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Pricing Stated per 100 Par vs Total Value:
  Root Cause: Forgetting that Excel's PRICE and YIELD functions output values per ₹100 face value, not total currency.
  Diagnostic Fix: Multiply output by `(FaceValue / 100)` to calculate total trade settlement value.
• Settlement Date >= Maturity Date (#NUM!):
  Root Cause: Settlement date must be strictly earlier than maturity date.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure settlement date precedes maturity date.
• Frequency Parameter Invalid Code:
  Root Cause: Passing invalid frequency integers (only 1 = Annual, 2 = Semi-Annual, 4 = Quarterly are supported).
  Diagnostic Fix: Use frequency code 2 for standard semi-annual corporate and sovereign bonds.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Semi-Annual Bond Price (=PRICE(Settlement, Maturity, Coupon, YTM, 100, 2)): Standard institutional formula for semi-annual bond pricing.
• Price Sensitivity Rule (% Price Change ≈ -MDURATION * YieldChange): Instant calculation of portfolio loss if interest rates rise by 1%.
• Day Count Convention (Basis: 1 (Actual/Actual) for G-Secs): Use basis 1 for Indian Government Securities and US Treasuries.
