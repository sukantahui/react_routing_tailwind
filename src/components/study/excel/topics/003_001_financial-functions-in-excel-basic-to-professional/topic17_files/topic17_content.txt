5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Breaking Balance Sheet Linkages:
  Root Cause: Hardcoding cash balances to force balance sheets to balance destroys model integrity.
  Diagnostic Fix: Cash must flow strictly from the Cash Flow Statement ending balance.
• Unbounded Terminal Perpetuity Formulas:
  Root Cause: Writing terminal value formulas without bounding checks produces nonsensical negative valuations when WACC < g.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure WACC > g and include validation flags.
• Incomplete Sign Discipline Across Statements:
  Root Cause: Mixing negative and positive signs inconsistently between Cash Flow and Balance Sheet statements.
  Diagnostic Fix: Enforce strict accounting sign discipline across all financial statements.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Audit First, Present Second (Verify all reconciliation cells): Never present a model with an active balance sheet mismatch flag.
• Modular Architecture (Separate Inputs, Calcs, and Outputs): Keep assumptions, calculation schedules, and presentation summaries on dedicated sheets.
• F9 Formula Stepping (F9 inside Formula Bar): Step through complex nested financial formulas to verify interim calculations.
