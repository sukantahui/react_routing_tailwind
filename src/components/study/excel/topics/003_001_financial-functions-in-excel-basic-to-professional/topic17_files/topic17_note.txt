COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Breaking Balance Sheet Linkages: Hardcoding cash balances to force balance sheets to balance destroys model integrity. -> Fix: Cash must flow strictly from the Cash Flow Statement ending balance.
- Unbounded Terminal Perpetuity Formulas: Writing terminal value formulas without bounding checks produces nonsensical negative valuations when WACC < g. -> Fix: Ensure WACC > g and include validation flags.
- Incomplete Sign Discipline Across Statements: Mixing negative and positive signs inconsistently between Cash Flow and Balance Sheet statements. -> Fix: Enforce strict accounting sign discipline across all financial statements.
