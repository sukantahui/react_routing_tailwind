1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Time Value of Money (TVM) principles: Present Value, Future Value, and Interest Rates is a foundational pillar of corporate financial modeling, investment banking, and capital budgeting.
It equips financial modelers with the exact mathematical tools to value assets, evaluate debt structures, and appraise investments.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: =PV(rate, nper, pmt, [fv], [type]) | =FV(rate, nper, pmt, [pv], [type]) | =NPER(rate, pmt, pv, [fv], [type]) | =RATE(nper, pmt, pv, [fv], [type])

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Discounting vs Compounding:
   Compounding projects current capital into future values (`FV = PV * (1+r)^n`); Discounting calculates the current worth of future cash inflows (`PV = FV / (1+r)^n`).

2. Cash Flow Sign Convention:
   Outflows (investments/deposits) must be negative (-); Inflows (returns/proceeds) must be positive (+). Violating this produces invalid results or #NUM! errors.

3. Rate and Period Matching Principle:
   Annual interest rates must be divided by payment frequency (e.g. `Rate/12`), and years multiplied by frequency (`Years*12`).

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Cash Flow Sign Reversal Trap:
  Root Cause: Entering both PV and PMT as positive numbers causes FV to return a negative or distorted output.
  Diagnostic Fix: Follow strict accounting signs: Cash Outflow is Negative (-), Cash Inflow is Positive (+).
• Rate/Period Frequency Mismatch:
  Root Cause: Using an annual rate (8%) with monthly periods (120 months) without dividing rate by 12 overcharges interest by 12x.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always align rate and nper: `Rate/12` with `Years*12` for monthly compounding.
• Annuity Type Misconfiguration (0 vs 1):
  Root Cause: Leaving type as 0 (End of period) for advance lease payments (Start of period) underestimates interest accrual.
  Diagnostic Fix: Set type = 1 for payments due at the beginning of the period (Annuity Due).

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Monthly TVM Alignment (=PV(Rate/12, Years*12, -MonthlyPMT)): The standard pattern for all monthly financial calculations.
• Beginning vs End of Period (Type: 1 (Start), Type: 0 (End)): Type 1 is mandatory for advance rent and upfront insurance annuities.
• Extract Implied Annual Rate (=RATE(Periods, -PMT, LoanAmt) * 12): Annualizes monthly implied interest rate cleanly.
