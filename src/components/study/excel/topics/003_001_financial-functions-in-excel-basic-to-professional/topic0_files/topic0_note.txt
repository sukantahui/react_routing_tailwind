PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Foundational Time Value of Money (TVM) mathematics: Present Value, Future Value, periodic interest compounding, and cash flow sign conventions

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
The #1 rule in Excel financial modeling: Respect the Cash Flow Sign Convention! Money leaving your pocket is NEGATIVE (-); money entering your pocket is POSITIVE (+).

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Cash Flow Sign Reversal Trap: Entering both PV and PMT as positive numbers causes FV to return a negative or distorted output. -> Fix: Follow strict accounting signs: Cash Outflow is Negative (-), Cash Inflow is Positive (+).
- Rate/Period Frequency Mismatch: Using an annual rate (8%) with monthly periods (120 months) without dividing rate by 12 overcharges interest by 12x. -> Fix: Always align rate and nper: `Rate/12` with `Years*12` for monthly compounding.
- Annuity Type Misconfiguration (0 vs 1): Leaving type as 0 (End of period) for advance lease payments (Start of period) underestimates interest accrual. -> Fix: Set type = 1 for payments due at the beginning of the period (Annuity Due).
