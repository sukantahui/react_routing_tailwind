KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Real Estate Appraisal Engine: Net Operating Income (NOI) / Cap Rate -> DCF Levered Cash Flows -> IRR & Equity Multiple

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Comprehensive commercial real estate investment appraisal: Net Operating Income (NOI), Capitalization Rate valuation, debt service coverage, and Levered IRR & Equity Multiple

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Real estate private equity uses two key metrics: Levered IRR (the annualized speed of return) and Equity Multiple (the total amount of cash returned). Always calculate both for your investment committee.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Deducting Debt Service Before Calculating NOI: NOI must measure property operating profitability BEFORE financing costs; deducting debt service inside NOI distorts Cap Rate valuation. -> Fix: Keep NOI strictly unlevered: `NOI = Effective Revenue - Operating Expenses`.
- Ignoring Capital Expenditure Reserves: Failing to budget for roof, HVAC, and structural replacement reserves inflates reported cash flows. -> Fix: Include Replacement Reserves below NOI to compute true Cash Flow Before Debt Service.
- Assuming Zero Vacancy in Long-Term Projections: Modeling 100% occupancy for 10 consecutive years violates real estate underwriting standards. -> Fix: Underwrite a realistic structural vacancy allowance (typically 5% to 10%).
