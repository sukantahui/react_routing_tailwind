1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Cumulative loan interest and principal: CUMIPMT and CUMPRINC is a foundational pillar of corporate financial modeling, investment banking, and capital budgeting.
It equips financial modelers with the exact mathematical tools to value assets, evaluate debt structures, and appraise investments.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: =CUMIPMT(rate, nper, pv, start_period, end_period, type) | =CUMPRINC(rate, nper, pv, start_period, end_period, type)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Multi-Period Interval Aggregation:
   Calculates total interest or principal paid between start_period (e.g. month 1) and end_period (e.g. month 12) without building 360-row tables.

2. Mandatory Non-Zero Type Argument:
   Unlike PMT, the 'type' argument (0 = end, 1 = start) is STRICTLY MANDATORY in CUMIPMT and CUMPRINC; omitting it causes a formula syntax error.

3. Strict Positive PV Requirement:
   CUMIPMT/CUMPRINC require pv to be entered as a POSITIVE number; entering negative PV returns a fatal #NUM! error.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Negative PV Argument (#NUM! Error):
  Root Cause: Entering PV as negative (e.g. -5000000) causes CUMIPMT and CUMPRINC to immediately return #NUM!.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always pass PV as a POSITIVE number in CUMIPMT/CUMPRINC (unlike standard PMT).
• Omitted 'Type' Argument Syntax Error:
  Root Cause: Omitting the 6th argument (type: 0 or 1) causes Excel to reject the formula.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always specify the 6th argument: 0 for end of period, 1 for start.
• Invalid Period Range (Start > End or < 1):
  Root Cause: Passing start_period > end_period or start_period < 1 returns #NUM!.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure 1 <= start_period <= end_period <= nper.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Fiscal Year 1 Interest (=CUMIPMT(Rate/12, NPER, Loan, 1, 12, 0)): Instantly calculate total Year 1 tax-deductible interest in one cell.
• Fiscal Year 2 Interest (=CUMIPMT(Rate/12, NPER, Loan, 13, 24, 0)): Calculates Year 2 interest without building an amortization table.
• Positive PV Rule (PV must be > 0): Remember: CUMIPMT requires positive loan amount!
