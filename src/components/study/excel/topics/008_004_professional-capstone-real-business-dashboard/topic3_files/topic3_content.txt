5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Overcrowding Dashboard with 20 Cluttered Visuals:
  Root Cause: Violating cognitive visual hierarchy, making dashboard unreadable for executives.
  Diagnostic Fix: Adhere to the 'Rule of 5': 4 KPI cards + max 4 clear visual charts.
• Inconsistent Color Palettes Across Charts:
  Root Cause: Using 15 different random colors creating visual chaos.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use a curated corporate palette: Navy `#0f172a`, Slate `#334155`, Sky Blue `#0284c7`, Emerald `#059669`.
• Leaving Gridlines Visible on Dashboard Canvas:
  Root Cause: Standard spreadsheet gridlines distracting from executive card presentation.
  Diagnostic Fix: Turn OFF gridlines: View > Uncheck 'Gridlines'.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Turn OFF Gridlines (View > Uncheck Gridlines (Alt + W + V + G)): Gives your dashboard a clean, sleek software canvas appearance.
• Report Connections (Right-click Slicer > Report Connections > Check All): Synchronizes all charts to filter simultaneously.
• KPI Delta Arrows (▲ +14.2% (Emerald) | ▼ -3.1% (Rose)): Delivers instant visual comprehension of performance deltas.
