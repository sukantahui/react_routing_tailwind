KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Capstone Architecture: Enterprise Business Problem -> Requirements Spec -> Multi-Source Data Architecture -> Star Schema Blueprint

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Architecting an institutional enterprise business intelligence dashboard: establishing business problem statements, defining data pipeline specifications, designing Star Schema dimensional models, and defining executive KPI scorecards

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Great dashboards start with great planning. Before you open Excel, know your numbers: What is your Fact table? What are your Dimension tables? What 5 numbers does the CEO need to see? Plan first, build second!

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Building Dashboards Without Clear Business Question Alignment: Designing 20 pretty charts that answer zero actionable business questions. -> Fix: Define top 5 executive business questions before placing a single chart.
- Single Giant Flat Table Architecture (Anti-Pattern): Creating an unmaintainable 50-column flat table instead of a clean Star Schema. -> Fix: Separate data into 1 Fact table and dedicated Dimension lookup tables.
- Underestimating Data Cleansing Complexity: Assuming raw corporate ERP data is clean and formatted. -> Fix: Build robust Power Query ETL pipelines to sanitize all inputs.
