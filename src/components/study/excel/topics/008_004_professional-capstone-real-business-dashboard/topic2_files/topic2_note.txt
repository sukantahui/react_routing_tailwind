KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Phase 2 Modeling: Power Pivot Star Schema (1:Many Relationships) + DAX Measure Suite (Revenue, Gross Margin %, YTD, SPLY Growth %)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Constructing the enterprise Star Schema data model and authoring the DAX Measure calculation suite: establishing 1-to-many relationships, configuring official Date tables, and authoring core KPIs, YTD metrics, and variances

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Phase 2 is the brain of your dashboard. Connect your Fact table to your Dimension tables with 1-to-many relationships, write your DAX measures using `DIVIDE` and `CALCULATE`, and your data model will calculate instantly across millions of rows.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Creating Many-to-Many Relationships Without Bridge Tables: Duplicate keys in dimension tables creating ambiguous relationships. → Fix: Ensure dimension tables contain 100% unique primary keys.
- Using Bi-Directional Cross-Filtering Indiscriminately: Bi-directional relationships causing unpredictable filter propagation and calculation performance degradation. → Fix: Keep relationships strictly single-directional (1-to-many).
- Omitting Continuous Date Table for Time Intelligence: Time intelligence DAX functions returning `#ERROR` on missing calendar dates. → Fix: Generate complete contiguous Date table in Power Query or DAX.
