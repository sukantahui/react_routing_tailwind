KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Formatting Standard: Indian Rupee: [₹-en-IN] #,##0.00 | Date: dd-mmm-yyyy | Header: Fill #0f172a, Font #ffffff Bold | AutoFit: Alt+H+O+I

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Evaluating proficiency in tabular data entry, professional custom number formatting, date standardization, border hierarchy, and auto-fitting column widths

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Formatting is your first impression. Never leave columns showing `###`! Right-align your numbers, use `dd-mmm-yyyy` for dates, and put a double-underline under your total row. It makes you look like an elite professional.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Truncated Cell Content (### Display Bug): Narrow columns hiding numbers as `###`. -> Fix: Press `Alt + H + O + I` to auto-fit all column widths.
- Centering Numerical Data Columns: Centering numbers impairs rapid visual decimal alignment for accountants. -> Fix: Right-align numbers; left-align text; center dates and codes.
- Using Bright Neon Background Fills: Unprofessional eye-straining colors in business reports. -> Fix: Use curated corporate palettes (Navy `#0f172a`, Slate `#1e293b`, subtle gray `#f8fafc`).
