5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Truncated Cell Content (### Display Bug):
  Root Cause: Narrow columns hiding numbers as `###`.
  Diagnostic Fix: Press `Alt + H + O + I` to auto-fit all column widths.
• Centering Numerical Data Columns:
  Root Cause: Centering numbers impairs rapid visual decimal alignment for accountants.
  Diagnostic Fix: Right-align numbers; left-align text; center dates and codes.
• Using Bright Neon Background Fills:
  Root Cause: Unprofessional eye-straining colors in business reports.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use curated corporate palettes (Navy `#0f172a`, Slate `#1e293b`, subtle gray `#f8fafc`).

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• AutoFit Columns (Alt + H + O + I): Instantly sizes columns to fit contents.
• Currency Format (Ctrl + Shift + $ (or custom ₹ format)): Applies clean standard currency formatting.
• Accounting Underline (Top thin border + bottom double line): The universal accounting standard for totals.
