KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Navigation Benchmark: Ctrl+Arrow (Jump Boundaries) | Ctrl+Home/End | Shift+F11 (New Sheet) | Ctrl+PageUp/PageDown (Switch Tabs)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Evaluating candidate fluency with Excel's core interface: keyboard navigation, ribbon shortcuts, formula bar ergonomics, zooming, and rapid sheet management

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Speed starts with your fingers! Master `Ctrl + Arrows` and `Ctrl + Shift + Arrows`. If you never touch the mouse, you will work 5 times faster than anyone else in your office.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Touching the Mouse for Basic Navigation: Scrolling 10,000 rows with the mouse scroll wheel. → Fix: Use `Ctrl + Down Arrow` to jump to table bottom instantly.
- Freezing Wrong Rows with Freeze Panes: Placing cursor in cell A1 before clicking Freeze Panes. → Fix: Place active cursor in cell B2 to freeze Row 1 and Column A simultaneously.
- Accidentally Hiding Formula Bar / Ribbon: Pressing Ctrl + F1 hiding ribbon tabs. → Fix: Press `Ctrl + F1` to restore full ribbon.
