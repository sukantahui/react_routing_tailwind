KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Chart Standard: Alt + F1 (Instant Embedded Chart) | F11 (Chart Sheet) | Elements: Data Labels, Deselect Gridlines, Clear Legend

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Constructing and styling core Excel visualizations: Clustered Column, Line, Bar, and Pie/Donut charts: configuring chart titles, data series, axis scales, legends, and data labels

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Never use 3D charts! Keep your charts clean, simple, and 2D. Delete the gray gridlines, delete the redundant legend, and make your chart title bold. It will look like a McKinsey presentation.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Using 3D Charts or Exploding Pie Charts: 3D perspective distorts visual proportions and misleads executive decision makers. → Fix: Always use clean 2D Clustered Column, Bar, or Line charts.
- Leaving Redundant Legends on Single-Series Charts: Displaying a legend saying 'Series 1' when chart title already explains data. → Fix: Delete legend on single-series charts to maximize chart canvas area.
- Truncated Axis Labels Due to Overcrowding: Trying to fit 30 monthly labels horizontally causing angled unreadable text. → Fix: Switch to a horizontal Bar chart for high-cardinality category comparisons.
