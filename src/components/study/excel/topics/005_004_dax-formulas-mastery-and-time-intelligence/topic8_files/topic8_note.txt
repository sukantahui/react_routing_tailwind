TEACHER'S ADVICE:
The #1 mistake beginners make with Time Intelligence: They pass `Sales[OrderDate]` into TOTALYTD! That will fail. Always pass `Calendar[Date]` from your dedicated, marked Date table. It works 100% of the time.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Using Fact Table Dates for Time Intelligence: Passing `Fact_Sales[OrderDate]` into `DATESYTD()` returns incorrect results because sales dates have gaps. -> Fix: Always pass the continuous date column from your dedicated Calendar table: `Dim_Calendar[Date]`.
- DateTime Timestamps in Date Column: Time components (`12/04/2026 15:30:00`) break date equality matching. -> Fix: Convert column data type to pure Date (without time) in Power Query.
- Calendar Ending Mid-Year: Calendar table ending on today's date causes full-year functions like `SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR()` to fail. -> Fix: Extend calendar to December 31st of the current or future year.
