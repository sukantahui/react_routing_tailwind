1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Semi-additive measures: Opening and Closing balances for inventory and banking (CLOSINGBALANCEMONTH) is an essential discipline in enterprise business intelligence, quantitative finance, and advanced data modeling.
It provides the core protocols required to construct complex multi-dimensional financial measures, calculate period-over-period growth, and evaluate dynamic rankings.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Semi-Additive DAX: CLOSINGBALANCEMONTH([Measure], Dim_Calendar[Date]) | OPENINGBALANCEMONTH | CALCULATE([Measure], LASTDATE(Dim_Calendar[Date]))

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. The Semi-Additive Problem:
   Sales are additive over time (Jan Sales + Feb Sales = Total Sales). Bank Balances and Inventory are NON-ADDITIVE over time (Jan Balance + Feb Balance is meaningless nonsense!).

2. Closing Balance Architecture:
   When aggregating across time, semi-additive measures must return the CLOSING SNAPSHOT of the last active date in the period rather than summing.

3. Native Balance Functions:
   `CLOSINGBALANCEMONTH([StockValue], Dim_Calendar[Date])` and `CLOSINGBALANCEYEAR()` automatically extract the ending period snapshot.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Summing Bank Balances Across Months (Fatal Accounting Error):
  Root Cause: Using standard `=SUM(Bank[Balance])` adds January ₹10L + February ₹10L = ₹20L (inventing money that does not exist).
  Diagnostic Fix: Always use semi-additive DAX: `CLOSINGBALANCEMONTH([BankBalance], Dim_Calendar[Date])`.
• Blank Closing Balance on Incomplete Months:
  Root Cause: If current month has no transactions on the 31st, `CLOSINGBALANCEMONTH` can return blank.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `CALCULATE([Balance], LASTNONBLANK(Dim_Calendar[Date], [Balance]))` to find the true last active day.
• Confusing Opening Balance with Closing Balance of Prior Month:
  Root Cause: Opening balance of March is mathematically identical to closing balance of February.
  Diagnostic Fix: undefined

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Closing Month Balance (CLOSINGBALANCEMONTH([Balance], Dim_Calendar[Date])): Returns the ending balance on the last day of the month.
• Last Non-Blank Balance (CALCULATE([Balance], LASTNONBLANK(Calendar[Date], [Balance]))): Handles months where transactions ended before the 31st.
• Additive vs Semi-Additive (Additive = Sum across all dims; Semi-Additive = Sum across dims, Snapshot over time): The fundamental taxonomy of financial measures.
