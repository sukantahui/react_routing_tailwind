Topic: Modifying filters with ALL, ALLEXCEPT, ALLSELECTED, and REMOVEFILTERS
Module: 005_004_dax-formulas-mastery-and-time-intelligence
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Filter Modifiers: ALL(Table/Col) | ALLEXCEPT(Table, Col) | ALLSELECTED(Table/Col) | REMOVEFILTERS(Table/Col)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Controlling and removing filter context using ALL, ALLEXCEPT, ALLSELECTED, and REMOVEFILTERS: calculating % of Total, category contributions, and market share ratios

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
To calculate % of Total: Write `DIVIDE([TotalSales], CALCULATE([TotalSales], ALLSELECTED(Dim_Product)))`. Now when users slice by Region or Year, the percentages will beautifully sum to exactly 100% every time.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Using ALL() When ALLSELECTED() is Required: Using `ALL()` causes '% of Total' to divide by national sales even when the user explicitly filtered for 'Eastern Region', displaying 3.2% instead of 25.0%. -> Fix: Use `ALLSELECTED()` so percentages sum to 100% of the active slicer selection.
- Passing Table to ALL() Instead of Column: Writing `ALL(Fact_Sales)` wipes filters across all dimensions, which is much slower than stripping a single column `ALL(Dim_Product[Category])`. -> Fix: Target specific dimension columns: `ALL(Dim_Product[Category])`.
- Division by Zero on Empty Categories: Missing safe division wrap. -> Fix: Always wrap in `=DIVIDE(Numerator, Denominator, 0)`.
