Topic 4: Modifying filters with ALL, ALLEXCEPT, ALLSELECTED, and REMOVEFILTERS

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Modifying filters with ALL, ALLEXCEPT, ALLSELECTED, and REMOVEFILTERS is an essential discipline in enterprise business intelligence, quantitative finance, and advanced data modeling.
It provides the core protocols required to construct complex multi-dimensional financial measures, calculate period-over-period growth, and evaluate dynamic rankings.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Filter Modifiers: ALL(Table/Col) | ALLEXCEPT(Table, Col) | ALLSELECTED(Table/Col) | REMOVEFILTERS(Table/Col)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. ALL (Total Filter Removal):
   Ignores ALL filters on the specified table or column, returning the global grand total regardless of row headers or slicers.

2. REMOVEFILTERS (Clear Intent Modifier):
   Modern DAX alias for `ALL()` used inside `CALCULATE` to explicitly declare that filters are being stripped.

3. ALLSELECTED (Visual Slicer Total):
   Removes row/column grid filters but PRESERVES user slicer selections, essential for calculating accurate '% of Filtered Slicer Total'.

4. ALLEXCEPT (Preserve Specific Dimension):
   Removes all filters on a table EXCEPT for the specified column(s), perfect for computing '% of Department Total'.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Using ALL() When ALLSELECTED() is Required:
  Root Cause: Using `ALL()` causes '% of Total' to divide by national sales even when the user explicitly filtered for 'Eastern Region', displaying 3.2% instead of 25.0%.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `ALLSELECTED()` so percentages sum to 100% of the active slicer selection.
• Passing Table to ALL() Instead of Column:
  Root Cause: Writing `ALL(Fact_Sales)` wipes filters across all dimensions, which is much slower than stripping a single column `ALL(Dim_Product[Category])`.
  Diagnostic Fix: Target specific dimension columns: `ALL(Dim_Product[Category])`.
• Division by Zero on Empty Categories:
  Root Cause: Missing safe division wrap.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always wrap in `=DIVIDE(Numerator, Denominator, 0)`.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• % of Grand Total (DIVIDE([Sales], CALCULATE([Sales], ALL(Dim_Product)))): Standard formula for % of grand national total.
• % of Sliced Total (DIVIDE([Sales], CALCULATE([Sales], ALLSELECTED(Dim_Product)))): Percentages always sum to 100% within active slicers.
• % of Parent Category (DIVIDE([Sales], CALCULATE([Sales], ALLEXCEPT(Product, Product[Category])))): Subcategory % share within its parent category.
