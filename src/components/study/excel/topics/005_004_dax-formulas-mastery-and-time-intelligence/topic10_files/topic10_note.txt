KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Period Comparisons: CALCULATE([TotalSales], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Dim_Calendar[Date])) | YoY Growth: DIVIDE([Sales] - [SalesLY], [SalesLY])

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Building comparative Period-over-Period analytics using SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, DATEADD, and PARALLELPERIOD: calculating Prior Year Sales, YoY Growth %, and flexible N-period time shifting

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
To calculate YoY Growth in DAX: Step 1: `Sales_LY := CALCULATE([TotalSales], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Dim_Calendar[Date]))`. Step 2: `YoY_Growth := DIVIDE([TotalSales] - [Sales_LY], [Sales_LY])`. It dynamically adapts to days, months, or years.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Comparing Incomplete Months (Apples-to-Oranges): Comparing 15 days of current month against full 30 days of prior year month. → Fix: Ensure date range is synchronized to equivalent elapsed days using `DATESBETWEEN()`.
- Division by Zero in YoY Growth Measure: Prior year sales being zero returns `#DIV/0!`. → Fix: Always wrap in `DIVIDE([Sales] - [SalesLY], [SalesLY], 0)`.
- Passing Inactive Date Column into DATEADD: Passing non-continuous fact table dates. → Fix: Always pass the master `Dim_Calendar[Date]` column.
