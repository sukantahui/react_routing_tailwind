Topic 10: Period-over-Period comparisons: SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, DATEADD, and PARALLELPERIOD
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Period-over-Period comparisons: SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, DATEADD, and PARALLELPERIOD is an essential discipline in enterprise business intelligence, quantitative finance, and advanced data modeling.
It provides the core protocols required to construct complex multi-dimensional financial measures, calculate period-over-period growth, and evaluate dynamic rankings.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Period Comparisons: CALCULATE([TotalSales], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Dim_Calendar[Date])) | YoY Growth: DIVIDE([Sales] - [SalesLY], [SalesLY])

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR (Exact 1-Year Shift):
   Shifts the active date selection back by exactly 1 year. If viewing 'June 2026', it evaluates the measure over 'June 2025'.

2. DATEADD (Universal Time Shifter):
   The flexible time shifter: `DATEADD(Dim_Calendar[Date], -1, MONTH)` shifts by 1 month; `DATEADD(Dim_Calendar[Date], -1, QUARTER)` shifts by 1 quarter.

3. PARALLELPERIOD (Full Period Shifter):
   Shifts the entire period regardless of granular date filters (e.g. shifts whole year parallelly).

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Comparing Incomplete Months (Apples-to-Oranges):
  Root Cause: Comparing 15 days of current month against full 30 days of prior year month.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure date range is synchronized to equivalent elapsed days using `DATESBETWEEN()`.
• Division by Zero in YoY Growth Measure:
  Root Cause: Prior year sales being zero returns `#DIV/0!`.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always wrap in `DIVIDE([Sales] - [SalesLY], [SalesLY], 0)`.
• Passing Inactive Date Column into DATEADD:
  Root Cause: Passing non-continuous fact table dates.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always pass the master `Dim_Calendar[Date]` column.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Prior Year Sales (CALCULATE([Sales], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR(Dim_Calendar[Date]))): The standard DAX formula for Last Year revenue.
• Prior Month Sales (CALCULATE([Sales], DATEADD(Dim_Calendar[Date], -1, MONTH))): Shifts time window back by exactly 1 month.
• Safe YoY Growth % (DIVIDE([Sales] - [SalesLY], [SalesLY], 0)): Calculates YoY growth percentage with zero math errors.
