1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Moving averages and rolling totals: DATESINPERIOD and DATESBETWEEN is an essential discipline in enterprise business intelligence, quantitative finance, and advanced data modeling.
It provides the core protocols required to construct complex multi-dimensional financial measures, calculate period-over-period growth, and evaluate dynamic rankings.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Rolling Window: CALCULATE([Measure], DATESINPERIOD(Dim_Calendar[Date], MAX(Dim_Calendar[Date]), -3, MONTH)) | DATESBETWEEN

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. DATESINPERIOD Dynamic Window Engine:
   `DATESINPERIOD(DateCol, StartDate, NumberOfIntervals, Interval)`. Anchors on `MAX(Dim_Calendar[Date])` and looks backward by -3 MONTH or -12 MONTH.

2. Rolling 12-Month Total (R12 / TTM):
   Sums the trailing 12 months of performance, eliminating seasonal quarterly peaks and valleys for executive trend analysis.

3. 3-Month Moving Average (Smoothing):
   `DIVIDE(CALCULATE([TotalSales], DATESINPERIOD(Dim_Calendar[Date], MAX(Dim_Calendar[Date]), -3, MONTH)), 3)`.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Omitting MAX(Date) Anchor in DATESINPERIOD:
  Root Cause: Passing a static date instead of `MAX(Dim_Calendar[Date])` freezes the rolling window to a fixed historical date.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always anchor with `MAX(Dim_Calendar[Date])` or `LASTDATE(Dim_Calendar[Date])`.
• Distortion in Initial Historical Months (Ramp-Up Glitch):
  Root Cause: Calculating a 12-month rolling average in Month 2 averages only 2 months of data, distorting trend charts.
  Diagnostic Fix: Check if 12 full months of historical data exist before calculating.
• Using DATESBETWEEN with Hardcoded Dates:
  Root Cause: Hardcoding start/end dates prevents the rolling window from moving dynamically with the calendar.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `DATESINPERIOD` for dynamic relative rolling windows.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Rolling 12-Month Total (TTM) (CALCULATE([Sales], DATESINPERIOD(Calendar[Date], MAX(Calendar[Date]), -12, MONTH))): Trailing 12-month revenue sum.
• 3-Month Moving Average (DIVIDE(CALCULATE([Sales], DATESINPERIOD(Calendar[Date], MAX(Calendar[Date]), -3, MONTH)), 3)): Smooths short-term monthly volatility.
• Anchor on MAX(Date) (MAX(Dim_Calendar[Date])): Ensures the window slides dynamically with active pivot rows.
