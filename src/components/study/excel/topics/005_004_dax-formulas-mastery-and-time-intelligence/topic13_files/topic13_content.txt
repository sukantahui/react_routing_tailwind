1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Advanced ranking and top-performer DAX: RANKX and TOPN is an essential discipline in enterprise business intelligence, quantitative finance, and advanced data modeling.
It provides the core protocols required to construct complex multi-dimensional financial measures, calculate period-over-period growth, and evaluate dynamic rankings.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Ranking & Top N: RANKX(ALL(Dim_Product), [TotalSales], , DESC, Dense) | TOPN(10, Dim_Customer, [TotalSales], DESC)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. RANKX Architecture & Context:
   `RANKX(Table, Expression, [Value], [Order], [Ties])`. Table argument must typically be wrapped in `ALL()` or `ALLSELECTED()` to establish the full ranking population.

2. Dynamic Slicer Re-Ranking:
   Wrapping in `ALLSELECTED(Dim_Customer[Name])` re-ranks customers dynamically from 1 to N within the active regional slicer selection.

3. TOPN Virtual Table Constructor:
   `TOPN(10, Dim_Product, [TotalSales])` generates a temporary 10-row table of top-performing products, passed to `CALCULATE()` to compute 'Top 10 Sales Share'.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• The 'Every Row is Rank 1' Bug (The Classic RANKX Trap):
  Root Cause: Passing a table without `ALL()` (e.g. `RANKX(Dim_Product, [TotalSales])`) restricts the ranking population to the single row in the pivot, so every product ranks #1 against itself.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always wrap the ranking entity in `ALL()` or `ALLSELECTED()`: `RANKX(ALL(Dim_Product[Name]), [TotalSales])`.
• Ranking Columns with Identical Values (Ties):
  Root Cause: Multiple entities with identical sales receiving identical rank numbers.
  Diagnostic Fix: Configure the 5th parameter `[Ties]` as `Dense` or `Skip`, or add secondary decimal tie-breakers.
• Performance Lag on 500,000 Row RANKX:
  Root Cause: Running RANKX over massive fact tables.
  Diagnostic Fix: Run RANKX exclusively over Dimension tables (`Dim_Customer`, `Dim_Product`).

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• The ALL() Wrap Mandatory (RANKX(ALL(Dim[Name]), [Measure])): Guarantees proper evaluation across the entire population.
• Top 10 Filter Measure (IF([Rank] <= 10, [TotalSales], BLANK())): Filters PivotTable to show only the top 10 entities.
• TOPN Aggregation (CALCULATE([Sales], TOPN(5, ALL(Product), [Sales]))): Directly computes sales for top 5 products.
