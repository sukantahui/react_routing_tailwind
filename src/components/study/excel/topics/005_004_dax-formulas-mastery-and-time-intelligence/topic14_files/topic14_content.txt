5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Measure Inconsistency Across Reports:
  Root Cause: Different analysts writing conflicting definitions for 'Gross Margin' across departmental spreadsheets.
  Diagnostic Fix: Centralize all core definitions in the master Power Pivot Data Model Measure Suite.
• Hardcoding Tax Rates or Fixed Values in DAX:
  Root Cause: Typing `* 0.18` inside DAX formulas breaks when tax legislation changes.
  Diagnostic Fix: Store tax rates in Dimension tables and link dynamically via relationships.
• Failing to Port to Power BI:
  Root Cause: Thinking DAX learned in Excel cannot be used in Power BI.
  Diagnostic Fix: Every single DAX measure written in Excel Power Pivot ports 100% identically into Microsoft Power BI.
