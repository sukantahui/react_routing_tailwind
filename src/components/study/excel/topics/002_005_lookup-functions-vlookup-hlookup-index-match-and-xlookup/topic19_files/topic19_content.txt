1. ENTERPRISE LOOKUP HUB ARCHITECTURE
In modern financial modeling and ERP integrations, dynamic lookup hubs connect disparate transactional tables to centralized master dimensions:
- Primary Key Integrity: Enforcing 100% uniqueness to prevent ambiguous multi-match returns.
- Left-Lookup & Two-Way Matrix Resolution: Retrieving data seamlessly regardless of grid orientation.
- Multi-Condition Querying: Filtering by composite keys (e.g. Region + Segment + Fiscal Period).
- Performance Scalability: Avoiding whole-column references and leveraging structured tables.
