PRACTICAL LAB GOALS:
1. Construct a 4-Visual Executive Business Chart Pack:
   - Visual 1: Multi-Branch Target vs Actual Performance (Clustered Column Chart)
   - Visual 2: 12-Month Revenue & Margin Trajectory (Combo Line & Column Chart)
   - Visual 3: Cost Center Budget Allocation (Doughnut Chart with Max 5 Slices)
   - Visual 4: In-Cell Quarterly Micro-Trends (Line Sparklines)

2. Alignment & Design Standards:
   - Palette: Primary Dark Navy (#0F172A), Accent Cyan (#0284C7), Highlight Emerald (#10B981)
   - Zero Chart Junk: No 3D tilts, no gray plot backgrounds, direct data labeling
   - Layout: Uniform card sizing, magnetic grid alignment, dynamic linked chart titles
