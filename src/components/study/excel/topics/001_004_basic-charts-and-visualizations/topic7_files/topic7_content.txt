1. EXECUTIVE CHART PACK ARCHITECTURE
An executive chart pack combines four primary analytical perspectives:
- Categorical Variance (Column / Bar): Answers "Who are the top/bottom performers?"
- Temporal Trajectory (Line / Area): Answers "Where is the business heading over time?"
- Structural Composition (Doughnut / Treemap): Answers "How is the pie sliced?"
- Granular Micro-Trends (Sparklines): Answers "What is the intra-year momentum?"

2. STEP-BY-STEP WORKSHOP PROTOCOL
Step 1: Data Preparation & Hygiene (Clean Table, No Blanks, Explicit Headers)
Step 2: Chart Creation & Series Selection (Alt+F1, Select Range)
Step 3: Axis & Legend Configuration (Zero Baseline, Secondary Axis, Remove Redundant Keys)
Step 4: Color Styling & Decluttering (Monochrome Base, Alert Color Accents, Direct Labels)
Step 5: Layout Assembly & Slicer Integration (Snap to Grid with Alt, Grouping Objects)
