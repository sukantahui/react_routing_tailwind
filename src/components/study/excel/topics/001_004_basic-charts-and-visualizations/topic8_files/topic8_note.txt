ASSESSMENT RUBRIC & DOMAINS:
1. Chart Typology & Selection (25 pts):
   - Choosing between Column, Bar, Line, Area, Pie, Doughnut, and Scatter.
2. Dual-Scale & Combo Charting (25 pts):
   - Secondary vertical axis assignment, scale harmonization, and zero baselines.
3. In-Cell Micro Visualization (20 pts):
   - Sparklines (Line, Column, Win/Loss), high/low markers, and tabular integration.
4. Information Design & Decluttering (30 pts):
   - Maximizing data-ink ratio, eliminating 3D distortion, direct data labeling, and accessibility.
