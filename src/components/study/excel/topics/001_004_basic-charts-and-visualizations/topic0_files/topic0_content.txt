Title: Chart Selection Guide: Matching Business Data Stories to Optimal Chart Types
Level: Beginner | Bloom's Level 2 & 3: Understand & Apply

Overview:
Master the art and science of data visualization: Select the perfect chart type based on your analytical story (Comparison, Time-Series Trend, Part-to-Whole Composition, or Distribution). Avoid chart junk, 3D distortions, and misleading dual-axes.

Technical Anatomy:
Signature: =CHART_RECOMMENDATION(DataType, AnalyticalGoal)
Return Type: Optimized Visual Encoding Specification

Real-World Business Scenarios:
[Barrackpore Multi-Branch Sales Comparison Selection]
Context: Choosing between a 3D Pie chart vs Horizontal Bar chart for 8 regional branches.
Applied: Horizontal Bar Chart sorted descending by sales
Result: Instant visual ranking across all 8 branches.

[Kolkata Corporate 3-Year Monthly Revenue Trend Visualization]
Context: Displaying 36 consecutive months of revenue to highlight seasonal spikes.
Applied: 2D Line Chart with Highlighted Markers
Result: Executive team identifies Q3 seasonal revenue acceleration.

[Shyamnagar Factory Operating Expense Budget Breakdown]
Context: Showing 4 cost components (Raw Materials 55%, Labor 25%, Power 12%, Overhead 8%).
Applied: Doughnut Chart (4 slices total)
Result: Clean board-ready budget composition visual.

[Ichapur Plant Tensile Strength Quality Distribution]
Context: Analyzing product variation across 500 manufactured steel batches.
Applied: Statistical Histogram Chart
Result: Normal distribution bell curve verified for ISO audit.
