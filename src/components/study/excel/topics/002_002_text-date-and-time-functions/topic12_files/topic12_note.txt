KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Shift & Overtime Engine: =MOD(Out - In, 1) * 24 | =MAX(0, Hours - 8) * OvertimeRate

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Building an enterprise employee shift duration, meal break deduction, and statutory overtime payroll calculation engine

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Building an error-free payroll timesheet engine is the ultimate test of Excel time arithmetic. Master MIN, MAX, and MOD to calculate wages with zero discrepancies.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Missing Break Subtraction: Failing to deduct mandatory 30-minute unpaid lunch breaks inflates payroll liabilities by 6.25%. -> Fix: Embed automatic break deduction formulas into gross hour calculations.
- Overtime Rate Double Multiplier Bug: Applying 1.5x overtime multiplier directly to total hours rather than only hours exceeding 8. -> Fix: Use `=(RegularHours * BaseRate) + (OvertimeHours * BaseRate * 1.5)`.
- Decimal vs Clock Minute Confusion: Treating '8.30' as 8 hours 30 minutes in decimal math (0.30 of an hour is 18 minutes, not 30). -> Fix: Always convert time using serial fraction math `* 24` so 8:30 displays as 8.50 decimal hours.
