2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Formula Code: Shift & Overtime Engine: =MOD(Out - In, 1) * 24 | =MAX(0, Hours - 8) * OvertimeRate

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Shift Gross Duration Calculation:
   Calculates total elapsed shift hours crossing midnight using `=MOD(PunchOut - PunchIn, 1) * 24`.

2. Automatic Meal Break Deduction:
   Applies conditional break deductions (e.g. `IF(GrossHours > 5, GrossHours - 0.5, GrossHours)`).

3. Statutory Overtime Tiering:
   Separates regular hours (`MIN(8, NetHours)`) from overtime hours (`MAX(0, NetHours - 8)`) to compute double-time wage disbursements.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Missing Break Subtraction:
  Root Cause: Failing to deduct mandatory 30-minute unpaid lunch breaks inflates payroll liabilities by 6.25%.
  Diagnostic Fix: Embed automatic break deduction formulas into gross hour calculations.
• Overtime Rate Double Multiplier Bug:
  Root Cause: Applying 1.5x overtime multiplier directly to total hours rather than only hours exceeding 8.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `=(RegularHours * BaseRate) + (OvertimeHours * BaseRate * 1.5)`.
• Decimal vs Clock Minute Confusion:
  Root Cause: Treating '8.30' as 8 hours 30 minutes in decimal math (0.30 of an hour is 18 minutes, not 30).
  Diagnostic Fix: Always convert time using serial fraction math `* 24` so 8:30 displays as 8.50 decimal hours.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Regular Hours Formula (=MIN(8, TotalHours)): Caps standard working shift hours at exactly 8 hours.
• Overtime Hours Formula (=MAX(0, TotalHours - 8)): Isolates only the overtime hours exceeding standard shift threshold.
• Total Wage Formula (=(Reg * Rate) + (OT * Rate * 1.5)): Standard statutory overtime pay calculation formula.
