1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Date differences and milestone calculations: DATEDIF and YEARFRAC is an essential discipline in corporate spreadsheet development and ETL workflows.
It provides the core formulas required to sanitize, manipulate, and calculate text, dates, and times.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Formula Code: =DATEDIF(start_date, end_date, unit) | =YEARFRAC(start_date, end_date, [basis])

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. The Undocumented DATEDIF Unit Codes:
   "Y" (full years), "M" (full months), "D" (days), "YM" (remaining months excluding years), "MD" (remaining days excluding months).

2. Composite Tenure Construction:
   Combines unit codes to build human-readable tenures: `=DATEDIF(A2, B2, "Y") & " Yrs, " & DATEDIF(A2, B2, "YM") & " Mos, " & DATEDIF(A2, B2, "MD") & " Days"`.

3. Financial Day Count Basis (YEARFRAC):
   YEARFRAC() returns fractional year duration using actual/actual (basis 1), 30/360 (basis 0), or actual/365 (basis 3) for bond interest accruals.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• DATEDIF #NUM! on Inverted Dates:
  Root Cause: Passing a start_date that is later than end_date triggers a #NUM! error.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure start_date <= end_date or wrap in IF(Start > End, DATEDIF(End, Start, ...), ...).
• The 'MD' Unit Known Bug:
  Root Cause: The 'MD' unit in DATEDIF can yield negative or inaccurate day counts in certain leap-year month boundaries.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `=end_date - EDATE(start_date, DATEDIF(start_date, end_date, "M"))` for bulletproof day accuracy.
• YEARFRAC Day Count Basis Confusion:
  Root Cause: Using default US 30/360 (basis 0) instead of Actual/Actual (basis 1) causes interest calculation discrepancies in banking audits.
  Diagnostic Fix: Verify the statutory financial contract convention before setting the basis parameter.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Complete Tenure String (DATEDIF Y & YM & MD): The standard formula for employee service tenure cards.
• Exact Age in Years (=DATEDIF(DOB, TODAY(), "Y")): Calculates completed age without rounding up premature birthdays.
• Banking Year Fraction (=YEARFRAC(A2, B2, 1)): Calculates precise financial year fractions under Actual/Actual day counts.
