KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
=TIME(hour, minute, second) | =HOUR(serial_time) | =MINUTE(serial_time) | =SECOND(serial_time) | =NOW()

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Time calculations, fractional day serial math (1 hour = 1/24), clock arithmetic, and elapsed duration formatting

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
The =MOD(End - Start, 1) trick is one of the most famous and useful formulas in Excel history. Memorize it for all shift, payroll, and timesheet models.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Overnight Shift Negative Result: Subtracting StartTime (22:00) from EndTime (06:00) yields negative number (-0.667) displaying ######. → Fix: Use `=MOD(EndTime - StartTime, 1)` to handle midnight boundary crossing seamlessly.
- Multiplying Time Directly by Wage Rate: Calculating `=TimeCell * HourlyRate` without multiplying by 24 results in 1/24th of the true pay amount. → Fix: Always multiply by 24: `=(EndTime - StartTime) * 24 * HourlyWage`.
- 24-Hour Rollover in Sums: Summing 30 hours of work using 'hh:mm' displays as '06:00' due to modulo 24 reset. → Fix: Apply custom number format `[h]:mm` to display cumulative hours over 24.
