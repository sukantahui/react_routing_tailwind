1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Time calculations: TIME, HOUR, MINUTE, SECOND, NOW, and handling elapsed hours (>24h) is an essential discipline in corporate spreadsheet development and ETL workflows.
It provides the core formulas required to sanitize, manipulate, and calculate text, dates, and times.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Formula Code: =TIME(hour, minute, second) | =HOUR(serial_time) | =MINUTE(serial_time) | =SECOND(serial_time) | =NOW()

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Fractional Day Storage Model:
   Time is stored as a decimal fraction of a 24-hour day (e.g. 06:00 AM = 0.25, 12:00 PM = 0.5, 06:00 PM = 0.75).

2. Clock Arithmetic & Overnight Shifts:
   Subtracting times across midnight (e.g. 10 PM to 6 AM) yields negative fractions. Solved cleanly with `=MOD(End - Start, 1)`.

3. Converting Time Serials to Decimal Hours:
   Multiply time serial by 24 to convert to decimal hours for billing (e.g. 01:30 * 24 = 1.50 billable hours).

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Overnight Shift Negative Result:
  Root Cause: Subtracting StartTime (22:00) from EndTime (06:00) yields negative number (-0.667) displaying ######.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `=MOD(EndTime - StartTime, 1)` to handle midnight boundary crossing seamlessly.
• Multiplying Time Directly by Wage Rate:
  Root Cause: Calculating `=TimeCell * HourlyRate` without multiplying by 24 results in 1/24th of the true pay amount.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always multiply by 24: `=(EndTime - StartTime) * 24 * HourlyWage`.
• 24-Hour Rollover in Sums:
  Root Cause: Summing 30 hours of work using 'hh:mm' displays as '06:00' due to modulo 24 reset.
  Diagnostic Fix: Apply custom number format `[h]:mm` to display cumulative hours over 24.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Overnight Shift Formula (=MOD(End - Start, 1)): Universal formula for shift durations crossing midnight.
• Decimal Hours Conversion (=(End - Start) * 24): Converts time serial to standard decimal hours for hourly billing.
• Current Timestamp Shortcut (Ctrl + Shift + ;): Inserts static unformatted current clock time.
