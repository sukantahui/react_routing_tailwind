KEY CONCEPTS:
1. CONVERT(number, from_unit, to_unit):
   - Mass: "g", "kg", "lbm", "ozm", "ton"
   - Distance: "m", "km", "in", "ft", "yd", "mi", "Nmi"
   - Thermodynamics: "C", "F", "K", "Rank", "Reau"
   - Time: "yr", "day", "hr", "mn", "sec"
   - Pressure: "Pa", "atm", "bar", "mmHg", "psi"
   - Energy: "J", "e", "c", "cal", "BTU", "Wh", "HPh"
   - Power: "W", "HP", "PS"

2. BITWISE LOGIC ENGINE (48-bit Precision):
   - BITAND(n1, n2): Bitwise AND masking
   - BITOR(n1, n2): Bitwise OR combination
   - BITXOR(n1, n2): Bitwise XOR difference/toggle
   - BITLSHIFT(n, shift): Multiplies by 2^shift
   - BITRSHIFT(n, shift): Divides by 2^shift

3. BASE CONVERSIONS:
   - BIN2DEC, DEC2BIN, DEC2HEX, HEX2DEC, HEX2BIN, OCT2BIN
