COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Monolithic 500-Line Single Macro (Anti-Pattern): Writing a single giant unmaintainable 500-line Sub procedure. → Fix: Decompose into clean, modular single-responsibility Sub procedures called from a Master Controller macro.
- Hardcoding File Paths or Sheet Indices: Hardcoded paths like `C:\Users\swadeep\` breaking on other team members' laptops. → Fix: Use relative paths: `ThisWorkbook.Path & "\Reports\"`.
- Unprotected Master Template Overwrites: Macro overwriting the master template file instead of saving a timestamped copy. → Fix: Save generated output as a new timestamped workbook: `"Report_" & Format(Now, "yyyymmdd_hhnn") & ".xlsx"`.
