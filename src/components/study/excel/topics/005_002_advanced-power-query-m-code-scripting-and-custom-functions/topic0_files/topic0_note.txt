1. THE M PROGRAMMING MODEL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Pure Functional & Declarative: Transformations produce new immutable structures.
• Case Sensitivity: Everything is case-sensitive (`Table.SelectRows` != `table.selectrows`).
• Let...In Structure:
    let
      Step_1 = SourceExpression,
      Step_2 = TransformationFunction(Step_1),
      Step_3 = FilterFunction(Step_2)
    in
      Step_3
• Lazy Evaluation (Call-by-Need): Steps are evaluated only if needed by the final `in` return.

2. STEP IDENTIFIERS & ACCESSORS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Quoted Identifiers: `#"Step With Spaces"` used when names contain spaces or special chars.
• Column Accessor (List): `Source[ColumnName]`
• Row Accessor (Record): `Source{0}` (0-based indexing)
• Cell Accessor (Scalar): `Source{0}[ColumnName]`
• Syntactic Sugar `each`: `each [Amt] * 1.18` is equivalent to `(_) => _[Amt] * 1.18`.

3. CLASSROOM RULES & ADVICE FROM SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Never fear the Advanced Editor—that is where real enterprise ETL power resides!
• Always end lines in the `let` block with a comma, except the final step before `in`.
• Keep step variable names descriptive and maintain clean chaining across transformation steps.
