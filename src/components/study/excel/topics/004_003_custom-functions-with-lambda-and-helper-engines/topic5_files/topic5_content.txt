1. FORMULA ANATOMY:
=LET(
  var1, expr1,
  var2, expr2,
  ...
  varN, exprN,
  final_calculation
)

2. PRACTICAL FINANCIAL PATTERNS:
- Loan EMI & Payment Spills:
  =LET(P, D5, r, E5/12, n, F5, emi, P*r*(1+r)^n/((1+r)^n-1), HSTACK(emi, emi*n, (emi*n)-P))

- Tax Pipeline:
  =LET(sub, B5:B10*C5:C10, cgst, sub*0.09, sgst, sub*0.09, gross, sub+cgst+sgst, HSTACK(sub, cgst, sgst, gross))

- Lookups with Sanitization:
  =LET(raw, XLOOKUP(id, IDs, Data), IF(ISBLANK(raw), "N/A", raw))
