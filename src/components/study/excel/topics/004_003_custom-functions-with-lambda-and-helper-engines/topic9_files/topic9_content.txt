Module: 004_003_custom-functions-with-lambda-and-helper-engines
Topic 9: Generating dynamic procedural grids with MAKEARRAY
================================================================================

1. FORMULA SIGNATURE:
=MAKEARRAY(rows, cols, LAMBDA(r, c, calculation))

2. KEY MATHEMATICAL PATTERNS:
- Multiplication Matrix:
  =MAKEARRAY(5, 5, LAMBDA(r, c, r * c))

- Coordinate Grid:
  =MAKEARRAY(5, 4, LAMBDA(r, c, "R" & r & "C" & c))

- Checkerboard / Alternating Flags:
  =MAKEARRAY(8, 8, LAMBDA(r, c, MOD(r + c, 2)))

- Identity Matrix:
  =MAKEARRAY(5, 5, LAMBDA(r, c, IF(r = c, 1, 0)))

- Sensitivity Grid:
  =MAKEARRAY(5, 4, LAMBDA(r, c, PMT(INDEX(E5:E8, r)/12, INDEX(F4:I4, c), -1000000)))
