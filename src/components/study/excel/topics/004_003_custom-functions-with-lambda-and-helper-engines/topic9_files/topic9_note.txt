1. WHAT IS THE MAKEARRAY HELPER FUNCTION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax: =MAKEARRAY(rows, cols, lambda)

Core Mechanics:
• Generates an (M x N) procedural matrix from scratch in RAM.
• LAMBDA must declare strictly 2 parameters: LAMBDA(r, c, calculation).
• Indices r and c are 1-based: r &in; [1..rows], c &in; [1..cols].

2. REAL-WORLD CORPORATE USE CASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Multiplication Tables: =MAKEARRAY(10, 10, LAMBDA(r, c, r * c))
• Sensitivity Analysis: =MAKEARRAY(10, 5, LAMBDA(r, c, PMT(INDEX(Rates,c)/12, r*12, -Loan)))
• Identity Matrix: =MAKEARRAY(N, N, LAMBDA(r, c, IF(r=c, 1, 0)))
• Procedural Calendar: =MAKEARRAY(5, 7, LAMBDA(r, c, LET(d, (r-1)*7+c, IF(d<=31, d, ""))))

3. CLASSROOM RULES & ADVICE FROM SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Rows and cols must be positive integers &ge; 1 (otherwise #CALC!).
• Combine with LET for multi-stage coordinate math and formatting!
