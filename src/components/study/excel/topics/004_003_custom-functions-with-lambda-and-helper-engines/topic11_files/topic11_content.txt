1. MATHEMATICAL FOLDING MODEL:
- Input Array: [x1, x2, x3, ... xN]
- Initial Seed: s0
- Step-by-step Accumulation:
    s1 = f(s0, x1)
    s2 = f(s1, x2)
    ...
    sN = f(s_{N-1}, xN)
- Final Return: sN (Scalar or Unified Composite Grid)

2. CORE FORMULA PATTERNS:
- Compounded Return:
  =REDUCE(1, E5:E8, LAMBDA(acc, r, acc * (1 + r)))

- Multi-Token Sanitization:
  =REDUCE(A2, {"-", ".", "/", " "}, LAMBDA(t, c, SUBSTITUTE(t, c, "")))

- Dynamic Multi-Sheet Stacking:
  =DROP(REDUCE("", {"Barrackpore", "Shyamnagar"}, LAMBDA(acc, s, VSTACK(acc, INDIRECT(s & "!A2:D20")))), 1)
