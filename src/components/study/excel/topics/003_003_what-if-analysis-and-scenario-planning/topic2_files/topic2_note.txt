THE 6 BUSINESS VARIATIONS:
1. Loan Interest Rate Sensitivity: Testing 7% to 12% on EMI, Total Interest, and Outflow.
2. Product Pricing Sensitivity: Testing Unit Price steps on Gross Revenue and Operating Margin.
3. Break-Even Volume Modeling: Testing Sales Volume on Contribution Margin & EBIT.
4. Capital Budgeting Discount Rate Sensitivity: Testing Cost of Capital (WACC) on Project NPV.
5. Commercial Real Estate Occupancy Yield: Testing Occupancy % on Net Operating Income (NOI).
6. Multi-Output FX Rate Matrix: Testing USD/INR variations across Revenue, Cost, and Forex Gain/Loss.
