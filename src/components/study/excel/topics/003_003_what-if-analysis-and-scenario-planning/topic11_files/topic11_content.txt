1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Solver Sensitivity and Answer reports for management decision review is an essential discipline in corporate decision modeling, capital allocation, and risk management.
It provides the core protocols required to stress-test financial projections, solve backwards for target goals, and mathematically optimize resource utilization.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Solver Results Dialog: Reports: Answer Report | Sensitivity Report (Shadow Prices / Allowable Increase) | Limits Report

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Answer Report (Executive Status):
   Documents original vs final values for Objective and Variables, and classifies each constraint as 'Binding' (Zero Slack) or 'Not Binding' (Surplus Slack).

2. Shadow Price (Dual Value):
   The marginal increase in Objective Profit gained by increasing a binding resource constraint by ONE additional unit (e.g. Shadow Price of ₹450/hr on Machine Hours).

3. Allowable Increase / Decrease Intervals:
   The sensitivity range over which the calculated Shadow Price remains valid without altering the optimal basis.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Sensitivity Report Disabled on Integer Models:
  Root Cause: Simplex LP cannot generate Sensitivity Reports (Shadow Prices) if any variable has an `int` or `bin` constraint.
  Diagnostic Fix: Solve the linear relaxation (remove integer constraints) to inspect shadow prices.
• Applying Shadow Price Beyond Allowable Increase:
  Root Cause: Assuming you can buy 10,000 extra machine hours at the same shadow price when Allowable Increase is only 120 hours.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always verify that requested resource expansions fall within the 'Allowable Increase' window.
• Misinterpreting Reduced Cost:
  Root Cause: Thinking Reduced Cost is an accounting expense; it actually represents how much the profit coefficient of an unproduced item must improve before it becomes profitable to manufacture.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use Reduced Cost to evaluate product redesign pricing targets.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Generate Reports (Select Answer & Sensitivity in Solver Results dialog): Creates dedicated audit report worksheets automatically.
• Shadow Price Rule (Marginal Value of 1 extra unit of resource): Tells management the exact maximum price to pay for overtime or extra materials.
• Binding = Bottleneck (Slack = 0): Identifies the exact operational constraints capping company profits.
