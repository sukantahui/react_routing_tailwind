1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Financial Amortization & Investment Valuation Lab (XNPV, XIRR) is an essential evaluation milestone in spreadsheet mastery, data modeling rigor, and executive reporting.
It measures candidate proficiency in applying formulas, structuring data, diagnosing errors, and building commercial-grade solutions.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Investment Valuation: =XNPV(DiscountRate, CashFlows, Dates) | =XIRR(CashFlows, Dates) | Loan Amortization: =PMT(r, n, pv) | =PPMT(...) | =IPMT(...)

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Exact-Day Discounting (XNPV / XIRR):
   Accounts for real-world irregular cash flow timing down to the exact day, eliminating standard NPV/IRR equal-interval distortion.

2. Dynamic Debt Amortization Modeling:
   Separates monthly loan repayments into interest expense (`IPMT`) and principal reduction (`PPMT`) across a 360-month loan schedule.

3. WACC & Capital Hurdle Rate Benchmarking:
   Evaluates project viability: Accept project if `XNPV > 0` and `XIRR > WACC` (Weighted Average Cost of Capital).

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Using Standard NPV with Irregular Dates:
  Root Cause: Standard `=NPV()` assumes all cash flows occur at strictly equal 1-year intervals, producing invalid valuations.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always use `=XNPV(rate, cash_flows, dates)` for real-world projects.
• Omitting Negative Sign on Initial Investment Outflow:
  Root Cause: Passing positive number for Year 0 investment in XIRR causes `#NUM!` error.
  Diagnostic Fix: Initial capital outflow MUST be negative: `-50000000`.
• Annual vs Monthly Interest Rate Mismatch in PMT:
  Root Cause: Passing 12% annual rate into monthly loan formula without dividing by 12.
  Diagnostic Fix: Divide annual rate by 12 and multiply years by 12: `=PMT(Rate/12, Years*12, -LoanAmount)`.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Always Use XNPV / XIRR (=XNPV(Rate, CashFlows, Dates)): The institutional standard for corporate capital budgeting.
• First Cash Flow Negative (Year 0 = -Investment): Mandatory requirement for XIRR calculation.
• Principal + Interest = Total Payment (PPMT + IPMT = PMT): The mathematical identity of debt amortization.
