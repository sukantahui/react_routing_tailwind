3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS

3. Tamper-Proof Sheet Protection Architecture:
   Unlocking data entry cells (Format Cells > Protection > Uncheck Locked) while keeping master formula cells locked and protected with passwords.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Protecting Sheet Without Unlocking Input Cells First:
  Root Cause: Protecting sheet locks ALL cells by default, preventing users from typing anywhere.
  Diagnostic Fix: Highlight input cells > Format Cells (Ctrl+1) > Protection > UNCHECK 'Locked' > Protect Sheet.
• Spaces in Named Ranges for Cascading Dropdowns:
  Root Cause: `INDIRECT("West Bengal")` fails because named ranges cannot contain spaces.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use `SUBSTITUTE`: `=INDIRECT(SUBSTITUTE(A2, " ", "_"))`.
• Allowing Users to Paste Over Validation Rules:
  Root Cause: Standard copy-paste overwrites cell validation rules.
  Diagnostic Fix: Protect sheet to disable formatting changes.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Unlock Input Cells First (Ctrl + 1 > Protection > Uncheck Locked): Essential step before protecting sheet.
• Cascading Dropdown (=INDIRECT(SUBSTITUTE(A2, " ", "_"))): Creates dynamic secondary dropdown lists.
• Custom Validation Formula (Data Validation > Allow: Custom): Enforces complex business rules on cell inputs.
