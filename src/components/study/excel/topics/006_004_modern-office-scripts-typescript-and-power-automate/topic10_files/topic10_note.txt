================================================================================
Topic: Processing incoming CSV attachments from emails and writing records to Excel tables in SharePoint/OneDrive
Module: 006_004_modern-office-scripts-typescript-and-power-automate
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Email Attachment Pipeline: Trigger (When email arrives with attachment) → For each attachment → Filter .csv/.xlsx → Pass content to Office Script → Ingest Rows to Table

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Automating the extraction and ingestion of email attachments: intercepting incoming supplier CSV/Excel files from Outlook, saving them to SharePoint, and parsing records into master tables via Office Scripts

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Imagine never having to download an email attachment again! Have your vendors email their CSV files to a dedicated address. Power Automate grabs the attachment, passes it to Office Scripts, and posts the data to your master spreadsheet automatically.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Base64 Encoding Confusion on File Content: Passing raw base64 binary strings instead of decoded text to CSV parsing scripts. → Fix: Use `base64ToString()` expression in Power Automate before passing CSV string to script.
- Processing Non-Target Attachments (Signatures / Logos): Looping through email signature `.png` logos and failing to parse as CSV. → Fix: Add condition: `If attachment name ends with .csv`.
- Corrupted CSV Due to Commas Inside Quoted Fields: Simple `row.split(",")` breaking on addresses like `"Flat 4B, Park Street"`. → Fix: Use a robust regex or state-machine CSV parser inside the TypeScript script.
