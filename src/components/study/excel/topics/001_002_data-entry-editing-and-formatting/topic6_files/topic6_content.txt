MODULE 1.2 - TOPIC 6
Title: Alignment, Text Wrapping, Center Across Selection vs Merge, and Executive Cell Styles
Level: Beginner | Bloom's Level 2 & 3: Understand & Apply

Overview:
Master executive spreadsheet formatting: Text Wrapping (Alt+H+W), Vertical/Horizontal Alignment, Text Orientation, Indentation for financial hierarchy, why 'Center Across Selection' is 10x superior to dangerous 'Merge &amp; Center', and corporate Cell Styles.

Technical Anatomy:
Signature: =CELL("alignment", A1)
Return Type: Visual Layout &amp; Structural Alignment

Real-World Business Scenarios:
[Kolkata Corporate Financial Balance Sheet Title Centering]
Context: Centering 'CODER & ACCOTAX ANNUAL BALANCE SHEET' across columns A to G without breaking column sorting.
Applied: Format Cells (Ctrl+1) → Alignment → Horizontal: Center Across Selection
Result: Executive centered banner with zero structural grid damage.

[Barrackpore Student Ledger Hierarchical Indentation]
Context: Structuring chart of accounts with Parent Accounts and indented Sub-Ledgers.
Applied: Home Tab → Increase Indent (Alt + H + 6)
Result: Clean audited financial statement hierarchy without messy leading spaces.

[Shyamnagar Multi-Column Header Text Wrapping]
Context: Fitting long descriptive headers ('Quarterly Gross Revenue After Trade Discount') without making columns 500 pixels wide.
Applied: Alt + H + W (Wrap Text)
Result: Compact, print-friendly multi-line column header layout.

[Ichapur Engineering Quality Inspection Rotated Headers]
Context: Angling test parameter column headers by 45 degrees to save horizontal printable width.
Applied: Format Cells → Alignment → Orientation: 45 Degrees
Result: Compact engineering quality matrix fitting perfectly on A4 printable width.
