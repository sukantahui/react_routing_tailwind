TOPIC 6: ALIGNMENT, TEXT WRAPPING, CENTER ACROSS SELECTION VS MERGE, AND EXECUTIVE CELL STYLES
Instructor: Sukanta Hui | Coder & AccoTax

Key Takeaways:
- Merge & Center physically fuses multiple cells into a single address (top-left cell), destroying all other cell addresses in that block.
- Merged cells break VBA automation, disable column sorting, break array formulas, and prevent single-column selection (Ctrl+Space).
- 'Center Across Selection' (Format Cells -> Alignment -> Horizontal: Center Across Selection) visually achieves the exact same title centering while keeping every column 100% intact!

Teacher Note:
Never, ever use Merge & Center! It breaks sorting, breaks VBA macros, and breaks array formulas. Always press Ctrl+1, go to Alignment, and select 'Center Across Selection'. Your spreadsheets will look identical but remain 100% robust and sortable!
