2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Multi-Condition Aggregation: =SUMIFS(SumRng, CritRng1, Crit1, CritRng2, Crit2) | =COUNTIFS(...) | =AVERAGEIFS(...) | =MEDIAN(IF(...))

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Multi-Dimensional SUMIFS Architecture:
   Syntax: `=SUMIFS(SumRange, CriteriaRange1, Criteria1, CriteriaRange2, Criteria2)`. Aggregates data intersecting multiple categorical and numerical conditions.

2. Dynamic Date Window Summation:
   Filtering transactions within specific date ranges: `CriteriaRange, ">="&StartDate, CriteriaRange, "<="&EndDate`.

3. Wildcard Text Matching (*, ?):
   Aggregating all product models containing 'Pro': `CritRange, "*Pro*"`.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Confusing SUMIF vs SUMIFS Argument Order:
  Root Cause: In `SUMIF`, the sum range is LAST; in `SUMIFS`, the sum range is FIRST! Mixing them causes formula errors.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always use `SUMIFS` (sum range FIRST) even for single-condition queries for consistency.
• Mismatched Range Dimensions in SUMIFS:
  Root Cause: Sum range `B2:B100` with criteria range `A2:A150` throws `#VALUE!` error.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure all range arguments have identical row and column dimensions.
• Forgetting Ampersand in Dynamic Comparison Operators:
  Root Cause: Writing `">=A1"` searches for the literal text '>A1' instead of comparing against cell value.
  Diagnostic Fix: Concatenate operator with cell reference: `">=" & A1`.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Always Use SUMIFS (=SUMIFS(SumRng, CritRng1, Crit1)): Consistent argument order across all conditional queries.
• Dynamic Cell Reference (">=" & StartDateCell): Mandatory concatenation syntax for cell dates/numbers.
• Wildcard Matching ("*" & SearchText & "*"): Matches partial text anywhere within cells.
