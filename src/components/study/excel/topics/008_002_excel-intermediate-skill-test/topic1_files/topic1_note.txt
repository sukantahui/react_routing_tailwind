KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Text/Date/Logic: =IFS(B2>1000000, "Tier 1", B2>500000, "Tier 2", TRUE, "Standard") | Date: =EDATE(A2, 3) | Text: =TEXTJOIN(", ", TRUE, A2:C2)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Testing complex logical branching and string/date manipulation: nested IFS, multi-condition AND/OR logic, TEXTJOIN, TEXTSPLIT, EDATE, EOMONTH, and DATEDIF calculations

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Switch from `IF` to `IFS`! It is so much cleaner. Just write `=IFS(Score>=90, "A", Score>=80, "B", TRUE, "C")`. Always put `TRUE` at the end as your safety net.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Reversing Inequality Order in IFS Statements: Placing `B2 > 100000` before `B2 > 1000000` causes all high numbers to trigger the smaller tier first. -> Fix: Order inequalities strictly from most restrictive (highest) to least restrictive.
- Omitting Final 'TRUE, Default' Catch-All in IFS: IFS returning `#N/A` when no condition matches. -> Fix: Always end IFS formulas with `TRUE, "DefaultValue"`.
- Using Hardcoded Date Strings ('15/08/2026') in Formulas: Regional date format misinterpretation causing formula errors. -> Fix: Use the `DATE(year, month, day)` function for reliable date construction.
