TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Congratulations on completing the Conditional Logic Module! Combining Boolean logic, multi-tier lookups, and financial guardrails allows you to build industrial-strength commercial models.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Paying Commission on Uncollected Revenue: Calculating commission on invoiced revenue without checking customer payment status. → Fix: Include =IF(PaymentStatus="CLEARED", Sales * Rate, 0) condition.
- Uncapped Commission Liability: Failing to implement upper bounds on incentive formulas, exposing company to runaway bonus payouts. → Fix: Use `=MIN(MaxCap, CalculatedCommission)` to enforce corporate bonus ceilings.
- Hardcoded Commission Rates: Hardcoding 5%, 8%, 12% in formulas requires updating 50,000 cells when sales plans change. → Fix: Store all commission tiers and bonus multipliers in a central Parameters table.
