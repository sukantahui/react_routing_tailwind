KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Progressive Income Tax Slabs: =SUMPRODUCT(--(Income > Slabs), Income - Slabs, TaxRatesDiff)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Calculating multi-tier progressive income tax liabilities using nested logical branching and the elegant SUMPRODUCT differential rate method

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
The SUMPRODUCT progressive tax formula is one of the most powerful financial modeling techniques ever created. Memorize it and replace those messy 7-level nested IF formulas forever.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Taxing Entire Income at Highest Bracket Rate: Applying the 30% rate to the whole ₹15 Lakhs income instead of progressive brackets overcharges tax by over ₹1.5 Lakhs. -> Fix: Always apply bracket-by-bracket differential rates.
- Forgetting the 4% Education Cess: Omitting statutory 4% Health & Education Cess results in statutory tax compliance failure. -> Fix: Multiply final computed tax by 1.04 to include mandatory cess.
- Nested IF Formula Maintenance Collapse: Writing a 7-level nested IF for tax slabs makes updating annual budget rates difficult. -> Fix: Use the SUMPRODUCT differential rate formula against a 3-column slab table.
