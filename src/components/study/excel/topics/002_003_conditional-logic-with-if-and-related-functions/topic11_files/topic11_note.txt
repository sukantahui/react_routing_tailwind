KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Automated Student Academic Grade & Honors Generator: IFS + AND + OR + VLOOKUP

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Building a production-grade automated student grading engine with distinctions, pass/fail thresholds, attendance criteria, and honors classification

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
A robust grading engine must test both aggregate performance and individual subject minimums. Master multi-criteria hierarchical checks to eliminate academic audit errors.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Overlooking Mandatory Minimum Subject Pass Rules: Calculating only overall average allows a student with 90 in one subject and 10 in another to pass. → Fix: Include =MIN(SubjectScores) >= 40 in pass criteria.
- Failing Attendance Prerequisite Check: Grading students who failed minimum statutory attendance requirements. → Fix: Evaluate attendance first: `=IF(Attendance < 0.75, "FAILED - LOW ATTENDANCE", ...)`.
- Hardcoded Grading Thresholds: Hardcoding 90, 80, 70 in formulas prevents grading curve adjustments. → Fix: Store grade boundaries in a dedicated Grade_Bands configuration table.
