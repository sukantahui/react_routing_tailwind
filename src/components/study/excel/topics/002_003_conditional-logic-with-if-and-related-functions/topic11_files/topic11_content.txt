5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Overlooking Mandatory Minimum Subject Pass Rules:
  Root Cause: Calculating only overall average allows a student with 90 in one subject and 10 in another to pass.
  Diagnostic Fix: Include =MIN(SubjectScores) >= 40 in pass criteria.
• Failing Attendance Prerequisite Check:
  Root Cause: Grading students who failed minimum statutory attendance requirements.
  Diagnostic Fix: Evaluate attendance first: `=IF(Attendance < 0.75, "FAILED - LOW ATTENDANCE", ...)`.
• Hardcoded Grading Thresholds:
  Root Cause: Hardcoding 90, 80, 70 in formulas prevents grading curve adjustments.
  Diagnostic Fix: Store grade boundaries in a dedicated Grade_Bands configuration table.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Minimum Subject Pass Check (=MIN(MarksRange) >= 40): Guarantees student passed all individual subject papers.
• Honors Distinction Formula (=IF(AND(Avg>=85, MIN(Marks)>=70), "Honors", "Pass")): Awards honors only when consistent subject excellence is demonstrated.
• Formatted Letter Grade Table (XLOOKUP with -1 Match): Translates composite scores into Letter Grades cleanly.
