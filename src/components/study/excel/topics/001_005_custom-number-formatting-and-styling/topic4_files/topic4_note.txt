Topic: Financial statement presentation: Wall Street accounting alignment using _) and *
Module: 001_005_custom-number-formatting-and-styling
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
_($* #,##0.00_);_($* (#,##0.00);_($* "-"_);_(@_)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Wall Street accounting layout with parenthesis alignment (_)) and repeat padding (* )

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
The mark of a seasoned financial modeler is perfect column alignment. Using _) and * creates boardroom-grade balance sheets that impress executive stakeholders.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Misaligned Positive/Negative Rows: Negative numbers with parentheses stick out further right than positive numbers without padding. → Fix: Add _) to positive and zero formats to reserve space equal to a closing parenthesis.
- Missing Currency Symbol Gap: Not using the * character causes currency symbols to hug numbers rather than left-align. → Fix: Include $* in the format to push the currency symbol to the far left of the cell.
- Ragged Right Margins: Failing to add _) to text entries causes text to cling to the right border. → Fix: Append _) to the 4th text section for uniform margin alignment.
