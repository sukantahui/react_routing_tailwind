Topic: Custom date serial formatting (dddd, mmmm dd, yyyy) and 12h/24h time formatting
Module: 001_005_custom-number-formatting-and-styling
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
dddd, mmmm dd, yyyy | hh:mm:ss AM/PM | [$-402]dd-mmm-yyyy

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Date tokens (d, dd, ddd, dddd, m, mm, mmm, mmmm, yy, yyyy) and time tokens (h, hh, m, mm, s, ss, AM/PM)

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Never allow ambiguous date formats on financial reports. Always format dates with 3-letter months (dd-mmm-yyyy) to prevent multi-million rupee audit errors.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Month vs Minute Collision (m): The token 'm' represents Month when alone, but Minute when immediately following 'h' or 'hh'. -> Fix: Always place minutes directly after hours (hh:mm) or use 's' after minutes (mm:ss).
- US vs UK Date Ambiguity: Using 08/09/2026 causes confusion whether it is August 9th or September 8th. -> Fix: Use 3-letter month tokens (dd-mmm-yyyy) for foolproof international clarity.
- Date Stored as Text: Typing '27th August 2026' as text prevents date math, filtering, and sorting. -> Fix: Enter true date serials and format visually with custom tokens.
