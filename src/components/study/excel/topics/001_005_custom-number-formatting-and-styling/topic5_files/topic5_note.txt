TOPIC 5 QUICK SUMMARY & INSTRUCTOR REFERENCE
================================================================================
Topic: Scaling large numbers into Thousands (K), Millions (M), and Billions (B)
Module: 001_005_custom-number-formatting-and-styling
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
#,##0.0, "K" /* Thousands */ | #,##0.0,, "M" /* Millions */ | #,##0.0,,, "B" /* Billions */

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Trailing comma scaling: Divide by 1,000 per comma (K, M, B) without altering underlying math

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Never divide your financial models by 1,000,000 with formulas! Use custom format scaling to keep pure raw data while presenting clean K, M, and B figures.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Accidental Formula Division: Dividing by 1,000,000 in formulas (/10^6) modifies underlying values and breaks dependent totals. → Fix: Use trailing commas in custom number format instead of formula division.
- Comma Placement Errors: Placing spaces between commas cancels the 1,000 divisor effect. → Fix: Keep trailing commas adjacent to digits before literal unit strings (e.g. #,##0.0,, "M").
- Over-Rounding Visuals: Scaling small values (e.g. ₹45,000) with M scaling shows '₹0.0M'. → Fix: Choose appropriate scale (K vs M) based on magnitude of data range.
