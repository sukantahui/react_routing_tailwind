KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
dd-mmm-yyyy | dddd, mmmm dd, yyyy | hh:mm:ss AM/PM | [$-409]yyyy-mm-dd

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Formatting raw Excel date serial numbers (integers) and time fractions (decimals) using custom tokens (d, dd, ddd, dddd, m, mm, mmm, mmmm, yy, yyyy, h, hh, s, ss, AM/PM).

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Never allow ambiguous date formats on financial reports (like 05/06/2026). Always format dates with 3-letter month abbreviations (dd-mmm-yyyy) to prevent multi-million rupee audit errors between US and UK/Indian standards.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Month vs Minute Collision (m): The token 'm' represents Month when alone, but Minute when immediately following 'h' or 'hh'. -> Fix: Always place minutes directly after hours (hh:mm) or before seconds (mm:ss).
- US vs UK Date Ambiguity: Using 08/09/2026 causes confusion whether it is August 9th or September 8th. -> Fix: Use 3-letter month tokens (dd-mmm-yyyy) for foolproof international clarity.
- Date Stored as Text: Typing '27th August 2026' as text prevents date math, filtering, sorting, and PivotTable hierarchy grouping. -> Fix: Enter true date serials and format visually with custom tokens.
