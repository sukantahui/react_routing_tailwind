1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Date & Time Custom Formatting is a critical discipline in financial engineering and enterprise reporting. Excel represents dates as whole integers (days elapsed since Jan 1, 1900) and time as decimal fractions (e.g. 0.5 = 12:00 PM). Custom date and time format tokens allow analysts to render these raw numeric values into human-readable, boardroom-ready displays without converting numbers into text or corrupting downstream calculations.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
• Day Tokens:
  - d      : Day number without leading zero (1..31)
  - dd     : Day number with forced leading zero (01..31)
  - ddd    : Short 3-letter weekday abbreviation (Mon..Sun)
  - dddd   : Full weekday name (Monday..Sunday)

• Month Tokens:
  - m      : Month number without leading zero (1..12)
  - mm     : Month number with forced leading zero (01..12)
  - mmm    : Short 3-letter month abbreviation (Jan..Dec)
  - mmmm   : Full month name (January..December)
  - mmmmm  : Single first letter of month (J, F, M, A, M, J, J, A, S, O, N, D)

• Year Tokens:
  - yy     : 2-digit year (00..99)
  - yyyy   : 4-digit year (1900..9999)

• Time Tokens:
  - h / hh : Hour without/with leading zero
  - m / mm : Minute without/with leading zero (when preceded by h/hh or followed by s/ss)
  - s / ss : Second without/with leading zero
  - AM/PM  : Converts 24-hour clock to 12-hour clock with AM or PM suffix

4. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Month vs Minute Collision (m):
  Root Cause: The token 'm' represents Month when alone, but Minute when immediately following 'h' or 'hh'.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always place minutes directly after hours (hh:mm) or before seconds (mm:ss).
• US vs UK Date Ambiguity:
  Root Cause: Using 08/09/2026 causes confusion whether it is August 9th or September 8th.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use 3-letter month tokens (dd-mmm-yyyy) for foolproof international clarity.
• Date Stored as Text:
  Root Cause: Typing '27th August 2026' as text prevents date math, filtering, sorting, and PivotTable hierarchy grouping.
  Diagnostic Fix: Enter true date serials and format visually with custom tokens.
