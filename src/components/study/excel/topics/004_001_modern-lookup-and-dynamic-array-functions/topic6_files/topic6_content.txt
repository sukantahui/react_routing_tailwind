TOPIC 6 CONCEPTUAL REFERENCE CONTENT
================================================================================
Title: Generating Numeric and Date Sequences with SEQUENCE (Rows, Columns, Start, Step)
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901

1. The Problem with Manual Dragging and Fill Handle:
Historically, creating serial numbers (1, 2, 3...) or date calendars required manually dragging the fill handle across hundreds of cells. If rows were deleted or added, serial numbers broke or required re-dragging, causing formula errors and maintenance headaches.

2. The Modern SEQUENCE Function:
`SEQUENCE` generates a live vector or matrix of sequential values in memory. When paired with dynamic row counting functions like `COUNTA` or `ROWS`, serial number columns expand and contract automatically as data grows.

3. Date Arithmetic & Step Intervals:
Because Excel represents dates as consecutive integer serials (e.g. `DATE(2026,1,1)` is serial `46023`), passing date formulas to `start` with step intervals of `1` (days), `7` (weeks), or `30` (approx months) creates instant dynamic financial schedules, amortization calendars, and Gantt timeline axes.

4. 2D Coordinate Transformations:
`SEQUENCE(5, 4, 101, 1)` populates across rows first (101, 102, 103, 104 in row 1, 105.. in row 2), allowing matrix layout generation, seating maps, and 2D index grids.
