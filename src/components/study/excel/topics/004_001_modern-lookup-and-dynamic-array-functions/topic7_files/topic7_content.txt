================================================================================
Title: Generating Random Datasets and Monte Carlo Inputs with RANDARRAY
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901

1. The Legacy RAND() and RANDBETWEEN() Bottlenecks:
In legacy Excel, generating random matrices required filling `=RAND()` or `=RANDBETWEEN(bottom, top)` across thousands of individual cells. This created heavy workbook bloat and dragged calculation performance.

2. The Modern RANDARRAY Function:
`RANDARRAY` generates an entire uniform random matrix in a single formula. It accepts custom minimum and maximum bounds directly and supports both floating-point numbers (for interest rates, volatility models, WACC sensitivity) and discrete integers (for simulated unit counts, transaction volumes, dice rolls).

3. Monte Carlo Simulation Engine:
By feeding `RANDARRAY` into financial discounted cash flow (DCF) models, corporate analysts can simulate 10,000 iterations of revenue growth and inflation rates to construct probability distribution curves and Value at Risk (VaR) matrices.

4. Random Sampling & Permutation:
Pairing `RANDARRAY` with `SORTBY` allows non-repeating random lottery sampling and lottery permutations: `=SORTBY(Candidates, RANDARRAY(ROWS(Candidates)))`.
