5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Unanchored Floating Objects:
  Root Cause: Leaving floating chart containers unlocked causes them to skew when stakeholders view on different resolutions.
  Diagnostic Fix: Lock all visual elements to cell grids with 'Don't move or size with cells'.
• Missing Zero-Baseline on Bar Charts:
  Root Cause: Truncating the vertical axis on bar charts distorts visual comparisons and misleads executives.
  Diagnostic Fix: Always start bar chart axes at zero.
• Over-Complicated Navigation:
  Root Cause: Requiring users to click through 10 menus to find basic summary numbers.
  Diagnostic Fix: Keep core KPIs front-and-center on the landing screen.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Clean Canvas First (Hide Gridlines & Headers): Always start dashboard design on a clean, grid-free worksheet canvas.
• Modular Card Reusability (Copy proven KPI card blocks): Reuse standardized KPI card containers across multiple dashboard projects.
• Test on Multiple Monitors (Verify layout on laptop & external display): Guarantees flawless executive presentation across all devices.
