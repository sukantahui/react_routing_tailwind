TOPIC 2 QUICK SUMMARY & INSTRUCTOR REFERENCE
================================================================================
Topic: Building KPI Summary Cards with dynamic change indicators (▲ / ▼) and conditional styling
Module: 003_004_basic-dashboards-and-reporting
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Custom Number Mask: [Color10]▲ +0.0%;[Red]▼ -0.0%;[Color16]▬ 0.0% | Dynamic KPI Formula: =TEXT(Val, "₹#,##0.0") & " (" & TEXT(Growth, "+0.0%") & ")"

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Building high-impact C-suite KPI Summary Cards with dynamic Unicode delta arrows (▲ / ▼ / ▬), custom color formatting, and integrated micro-sparklines

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Executives look at KPI cards first. Make the main number huge (24pt font), the label small and muted (10pt uppercase), and add a clean delta badge like '▲ +12.4% vs LY'. It looks like a Bloomberg terminal.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Reversed Direction Logic for Cost Metrics: Displaying a green ▲ arrow for an INCREASE in operating costs (where cost increases are actually negative alerts). → Fix: Invert format mask for expenses: `[Red]▲ +0.0%;[Color10]▼ -0.0%` so cost increases display in red.
- Overcrowding Card Borders with Dark Shadows: Heavy 3D drop-shadows and thick dark borders make cards look dated and clunky. → Fix: Use subtle 1px border outlines (#334155) with soft neutral backgrounds.
- Unanchored Card Shapes Drifting on Grid Resizing: Drawing cards as freeform shapes causes them to drift when columns are resized. → Fix: Build KPI cards directly inside cell grids using merged cells and cell borders for perfect alignment.
