1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Building KPI Summary Cards with dynamic change indicators (▲ / ▼) and conditional styling is an essential discipline in corporate business intelligence, executive reporting, and visual analytics.
It provides the core protocols required to transform dense tabular data into intuitive, interactive, single-screen command centers.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Custom Number Mask: [Color10]▲ +0.0%;[Red]▼ -0.0%;[Color16]▬ 0.0% | Dynamic KPI Formula: =TEXT(Val, "₹#,##0.0") & " (" & TEXT(Growth, "+0.0%") & ")"

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Three-Tier Card Information Hierarchy:
   Tier 1 (Top): Small muted metric title (e.g. 'TOTAL REVENUE'); Tier 2 (Center): Massive bold KPI number (e.g. '₹45.8 Cr'); Tier 3 (Bottom): Dynamic delta badge (e.g. '▲ +12.4% vs LY').

2. Unicode Delta Formatting Masks:
   Uses embedded Unicode characters inside custom number formats: `[Color10]▲ +0.0%;[Red]▼ -0.0%;[Color16]▬ 0.0%` for automatic color-coded arrows.

3. Dynamic Text Formula Integration:
   Creates self-updating card subtitles: `="▲ " & TEXT(Growth, "0.0%") & " vs target (" & TEXT(Variance, "₹#,##0") & ")"`.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Reversed Direction Logic for Cost Metrics:
  Root Cause: Displaying a green ▲ arrow for an INCREASE in operating costs (where cost increases are actually negative alerts).
  Diagnostic Fix: Invert format mask for expenses: `[Red]▲ +0.0%;[Color10]▼ -0.0%` so cost increases display in red.
• Overcrowding Card Borders with Dark Shadows:
  Root Cause: Heavy 3D drop-shadows and thick dark borders make cards look dated and clunky.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use subtle 1px border outlines (#334155) with soft neutral backgrounds.
• Unanchored Card Shapes Drifting on Grid Resizing:
  Root Cause: Drawing cards as freeform shapes causes them to drift when columns are resized.
  Diagnostic Fix: Build KPI cards directly inside cell grids using merged cells and cell borders for perfect alignment.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Unicode Delta Characters (▲ (Alt+30) | ▼ (Alt+31)): Embed real Unicode arrows directly into custom number formats.
• Expense Card Color Inversion ([Red]▲ +0.0%;[Green]▼ -0.0%): Inverts colors so rising costs show as red alerts.
• Grid-Aligned KPI Cards (Format cell ranges directly): Building cards in cell grids guarantees they never drift or misalign.
