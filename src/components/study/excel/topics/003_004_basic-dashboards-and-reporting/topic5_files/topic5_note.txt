KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Waterfall Chart: Insert > Waterfall | Format Data Point: Set as Total (Clear/Subtotal Pillars) | Connector Lines

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Building professional corporate Waterfall charts (Bridge charts) to visualize P&L walks, EBITDA reconciliations, and budget-to-actual variance waterfalls

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
The Waterfall chart is the language of CFOs. Always format your starting and ending pillars in Navy Blue, positive gains in Green, and negative expenses in Red. It tells a complete financial story in 5 seconds.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Forgetting to 'Set as Total' on Ending Milestone Bars: Leaving Net Income or Subtotals as floating variance bars causes them to stack on top of expenses rather than anchoring to the baseline. → Fix: Right-click the subtotal column > select 'Set as Total'.
- Inconsistent Sign Input Convention: Entering expense items as positive numbers causes the Waterfall chart to display cost additions as green increases rather than red subtractions. → Fix: Enter expenses as negative numbers (-) in the data table.
- Selecting Unsupported Waterfall Chart Options: Attempting to create a native PivotChart Waterfall (Excel does not support Waterfall charts directly on PivotTables). → Fix: Link a standard formula range to the PivotTable output and build the Waterfall chart from that range.
