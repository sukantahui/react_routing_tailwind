5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Information Overload (Dashboard Bloat):
  Root Cause: Crowding 25 disparate charts onto a single screen with no clear visual hierarchy.
  Diagnostic Fix: Follow the rule of 5: 1 primary trend, 1 composition chart, 1 detailed table, and 4 top KPI cards.
• Broken Cross-Sheet Formula Linkages:
  Root Cause: Hardcoding numbers when transferring data from calculation sheets to the dashboard canvas.
  Diagnostic Fix: Maintain strict dynamic formula links (`=Calcs!C10`) across all dashboard card elements.
• Neglecting Mobile / Laptop Screen Aspect Ratios:
  Root Cause: Designing only for massive 32-inch desktop monitors causes dashboard to clip on executive 14-inch laptops.
  Diagnostic Fix: Design within standard 1366x768 / 1920x1080 responsive grid boundaries.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• The 5-Second Executive Rule (Key metrics visible in 5 seconds): Executives must understand business health within 5 seconds of opening.
• Single Source of Truth Sheet (Dedicated 'Calcs' engine tab): Keeps messy background formulas completely separate from the clean presentation canvas.
• Export to Interactive PDF (File > Export > PDF): Delivers publication-grade executive briefing documents.
