Topic 5: Extracting Top N or Bottom N records from arrays with TAKE
================================================================================

1. BOUNDARY SLICING MECHANICS:
The TAKE function extracts a contiguous sub-matrix from the perimeter of a 2D array.

2. PARAMETER BEHAVIOR:
- rows > 0: Slices from top down.
- rows < 0: Slices from bottom up.
- columns > 0: Slices from left to right.
- columns < 0: Slices from right to left.
- rows = 0 or cols = 0: Throws #CALC! (empty array).
- |rows| > total_rows: Returns all available rows gracefully (no error).

3. PRACTICAL COMPOSITION PATTERNS:
- Top 5 Leaderboard:
  =TAKE(SORT(ScoreTable, ScoreCol, -1), 5)

- Bottom 3 Underperformers:
  =TAKE(SORT(ScoreTable, ScoreCol, -1), -3)

- Top & Bottom Consolidated Card:
  =LET(s, SORT(DataTable, ColIdx, -1), VSTACK(TAKE(s, 5), TAKE(s, -5)))

- Dynamic Rolling 12-Month Window:
  =TAKE(ContinuousMonthlyData, -12)
