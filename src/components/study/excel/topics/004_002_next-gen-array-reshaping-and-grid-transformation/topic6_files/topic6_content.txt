Topic 6: Excluding headers, totals, or metadata rows/columns with DROP
================================================================================

1. BOUNDARY EXCLUSION MECHANICS:
The DROP function removes peripheral rows and columns from the outer bounds
of an array, returning the remaining inner body.

2. PARAMETER BEHAVIOR:
- rows > 0: Excludes top rows.
- rows < 0: Excludes bottom rows.
- columns > 0: Excludes left columns.
- columns < 0: Excludes right columns.
- |rows| >= total_rows or |cols| >= total_cols: Throws #CALC! (empty array).

3. PRACTICAL ETL PATTERNS:
- Master Stacking without Duplicate Headers:
  =VSTACK(Branch1_All, DROP(Branch2_All, 1), DROP(Branch3_All, 1))

- Dual-Ended Boundary Stripping:
  =DROP(DROP(RawReport, 2), -1)

- In-Memory Subtotal Calculation:
  =SUM(DROP(DROP(RevenueColumn, 1), -1))
