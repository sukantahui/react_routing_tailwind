1. THE 4-PHASE DATA SANITIZATION PROTOCOL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 1: Strip HTML/XML Tags: <[^>]+> → ""
Phase 2: Remove Non-Alphanumeric Noise: [^a-zA-Z0-9\s.,-] → ""
Phase 3: Normalize Irregular Whitespace: \s+ → " " with TRIM()
Phase 4: Case & Typography Normalization: PROPER() / UPPER()

2. POWERFUL CORPORATE SANITIZATION FORMULAS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Full HTML & Whitespace Cleanser:
  =PROPER(TRIM(REGEXREPLACE(REGEXREPLACE(A2, "<[^>]+>", ""), "\s+", " ")))

• Strip Control Characters:
  =REGEXREPLACE(A2, "[\x00-\x1F]", "")

• Vectorized Column Cleanser:
  =MAP(A5:A500, LAMBDA(c, TRIM(REGEXREPLACE(REGEXREPLACE(c, "<[^>]+>", ""), "\s+", " "))))
