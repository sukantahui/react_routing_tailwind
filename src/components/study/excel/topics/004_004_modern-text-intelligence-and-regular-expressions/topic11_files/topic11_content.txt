1. FORMULA EXAMPLES:
- Log Line Parser (Timestamp, Level, User, IP, Message):
  =REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "^\[([^\]]+)\]\s+\[([^\]]+)\]\s+\[User:\s*([^\]]+)\]\s+\[IP:\s*([^\]]+)\]\s+(.+)$", 2)

- Invoice Text Block Parser:
  =TEXTSPLIT(B5, ": ", " | ")

- Lookbehind Entity Isolators:
  =REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "(?<=INV_NO=)[A-Z0-9-]+")
  =NUMBERVALUE(REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "(?<=INR\s)[0-9,.]+"))

- Multi-Line Block Parser:
  =TEXTSPLIT(B5, CHAR(9), CHAR(10))

- Vectorized Multi-Row Parser:
  =DROP(REDUCE("", B5:B20, LAMBDA(acc, line, VSTACK(acc, REGEXEXTRACT(line, "^\[([^\]]+)\]\s+\[([^\]]+)\]\s+\[User:\s*([^\]]+)\]\s+(.+)$", 2)))), 1)
