1. WHAT IS THE TEXTSPLIT FUNCTION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax: =TEXTSPLIT(text, col_delimiter, [row_delimiter], [ignore_empty], [match_mode], [pad_with])

Core Capabilities:
• Splits text horizontally across columns OR vertically down rows.
• Supports multiple delimiters via array constants: {",", ";", "|", "/"}.
• Creates 2D matrices when both col_delimiter and row_delimiter are supplied.

2. POWERFUL CORPORATE SPLITTING FORMULAS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Multi-Delimiter Splitting: =TEXTSPLIT(A2, {",", ";", "|"}, , TRUE)
• Key-Value 2D Table: =TEXTSPLIT(A2, "=", ";")
• Vertical List Splitting: =TEXTSPLIT(A2, , ",")
• Splitting by Line Break: =TEXTSPLIT(A2, , CHAR(10))
• Extracting Nth Item: =INDEX(TEXTSPLIT(A2, ","), 3)

3. CLASSROOM RULES & ADVICE FROM SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Always set ignore_empty = TRUE when splitting text with irregular spacing.
• Use row_delimiter = ";" and col_delimiter = "=" to turn key-value pairs into 2D tables!
• Combine TEXTSPLIT with TOCOL, SORT, and UNIQUE to create instant deduplicated lists!
