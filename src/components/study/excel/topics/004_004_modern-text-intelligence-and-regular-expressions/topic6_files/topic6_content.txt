Topic 6: Splitting complex strings by multiple delimiters with TEXTSPLIT
================================================================================

1. FORMULA EXAMPLES:
- Multi-Delimiter Column Split:
  =TEXTSPLIT(B5, {",", ";", "|"}, , TRUE)

- 2D Key-Value Matrix:
  =TEXTSPLIT(B5, "=", ";")

- Vertical Row Split:
  =TEXTSPLIT(B5, , ",")

- Split on Newline:
  =TEXTSPLIT(B5, , CHAR(10))

- Extract Last Token:
  =TAKE(TOCOL(TEXTSPLIT(B5, ",")), -1)

- Dynamic Array Processing Pipeline:
  =LET(
    tokens, TOCOL(TEXTSPLIT(B5, {",", ";", "/"})),
    SORT(UNIQUE(FILTER(tokens, tokens<>"")))
  )
