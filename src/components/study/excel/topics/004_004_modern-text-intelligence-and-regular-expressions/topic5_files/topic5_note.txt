1. WHAT IS THE REGEXREPLACE FUNCTION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Syntax: =REGEXREPLACE(text, pattern, replacement, [occurrence], [case_sensitivity])

Core Mechanics:
• Replaces occurrences of a regex pattern with replacement text or backreferences.
• Backreferences ($1, $2, $3) insert text matched by capturing groups.
• occurrence: 0 = All instances (default), 1..N = Specific instance only.

2. ESSENTIAL CORPORATE TRANSFORMATION PATTERNS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Masking Aadhaar: =REGEXREPLACE(A2, "^\d{4}-\d{4}-(\d{4})$", "XXXX-XXXX-$1")
• Masking Credit Cards: =REGEXREPLACE(A2, "^\d{4}-\d{4}-\d{4}-(\d{4})$", "XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-$1")
• Stripping HTML Tags: =REGEXREPLACE(A2, "<[^>]+>", "")
• Reordering Dates (DD/MM/YYYY &rarr; YYYY-MM-DD): =REGEXREPLACE(A2, "^(\d{2})/(\d{2})/(\d{4})$", "$3-$2-$1")
• Normalizing Excess Whitespace: =TRIM(REGEXREPLACE(A2, "\s+", " "))

3. CLASSROOM RULES & ADVICE FROM SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Never deploy nested SUBSTITUTE functions when 1 clean REGEXREPLACE can do the job!
• Always mask sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) before sharing reports.
• Leverage backreferences ($1, $2) to restructure dates, names, and phone numbers in 1 step!
