Topic 5: Cleaning, masking, and reformatting text with REGEXREPLACE
================================================================================

1. FORMULA EXAMPLES:
- Aadhaar Masking:
  =REGEXREPLACE(C5, "^\d{4}-\d{4}-(\d{4})$", "XXXX-XXXX-$1")

- Credit Card Masking:
  =REGEXREPLACE(C5, "^\d{4}-\d{4}-\d{4}-(\d{4})$", "XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-$1")

- HTML Tag Stripping:
  =REGEXREPLACE(D5, "<[^>]+>", "")

- Phone Number Standardization:
  =REGEXREPLACE(E5, "^(\d{3})(\d{3})(\d{4})$", "($1) $2-$3")

- Date Format Transposition (DD/MM/YYYY → YYYY-MM-DD):
  =REGEXREPLACE(F5, "^(\d{2})/(\d{2})/(\d{4})$", "$3-$2-$1")

- Non-Alphanumeric Punctuation Removal:
  =REGEXREPLACE(B5, "[^a-zA-Z0-9\s]", "")
