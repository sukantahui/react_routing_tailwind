Topic 4: Global vs first-match extractions and capturing groups in REGEXEXTRACT
================================================================================

1. FORMULA EXAMPLES:
- Global All Matches (Mode 1):
  =REGEXEXTRACT("Invoice 101, Invoice 202, Invoice 303", "\d+", 1)
  Spills: 101 | 202 | 303

- Capturing Groups Decomposition (Mode 2):
  =REGEXEXTRACT("+91-(033)-2592-1144 Ext: 204", "^\+(\d+)-\((\d+)\)-([\d-]+)\s*Ext:\s*(\d+)$", 2)
  Spills: 91 | 033 | 2592-1144 | 204

- Vertical Keyword Extraction:
  =TOCOL(REGEXEXTRACT(A2, "\bINV-\d+\b", 1))

- Specific Capturing Group:
  =CHOOSECOLS(REGEXEXTRACT(A2, "^(\w+)\s+(\w+)$", 2), 2)
