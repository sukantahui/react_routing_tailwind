Topic 3: Extracting substrings matching regex patterns with REGEXEXTRACT
================================================================================

1. FORMULA EXAMPLES:
- Extract Invoice ID:
  =REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "INV-\d{5}")

- Extract Date:
  =REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "\b\d{2}[/-]\d{2}[/-]\d{4}\b")

- Extract Monetary Amount:
  =NUMBERVALUE(REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "(?<=INR\s)\d+(\.\d{2})?"))

- Complete Extraction Pipeline:
  =HSTACK(
    REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "INV-\d{5}"),
    REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "\b\d{2}-\d{2}-\d{4}\b"),
    NUMBERVALUE(REGEXEXTRACT(B5, "(?<=INR\s)\d+(\.\d{2})?"))
  )
