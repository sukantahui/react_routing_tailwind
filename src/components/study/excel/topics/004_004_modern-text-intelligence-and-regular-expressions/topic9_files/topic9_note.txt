1. VECTORIZED REGEX ARCHITECTURE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• FILTER + REGEXTEST: Instant extraction of compliant vs non-compliant rows.
• BYROW + REGEXEXTRACT: Multi-group decomposition across entire tables.
• TOCOL + REGEXEXTRACT (mode 1): Flattening global matches into 1D columns.
• MAP + REGEXREPLACE: Mass data sanitization and masking across 2D grids.
• REDUCE + REGEXREPLACE: Sequential multi-pattern cleansing pipelines.

2. ESSENTIAL CORPORATE VECTORIZED PIPELINES:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Compliant Vendor Filter: =FILTER(A5:E20, REGEXTEST(C5:C20, "^[A-Z]{5}\d{4}[A-Z]$"))
• Unique Invoice Inventory: =SORT(UNIQUE(TOCOL(REGEXEXTRACT(B5:B100, "INV-\d+", 1), 3)))
• Column-Wide Name Splitting: =BYROW(A5:A20, LAMBDA(r, REGEXEXTRACT(r, "^(\w+)\s+(.+)$", 2)))
• Total Compliant Count: =SUM(--REGEXTEST(C5:C100, "^[A-Z]{5}\d{4}[A-Z]$"))

3. CLASSROOM RULES & ADVICE FROM SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Never loop with VBA when dynamic array vectorization can process 50,000 rows in RAM!
• Always use TOCOL(..., 3) to discard errors and blanks from global extractions.
• Combine LET with REGEXTEST and PIVOTBY for automated executive compliance reporting!
