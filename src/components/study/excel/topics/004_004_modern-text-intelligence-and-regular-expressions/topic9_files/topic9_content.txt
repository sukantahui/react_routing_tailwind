Topic 9: Combining Regex functions with dynamic array functions (FILTER, BYROW, TOCOL)

1. FORMULA EXAMPLES:
- Vectorized Filter on PAN:
  =FILTER(A5:E20, REGEXTEST(C5:C20, "^[A-Z]{5}\d{4}[A-Z]$"))

- 2D Row-Wise Decomposition via BYROW:
  =BYROW(A5:A20, LAMBDA(r, REGEXEXTRACT(r, "^(\w+)\s+(.+)$", 2)))

- Flattened Unique Invoice Codes via TOCOL:
  =SORT(UNIQUE(TOCOL(REGEXEXTRACT(B5:B100, "INV-\d+", 1), 3)))

- Mass Cleansing via MAP:
  =MAP(A5:C100, LAMBDA(cell, TRIM(REGEXREPLACE(cell, "<[^>]+>", ""))))

- Multi-Criteria Filtering:
  =FILTER(A5:E20, REGEXTEST(C5:C20, "^[A-Z]{5}\d{4}[A-Z]$") * REGEXTEST(D5:D20, "^[\w.%+-]+@[\w.-]+\.[a-zA-Z]{2,}$"))
