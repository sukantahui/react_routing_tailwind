1. THE FOUR PILLARS OF REGULAR EXPRESSIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Metacharacters: Special tokens like . (any char), \d (digit), \w (word), \s (space).
2. Character Classes: [A-Z] (uppercase), [0-9] (digits), [^0-9] (negated/non-digits).
3. Quantifiers: + (1+), * (0+), ? (0 or 1), {n} (exact n), {n,m} (range).
4. Anchors: ^ (start of string), $ (end of string), \b (word boundary).

2. COMMON BUSINESS PATTERNS IN INDIA:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• PAN Card: ^[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]$
• PIN Code: ^[1-9][0-9]{5}$
• Mobile Number: ^[6-9][0-9]{9}$
• Invoice ID: \bINV-\d{4,6}\b

3. CLASSROOM RULES & ADVICE FROM SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Avoid loose wildcard patterns like `.*` in validation—they cause greedy false matches!
• Always use specific character sets (e.g. `[A-Z]` instead of `\w` when numbers are forbidden).
• Bookend validation formulas with `^` and `$`!
