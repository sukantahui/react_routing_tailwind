Topic: Watch Window: Monitoring key KPI cells across multiple worksheets and workbooks
Module: 003_002_data-validation-protection-and-cleaning-techniques
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Watch Window: Formulas > Watch Window (Alt + M + W) -> Add Watch (Select Cells Across Sheets/Files)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Monitoring key performance indicators, summary balance sheet totals, and debt covenant cells across different sheets simultaneously using the floating Watch Window console

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
If you build multi-sheet models, the Watch Window is your best friend. Put your Balance Sheet Check, Net Income, and IRR in the Watch Window, and watch them update live as you tweak assumptions.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Watch Window Forgotten in Closed Workbooks: Watching cells in external workbooks causes Excel to attempt background links on startup. -> Fix: Delete obsolete watches when project modeling concludes (Click cell in Watch Window > Delete Watch).
- Docking Window Accidentally Over Grids: Accidentally docking the Watch Window can obscure formula bars. -> Fix: Double-click the Watch Window title bar to undock and float it freely on a second monitor.
- Performance Lag on 500+ Watched Cells: Adding hundreds of individual cells to the Watch Window can introduce minor UI lag during massive recalculations. -> Fix: Limit Watch Window to the top 10-15 executive summary KPI cells.
