TOPIC 2 QUICK SUMMARY & INSTRUCTOR REFERENCE
================================================================================
Topic: Dimensional Modeling principles: Fact Tables vs Dimension Tables (Star Schema vs Snowflake Schema)
Module: 005_003_power-pivot-and-data-modeling
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Schema Design: Star Schema (Central FactTable + Surrounding DimensionTables) | Snowflake Schema (Normalized Dimensions)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Architecting enterprise dimensional schemas: differentiating Fact Tables (metrics/transactions) from Dimension Tables (business context/filters), and optimizing Star vs Snowflake schemas

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
The #1 secret to mastering Power Pivot and DAX: Build a Star Schema! Put your numbers in the central Fact table, put your categories in the surrounding Dimension tables, and connect them with 1-to-Many relationships. Everything else becomes easy.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Building a Flat 'Mega-Table' (De-Normalized Anti-Pattern): Merging all customer, product, and regional text attributes into a single 50-column fact table destroys memory compression. → Fix: Decompose flat tables into a normalized Star Schema: 1 Fact table + clean Dimension lookup tables.
- Excessive Snowflake Normalization: Splitting dimensions into 5 cascading tiers (Product → Subcategory → Category → Department → Group) slows down DAX filter traversal. → Fix: Collapse multi-tier dimensions back into a single clean Star Schema dimension table.
- Putting Descriptive Attributes in the Fact Table: Leaving customer addresses and telephone numbers in the 5-million row fact table wastes gigabytes of RAM. → Fix: Store descriptive attributes exclusively in Dimension tables.
