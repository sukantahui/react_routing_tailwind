KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Pivot Quadrants: Filters (Page Slicers) | Columns (X-Axis) | Rows (Y-Axis) | Values (Aggregates)

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Mastering the PivotTable Field List interface and orchestrating multi-level hierarchical groupings across Filters, Columns, Rows, and Values

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Keep your Columns quadrant compact! Reserve Columns for categories with 2 to 5 items (like Quarters or Years). Put high-cardinality fields in Rows to keep your reports easy to read.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Unintended Count Instead of Sum: If a numeric column contains even a single blank cell or text value, Excel defaults to 'Count of Field' instead of 'Sum of Field'. -> Fix: Right-click value field > Summarize Values By > Sum, and clean source data.
- Too Many Fields in Columns Quadrant: Placing high-cardinality fields (like Customer Name) in Columns creates an unusable 500-column horizontal spreadsheet. -> Fix: Keep Columns for low-cardinality attributes (e.g. Years, Quarters, Statuses).
- Disappearing Field List Task Pane: Accidentally closing the PivotTable Field List pane leaves users unable to modify field layouts. -> Fix: Click inside PivotTable > PivotTable Analyze tab > Click 'Field List' button.
