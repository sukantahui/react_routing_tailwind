Topic 9: Time-series filtering with Timelines
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
Time-series filtering with Timelines is an essential discipline in corporate business intelligence and reporting.
It provides the foundation for dynamic in-memory summarization, multi-dimensional slicing, and interactive dashboards.

2. SYNTAX & TOKEN STRUCTURE
Operational Architecture: Timeline Component: Year | Quarter | Month | Day Sliding Granularity Window

3. CORE TECHNICAL MECHANICS
1. Dedicated Date-Field Filtering Component:
   Timelines recognize true date serial fields and render continuous horizontal timeline sliders with interactive drag handles.

2. Dynamic Granularity Switching:
   Users can switch temporal zoom levels instantly between Years, Quarters, Months, and Days via a simple header dropdown.

3. Continuous Date Range Scrubbing:
   Drag edge handles to expand or contract historical observation windows (e.g. Q2 2025 through Q3 2026) across connected pivot reports.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Timeline Disabled ('No Valid Date Fields'):
  Root Cause: If the source column contains text dates or blank cells, Excel refuses to insert a Timeline.
  Diagnostic Fix: Ensure source date column contains 100% pure serial dates without blank cells.
• Non-Contiguous Date Selection Limitation:
  Root Cause: Timelines only support continuous date ranges; you cannot select Jan 2025 and Mar 2025 while excluding Feb.
  Diagnostic Fix: Use a standard Slicer with a 'Month' grouping field for non-contiguous date filtering.
• Theme Styling Inconsistency:
  Root Cause: Default bright blue timeline clashes with dark corporate dashboard color schemes.
  Diagnostic Fix: Apply matching custom Timeline styles from the Timeline ribbon tab.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Insert Timeline Shortcut (PivotTable Analyze > Insert Timeline (Alt + J + T + T)): Quickly insert a time-series slider on any date field.
• Switch Granularity (Timeline dropdown: Years / Quarters / Months): Instantly adjust analytical zoom from macro-years to micro-days.
• Synchronize Across Pivots (Right-click Timeline > Report Connections): Link the timeline to update all time-series charts across the dashboard.
