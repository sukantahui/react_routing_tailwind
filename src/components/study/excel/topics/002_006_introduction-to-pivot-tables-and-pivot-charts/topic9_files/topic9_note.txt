Topic: Time-series filtering with Timelines
Module: 002_006_introduction-to-pivot-tables-and-pivot-charts
Course: EXCEL-PRO-901: Microsoft Excel From Zero to Ultra Expert
Instructor: Sukanta Hui (Coder & AccoTax)

KEY FORMULA / SYNTAX:
Timeline Component: Year | Quarter | Month | Day Sliding Granularity Window

PRIMARY CONCEPT:
Implementing interactive Timeline slicers to filter time-series data dynamically across Years, Quarters, Months, and Days with visual range sliders

TEACHER'S ADVICE:
Timelines provide the most intuitive UI for historical analysis. Always pair a Timeline (for continuous periods) with categorical Slicers (for regions and products) on executive dashboards.

COMMON TRAPS TO AVOID:
- Timeline Disabled ('No Valid Date Fields'): If the source column contains text dates or blank cells, Excel refuses to insert a Timeline. → Fix: Ensure source date column contains 100% pure serial dates without blank cells.
- Non-Contiguous Date Selection Limitation: Timelines only support continuous date ranges; you cannot select Jan 2025 and Mar 2025 while excluding Feb. → Fix: Use a standard Slicer with a 'Month' grouping field for non-contiguous date filtering.
- Theme Styling Inconsistency: Default bright blue timeline clashes with dark corporate dashboard color schemes. → Fix: Apply matching custom Timeline styles from the Timeline ribbon tab.
