5. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ERRORS
• Inconsistent Color Palette Across Charts:
  Root Cause: Using random default colors on different charts creates a chaotic, amateurish dashboard.
  Diagnostic Fix: Enforce a unified 3-color palette (e.g. Navy primary, Emerald accent, Slate neutral) across all charts.
• Overcrowding the Dashboard Canvas:
  Root Cause: Squeezing 10 dense tables onto a single screen overwhelms executive decision-makers.
  Diagnostic Fix: Follow the 4-quadrant rule: Top KPIs, Time Trend, Category Breakdown, Top 10 Detail.
• Forgetting to Lock Protection & Gridlines:
  Root Cause: Leaving raw gridlines and unlocked pivot controls allows accidental layout disruption.
  Diagnostic Fix: Turn off worksheet gridlines (View > uncheck Gridlines) and lock slicer positions.

6. PRO TIPS & SHORTCUTS
• Turn Off Gridlines (View tab > Uncheck Gridlines (Alt + W + V + G)): Instantly gives your dashboard a sleek, modern software application feel.
• Executive KPI Card Layout (Single-cell pivot summaries): Create high-impact big-number callouts for Total Revenue, Margin %, and Active Clients.
• Template Master Export (Save as .xltx): Preserve the complete dashboard architecture for future fiscal reporting cycles.
