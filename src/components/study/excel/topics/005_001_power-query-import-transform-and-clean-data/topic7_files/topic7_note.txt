1. DATE & TIME TRANSFORMATION SUITE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Age: Number.RoundDown(Duration.Days(DateTime.Date(DateTime.LocalNow()) - [BirthDate]) / 365.25)
• Period Boundaries: Date.StartOfMonth, Date.EndOfMonth, Date.StartOfYear, Date.EndOfYear
• Names & Quarters: Date.MonthName, Date.QuarterOfYear, Date.DayOfWeekName
• Relative Dates: Date.IsInCurrentYear, Date.IsInPreviousNDays([Date], 30)
• Durations: [End] - [Start] → Duration.Days, Duration.TotalHours

2. NUMBER TRANSFORMATION SUITE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Rounding: Number.Round (Banker's), Number.RoundUp (Ceiling), Number.RoundDown (Floor)
• Standard Rounding: Number.Round([Val], 2, RoundingMode.AwayFromZero)
• Parity & Math: Number.IsEven, Number.IsOdd, Number.Mod, Number.Abs, Number.Sign

3. CLASSROOM RULES & ADVICE FROM SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Be aware of Banker's Rounding: 2.5 rounds to 2 unless RoundingMode.AwayFromZero is set!
• Build Fiscal Calendars (April-March) with M conditional date logic.
• Strip time components with DateTime.Date when joining dates with dimension keys.
