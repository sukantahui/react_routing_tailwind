1. AVL ROTATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Case            Balance Factor  Unbalance Cause     Rotation Required
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LL Case         > +1            Left child left     Single Right Rotate
RR Case         < -1            Right child right   Single Left Rotate
LR Case         > +1            Left child right    Left Rotate child + Right Rotate parent
RL Case         < -1            Right child left    Right Rotate child + Left Rotate parent
