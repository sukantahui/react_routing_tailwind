1. QUICK SORT VS MERGE SORT COMPARISON
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature             Quick Sort                  Merge Sort
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Strategy            Divide & Conquer (Pivot)    Divide & Conquer (Midpoint)
Average Time        O(n log n)                  O(n log n)
Worst Time          O(n^2)                      O(n log n) Guaranteed
Auxiliary Space     O(log n) stack call frames  O(n) external array buffers
Stability           Unstable                    Stable
Best Datastructure  Arrays (Cache Locality)     Linked Lists (No array copying)
