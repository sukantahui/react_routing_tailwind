2. COMMON BOARD EXAM TRAPS & HOW TO AVOID THEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trap 1: Confusing Process (Rectangle) and Input/Output (Parallelogram)
  -> Writing `print(x)` or `read y` inside a rectangle causes immediate deduction of 0.5 to 1 mark.

Trap 2: Forgetting Arrowheads on Flow Lines
  -> Drawing plain lines without arrowheads makes the direction of execution ambiguous. Always include clear arrows!

Trap 3: Unlabeled Decision Outgoing Lines
  -> Every line leaving a diamond must be labeled with "True"/"False" or "Yes"/"No".

Trap 4: Off-by-One Loop Boundary Errors in Dry Runs
  -> Carefully observe whether a loop uses `<` or `<=`.
  -> Trace tables must document the exact terminating step where the condition fails.

Trap 5: Accumulator Reset Bug
  -> Accumulators (e.g. `sum = 0`, `fact = 1`) must ALWAYS be initialized OUTSIDE and BEFORE the loop begins.
