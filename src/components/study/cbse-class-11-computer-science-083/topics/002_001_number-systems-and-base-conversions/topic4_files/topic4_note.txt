1. THE MATHEMATICAL RELATIONSHIP:
Because 16 = 2⁴, exactly FOUR binary bits (one Nibble) correspond to ONE hexadecimal digit (0 to 9, A to F).

2. HEXADECIMAL TO 4-BIT BINARY CONVERSION TABLE:
| Hex Digit | Decimal Value | 4-Bit Binary (Nibble) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 0 | 0 | 0000 |
| 1 | 1 | 0001 |
| 2 | 2 | 0010 |
| 3 | 3 | 0011 |
| 4 | 4 | 0100 |
| 5 | 5 | 0101 |
| 6 | 6 | 0110 |
| 7 | 7 | 0111 |
| 8 | 8 | 1000 |
| 9 | 9 | 1001 |
| A | 10 | 1010 |
| B | 11 | 1011 |

| D | 13 | 1101 |
| E | 14 | 1110 |
| F | 15 | 1111 |

3. BINARY TO HEXADECIMAL CONVERSION PROCEDURE:
- Step 1: Divide the binary integer into groups of 4 bits (nibbles), starting from the RIGHT (LSB) moving towards the LEFT.
- Step 2: If the leftmost group has fewer than 4 bits, pad with leading zeros on the left.
- Step 3: Replace each 4-bit nibble with its corresponding hex character (0-9, A-F).

Example 1: Convert (1101111101)₂ to Hexadecimal
Group from right: (11) (0111) (1101)
Pad left group:   (0011) (0111) (1101)
Convert nibbles:    3       7       D
Result: (1101111101)₂ = (37D)₁₆

4. HEXADECIMAL TO BINARY CONVERSION PROCEDURE:
- Step 1: Take each hexadecimal digit individually.
- Step 2: Write its exact 4-bit binary representation (e.g., A -> 1010, 3 -> 0011).
- Step 3: Concatenate all 4-bit nibbles together.

Example 2: Convert (9E5)₁₆ to Binary
9 -> 1001
E -> 1110 (14)
5 -> 0101
Result: (9E5)₁₆ = (100111100101)₂

5. CBSE EXAMINATION PITFALLS & TIPS:
- Common Mistake: Writing 3 as "11" instead of the full 4-bit nibble "0011". Every single hex digit MUST expand to exactly 4 binary bits!
- Hexadecimal characters A-F must be written in uppercase in board exam answers.
