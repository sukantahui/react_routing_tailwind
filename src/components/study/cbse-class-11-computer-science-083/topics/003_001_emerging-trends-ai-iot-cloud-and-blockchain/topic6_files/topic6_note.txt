1. WHAT IS BIG DATA?
   - Big Data refers to extremely massive, complex, and fast-accumulating datasets
     that exceed the processing, storage, and management capacities of traditional
     relational database management systems (RDBMS) like standard MySQL or Oracle.
   - Requires distributed architectures (e.g., Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark, NoSQL).

2. THE 5 FUNDAMENTAL 'Vs' OF BIG DATA:

   A. VOLUME (The Colossal Quantity):
      - Represents the gigantic scale and amount of data generated every second.
      - Measured in Terabytes (TB), Petabytes (PB), Exabytes (EB), Zettabytes (ZB), and Yottabytes (YB).
      - Sources: Social media posts, global financial stock tickers, IoT sensor streams,
        high-resolution satellite imagery.

   B. VELOCITY (The Speed of Generation & Ingestion):
      - Refers to the lightning-fast rate at which data is generated, streamed, and processed.
      - Requires real-time or near-real-time streaming analytics (e.g., credit card fraud detection
        must occur in milliseconds before transaction approval).
      - Examples: Twitter feeds, online trading, live GPS vehicle tracking, smart power grids.

   C. VARIETY (The Diversity of Data Formats):
      - Big Data is not just clean tabular SQL rows; it arrives in diverse types:
        1. Structured Data: Highly organized in fixed rows & columns with schemas (SQL tables, CSV).
        2. Semi-Structured Data: Data with flexible metadata/tags but no strict table schema (JSON, XML, NoSQL).
        3. Unstructured Data: Data with no predefined conceptual structure (Videos, PDF documents,
           audio recordings, JPEG photos, satellite scans). [Comprises ~80% of enterprise data].

   D. VERACITY (The Trustworthiness & Quality):
      - Refers to the reliability, accuracy, noise level, and trustworthiness of the collected data.
      - Incomplete records, duplicate entries, sensor glitches, typos, and malicious bots
        compromise data veracity.
      - Data Preprocessing (cleaning, deduplication, imputation) is mandatory before analytics.

   E. VALUE (The Ultimate Actionable Insight):
      - The most vital V: having petabytes of data is useless unless it is transformed into
        actionable insights, strategic business value, predictive forecasts, or societal benefits.
      - Examples: Personalized cancer treatment protocols, predictive traffic decongestion,
        targeted marketing recommendations.

3. DATA SCALE UNITS HIERARCHY (FOR CBSE BOARD EXAMS):
   1 Byte (B)       = 8 bits
   1 Kilobyte (KB)  = 1,024 Bytes
   1 Megabyte (MB)  = 1,024 KB
   1 Gigabyte (GB)  = 1,024 MB
   1 Terabyte (TB)  = 1,024 GB
   1 Petabyte (PB)  = 1,024 TB
   1 Exabyte (EB)   = 1,024 PB
   1 Zettabyte (ZB) = 1,024 EB
   1 Yottabyte (YB) = 1,024 ZB

4. REAL-WORLD BIG DATA APPLICATIONS:
   - Healthcare: Epidemic disease outbreak tracking, genomic sequencing analysis.
   - Banking & FinTech: Real-time fraud detection, automated credit risk scoring.
   - Smart Cities: Traffic signal timing optimization based on live camera feeds.
   - E-Commerce: Dynamic pricing algorithms, hyper-personalized product recommendation.
