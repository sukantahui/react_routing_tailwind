1. STRUCTURE OF THE FINAL EXAMINATION:
   - Total Questions: 30 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
   - Syllabus Coverage:
     * Questions 1 - 5   : Artificial Intelligence, Turing Test, Weak vs Strong AI
     * Questions 6 - 9   : Machine Learning (Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement) & Deep Learning
     * Questions 10 - 13 : Natural Language Processing (NLP), Speech Recognition & Chatbots
     * Questions 14 - 17 : Immersive Experiences (AR, VR, MR, 3-DoF vs 6-DoF)
     * Questions 18 - 21 : Robotics Subsystems (Sensors, Actuators, Controllers, da Vinci Surgery)
     * Questions 22 - 24 : Big Data 5 Vs (Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity, Value) & Storage Scale
     * Questions 25 - 27 : Internet of Things (IoT Architecture, RFID FASTag, Smart Cities)
     * Questions 28 - 29 : Cloud Computing Delivery (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) & Deployment Models (Public, Private, Hybrid)
     * Question 30       : Grid Computing & Cryptographic Blockchain (SHA-256, Proof of Work)

2. SUBJECTIVE QUESTION PATTERNS FOR 2-MARK & 3-MARK BOARD QUESTIONS:
   Q1. Differentiate between Supervised Learning and Unsupervised Learning with suitable examples.
   Q2. Explain the 'Sense-Plan-Act' control cycle in an autonomous robot.
   Q3. List and explain the 5 Vs of Big Data.
   Q4. Contrast Augmented Reality (AR) with Virtual Reality (VR) across three parameters.
   Q5. What is Cloud Bursting in a Hybrid Cloud deployment?
   Q6. How does SHA-256 previous-hash chaining enforce immutability in a blockchain?
   Q7. Explain the role of passive RFID technology in FASTag highway toll collection.

3. GOLDEN RULES FOR MAXIMUM MARKS IN CBSE CLASS 11 CS (083):
   - Never write single-line answers for 2-mark or 3-mark questions.
   - Always mention the standard NIST definition for Cloud Computing.
   - Use tabular layouts with clear column headers for distinction questions.
   - Always accompany ML algorithms with classification vs regression distinctions.
