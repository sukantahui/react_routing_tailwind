1. WHAT IS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (AI)?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of Computer Science dedicated to creating systems, algorithms, and machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.
* Key Cognitive Capabilities of AI:
  - Perception (Computer Vision, Speech Recognition)
  - Reasoning & Deductive Logic (Inference Engines, Expert Systems)
  - Learning & Adaptation (Machine Learning, Neural Networks)
  - Problem Solving & Planning (Search Algorithms, Game Theory)
  - Natural Language Understanding (NLP, Sentiment Analysis)

2. HISTORICAL MILESTONES IN AI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 1950 - Alan Turing: Proposed the famous "Turing Test" (Imitation Game) to evaluate whether a machine can exhibit indistinguishable human intelligence.
* 1956 - John McCarthy: Coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" at the Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence. Known as the "Father of AI".
* 1966 - ELIZA: First natural language conversational program (Joseph Weizenbaum).
* 1997 - IBM Deep Blue: Defeated reigning World Chess Champion Garry Kasparov.
* 2016 - Google DeepMind AlphaGo: Defeated World Go Champion Lee Sedol using Deep Reinforcement Learning.
* 2022+ - Generative AI & Large Language Models: Transformer architectures powering modern conversational AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude).

3. THE THREE LEVELS OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(A) Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI / Weak AI):
    - Dedicated to ONE specific task or narrow domain.
    - Cannot generalize beyond its programmed domain.
    - All currently existing AI systems are Weak / Narrow AI.
    - Examples: Apple Siri, Google Maps navigation, Spam filters, Chess engines, Medical diagnostic scanners.

(B) Artificial General Intelligence (AGI / Strong AI):
    - Hypothetical future AI possessing human-level cognitive versatility.
    - Can learn, reason, comprehend context, solve unfamiliar problems, and transfer knowledge across unrelated domains like a human brain.
    - Currently in active global scientific research.

(C) Artificial Superintelligence (ASI):
    - Theoretical evolutionary stage where machine intelligence surpasses the collective intellectual capabilities of all human minds combined across every field (science, arts, emotional intelligence).

4. COMPONENTS OF AN EXPERT SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An Expert System is an AI program that emulates the decision-making ability of a human specialist.
Composed of three primary components:
1. Knowledge Base: Repository of domain-specific facts, rules (IF-THEN statements), and heuristics provided by human domain experts.
2. Inference Engine: The reasoning brain that applies logical rules to user-supplied facts to deduce conclusions.
3. User Interface: Allows non-expert users to query the system and receive expert recommendations with explanations.
* Examples: MYCIN (medical diagnosis for bacterial blood infections), DENDRAL (chemical structure analysis).

5. REAL-WORLD APPLICATIONS OF AI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Healthcare: MRI/CT tumor detection, genomic drug discovery, robotic surgical assistants.
* Transportation: Autonomous self-driving vehicles (Tesla, Waymo), automated air traffic management.
* Finance: Algorithmic high-frequency trading, automated fraud transaction detection.
* E-Commerce: Personalized product recommendation engines (Amazon, Netflix).
