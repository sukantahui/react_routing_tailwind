2. THE 5 CAPSTONE WORKED EXERCISES & SOLUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXERCISE 1: High-Value Student Roster with GST Calculation
-- Question: Find names of Barrackpore students with admission fee > ₹4000 and calculate 18% GST.
-- Relational Algebra:
   $$\pi_{\text{full\_name, admission\_fee}, \text{admission\_fee} \times 1.18 \rightarrow \text{fee\_with\_gst}}(\sigma_{\text{city='Barrackpore'} \land \text{admission\_fee} > 4000}(\text{Students}))$$
-- SQL:
   SELECT full_name, admission_fee, ROUND(admission_fee * 1.18, 2) AS fee_with_gst
   FROM students
   WHERE city = 'Barrackpore' AND admission_fee > 4000;

EXERCISE 2: Left Anti-Join for Inactive / Unenrolled Students
-- Question: Find students who have NEVER enrolled in any course.
-- Relational Algebra:
   $$\text{Students} \triangleright \text{Enrollments} \equiv \text{Students} - (\text{Students} \ltimes \text{Enrollments})$$
-- SQL:
   SELECT s.* FROM students s
   LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id
   WHERE e.student_id IS NULL;

EXERCISE 3: Relational Division for Universal Core Completers
-- Question: Find students who have completed ALL courses listed in `Core_Courses`.
-- Relational Algebra:
   $$\pi_{\text{student\_id, course\_id}}(\text{Enrollments}) \div \text{Core\_Courses}$$
-- SQL:
   SELECT s.student_id, s.full_name
   FROM enrollments e JOIN students s ON e.student_id = s.student_id
   WHERE e.course_id IN (SELECT course_id FROM core_courses)
   GROUP BY s.student_id, s.full_name
   HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT e.course_id) = (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM core_courses);

EXERCISE 4: City-Wise Revenue Breakdown with HAVING Filter
-- Question: Find cities with at least 2 enrolled students and average fee >= ₹4500.
-- Relational Algebra:
   $$\sigma_{\text{cnt} \ge 2 \land \text{avg} \ge 4500}({}_{\text{city}} \mathcal{G}_{\text{COUNT}(*) \rightarrow \text{cnt}, \text{AVG(fee)} \rightarrow \text{avg}}(\text{Students}))$$
-- SQL:
   SELECT city, COUNT(*) AS student_count, AVG(admission_fee) AS avg_fee
   FROM students
   GROUP BY city
   HAVING COUNT(*) >= 2 AND AVG(admission_fee) >= 4500;

EXERCISE 5: 360° Academic Audit (Full Outer Join Emulation)
-- Question: Produce a complete roster showing all students and courses, including unmatched rows.
-- Relational Algebra: $\text{Students} \mathbin{\unicode{x27D7}} \text{Courses}$
-- SQL:
   SELECT s.student_id, s.full_name, c.course_id, c.course_title
   FROM students s LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id LEFT JOIN courses c ON e.course_id = c.course_id
   UNION
   SELECT s.student_id, s.full_name, c.course_id, c.course_title
   FROM students s RIGHT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id RIGHT JOIN courses c ON e.course_id = c.course_id;


3. COMMON PITFALLS & DANGER ZONES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Forgetting to project out extra columns before Relational Division ($\div$).
2. Using `UNION ALL` instead of `UNION` in Full Outer Join emulations.
3. Filtering right-table attributes in `WHERE` instead of `ON` during Left Outer Joins.
