1. WHAT ARE OUTER JOINS IN RELATIONAL ALGEBRA?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard Inner Joins (⨝) discard tuples from either relation that do not satisfy the join condition.
Outer Joins extend relational algebra to PRESERVE unmatched (dangling) tuples by padding missing
attributes from the non-matching relation with `NULL` values.

THE 3 TYPES OF OUTER JOINS:
1. LEFT OUTER JOIN ($R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D5}}_\theta S$ or $R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D5}} S$):
   - Preserves ALL tuples from the Left relation ($R$).
   - If a tuple in $R$ has no matching tuple in $S$, attributes of $S$ are padded with `NULL`.
   - Mathematical Derivation:
     $$R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D5}}_\theta S = (R \bowtie_\theta S) \cup ((R \triangleright_\theta S) \times \text{NULL}_S)$$

2. RIGHT OUTER JOIN ($R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D6}}_\theta S$):
   - Preserves ALL tuples from the Right relation ($S$).
   - If a tuple in $S$ has no matching tuple in $R$, attributes of $R$ are padded with `NULL`.
   - Commutative Equivalence: $R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D6}}_\theta S \equiv S \mathbin{\unicode{x27D5}}_{\theta'} R$.

3. FULL OUTER JOIN ($R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D7}}_\theta S$):
   - Preserves ALL tuples from BOTH relations $R$ and $S$.
   - Unmatched tuples from either side are padded with `NULL`s.
   - Mathematical Derivation:
     $$R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D7}}_\theta S = (R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D5}}_\theta S) \cup (R \mathbin{\unicode{x27D6}}_\theta S)$$


2. FULL OUTER JOIN EMULATION IN MYSQL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Standard ANSI SQL provides `FULL OUTER JOIN`, but MySQL does NOT natively support the `FULL JOIN` syntax.
• In MySQL, Full Outer Join is emulated via `UNION` of Left and Right Outer Joins:
  `SELECT * FROM students s LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id`
  `UNION`
  `SELECT * FROM students s RIGHT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id;`


3. REAL-WORLD CASE STUDIES (BARRACKPORE & KOLKATA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE 1: Complete Student Roster with Enrolled Courses (Left Outer Join)
-- Relational Algebra: $\text{Students} \mathbin{\unicode{x27D5}} \text{Enrollments}$
-- Shows all students (even those with 0 enrolled courses showing NULL).
SELECT s.student_id, s.full_name, e.course_id, e.grade
FROM students s
LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id;

CASE 2: Complete Course Catalog with Student Counts (Right Outer Join)
-- Relational Algebra: $\text{Enrollments} \mathbin{\unicode{x27D6}} \text{Courses}$
-- Shows all courses (even new courses with 0 enrolled students).
SELECT c.course_id, c.course_title, e.student_id
FROM enrollments e
RIGHT JOIN courses c ON e.course_id = c.course_id;

CASE 3: Complete 360° Academic Audit (Full Outer Join Emulation)
-- Relational Algebra: $\text{Students} \mathbin{\unicode{x27D7}} \text{Courses}$
SELECT s.student_id, s.full_name, c.course_id, c.course_title
FROM students s LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id LEFT JOIN courses c ON e.course_id = c.course_id
UNION
SELECT s.student_id, s.full_name, c.course_id, c.course_title
FROM students s RIGHT JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id RIGHT JOIN courses c ON e.course_id = c.course_id;


4. COMMON PITFALLS & DANGER ZONES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Converting Left Join to Inner Join via `WHERE` clause:
   - Writing `WHERE e.grade = 'A+'` filters out all `NULL` rows produced by the Left Join!
   - Fix: Move filter into the `ON` condition: `LEFT JOIN enrollments e ON s.id = e.id AND e.grade = 'A+'`.
2. Using `UNION ALL` instead of `UNION` in Full Outer Join emulation:
   - `UNION ALL` duplicates the inner join matched rows! Always use `UNION` (which deduplicates).
