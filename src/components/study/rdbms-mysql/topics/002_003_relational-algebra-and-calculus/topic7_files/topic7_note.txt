1. THE THETA JOIN (⨝θ) DEFINITION & MATHEMATICAL SEMANTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Theta Join (denoted by the bowtie symbol with a theta subscript, $\bowtie_\theta$)
is a binary relational algebra operator that combines related tuples from two relations
based on a specified comparison condition $\theta$.

MATHEMATICAL DEFINITION:
$$R \bowtie_\theta S = \sigma_\theta(R \times S)$$
where $\theta$ is a boolean predicate of the form:
$$A_i \ \theta \ B_j$$
where $\theta \in \{ =, \neq, <, \le, >, \ge \}$, $A_i$ is an attribute of $R$, and $B_j$ is an attribute of $S$.

KEY MATHEMATICAL PROPERTIES:
1. DEGREE (ARITY):
   $$\text{Degree}(R \bowtie_\theta S) = \text{Degree}(R) + \text{Degree}(S)$$
   (Both join key columns $A_i$ and $B_j$ are preserved in the output schema).
2. CARDINALITY BOUNDS:
   $$0 \le |R \bowtie_\theta S| \le |R| \times |S|$$
3. COMMUTATIVE (UP TO TUPLE ORDER):
   $$R \bowtie_\theta S \equiv S \bowtie_{\theta'} R$$


2. EQUIJOIN (A SPECIAL CASE OF THETA JOIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An Equijoin is a Theta Join where the comparison operator $\theta$ is STRICTLY EQUALITY ($=$):
$$\theta \equiv (A_i = B_j)$$
• Example: $\text{Students} \bowtie_{\text{Students.student\_id} = \text{Enrollments.student\_id}} \text{Enrollments}$
• Equijoins preserve BOTH matching columns in the result schema (unlike Natural Join, which projects away the duplicate column).


3. NON-EQUI THETA JOINS (RANGE & INEQUALITY JOINS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When $\theta \in \{ <, \le, >, \ge, \text{BETWEEN} \}$, it is a Non-Equi Join:
• Salary Grade Band Matching: $\text{Employees} \bowtie_{\text{salary} \ge \text{min\_sal} \land \text{salary} \le \text{max\_sal}} \text{Salary\_Grades}$
• Temporal Interval Overlaps: $\text{Bookings} \bowtie_{\text{start\_time} < \text{other.end\_time} \land \text{end\_time} > \text{other.start\_time}} \text{Bookings}$


4. REAL-WORLD CASE STUDIES (BARRACKPORE & KOLKATA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE 1: Equijoin on Student Enrollments (Mamata's Academy)
-- Relational Algebra:
-- $\text{Students} \bowtie_{\text{Students.student\_id} = \text{Enrollments.student\_id}} \text{Enrollments}$
SELECT s.student_id, s.full_name, e.course_id, e.enrolled_at
FROM students s
INNER JOIN enrollments e ON s.student_id = e.student_id;

CASE 2: Non-Equi Fee Band Matching (Debangshu's Kolkata Scholarship Audit)
-- Relational Algebra:
-- $\text{Students} \bowtie_{\text{Students.admission\_fee} \ge \text{Bands.min\_fee} \land \text{Students.admission\_fee} \le \text{Bands.max\_fee}} \text{Fee\_Bands}$
SELECT s.full_name, s.admission_fee, b.band_tier, b.scholarship_pct
FROM students s
INNER JOIN fee_bands b ON s.admission_fee >= b.min_fee AND s.admission_fee <= b.max_fee;


5. COMMON PITFALLS & DANGER ZONES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Confusing Equijoin with Natural Join:
   - Equijoin keeps BOTH columns (`R.id` and `S.id`); Natural Join keeps only ONE single merged column.
2. Inefficient Nested Loop Scans on Non-Equi Joins:
   - Non-equi joins ($>, <$) cannot use Hash Join algorithms and often require Range B-Tree index scans.


6. BEST PRACTICES & SUMMARY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[x] Use Theta Join notation: $R \bowtie_\theta S \equiv \sigma_\theta(R \times S)$.
[x] Identify Equijoins as theta joins with equality ($=$) predicates.
[x] Use Non-Equi joins for salary bands, grading tiers, and temporal interval overlap checks.
[x] Index join foreign keys with B-Tree indexes to enable sub-millisecond Equijoin performance.
