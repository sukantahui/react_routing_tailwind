1. HIGH AVAILABILITY (HA) & THE "NINES" OF AVAILABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
High Availability (HA) refers to an architectural design that ensures a database system
remains operational and accessible with minimal unplanned downtime over a given period:

Availability Level    Downtime per Year    Downtime per Month    Classification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
99.0%  (2 Nines)      3.65 days            7.31 hours            Basic
99.9%  (3 Nines)      8.76 hours           43.8 minutes          Standard Business
99.99% (4 Nines)      52.56 minutes        4.38 minutes          Enterprise High Availability
99.999% (5 Nines)     5.26 minutes         25.9 seconds          Mission-Critical / Telco / Tier-1 Bank

3. DISASTER RECOVERY METRICS: RPO & RTO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. RPO (Recovery Point Objective):
   - The maximum acceptable age of data that can be lost when an unexpected disaster strikes.
   - RPO = 0 means ZERO data loss tolerance (requires synchronous or lossless semi-sync replication).

2. RTO (Recovery Time Objective):
   - The maximum acceptable duration of time allowed to restore database availability after an outage.
   - RTO < 30 seconds requires automated failover clustering (e.g. MySQL InnoDB Cluster / Orchestrator).

4. CAP THEOREM & PACELC IN MYSQL REPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In the event of a network partition (P):
  - Asynchronous Replication chooses Availability (A) over strict Consistency (C) -> RPO > 0.
  - Lossless Semi-Synchronous Replication and MySQL Group Replication prioritize Consistency (C) -> RPO = 0.

5. PRODUCTION BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Eliminate all Single Points of Failure (SPOFs) by pairing primary databases with standby replicas.
2. Define explicit RPO and RTO SLAs before choosing replication topologies.
3. Use Read-Replicas to offload read-heavy reporting queries from the transactional primary.
4. Enforce automated health checks and split-brain fencing on all HA clusters.
