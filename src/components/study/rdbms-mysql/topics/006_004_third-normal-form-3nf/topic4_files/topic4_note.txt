2. THEORETICAL FOUNDATION & MATRICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core Concept: Relational normalization ensures that data dependencies are modeled
  strictly according to business invariants, preventing update, deletion, and insertion anomalies.
- Formal Rule: 1. Transitive Dependency (X -> Y and Y -> Z where X is Primary Key)
- Decomposition Strategy: Extract Department entity into its own relation with dept_id as Primary Key.

3. BEFORE-VS-AFTER SCHEMA TRANSFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNNORMALIZED / NON-COMPLIANT STATE:
In employee(emp_id, emp_name, dept_id, dept_name, dept_location), dept_location depends on dept_id, which depends on emp_id.
Root Cause: Violates 3NF: No non-prime attribute may be transitively dependent on a candidate key through another non-prime attribute.

NORMALIZED STATE:
Updating department location now requires modifying exactly 1 row in the departments table.

4. SENIOR ARCHITECTURAL PITFALLS & BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- PITFALL: Whenever a non-key determines another non-key, update anomalies occur when department location changes.
- BEST PRACTICE: Updating department location now requires modifying exactly 1 row in the departments table.
- ACID INVARIANT: Always declare explicit foreign keys with ON DELETE RESTRICT to protect transactional ledgers.
