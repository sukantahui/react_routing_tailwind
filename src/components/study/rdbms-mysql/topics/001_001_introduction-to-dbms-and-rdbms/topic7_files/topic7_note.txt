2. KEY THEORETICAL PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Data Integrity & Persistence:
   Relational databases guarantee structured storage, minimal redundancy,
   and deterministic querying via ANSI SQL.

2. Relational Modeling & Constraints:
   Entity identity, domain validation, and referential constraints (Primary Keys,
   Foreign Keys, Unique checks) protect data against modification anomalies.

3. Real-World Applications:
   Modern software architectures—from retail billing systems in Barrackpore to
   core banking ledgers in Kolkata—rely on these principles for ACID reliability.

3. SENIOR PITFALLS & BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Avoid treating unstructured files as databases for concurrent multi-user workloads.
- Always enforce domain constraints at the database level rather than application memory.
- Choose explicit data types and avoid storing delimited strings in scalar columns.

4. TEACHER'S MASTER SUMMARY - SIR SUKANTA HUI
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mastering fundamentals gives you the confidence to architect large enterprise systems.
Observe the data flows around you, distinguish between raw data and meaningful information,
and uphold strict relational design standards!
