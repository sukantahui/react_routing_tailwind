2. THEORETICAL FOUNDATION & MATRICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Core Concept: Relational normalization ensures that data dependencies are modeled
  strictly according to business invariants, preventing update, deletion, and insertion anomalies.
- Formal Rule: 1. Join Dependencies (*[R1, R2, R3]) and 3-Way Cyclic Constraints
- Decomposition Strategy: Split into 3 binary tables: SP(supplier, part), PJ(part, project), and SJ(supplier, project).

3. BEFORE-VS-AFTER SCHEMA TRANSFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNNORMALIZED / NON-COMPLIANT STATE:
In Supplier-Part-Project (SPJ), a supplier S supplies part P to project J only if S supplies P, P is used in J, and S supplies J.
Root Cause: This 3-way cyclic constraint cannot be decomposed into two binary tables without loss of constraint, but CAN be decomposed into 3 tables.

NORMALIZED STATE:
Zero spurious tuples upon 3-way natural join; complete integrity preserved.

4. SENIOR ARCHITECTURAL PITFALLS & BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- PITFALL: Only a 3-way natural join of (SP ⨝ PJ ⨝ SJ) faithfully reconstructs the original SPJ relation.
- BEST PRACTICE: Zero spurious tuples upon 3-way natural join; complete integrity preserved.
- ACID INVARIANT: Always declare explicit foreign keys with ON DELETE RESTRICT to protect transactional ledgers.
