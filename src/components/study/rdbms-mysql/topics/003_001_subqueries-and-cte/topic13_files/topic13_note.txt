1. MODULE 003_001 CAPSTONE LAB OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This capstone practical lab consolidates the comprehensive toolkit of advanced 
subquery and CTE patterns learned across Topics 0 through 12.

2. THE FOUR CAPSTONE CHALLENGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Challenge 1: The Executive Dean's Merit Scholarship Pipeline
- Architecture: 4-stage chained CTE combining raw filtering, department-level 
  pre-aggregations, `DENSE_RANK()` window ranking, and scholarship tier assignment.

Challenge 2: Universal Prerequisite Dependency Resolution
- Architecture: Recursive Bottom-Up CTE resolving arbitrary multi-tier course 
  prerequisite trees in real-time.

Challenge 3: Continuous 30-Day Revenue & Attendance Gap Audit
- Architecture: Recursive Date Grid generator + `LEFT JOIN` zero-fill + Anti-Join 
  identifying unrecorded student attendance dates.

Challenge 4: Correlated Defaulter Account Settlement & Purge
- Architecture: Multi-table `UPDATE ... JOIN` flagging defaulters based on correlated 
  payment totals, followed by safe `DELETE` using `NOT EXISTS`.
