1. WHAT ARE DATE EXTRACTION & FORMATTING FUNCTIONS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Date extraction and formatting functions allow SQL developers to isolate specific components 
of a date/time (e.g. year, month name, day of week), format raw timestamps into executive 
reporting strings, and parse non-standard text strings back into relational date types.

Key Functions Covered:
1. Component Extractors: `YEAR()`, `MONTH()`, `DAY()` / `DAYOFMONTH()`, `HOUR()`, `MINUTE()`, `SECOND()`.
2. Calendar Information: `MONTHNAME()`, `DAYNAME()`, `DAYOFWEEK()`, `DAYOFYEAR()`, `QUARTER()`, `WEEK()`.
3. Standard ANSI Extractor: `EXTRACT(unit FROM date)`.
4. Output String Formatter: `DATE_FORMAT(date, format_mask)`.
5. Input String Parser: `STR_TO_DATE(str, format_mask)`.


2. DATE_FORMAT() SPECIFIERS CHEATSHEET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Format specifiers begin with a `%` symbol:
• `%Y`: 4-digit Year (`2026`).
• `%y`: 2-digit Year (`26`).
• `%M`: Full Month Name (`August`).
• `%b`: Abbreviated Month Name (`Aug`).
• `%m`: 2-digit Month (`01` to `12`).
• `%c`: Month without leading zero (`1` to `12`).
• `%d`: 2-digit Day of month with leading zero (`01` to `31`).
• `%e`: Day of month without leading zero (`1` to `31`).
• `%D`: Day of month with English suffix (`24th`, `1st`, `2nd`, `3rd`).
• `%W`: Full Weekday Name (`Monday`).
• `%a`: Abbreviated Weekday (`Mon`).
• `%H`: 24-hour Hour (`00` to `23`).
• `%h` / `%I`: 12-hour Hour (`01` to `12`).
• `%i`: 2-digit Minute (`00` to `59`).
• `%s` / `%S`: 2-digit Second (`00` to `59`).
• `%p`: AM or PM.

Real-World Indian Reporting Format:
`DATE_FORMAT(admission_date, '%d-%b-%Y')` ➔ `'24-Aug-2026'`.
`DATE_FORMAT(NOW(), '%W, %D %M %Y at %h:%i %p')` ➔ `'Monday, 24th August 2026 at 02:30 PM'`.


3. STR_TO_DATE(): PARSING RAW USER INPUT STRINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When importing external CSV files or third-party bank statements with non-standard date text:
• Input: `'24/08/2026'` (DD/MM/YYYY Indian format).
• Query: `STR_TO_DATE('24/08/2026', '%d/%m/%Y')` ➔ Produces valid `DATE` `'2026-08-24'`.
• US Format: `STR_TO_DATE('08-24-2026', '%m-%d-%Y')` ➔ `'2026-08-24'`.


4. EXTRACT(UNIT FROM DATE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard ANSI SQL syntax to extract specific temporal parts:
• `EXTRACT(YEAR FROM NOW())` ➔ `2026`.
• `EXTRACT(MONTH FROM NOW())` ➔ `8`.
• `EXTRACT(QUARTER FROM NOW())` ➔ `3`.
• `EXTRACT(YEAR_MONTH FROM NOW())` ➔ `202608`.


5. BENCHMARK ACADEMY & COMMERCE EXAMPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
```sql
-- Generating monthly revenue summaries and executive formatted dates:
SELECT 
    student_id,
    student_name,
    DATE_FORMAT(admission_date, '%d-%b-%Y') AS display_date,
    DAYNAME(admission_date) AS day_enrolled,
    MONTHNAME(admission_date) AS enrollment_month,
    QUARTER(admission_date) AS financial_quarter,
    DATE_FORMAT(NOW(), '%D %M %Y (%h:%i %p)') AS printable_timestamp
FROM students;
```
