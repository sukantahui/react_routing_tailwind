================================================================================
TOPIC 9: WORKED EXAMPLE 3 – PROFIT MAXIMIZATION WITH TWO PRODUCTS
================================================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------
A company manufactures two products: Product X and Product Y.
The company has three resources: Machining, Assembly, and Packaging.

Resource requirements per unit:
              Product X   Product Y   Available
Machining       2 hrs       4 hrs      100 hrs
Assembly        3 hrs       2 hrs       90 hrs
Packaging       1 hr        2 hrs       50 hrs

Profit per unit: Product X = ₹60, Product Y = ₹80

The company wants to maximize total profit.

2. STEP-BY-STEP FORMULATION
---------------------------

STEP 1: Read and Understand
- Goal: Maximize profit
- Products: X and Y (2 products)
- Resources: Machining, Assembly, Packaging (3 resources)
- No demand constraints

STEP 2: Identify Decision Variables
- x₁ = number of units of Product X produced
- x₂ = number of units of Product Y produced

STEP 3: Identify the Objective
- Profit per unit: X = ₹60, Y = ₹80
- Maximize Z = 60x₁ + 80x₂

STEP 4: Identify Constraints
- Machining: 2x₁ + 4x₂ ≤ 100
- Assembly: 3x₁ + 2x₂ ≤ 90
- Packaging: 1x₁ + 2x₂ ≤ 50

STEP 5: Add Non-negativity
- x₁ ≥ 0, x₂ ≥ 0

STEP 6: Review and Validate
- Test x₁ = 0, x₂ = 25:
  Machining = 100 ≤ 100 ✓
  Assembly = 50 ≤ 90 ✓
  Packaging = 50 ≤ 50 ✓

- Test x₁ = 30, x₂ = 0:
  Machining = 60 ≤ 100 ✓
  Assembly = 90 ≤ 90 ✓
  Packaging = 30 ≤ 50 ✓

STEP 7: Document
- Final Model:
  Maximize Z = 60x₁ + 80x₂
  Subject to:
    Machining: 2x₁ + 4x₂ ≤ 100
    Assembly: 3x₁ + 2x₂ ≤ 90
    Packaging: x₁ + 2x₂ ≤ 50
    x₁ ≥ 0, x₂ ≥ 0

3. GRAPHICAL SOLUTION
---------------------
Step 1: Plot constraints on x₁-x₂ plane
Step 2: Identify feasible region (intersection of all half-planes)
Step 3: Find corner points:
  - (0, 0)
  - (0, 25)
  - (20, 15)
  - (30, 0)
  - (0, 0) is also a corner

Step 4: Evaluate objective at each corner:
  - (0, 0): Z = 0
  - (0, 25): Z = 60(0) + 80(25) = ₹2,000
  - (20, 15): Z = 60(20) + 80(15) = 1,200 + 1,200 = ₹2,400
  - (30, 0): Z = 60(30) + 80(0) = ₹1,800

Step 5: Optimal solution: (20, 15) with profit = ₹2,400

4. INTERPRETATION
-----------------
- Produce 20 units of Product X and 15 units of Product Y.
- Total profit = ₹2,400.
- Resource usage at optimum:
  - Machining: 2(20) + 4(15) = 40 + 60 = 100 hrs (fully used)
  - Assembly: 3(20) + 2(15) = 60 + 30 = 90 hrs (fully used)
  - Packaging: 20 + 30 = 50 hrs (fully used)
  - All resources are binding!

5. WHAT WE LEARN
----------------
- All three resources are fully used at the optimum.
- Profit contribution: X = ₹60/unit, Y = ₹80/unit.
- The optimal solution occurs at the intersection of Machining and Assembly constraints.
- The Packaging constraint is also binding at this point.

6. KEY TAKEAWAYS
-----------------
- Profit maximization problems often involve selecting the optimal mix.
- The graphical method works for 2-variable problems.
- Multiple constraints can be binding at the optimal solution.
- The optimal solution occurs at a corner point of the feasible region.
