TOPIC 46 – THE BEST FINANCIAL OUTCOME

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS MAXIMUM PROFIT?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The maximum profit is the highest value of the profit function (Z) that can
be achieved within the feasible region.

- The highest Z value among all corner points
- Tells you the best possible financial outcome
- Must be achievable (feasible)

Key idea: The maximum profit is the answer to profit maximization problems!

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO FIND MAXIMUM PROFIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Evaluate Z at every corner point
Step 2: Compare all Z values
Step 3: Select the highest value
Step 4: State the maximum profit with context

Example: Z values: 0, 15, 24, 22, 20
  → Maximum profit = ₹24 at (4,3)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EXAMPLES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Profit Maximization:
  Corner values: 0, 15, 24, 22, 20
  Maximum profit: ₹24 at (4,3)
  Context: Produce 4 X and 3 Y for ₹24 profit

Factory Production:
  Corner values: 0, 20, 16, 12
  Maximum profit: ₹20 at (4,0)
  Context: Produce 4 X and 0 Y for ₹20 profit

Multiple Optima:
  Corner values: 0, 10, 10
  Maximum profit: ₹10 at (10,0) or (0,10)
  Context: Either mix gives ₹10 profit
