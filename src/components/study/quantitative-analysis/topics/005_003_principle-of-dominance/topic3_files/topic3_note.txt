--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE NEED FOR MODIFIED DOMINANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In many game matrices, NO single pure row dominates another pure row, and NO single
pure column dominates another pure column. The standard dominance check stalls (Deadlock).

THE SOLUTION: MODIFIED DOMINANCE (CONVEX COMBINATION)
• A pure strategy may be dominated by a WEIGHTED AVERAGE (Convex Combination)
  of two or more other strategies!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• MODIFIED ROW DOMINANCE (PLAYER A):
  - Row j is dominated if there exist weights lambda_1, lambda_2 >= 0 (lambda_1 + lambda_2 = 1) such that:
        lambda_1 * a_{i_1, k} + lambda_2 * a_{i_2, k} >= a_{j, k}    for all columns k = 1, ..., n
  - ELIMINATE ROW j.

• MODIFIED COLUMN DOMINANCE (PLAYER B):
  - Column s is dominated if there exist weights mu_1, mu_2 >= 0 (mu_1 + mu_2 = 1) such that:
        mu_1 * a_{k, j_1} + mu_2 * a_{k, j_2} <= a_{k, s}          for all rows k = 1, ..., m
  - ELIMINATE COLUMN s.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE STANDARD 50-50 SIMPLE ARITHMETIC MEAN TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In practical examination problems, test lambda = 0.5 first:
• Average Row = (Row_1 + Row_2) / 2
  - If Average Row >= Row_3 across ALL columns, eliminate Row 3!
• Average Col = (Col_1 + Col_2) / 2
  - If Average Col <= Col_3 across ALL rows, eliminate Col 3!

4. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Precision Foundry Alloy Blending Modified Dominance (Debangshu)
Debangshu in Barrackpore had 3 casting schedules. Pure dominance failed. A 50-50 average
of Schedule 1 [₹40k, ₹20k] and Schedule 2 [₹20k, ₹40k] yielded [₹30k, ₹30k], strictly
dominating Schedule 3 [₹25k, ₹25k] and breaking the operational deadlock.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Cold-Chain Vaccine Logistics Hybrid Transport Dominance (Mamata & Mahima)
Mamata and Mahima in Kolkata averaged Reefer Truck (Col 1) and Air Courier (Col 2) to
synthesize a hybrid route [₹30k, ₹30k] that dominated Expensive Multi-Modal Transport (Col 3 = [₹35k, ₹35k]).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: Supermarket FMCG Retail Promotional Modified Dominance (Susmita)
Susmita in Ichapur eliminated a stagnant loyalty scheme (Strategy A3) by proving that
an equal blend of Strategy A1 (Bundle Discount) and Strategy A2 (Weekend Flash) dominated A3 by ₹5,000.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: Educational High-Tech Lab Patent Licensing Modified Dominance (Abhronila)
Abhronila used convex combination dominance in Jadavpur to prove that a 60-40 blend
of two standard licensing terms strictly dominated an aggressive arbitration clause.

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Test simple 50-50 averages first before trying arbitrary weights lambda.
- The average row must be GREATER than or equal to the target row across ALL columns.
- The average column must be LESS than or equal to the target column across ALL rows.
- State all convex averages and payoffs in Indian Rupees (₹).
