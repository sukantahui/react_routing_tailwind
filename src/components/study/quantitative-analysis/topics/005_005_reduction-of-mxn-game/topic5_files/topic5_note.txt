1. THE 4 DIMENSIONS OF MANAGERIAL INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. STRATEGY SUPPORT & RESOURCE ALLOCATION:
   • The surviving rows form the ACTIVE STRATEGY SUPPORT supp(p*).
   • Probabilities p* translate directly into proportional enterprise allocations:
     e.g., p* = [0.60, 0.00, 0.40, 0.00] means allocate 60% of shift hours to Strategy 1,
     40% to Strategy 3, and completely defund Strategies 2 and 4!

2. DEFUNDING OPERATIONAL DEAD-WEIGHT:
   • Pruned rows/columns are structurally dominated. In a corporate setting, continuing
     to invest in dominated options causes guaranteed margin bleed.

3. FINANCIAL VALUATION (v* in ₹):
   • Game Value v* is the long-run guaranteed expected profit per operational cycle.
   • A risk-neutral firm should accept any settlement or contractual buyout >= v*.

4. POST-OPTIMALITY & SENSITIVITY THRESHOLDS:
   • Evaluates how much a matrix cell payoff must change before a pruned strategy
     becomes non-dominated and enters the active support.

3. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never deploy a single pure strategy when the equilibrium prescribes a mixed support!
- Do not allocate capital or marketing budget to pruned strategies.
- Use v* to set baseline cash reserve thresholds in corporate treasuries.
- State all enterprise valuations in Indian Rupees (₹).
