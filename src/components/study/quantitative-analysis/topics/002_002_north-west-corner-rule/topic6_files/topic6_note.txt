1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

Purpose:
This topic presents a collection of numerical exercises designed to help you 
practice finding initial basic feasible solutions using different methods.

Key Insight:
Practice makes perfect. The more problems you solve, the more comfortable 
you'll become with the allocation procedures and the easier it will be to 
handle any transportation problem.

2. PROBLEM 1: SIMPLE 3×3 NW CORNER
================================================================================

Problem Data:
---------------------------------------------------------
Sources: 3 with supplies 50, 40, 30
Destinations: 3 with demands 40, 50, 30
Cost Matrix: Given in the table

Solution Steps:
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Start at (1,1): allocate min(50,40) = 40
2. Move to (1,2): allocate min(10,50) = 10
3. Move to (2,2): allocate min(40,40) = 40
4. Move to (3,3): allocate min(30,30) = 30

Result:
---------------------------------------------------------
Allocations: x₁₁=40, x₁₂=10, x₂₂=40, x₃₃=30
Total Cost: ₹840
Degeneracy: Yes (4 allocations < m+n-1=5)

3. PROBLEM 2: 2×3 LEAST COST
================================================================================

Problem Data:
---------------------------------------------------------
Sources: 2 with supplies 60, 50
Destinations: 3 with demands 40, 30, 40
Cost Matrix: Given in the table

Solution Steps:
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Find cheapest cell: (2,3) with cost 7
2. Allocate min(50,40) = 40
3. Next cheapest: (1,2) with cost 8
4. Allocate min(60,30) = 30
5. Continue until complete

Result:
---------------------------------------------------------
Allocations: x₂₃=40, x₁₂=30, x₂₁=10, x₁₁=30
Total Cost: ₹970
Degeneracy: No (4 allocations = m+n-1)

================================================================================

Problem Data:

6. KEY TAKEAWAYS
================================================================================

From These Exercises:
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Different methods give different solutions
2. NW Corner is fastest but poorest quality
3. Least Cost gives better quality solutions
4. VAM gives the best initial solutions
5. Degeneracy is common and must be handled
6. Always verify your solutions

7. COMMON MISTAKES
================================================================================

❌ Incorrect min Calculation:
   Using wrong values for min(Sᵢ, Dⱼ).

❌ Not Updating Values:
   Forgetting to update supplies and demands.

❌ Not Handling Degeneracy:
   Ignoring degeneracy in the solution.

❌ Cost Calculation Errors:
   Arithmetic errors in total cost.

❌ Rushing the Process:
   Making errors by going too fast.

❌ Not Verifying:
   Not checking row and column
