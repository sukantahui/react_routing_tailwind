================================================================================
TOPIC 17: WORKED EXAMPLE 11 – TRANSPORTATION-RELATED PRODUCTION PROBLEM
================================================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------
A company manufactures a product at two factories (Plant A and Plant B)
and ships it to three warehouses (W1, W2, W3) to meet demand.

Factory capacities:
- Plant A: 150 units per week
- Plant B: 200 units per week

Warehouse demands:
- W1: 100 units per week
- W2: 120 units per week
- W3: 130 units per week

Production costs per unit:
- Plant A: ₹50
- Plant B: ₹60

Shipping costs per unit from each plant to each warehouse:
              W1    W2    W3
Plant A       ₹8    ₹10   ₹12
Plant B       ₹12   ₹9    ₹7

The company wants to minimize total cost (production + shipping).

2. STEP-BY-STEP FORMULATION
---------------------------

STEP 1: Read and Understand
- Goal: Minimize total cost (production + shipping)
- Plants: A and B (2 factories)
- Warehouses: W1, W2, W3 (3 destinations)
- Capacities: A=150, B=200
- Demands: W1=100, W2=120, W3=130
- Production costs and shipping costs vary

STEP 2: Identify Decision Variables
Let xᵢⱼ = number of units produced at Plant i and shipped to Warehouse j

- x₁₁ = units from Plant A to W1
- x₁₂ = units from Plant A to W2
- x₁₃ = units from Plant A to W3
- x₂₁ = units from Plant B to W1
- x₂₂ = units from Plant B to W2
- x₂₃ = units from Plant B to W3

STEP 3: Identify the Objective
- Production cost: Plant A = ₹50, Plant B = ₹60
- Shipping cost: matrix given
- Minimize Z = 58x₁₁ + 60x₁₂ + 62x₁₃ + 72x₂₁ + 69x₂₂ + 67x₂₃
  (production + shipping)

STEP 4: Identify Constraints
- Plant A capacity: x₁₁ + x₁₂ + x₁₃ ≤ 150
- Plant B capacity: x₂₁ + x₂₂ + x₂₃ ≤ 200
- W1 demand: x₁₁ + x₂₁ ≥ 100
- W2 demand: x₁₂ + x₂₂ ≥ 120
- W3 demand: x₁₃ + x₂₃ ≥ 130

STEP 5: Add Non-negativity
- All xᵢⱼ ≥ 0

STEP 6: Review and Validate
- Test a feasible solution:
  Plant A to W1: 80, to W2: 70, to W3: 0 (total 150)
  Plant B to W1: 20, to W2: 50, to W3: 130 (total 200)
  Check demands: W1=100 ✓, W2=120 ✓, W3=130 ✓
  Check capacities: A=150 ✓, B=200 ✓

STEP 7: Document
- Final Model:
  Minimize Z = 58x₁₁ + 60x₁₂ + 62x₁₃ + 72x₂₁ + 69x₂₂ + 67x₂₃
  Subject to:
    Plant A: x₁₁ + x₁₂ + x₁₃ ≤ 150
    Plant B: x₂₁ + x₂₂ + x₂₃ ≤ 200
    W1: x₁₁ + x₂₁ ≥ 100
    W2: x₁₂ + x₂₂ ≥ 120
    W3: x₁₃ + x₂₃ ≥ 130
    xᵢⱼ ≥ 0

3. SOLUTION APPROACH
---------------------
This is a transportation problem with production costs. We can solve using
the transportation simplex method or by using LP solvers.

Optimal Solution (found by solving the LP):
- x₁₁ = 100 (Plant A to W1)
- x₁₂ = 50 (Plant A to W2)
- x₁₃ = 0 (Plant A to W3)
- x₂₁ = 0 (Plant B to W1)
- x₂₂ = 70 (Plant B to W2)
- x₂₃ = 130 (Plant B to W3)

Total production = 100 + 50 + 0 + 0 + 70 + 130 = 350 units

Cost calculation:
- Production cost: 150×50 + 200×60 = 7,500 + 12,000 = 19,500
- Shipping cost: 100×8 + 50×10 + 0×12 + 0×12 + 70×9 + 130×7 = 800 + 500 + 0 + 0 + 630 + 910 = 2,840
- Total cost: 19,500 + 2,840 = ₹22,340

4. INTERPRETATION
-----------------
- Plant A operates at full capacity (150 units).
- Plant B operates at full capacity (200 units).
- All warehouse demands are met exactly (equality).
- Plant A serves W1 and W2; Plant B serves W2 and W3.
- Total cost = ₹22,340.

5. KEY INSIGHTS
---------------
- Transportation problems involve shipping from sources to destinations.
- Production costs add complexity to the model.
- The optimal solution balances production and shipping costs.
- Both plants operate at full capacity.
- All demands are met exactly.

6. REAL-WORLD APPLICATIONS
---------------------------
- Supply chain optimization.
- Logistics and distribution planning.
- Manufacturing network design.
- Global sourcing and production planning.

7. WHAT WE LEARN
----------------
- How to formulate transportation problems with production costs.
- How to handle multiple sources and destinations.
- How to minimize total cost including production and shipping.
- How to balance supply and demand.

8. KEY TAKEAWAYS
-----------------
- Transportation problems are a special class of LP.
- Production costs can be incorporated into the objective.
- The optimal solution may use some routes and not others.
- All demands are typically met in the optimal solution.
