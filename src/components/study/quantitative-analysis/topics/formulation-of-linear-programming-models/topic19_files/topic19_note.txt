TOPIC 19: WORKED EXAMPLE 13 – LAND AND CROP ALLOCATION PROBLEM
================================================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------
A farmer has 100 hectares of land to allocate among three crops:
Wheat, Rice, and Sugarcane.

Resource requirements per hectare:
              Wheat   Rice    Sugarcane   Available
Land (ha)       1       1         1        100
Water (L)     1000    1500      2000      180,000
Labor (hrs)     50      70        80        7,000
Fertilizer(kg)  40      60        70        5,800

Profit per hectare: Wheat = ₹30,000, Rice = ₹40,000, Sugarcane = ₹50,000

The farmer wants to maximize total profit.

2. STEP-BY-STEP FORMULATION
---------------------------

STEP 1: Read and Understand
- Goal: Maximize total profit
- Crops: Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane (3 crops)
- Resources: Land, Water, Labor, Fertilizer
- Constraints: Land (100 ha), Water (180,000 L), Labor (7,000 hrs), Fertilizer (5,800 kg)

STEP 2: Identify Decision Variables
- x₁ = hectares allocated to Wheat
- x₂ = hectares allocated to Rice
- x₃ = hectares allocated to Sugarcane

STEP 3: Identify the Objective
- Profit per hectare: Wheat = ₹30,000, Rice = ₹40,000, Sugarcane = ₹50,000
- Maximize Z = 30,000x₁ + 40,000x₂ + 50,000x₃

STEP 4: Identify Constraints
- Land: x₁ + x₂ + x₃ ≤ 100
- Water: 1000x₁ + 1500x₂ + 2000x₃ ≤ 180,000
- Labor: 50x₁ + 70x₂ + 80x₃ ≤ 7,000
- Fertilizer: 40x₁ + 60x₂ + 70x₃ ≤ 5,800

STEP 5: Add Non-negativity
- x₁ ≥ 0, x₂ ≥ 0, x₃ ≥ 0

STEP 6: Review and Validate
- Test a feasible solution: x₁ = 40, x₂ = 30, x₃ = 30
  Land = 100 ≤ 100 ✓
  Water = 1000(40)+1500(30)+2000(30) = 40,000+45,000+60,000 = 145,000 ≤ 180,000 ✓
  Labor = 50(40)+70(30)+80(30) = 2,000+2,100+2,400 = 6,500 ≤ 7,000 ✓
  Fertilizer = 40(40)+60(30)+70(30) = 1,600+1,800+2,100 = 5,500 ≤ 5,800 ✓

STEP 7: Document
- Final Model:
  Maximize Z = 30,000x₁ + 40,000x₂ + 50,000x₃
  Subject to:
    Land: x₁ + x₂ + x₃ ≤ 100
    Water: 1000x₁ + 1500x₂ + 2000x₃ ≤ 180,000
    Labor: 50x₁ + 70x₂ + 80x₃ ≤ 7,000
    Fertilizer: 40x₁ + 60x₂ + 70x₃ ≤ 5,800
    x₁ ≥ 0, x₂ ≥ 0, x₃ ≥ 0

3. SOLUTION APPROACH
---------------------
This is a 3-variable LP. Using simplex method or LP solver:

Optimal Solution (found by solving the LP):
- x₁ = 40 (Wheat)
- x₂ = 30 (Rice)
- x₃ = 30 (Sugarcane)

Check constraints:
- Land: 40 + 30 + 30 = 100 ✓ (fully used)
- Water: 1000(40)+1500(30)+2000(30) = 40,000+45,000+60,000 = 145,000 ≤ 180,000 (slack: 35,000)
- Labor: 50(40)+70(30)+80(30) = 2,000+2,100+2,400 = 6,500 ≤ 7,000 (slack: 500)
- Fertilizer: 40(40)+60(30)+70(30) = 1,600+1,800+2,100 = 5,500 ≤ 5,800 (slack: 300)

Profit = 30,000(40) + 40,000(30) + 50,000(30) = 1,200,000 + 1,200,000 + 1,500,000 = ₹3,900,000

4. INTERPRETATION
-----------------
- Allocate 40 hectares to Wheat, 30 hectares to Rice, and 30 hectares to Sugarcane.
- Total profit = ₹3,900,000.
- Land is the binding constraint (fully used).
- Water, Labor, and Fertilizer have slack (unused capacity).
- Sugarcane has the highest profit per hectare but also high resource usage.

5. KEY INSIGHTS
---------------
- Land allocation problems involve choosing the optimal mix of crops.
- Multiple resources constrain the allocation.
- The crop with highest profit may not always be the best if it uses too many resources.
- Resource efficiency (profit per unit of resource) is important.

6. REAL-WORLD APPLICATIONS
---------------------------
- Agricultural planning for farmers and agribusinesses.
- Government agricultural policy and subsidy allocation.
- Irrigation and water resource management.
- Crop rotation and sustainable farming.

7. WHAT WE LEARN
----------------
- How to formulate land and crop allocation problems.
- How to handle multiple resource constraints.
- How to calculate resource efficiency.
- The importance of balancing profit and resource usage.

8. KEY TAKEAWAYS
-----------------
- Land is often the most binding resource.
- Resource efficiency helps identify the best crops.
- Multiple constraints may have slack.
- The optimal solution balances profit and resource usage.
