1. INTRODUCTION
---------------
Linear Programming is not just a mathematical tool—it is a powerful
decision-making framework used across industries, governments, and
social sectors. This topic explores the diverse applications of LP
in the real world.

2. BUSINESS AND INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS
---------------------------------------
Manufacturing:
- Production planning and scheduling
- Product mix optimization
- Capacity planning
- Inventory management

Logistics and Supply Chain:
- Transportation and distribution
- Route optimization
- Warehouse location
- Fleet management

Finance and Investment:
- Portfolio optimization
- Asset allocation
- Risk management
- Budgeting

Marketing:
- Advertising budget allocation
- Media mix optimization
- Pricing strategies
- Customer segmentation

8. KEY INSIGHTS
---------------
- LP is applicable to almost any industry
- The same mathematical tools solve different problems
- Understanding the problem is key to formulation
- LP helps make data-driven decisions

9. COMMON MISTAKES
-------------------
- Thinking LP is only for business
- Overlooking social applications
- Not considering all constraints
- Forgetting to include real-world limitations

10. BEST PRACTICES
-------------------
- Understand the problem domain
- Identify all stakeholders
- Consider all constraints
- Validate with domain experts
- Use real data when possible

11. TIPS & TRICKS
-----------------
- Start with a simple model, then add complexity
- Collaborate with domain experts
- Use sensitivity analysis for robustness
- Document assumptions clearly

12. SUMMARY
-----------
LP is one of the most versatile optimization tools ever developed.
From manufacturing to healthcare, from education to environmental
protection, LP helps make better decisions. Mastering LP opens
doors to solving problems in almost any field.

"Think about: Where else could LP be applied? What problems in
your community could be solved using LP?"
