--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTRODUCTION & PHILOSOPHY OF PERT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROGRAM EVALUATION AND REVIEW TECHNIQUE (PERT) was developed in 1958 by the US Navy
Special Projects Office for the Polaris Missile submarine program.

CORE PHILOSOPHICAL PURPOSE:
• Unlike CPM (which assumes known deterministic durations for repetitive construction),
  PERT is designed specifically for NON-REPETITIVE, RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT (R&D),
  and highly uncertain engineering projects where task durations cannot be predicted exactly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE THREE TIME ESTIMATE MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To capture uncertainty, every activity is assigned THREE time estimates:

1. OPTIMISTIC TIME (t_o or a):
   • The minimum possible duration under ideal, flawless operating conditions
     (probability of occurrence p <= 1%).

2. MOST LIKELY TIME (t_m or m):
   • The modal, most probable duration under normal conditions.

3. PESSIMISTIC TIME (t_p or b):
   • The maximum possible duration under adverse, worst-case disruptions, excluding
     catastrophes like wars or earthquakes (probability of occurrence p <= 1%).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BETA DISTRIBUTION & STATISTICAL EQUATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Activity durations follow a unimodal BETA DISTRIBUTION:

1. EXPECTED ACTIVITY DURATION (t_e / mu):
       t_e = (t_o + 4*t_m + t_p) / 6

2. ACTIVITY STANDARD DEVIATION (sigma):
       sigma = (t_p - t_o) / 6

3. ACTIVITY VARIANCE (sigma^2):
       sigma^2 = [ (t_p - t_o) / 6 ]^2

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CENTRAL LIMIT THEOREM (CLT) & PROJECT DELIVERABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
By the Central Limit Theorem, the sum of multiple independent random activity
durations along the Critical Path approaches a GAUSSIAN NORMAL DISTRIBUTION:

1. EXPECTED PROJECT DURATION (mu_project):
       mu_project = sum_{i in Critical Path} t_e(i)

2. PROJECT VARIANCE (sigma^2_project):
       sigma^2_project = sum_{i in Critical Path} sigma^2(i)
   (CRITICAL RULE: Variances add linearly; standard deviations DO NOT add!)

3. PROJECT STANDARD DEVIATION (sigma_project):
       sigma_project = sqrt( sigma^2_project )

4. STANDARD NORMAL Z-SCORE:
       Z = (T_S - mu_project) / sigma_project
   where T_S is the Target Scheduled Completion Date.

5. PROBABILITY OF COMPLETING ON OR BEFORE T_S:
       P(T <= T_S) = Phi(Z)
   • If T_S = mu_project ➔ Z = 0 ➔ P = 50% (Standard 50-50 odds)
   • If Z = +1.00 ➔ P = 84.13%
   • If Z = +2.00 ➔ P = 97.72%
   • If Z = +3.00 ➔ P = 99.87%

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CPM VS. PERT COMPARISON MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature               | CPM (Deterministic)           | PERT (Probabilistic)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Origin                | DuPont & Univac (1957)        | US Navy Polaris (1958)
Orientation           | Activity-oriented             | Event-oriented
Time Estimates        | Single Deterministic Time (d) | Three Time Estimates (t_o, t_m, t_p)
Underlying Curve      | Fixed Point                   | Beta Distribution (CLT -> Normal)
Cost Optimization     | Direct Crashing (Cost Slope)  | Risk & Probability Buffers
Primary Application   | Construction, Plant Shutdowns | R&D, Aerospace, New Tech Launches
Financial Units       | Indian Rupees (₹)             | Indian Rupees (₹)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never sum standard deviations directly; ALWAYS sum variances along the Critical Path!
- Remember: at T_S = mu_project, the deliverability probability is exactly 50% (never 100%).
- Non-critical activities do NOT contribute to project mean duration or variance.
- State all contingency reserves, R&D budgets, and risk funds in Indian Rupees (₹).
