--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DEFINITION & MATHEMATICAL NATURE OF A PURE STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A PURE STRATEGY is a deterministic decision rule in which a player selects ONE
specific course of action with 100% certainty (Probability p = 1.0) and zero randomization.

MATHEMATICAL REPRESENTATION:
• For an m-strategy Player A, choosing pure strategy A_k is represented by the
  unit standard basis vector:
        p = (0, 0, ..., 1, ..., 0)^T     (where p_k = 1.0 and p_i = 0 for all i != k)

GEOMETRIC INTERPRETATION:
• In the mixed strategy probability simplex, Pure Strategies are the extreme
  VERTICES (corner points) of the simplex.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHEN IS A PURE STRATEGY OPTIMAL?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Pure Strategy is optimal and strategically unexploitable IF AND ONLY IF the game
possesses a SADDLE POINT in pure strategies:

        Maximin == Minimax == v*   (at cell (i*, j*))

THE STABILITY PRINCIPLE:
• When a saddle point exists, neither Player A nor Player B can improve their payoff
  by unilaterally deviating from their optimal pure strategy (A_i*, B_j*).
• If NO saddle point exists (Maximin < Minimax), playing a pure strategy leaves a player
  predictable and vulnerable to exploitation by an intelligent rival.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PURE STRATEGY PROFILE & DIRECT PAYOFF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Player A chooses pure strategy A_i and Player B chooses pure strategy B_j:
• Joint Action Profile: (A_i, B_j)
• Direct Payoff to Player A: u_A(A_i, B_j) = a_ij  (in Indian Rupees ₹)
• Direct Payoff to Player B: u_B(A_i, B_j) = -a_ij (in Zero-Sum Games)

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never play a deterministic pure strategy if Maximin < Minimax; you will be exploited!
- Pure strategies correspond to the standard basis vectors e_k in linear algebra.
- Direct lookup in the payoff matrix gives the exact outcome of any pure strategy pair (A_i, B_j).
- Always state financial returns and security levels in Indian Rupees (₹).
