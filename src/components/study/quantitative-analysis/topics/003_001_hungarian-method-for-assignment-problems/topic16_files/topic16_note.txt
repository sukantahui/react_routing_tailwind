5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In unbalanced models, always augment dummy lines with ₹0 costs.
- In maximization models, convert to regret matrix FIRST before adding dummy lines.
- Always pull unit rates from the ORIGINAL matrix in Indian Rupees (₹).
- Explicitly state which jobs are outsourced or which personnel are idle.
