--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE MINIMIZATION OBJECTIVE FORMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The canonical Assignment Problem is structured as a pure MINIMIZATION MODEL:

    Minimize   Z = Sum_{i=1}^n Sum_{j=1}^n c_ij * x_ij
    Subject to:
               Sum_{j=1}^n x_ij = 1    (for all i = 1, 2, ..., n)
               Sum_{i=1}^n x_ij = 1    (for all j = 1, 2, ..., n)
               x_ij in {0, 1}          (for all i, j)

WHY IT IS THE NATIVE HUNGARIAN FORM:
The Hungarian Method operates natively on minimization problems because subtracting
row minimums u_i and column minimums v_j creates non-negative opportunity costs:
    c_ij' = c_ij - u_i - v_j >= 0
where a zero (c_ij' = 0) represents maximum relative economic efficiency!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE 5 INDUSTRIAL OPERATIONAL METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FINANCIAL EXPENDITURE (₹):
   • Wages, subcontracting rates, tooling wear, fuel surcharge.
   • Objective: Minimize total budget expenditure in Indian Rupees (₹).

2. PROCESS & CYCLE TIME (Hours / Minutes):
   • Assembly line durations, furnace heating schedules, lab testing.
   • Objective: Minimize total labor hours or critical-path throughput time.

3. TRANSIT & LOGISTICS DISTANCE (Kilometers):
   • Fleet courier routing, municipal garbage collection, vaccine dispatch.
   • Objective: Minimize total vehicle kilometers to reduce fuel and carbon emissions.

4. DEFECT COUNTS & QUALITY PENALTIES:
   • Machine error rates, surgical complication indices, misprint counts.
   • Objective: Maximize final product quality by minimizing expected defect occurrences.

5. ENERGY & CARBON FOOTPRINT (kWh / kg CO2):
   • High-voltage furnace power allocation, data center cooling server load.
   • Objective: Minimize environmental carbon impact.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MULTI-CRITERIA WEIGHTED MINIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When combining disparate metrics (e.g. monetary cost in ₹ and delay in hours):
    c_ij = w_1 * Cost_{ij} + w_2 * (HourlyWage * Time_{ij})
where w_1 and w_2 are managerial priority weights (w_1 + w_2 = 1.0).

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Always verify the unit of measurement before reporting Z* (₹, minutes, km, defects).
- Never run Hungarian reductions on a profit matrix without converting to regret first!
- Verify that every cost entry c_ij represents a true performance penalty.
- Report all monetary figures in Indian Rupees (₹).
