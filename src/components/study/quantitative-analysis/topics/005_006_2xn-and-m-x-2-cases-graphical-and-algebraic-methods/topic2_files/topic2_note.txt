1. GEOMETRIC FOUNDATION OF THE GRAPHICAL METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why does the Graphical Method work for 2xn and mx2 games?
• For any player with 2 strategies: p_1 + p_2 = 1 ➔ p_2 = 1 - p_1.
• The probability space is a ONE-DIMENSIONAL SIMPLEX (a line segment p_1 in [0, 1]).
• All expected payoff functions are linear straight lines:
      E(p, B_j) = (a_1j - a_2j) * p_1 + a_2j  (for 2xn games)
      E(A_i, q) = (a_i1 - a_i2) * q_1 + a_i2  (for mx2 games)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE DUAL PERSPECTIVES: 2xn vs mx2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. 2xn GRAPHICAL METHOD (ROW PLAYER A PERSPECTIVE):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Horizontal Axis: p_1 in [0, 1] (Probability of playing A_1).
• Left Axis (p_1 = 0): Payoffs a_2j (Pure A_2).
• Right Axis (p_1 = 1): Payoffs a_1j (Pure A_1).
• Lower Envelope: f_lower(p_1) = min_{j} [ E(p, B_j) ].
• Optimization Goal: MAXIMIN PEAK ➔ v* = max_{p_1} f_lower(p_1) (in ₹).
• Active Submatrix: Formed by the 2 columns intersecting at the peak.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. mx2 GRAPHICAL METHOD (COLUMN PLAYER B PERSPECTIVE):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Horizontal Axis: q_1 in [0, 1] (Probability of playing B_1).
• Left Axis (q_1 = 0): Payouts a_i2 (Pure B_2).
• Right Axis (q_1 = 1): Payouts a_i1 (Pure B_1).
• Upper Envelope: f_upper(q_1) = max_{i} [ E(A_i, q) ].
• Optimization Goal: MINIMAX TROUGH ➔ v* = min_{q_1} f_upper(q_1) (in ₹).
• Active Submatrix: Formed by the 2 rows intersecting at the trough.

4. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never confuse the axes: left axis is p1=0 (Row 2 / Col 2), right axis is p1=1 (Row 1 / Col 1).
- 2xn requires the LOWER envelope; mx2 requires the UPPER envelope.
- The optimal intersection must be formed by lines with opposite slopes.
- State all game values in Indian Rupees (₹).
