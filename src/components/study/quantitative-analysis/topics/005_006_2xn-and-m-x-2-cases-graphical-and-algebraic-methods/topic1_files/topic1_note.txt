1. THE STRUCTURE & FORMULATION OF mx2 GAMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An mx2 Two-Person Zero-Sum Game occurs when:
• Player A (Row Maximizer) has m strategies: {A_1, A_2, ..., A_m} where m >= 3.
• Player B (Column Minimizer) has exactly 2 strategies: {B_1, B_2}.

Payoff Matrix:
      A = [ [a_11, a_12],
            [a_21, a_22],
            ...,
            [a_m1, a_m2] ]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PARAMETERIZATION & EXPECTED PAYOUT LINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PLAYER B's STRATEGY PARAMETERIZATION:
   • Let q_1 = Probability of playing Column B_1 (0 <= q_1 <= 1).
   • Then q_2 = 1 - q_1 = Probability of playing Column B_2.
   • Vector: q = (q_1, 1 - q_1)^T.

2. EXPECTED PAYOUT LINE EQUATIONS:
   • If Player A plays pure Row A_i (i in {1, ..., m}):
         E(A_i, q) = q_1 * a_i1 + (1 - q_1) * a_i2
         E(A_i, q) = (a_i1 - a_i2) * q_1 + a_i2
   • Endpoints of Line i:
         At q_1 = 0 (Pure B_2):  E(A_i, 0) = a_i2  (Left Vertical Axis)
         At q_1 = 1 (Pure B_1):  E(A_i, 1) = a_i1  (Right Vertical Axis)
   • Slope of Line i:  m_i = a_i1 - a_i2.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE UPPER ENVELOPE & MINIMAX PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• For any choice of q_1 by Player B, rational Player A chooses the row that MAXIMIZES
  Player A's return (maximizing Player B's loss):
      f_upper(q_1) = max_{i in {1, ..., m}} [ E(A_i, q) ]

• Player B anticipates this maximizing behavior and selects q_1* to MINIMIZE this upper boundary:
      v* = min_{q_1 in [0, 1]} [ f_upper(q_1) ]  (in ₹)

• The lowest trough on the upper envelope occurs at the intersection of two lines with
  opposite slope signs (one rising, one falling).

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never plot the lower envelope for mx2 games! (Player B requires the UPPER envelope / Minimax).
- Remember: at q1 = 0, the payout is ai2 (Column 2), NOT ai1.
- Ensure the two intersecting lines forming the trough have opposite slopes.
- State all game values in Indian Rupees (₹).
