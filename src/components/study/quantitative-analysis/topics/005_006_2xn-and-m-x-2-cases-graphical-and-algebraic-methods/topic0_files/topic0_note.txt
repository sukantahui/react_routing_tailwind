1. THE STRUCTURE & FORMULATION OF 2xn GAMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A 2xn Two-Person Zero-Sum Game occurs when:
• Player A (Row Maximizer) has exactly 2 strategies: {A_1, A_2}.
• Player B (Column Minimizer) has n strategies: {B_1, B_2, ..., B_n} where n >= 3.

Payoff Matrix:
      A = [ [a_11, a_12, ..., a_1n],
            [a_21, a_22, ..., a_2n] ]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PARAMETERIZATION & EXPECTED PAYOFF LINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PLAYER A's STRATEGY PARAMETERIZATION:
   • Let p_1 = Probability of playing Row A_1 (0 <= p_1 <= 1).
   • Then p_2 = 1 - p_1 = Probability of playing Row A_2.
   • Vector: p = (p_1, 1 - p_1)^T.

2. EXPECTED PAYOFF LINE EQUATIONS:
   • If Player B plays pure Column B_j (j in {1, ..., n}):
         E(p, B_j) = p_1 * a_1j + (1 - p_1) * a_2j
         E(p, B_j) = (a_1j - a_2j) * p_1 + a_2j
   • Endpoints of Line j:
         At p_1 = 0 (Pure A_2):  E(0, B_j) = a_2j  (Left Vertical Axis)
         At p_1 = 1 (Pure A_1):  E(1, B_j) = a_1j  (Right Vertical Axis)
   • Slope of Line j:  m_j = a_1j - a_2j.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE LOWER ENVELOPE & MAXIMIN PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• For any choice of p_1 by Player A, rational Player B chooses the column that MINIMIZES
  Player A's return:
      f_lower(p_1) = min_{j in {1, ..., n}} [ E(p, B_j) ]

• Player A anticipates this minimizing behavior and selects p_1* to MAXIMIZE this lower boundary:
      v* = max_{p_1 in [0, 1]} [ f_lower(p_1) ]  (in ₹)

• The highest peak on the lower envelope occurs at the intersection of two lines with
  opposite slope signs (one rising, one falling).

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never plot the upper envelope for 2xn games! (Player A requires the LOWER envelope).
- Remember: at p1 = 0, the payoff is a2j (Row 2), NOT a1j.
- Ensure slopes of the two peak lines have opposite signs.
- State all game values in Indian Rupees (₹).
