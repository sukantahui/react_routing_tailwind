1. THE 4-STEP GAME MATRIX FORMULATION METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Translating an ambiguous, real-world verbal business conflict into a rigorous
Game Theory Payoff Matrix requires following 4 systematic steps:

• STEP 1: IDENTIFY THE PLAYERS:
  - Designate Player A (Row Player / Maximizer) and Player B (Column Player / Minimizer).
  - Verify that the game is a Two-Person Zero-Sum interaction.

• STEP 2: DEFINE THE STRATEGY SETS:
  - Formulate exhaustive, mutually exclusive action sets:
        S_A = {A_1, A_2, ..., A_m}  and  S_B = {B_1, B_2, ..., B_n}

• STEP 3: QUANTIFY NET PAYOFFS (IN INDIAN RUPEES ₹):
  - For every strategy combination (A_i, B_j), compute:
        a_ij = Net Revenue / Gain to Player A - Net Costs incurred by Player A
  - Apply the standard sign convention (+ for gain to A, - for payout from A).

• STEP 4: ASSEMBLE & VALIDATE THE NORMAL FORM MATRIX:
  - Construct the m x n rectangular table.
  - Compute Row Minima and Column Maxima.
  - Validate Maximin <= Minimax and check for Saddle Points.

2. FOUR MAJOR FORMULATION PARADIGMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. ADVERTISING & MARKETING COMPETITION MODEL:
   • Strategies: Digital Ads (A1), TV Broadcast (A2), Newspaper/Print (A3).
   • Payoffs: Net market share revenue gained or lost in ₹ Thousands.

2. SEALED-BID PROCUREMENT TENDER MODEL:
   • Strategies: High Bid Price (A1), Medium Bid Price (A2), Low Bid Price (A3).
   • Payoffs: Winning contract profit in ₹ Lakhs if bid < rival's bid; ₹0 otherwise.

3. RETAIL PRICING & PROMOTIONAL DISCOUNT MODEL:
   • Strategies: Regular Pricing (A1), 10% Discount (A2), 20% Clearance (A3).
   • Payoffs: Footfall conversion profit in ₹.

4. LEGAL SETTLEMENT VS LITIGATION MODEL:
   • Strategies: Accept Settlement (A1), Proceed to Trial (A2).
   • Payoffs: Net damages awarded minus legal fees in ₹.

3. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Precision Foundry Sealed-Bid Tender Formulation (Debangshu)
Debangshu in Barrackpore formulated a 3x3 bidding game for a ₹5 Lakh casting tender:
Strategies: High (₹4.8L), Med (₹4.2L), Low (₹3.8L). Matrix analysis isolated the optimal
medium-bid pure saddle point.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Cold-Chain Hospital Supply Tender Formulation (Mamata & Mahima)
Mamata and Mahima in Kolkata formulated a 2x2 delivery warranty matrix (Reliable vs Economy transport),
verifying that reliable transport strictly dominated economy transport by ₹40,000.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: Supermarket FMCG Retail Discount Matrix (Susmita)
Susmita in Ichapur formulated a 2x2 weekend promotional matrix, converting store footfall
capture rates into signed net margin cells in ₹.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: Educational High-Tech Lab Patent Negotiation Matrix (Abhronila)
Abhronila formulated a ₹55 Lakh university patent licensing game in Jadavpur, translating
royalty proposals into a 3x3 normal form matrix and identifying an equitable settlement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never omit potential strategies; strategy sets MUST be collectively exhaustive.
- Always deduct operational costs from gross revenues before populating matrix cells.
- State all payoffs in uniform units (e.g. ₹ Thousands) to prevent mathematical errors.
- Always include the Indian Rupee (₹) currency symbol.
