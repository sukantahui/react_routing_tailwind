--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DEFINITION & ESSENCE OF THE PAYOFF CONCEPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A PAYOFF is the numerical value, utility, or monetary gain/loss in Indian Rupees (₹)
that a player receives at the conclusion of a game as a direct consequence of the
joint strategy profile selected by all participants.

MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION:
• For joint strategy choice (A_i, B_j):
  - Payoff to Player A: u_A(A_i, B_j) = a_ij
  - Payoff to Player B: u_B(A_i, B_j) = b_ij = -a_ij (in Zero-Sum Games)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. VON NEUMANN-MORGENSTERN (VNM) UTILITY THEORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under the axioms of rational choice (Completeness, Transitivity, Continuity, Independence):
• Rational players maximize their EXPECTED UTILITY under uncertainty.
• Payoff entries represent cardinal utilities that can be averaged using probability weights.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EXPECTED PAYOFF EQUATION FOR MIXED STRATEGIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When Player A plays probability vector p = (p_1, p_2, ..., p_m) and Player B plays
probability vector q = (q_1, q_2, ..., q_n):

EXPECTED PAYOFF TO PLAYER A:
    E(p, q) = sum_{i=1}^m sum_{j=1}^n p_i * q_j * a_ij
            = p^T * A * q

EXPECTED PAYOFF TO PLAYER B:
    E_B(p, q) = -E(p, q) = -p^T * A * q

ZERO-SUM CONSERVATION PROPERTY:
    E_A(p, q) + E_B(p, q) = 0   (strictly holds for all probability vectors p and q!)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INTERPRETATION OF MATRIX ENTRIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• a_ij > 0 (Positive Payoff): Player A GAINS ₹a_ij; Player B PAYS / LOSES ₹a_ij.
• a_ij < 0 (Negative Payoff): Player A LOSES ₹|a_ij|; Player B GAINS ₹|a_ij|.
• a_ij = 0 (Zero Payoff): Breakeven draw; no net transfer of capital.

6. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Matrix entries a_ij are ALWAYS from the perspective of Player A (Row Player).
- In zero-sum games, never define a separate matrix for Player B; Player B's payoff is simply -a_ij.
- Linear scaling u' = a*u + b (where a > 0) preserves optimal strategy rankings.
- Always state monetary payoffs in Indian Rupees (₹).
