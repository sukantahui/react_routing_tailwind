ESSENTIAL SHORT QUESTIONS & REVISION SUMMARY:

Q1. Define a Medoid.
A1. An actual, existing observation in a cluster whose average dissimilarity to all
    other points in that cluster is minimal.

Q2. State the primary objective function of K-Medoids.
A2. J = \sum_{i=1}^N D(x_i, m_{y_i}) where m_{y_i} is the medoid assigned to x_i.

Q3. Why is K-Medoids more robust than K-Means?
A3. Because it minimizes absolute distance (L1/arbitrary) rather than squared Euclidean
    distance (L2^2), providing a 50% breakdown point against extreme outliers.

Q4. What is the PAM algorithm?
A4. Partitioning Around Medoids, introduced by Kaufman & Rousseeuw (1987), using
    BUILD and SWAP phases to find optimal medoids.

Q5. What is the time complexity of PAM?
A5. O(K(N - K)^2) per iteration.

Q6. How does CLARA scale K-Medoids?
A6. By applying PAM to multiple small random subsamples and picking the best overall medoids.

Q7. What is FastPAM?
A7. An exact optimization of PAM that caches nearest and second-nearest medoid
    distances to achieve an O(N) speedup (complexity O(K(N-K))).
