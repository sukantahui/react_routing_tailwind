1. WHAT IS THE MEDOID UPDATE STEP?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In alternate / Voronoi iteration (Lloyd-style K-Medoids), once cluster memberships
  are assigned, each cluster independently recalculates its internal medoid.
- For each cluster C_k, the updated medoid m_k^* is chosen as:
    m_k^* = \arg\min_{y \in C_k} \sum_{x \in C_k} D(x, y)

2. SYSTEMATIC SELECTION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Form the sub-matrix of pairwise distances among points in C_k.
2. For every member point i \in C_k, sum its distances to all other members in C_k:
     TotalDist(i) = \sum_{j \in C_k} D(i, j)
3. Designate the point with the minimum TotalDist as the new medoid m_k^*.
4. Repeat for all K clusters.

3. COMPLEXITY & GUARANTEES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Complexity per cluster: O(|C_k|^2 \cdot d)
- Sum across all clusters: \sum O(|C_k|^2) \le O(N^2)
- Monotonically reduces or maintains the intra-cluster cost at each step!
