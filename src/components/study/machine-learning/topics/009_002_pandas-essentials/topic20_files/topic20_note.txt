1. SYSTEMATIC 6-STEP EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS (EDA) CHECKLIST:
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Step 1: First Impressions & Structure:
           - Call `df.head(5)` and `df.tail(5)`.
           - Inspect `df.shape` (N rows, M columns).
   
   Step 2: Schema & Memory Footprint:
           - Call `df.info()` to verify column dtypes and memory usage.
           - Confirm integer IDs are not mistakenly cast as floats or objects.
   
   Step 3: Missing Value Audit:
           - Calculate `df.isna().sum()` and `df.isna().mean() * 100`.
   
   Step 4: Cardinality & Uniqueness:
           - Call `df.nunique()` to identify constant features (nunique=1) or high-cardinality IDs.
   
   Step 5: Statistical Distribution:
           - Numerical: `df.describe()` (mean, std, min, 25%, 50%, 75%, max).
           - Categorical: `df.describe(include=['object', 'category'])` (count, unique, top, freq).
           - Calculate skewness (`df.skew()`) to spot asymmetric tails.
   
   Step 6: Relationship & Correlation Mapping:
           - `df.corr(numeric_only=True)` to assess linear collinearity.
           - `pd.crosstab(df['CategoryA'], df['CategoryB'])` to inspect multi-factor distributions.

2. CRITICAL TAKEAWAYS FOR MACHINE LEARNING READINESS:
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Never feed raw uninspected datasets into ML estimators!
   - EDA reveals corrupted columns, unencoded text, missing entries, and outlier anomalies before model training begins.
