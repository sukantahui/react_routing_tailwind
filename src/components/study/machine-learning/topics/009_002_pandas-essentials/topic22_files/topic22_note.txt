1. REGIONAL SALES AGGREGATION PATTERNS:
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Multi-Metric Regional Summary:
     `df.groupby('Region', as_index=False).agg(`
     `    Total_Orders=('OrderID', 'count'),`
     `    Total_Units=('Units_Sold', 'sum'),`
     `    Total_Revenue=('Revenue_INR', 'sum'),`
     `    Avg_Order_Value=('Revenue_INR', 'mean')`
     `).sort_values(by='Total_Revenue', ascending=False)`

2. PIVOT TABLES (pd.pivot_table) VS GROUPBY:
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - While `df.groupby(['Region', 'Category'])` creates long stacked rows, `pd.pivot_table()` reshapes data into a clean 2D business matrix:
     `pd.pivot_table(`
     `    df,`
     `    values='Revenue_INR',`
     `    index='Region',`
     `    columns='Category',`
     `    aggfunc='sum',`
     `    fill_value=0,`
     `    margins=True,`
     `    margins_name='Grand_Total'`
     `)`
   - `margins=True` automatically computes subtotals and grand totals across both rows and columns.

3. CALCULATING REGIONAL MARKET SHARE (%):
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Step 1: Compute grand total revenue `tot = df['Revenue_INR'].sum()`.
   - Step 2: Group by Region and divide:
     `df.groupby('Region')['Revenue_INR'].sum().apply(lambda v: (v / tot) * 100).round(2)`
   - Allows business stakeholders to pinpoint top revenue-generating territories immediately.
