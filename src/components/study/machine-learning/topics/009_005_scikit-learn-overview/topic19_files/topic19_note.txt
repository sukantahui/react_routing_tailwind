1. END-TO-END UNSUPERVISED PIPELINE BLUEPRINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Data Ingestion & Scaling:
   - Always standardize multi-dimensional numeric inputs with `StandardScaler`!
2. Picking Optimal K:
   - Elbow Method: Plot Inertia vs $K$ to spot where curve bends.
   - Silhouette Analysis: Measure cluster cohesion vs separation (Score between -1.0 and +1.0; > 0.5 indicates strong separation).
3. Pipeline Fitting:
   - `make_pipeline(StandardScaler(), KMeans(n_clusters=3, init='k-means++', n_init=10))`.
4. Centroid Inversion:
   - Call `scaler.inverse_transform(kmeans.cluster_centers_)` to map normalized mathematical coordinates back to real business metrics.
5. Qualitative Persona Labeling:
   - Translate numerical cluster centers into descriptive human personas (e.g. "Power Coders", "At-Risk Students").
6. Inference Triage:
   - Assign unseen user records dynamically using `pipeline.predict(X_new)`.

2. SILHOUETTE SCORE FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each sample $i$:
    $s(i) = \frac{b(i) - a(i)}{\max(a(i), b(i))}$
Where:
- $a(i)$: Mean intra-cluster distance (distance to points in own cluster).
- $b(i)$: Mean nearest-cluster distance (distance to points in closest neighboring cluster).
- $s \approx +1$: Sample is well clustered far from other clusters.
- $s \approx 0$: Sample is on or near the decision boundary between two clusters.
- $s < 0$: Sample might be assigned to the wrong cluster.
