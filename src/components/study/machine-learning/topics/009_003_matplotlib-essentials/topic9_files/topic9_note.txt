MATPLOTLIB ESSENTIALS: TOPIC 9 NOTE
=============================================================================
TOPIC: Axis Limits, Margins, Inversion & Aspect Ratio Control
MENTOR: Sukanta Hui | Coder & AccoTax (Barrackpore)
=============================================================================

1. EXPLICIT AXIS LIMITS:
   - `ax.set_xlim(left, right)` or `plt.xlim(xmin, xmax)`
   - `ax.set_ylim(bottom, top)` or `plt.ylim(ymin, ymax)`
   - Get current limits: `xmin, xmax = ax.get_xlim()`
   - Setting limits automatically clamps zoom region and prevents misleading autoscaling.

2. MARGINS & TIGHT AUTOSCALING:
   - `ax.margins(x=0.0, y=0.05)`: Controls whitespace padding percentage around the data envelope.
   - `ax.autoscale(enable=True, axis='both', tight=True)`: Removes all whitespace padding up to data extrema.

3. INVERTING AXIS DIRECTIONS:
   - `ax.invert_yaxis()` or `ax.set_ylim(top, bottom)` (reversed bounds)
   - `ax.invert_xaxis()`
   - Typical use cases: Matrix indices (row 0 at top), geological depth plots, leaderboard ranking tables.

4. ASPECT RATIO NORMALIZATION:
   - `ax.set_aspect('equal')`: Ensures 1 unit along the X-axis is physically equal in pixels to 1 unit along the Y-axis.
   - Essential for: PCA projections, K-Means clustering, SVM margins, geographical coordinates where Euclidean distance distortion ruins interpretation.

5. NON-LINEAR SCALES:
   - `ax.set_xscale('log')` / `ax.set_yscale('log')`: Logarithmic base-10 scale (strictly positive values).
   - `ax.set_yscale('symlog', linthresh=0.01)`: Symmetrical log allowing zero and negative numbers with a linear threshold near zero.
   - `ax.set_yscale('logit')`: Scales probabilities in (0, 1) to logits.
