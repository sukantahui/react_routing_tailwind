4. Disjoint Set Testing: isdisjoint() and short-circuit optimization vs bool(A & B)
5. Mixed-type comparisons with methods vs strict operator requirements
6. Time Complexity: O(len(A)) for subset, O(min(len(A), len(B))) for isdisjoint
