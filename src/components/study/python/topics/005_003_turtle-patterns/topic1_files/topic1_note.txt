1. THE FUNDAMENTAL POLYGON THEOREM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a turtle traces any closed convex regular polygon with `N` sides, it starts
and ends pointing in the exact same direction. Therefore, the sum of all exterior
turn angles must equal exactly 360 degrees (one full revolution).

Key Equations:
1. Single Exterior Turn Angle:
   theta = 360 / N

2. Single Interior Angle:
   phi = 180 - theta = ((N - 2) * 180) / N

3. Sum of All Interior Angles:
   Sum = (N - 2) * 180

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  -------------|-----------|-----------------------|-------------------------

  Square       | 4         | 90°                   | 90°
  Pentagon     | 5         | 72°                   | 108°

3. CIRCUMSCRIBED POLAR COORDINATES (CENTERED POLYGONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To center an N-sided polygon on `(cx, cy)` with circumscribed radius `R`:
For each vertex `i` from `0` to `N - 1`:
- angle = (2 * pi * i / N) - (pi / 2)  # -pi/2 points apex up
- vx = cx + R * cos(angle)
- vy = cy + R * sin(angle)

Connecting these vertices produces a perfectly centered regular polygon!
