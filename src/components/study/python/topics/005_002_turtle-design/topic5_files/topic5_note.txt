1. THE TURTLE SPEED HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Turtle animation speed is configured via `t.speed(s)`:

  Value | String Alias | Description
  ------|--------------|---------------------------------------------
  1     | 'slowest'    | Ultra-slow pedagogical tracing (ideal for teaching)
  3     | 'slow'       | Slow deliberate motion
  6     | 'normal'     | Default standard drawing speed
  10    | 'fast'       | Fast animation
  0     | 'fastest'    | No animation delay (Fastest possible standard mode)

2. CRITICAL QUIRK: WHY IS 0 FASTER THAN 10?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Beginners often assume `speed(10)` is maximum speed because 10 is the largest integer.
However, `speed(0)` is a special sentinel flag in Turtle that means:
"Disable internal sleep timer delays completely" (0 milliseconds of delay).
Therefore: `speed(1)` is slowest, `speed(10)` is very fast, and `speed(0)` is FASTEST.

3. SPEED(0) VS SCREEN.TRACER(0)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `t.speed(0)`: Eliminates inter-step sleep delays, but Tkinter still redraws
  the canvas on every single vector movement (visible fast drawing).
- `screen.tracer(0)`: Disables screen canvas updates entirely. Movements happen
  in memory in microseconds. You MUST call `screen.update()` to paint the
  completed image onto the screen.

When to use `tracer(0)`:
- High-density fractals (Koch snowflakes, Sierpinski triangles with 1,000+ lines).
- Complex mathematical mandalas and recursive branching trees.
- Real-time interactive 60 FPS games.
