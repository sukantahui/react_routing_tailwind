1. FUNCTIONS ARE FIRST-CLASS OBJECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  In Python, functions are instances of `types.FunctionType`.
  They can be:
    1. Assigned to variables: `alias = my_func`
    2. Passed as arguments:   `execute(my_func)`
    3. Returned from functions: `return custom_calc`
    4. Stored in collections: `table = {"ACTION": my_func}`
