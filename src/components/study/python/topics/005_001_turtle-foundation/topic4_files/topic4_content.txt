OVERVIEW:
Comprehensive master guide to controlling and inspecting the Turtle pen cursor's physical and visual behavior on the 2D canvas. Deep dive into querying Cartesian position (`t.pos()`, `t.xcor()`, `t.ycor()`), compass heading angles (`t.heading()`, 0° to 360°), cursor visibility toggling (`t.showturtle()` / `t.st()`, `t.hideturtle()` / `t.ht()`), inspecting visibility state (`t.isvisible()`), understanding the dramatic rendering performance gains of hiding the cursor during complex mathematical and recursive loops, managing pen drawing states (`t.penup()`, `t.pendown()`, `t.isdown()`), and customizing pen width and stroke colors. Complete real-world institutional cursor Heads-Up Display (HUD) case study tracking real-time pen telemetry for students Mamata, Mahima, and Susmita across Barrackpore, Kolkata, and Ichapur.
