Comprehensive master guide to rotational kinematics, angular orientation, polygon turning geometry, and degree modes in Python Turtle Graphics. Deep dive into relative rotational turning with `left(angle)` (alias `lt()`) and `right(angle)` (alias `rt()`), absolute directional heading with `setheading(angle)` (alias `seth()`), understanding the fundamental exterior angle theorem for regular $N$-sided polygons ($\text{turn\_angle} = 360^\circ / N$), switching between standard sexagesimal degrees and trigonometric radians via `screen.degrees()` and `screen.radians()`, modulo 360 angular normalization, and preventing rotational cumulative drift. Complete real-world institutional multi-angle campus compass navigation case study guiding students Mamata, Mahima, and Susmita along exact radial bearings across Barrackpore, Kolkata, and Ichapur.

4. Degrees vs Radians Mode (`screen.degrees()` vs `screen.radians()`)

6. Interior vs Exterior Angles in Geometric Art
