1. WHAT IS FORWARD EULER INTEGRATION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Euler integration is the numerical method of approximating continuous physics
by stepping discrete delta time intervals (`dt`):
1. Acceleration updates Velocity: `vy -= gravity * dt`
2. Velocity updates Position:     `x += vx * dt`, `y += vy * dt`

2. SIMULATING GRAVITATIONAL PULL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gravity is a constant downward acceleration acting on the vertical velocity:
- On every frame tick: `vy = vy - g`
- As a result, the projectile rises decelerating to its peak apex (`vy = 0`),
  then accelerates downward, tracing a perfect mathematical parabola!

3. BOUNCING RESTITUTION & FRICTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Coefficient of Restitution (`elasticity`):
  Fraction of velocity retained after bounce: `vy = -vy * elasticity` (0.0 < e <= 1.0).
- Ground Friction:
  Horizontally decelerates rolling objects: `vx = vx * friction` (e.g. 0.98).
