1. THE ROOT CAUSES OF SCREEN FLICKERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flicker occurs when the monitor displays the canvas while it is being partially
cleared or redrawn. In unbuffered graphics:
1. `t.clear()` wipes the canvas (User sees a flash of white/blank background).
2. The turtle redraws the shape (User sees intermediate line strokes).
3. Repeating this 30 to 60 times a second creates violent strobe flickering.

2. THE 3-PILLAR FLICKER CURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-Buffering with `screen.tracer(0)`:
   Forces the erase (`clear()`) and redraw to happen strictly in offscreen RAM.
   The user never sees the blank canvas or intermediate line strokes.

2. Single GPU Flush with `screen.update()`:
   Swaps the completed frame atomically to the display in a fraction of a millisecond.

3. Multi-Turtle Layer Isolation:
   Use separate Turtle objects for static backdrops (`bg_turtle`) and dynamic
   sprites (`sprite_turtle`). Only the sprite turtle is cleared per frame,
   saving 90% of redraw computation.
