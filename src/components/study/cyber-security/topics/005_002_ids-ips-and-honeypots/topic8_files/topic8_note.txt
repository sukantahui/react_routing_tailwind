1. DEFINITION & THE GOLDEN HONEYPOT AXIOM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Honeypot is a decoy security asset (server, database, application, or credential) intentionally deployed 
on a network with NO authorized production purpose.

The Golden Honeypot Axiom (Lance Spitzner, The Honeynet Project):
  "A honeypot has no authorized legitimate business traffic. Therefore, ANY packet, connection attempt, 
   or login interaction touching a honeypot is 100% MALICIOUS or ANOMALOUS by definition."

Key Value Proposition:
  - While traditional IDS sensors generate thousands of false alarms, a honeypot generates ZERO FALSE POSITIVES. 
    Every alert is a high-confidence True Positive representing active reconnaissance, lateral movement, or insider threat.


2. THE 4 STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES OF DECEPTION TECHNOLOGY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HIGH-FIDELITY EARLY WARNING:
   - Catches attackers during the initial reconnaissance and lateral movement phase before they reach real crown jewels.
2. ADVERSARY TIME DILATION & RESOURCE EXHAUSTION:
   - Forces attackers to waste hours probing fake simulated networks, increasing the likelihood they make a mistake.
3. ZERO-DAY THREAT INTELLIGENCE:
   - Captures novel zero-day exploits, rootkits, and malware payloads in flight for reverse engineering.
4. EVIDENCE COLLECTION & ATTRIBUTION:
   - Records keystrokes, downloaded toolkits, and attacker C2 IP addresses for law enforcement and CERT-In.


3. THE SPECTRUM OF DECEPTION ASSETS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DECOY SERVERS (HONEYPOTS):
   - Fake SSH, RDP, Web, and Database servers simulating vulnerable services across internal subnets.
2. HONEYTOKENS (CANARYTOKENS):
   - Fake embedded credentials, AWS API access keys, or SQL connection strings planted in memory, source code, 
     or browser caches. If an attacker uses the token, a webhook alert fires immediately.
3. HONEYFILES (BAIT DOCUMENTS):
   - Fake sensitive files (e.g. `salaries_2026.docx`, `employee_tax_pan_cards.xlsx`) embedded with invisible web bugs 
     or DNS beacons that trigger alerts when opened.
4. HONEYCREDENTIALS (HONEY ACCOUNTS):
   - Fake domain administrator accounts in Active Directory (e.g. `svc_backup_admin`). Legitimate staff never use 
     it; any Kerberos ticket request (AS-REQ) immediately exposes an active Kerberoasting attack.


4. LEGAL, ETHICAL & CONTAINMENT CONSIDERATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. ENTRAPMENT VS ENTICEMENT:
   - Enticement (Legal): Placing a tempting decoy on an existing private network and observing who attacks it.
   - Entrapment (Illegal in criminal law): Actively tricking or coercing an innocent party into committing an 
     illegal act they would not otherwise attempt.
2. DATA CONTAINMENT & EGRESS FILTERING:
   - The critical risk: If an adversary fully compromises a high-interaction honeypot, they may attempt to use 
     it as a pivot or launchpad to attack real production systems or external targets.
   - Mitigation: Strict egress firewall rules and rate-limiting routers isolate the honeypot in a quarantined 
     sandbox, allowing ingress attacks but preventing outbound pivots.
