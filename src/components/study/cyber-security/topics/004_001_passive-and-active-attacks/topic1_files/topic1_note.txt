1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PHYSICAL FOUNDATIONS OF INTERCEPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Passive interception threats target the CONFIDENTIALITY (Pillar "C") of communication 
systems without modifying data payloads, injecting rogue packets, or altering network latency.

  * EAVESDROPPING : The software/logical capture of data packets traversing a communication 
    channel (e.g. Promiscuous mode sniffing, unencrypted Wi-Fi radio capture, SPAN abuse).
  * WIRETAPPING   : The physical/hardware interception of electronic or optical signals directly 
    from the transmission medium (e.g. Optical fiber macrobending, copper inductive clamps).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PHYSICS & MATHEMATICAL PRINCIPLES OF WIRETAPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. OPTICAL FIBER MACROBENDING TAP :
   - In standard fiber, Total Internal Reflection (TIR) confines light within the core:
       Critical Angle: θ_c = arcsin(n_cladding / n_core)
   - When a mechanical macrobending tap curves the glass fiber beyond its critical radius, 
     a fraction (1% to 3%) of photons leak through the cladding via evanescent wave refraction.
   - Optical Attenuation Equation:
       α = - (10 / L) * log10( P_out / P_in )
   - An optical tap causing -0.25 dB power loss remains invisible to standard network alarms!

B. COPPER INDUCTIVE CLAMP TAP :
   - Current flowing through copper conductors generates an alternating concentric magnetic field:
       B = (μ * I) / (2 * π * r)
   - An adversary clamps an inductive coil around unshielded twisted pair (UTP) cables. 
     By Faraday's Law of Induction (e = -N * dΦ/dt), electrical signals are captured 
     without stripping wire insulation or cutting the circuit!

C. TEMPEST / VAN ECK PHREAKING (RF EMANATIONS) :
   - High-frequency clocks in display cables (HDMI/DisplayPort) and CPUs radiate electromagnetic 
     waves that propagate through building walls.
   - Field Strength:
       E = sqrt(30 * P_t * G_t) / d
   - Sensitive radio receivers tuned to harmonic frequencies can reconstruct live desktop screens.

4. DEFENSIVE ENGINEERING & DETECTION PARADOX
================================================================================

THE DETECTION PARADOX :
  * Because wiretaps and promiscuous sniffers emit ZERO outbound packets, standard routers, 
    firewalls, and SIEM tools receive ZERO log footprints!
  * Detection requires specialized physical instrumentation:
    1. Optical Time-Domain Reflectometry (OTDR): Measures pulse time-delay:
         d = (c * t) / (2 * n_core)
       Detects attenuation drops as small as 0.05 dB at exact distances (e.g. 14.2 km).
    2. Time-Domain Reflectometry (TDR): Identifies copper cable impedance mismatches.

THE DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH BLUEPRINT :
  1. Layer 1: Armored conduit trays, OTDR optical monitoring, TEMPEST Faraday cages.
  2. Layer 2: IEEE 802.1AE MACsec line-rate encryption (AES-256-GCM) on all switch trunks.
  3. Layer 3: IPsec Tunnel Mode with Encapsulating Security Payload (ESP).
  4. Layer 7: TLS 1.3 with Encrypted Client Hello (ECH) and DNS over HTTPS (DoH).

5. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INDIAN TELEGRAPH ACT 1885 (SECTION 26) :
    - Unlawful interception or wiretapping carries criminal imprisonment up to 3 years.

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 43(a) : Civil damages up to ₹1 CRORE for unauthorized extraction/copying of data.
    - Section 66    : Criminal penalties (up to 3 years prison) for hacking & unauthorized interception.
    - Section 69    : Strict legal procedure for lawful interception—authorized EXCLUSIVELY by 
      the Union Home Secretary or State Home Secretary.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5)  : Mandates reasonable technical security safeguards to prevent passive leaks.
    - Section 33    : Penalties up to ₹250 CRORES for failure to protect citizen data.
