1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & DIGITAL WATERMARKING DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Digital Watermarking is the science of embedding an imperceptible, permanent digital signal 
(such as copyright metadata, recipient identifier, or serial number) directly into the 
host multimedia signal (images, audio, video, 3D models):

          I' = Embed( I_cover, Watermark_W, Key_K )  |  W_extracted = Detect( I', Key_K )

* ENCRYPTION VS DIGITAL WATERMARKING:
  - Encryption protects data in transit, but once decrypted, content has zero protection.
  - Watermarking permanently modifies the host signal, surviving decoding, copying, and re-broadcasting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WATERMARK TAXONOMY & CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. BY ROBUSTNESS:
     - ROBUST       : Survives aggressive signal processing (JPEG compression, cropping, filtering) 
                      to prove legal copyright ownership in court.
     - FRAGILE      : Breaks on any 1-bit alteration to detect unauthorized tampering in medical/legal records.
     - SEMI-FRAGILE : Tolerates benign lossy compression (JPEG 80%) but flags malicious content tampering.

  2. BY DETECTION REQUIREMENT:
     - BLIND     : Detector extracts watermark without requiring the original unwatermarked cover media.
     - NON-BLIND : Detector requires the pristine original image to isolate and subtract the watermark signal.

  3. BY VISIBILITY:
     - VISIBLE   : Semi-transparent visual logo/stamp (e.g. broadcast network logo).
     - INVISIBLE : Perceptually transparent (PSNR > 38 dB, SSIM > 0.98), imperceptible to human eyes.

4. FIDELITY & DETECTION METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PEAK SIGNAL-TO-NOISE RATIO (PSNR):
  - PSNR = 10 * log10( 255^2 / MSE ) (Target: > 38 dB for imperceptibility).

* STRUCTURAL SIMILARITY INDEX (SSIM):
  - Measures luminance, contrast, and structural preservation (Target: > 0.98).

* NORMALIZED CROSS-CORRELATION (NC) & BIT ERROR RATE (BER):
  - NC evaluates watermark extraction fidelity (NC = 1.0 indicates perfect extraction).

5. INDIAN STATUTORY COMPLIANCE & LEGAL PENALTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* INDIAN COPYRIGHT ACT 1957 SECTION 65A:
  - Protection of Technological Protection Measures (TPM / DRM). Circumventing watermarks or DRM 
    with intent to infringe copyright is punishable with imprisonment up to 2 years and fines.

* INDIAN COPYRIGHT ACT 1957 SECTION 65B:
  - Protection of Rights Management Information (RMI). Removing or altering digital watermarks or 
    embedded digital signatures carries criminal liability of up to 2 years imprisonment.

* DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023:
  - Forensic watermarking (Traitor Tracing) enforces accountability for insider hospital EHR leaks.

7. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL WATERMARKING HABITS:
  1. Default to DWT-DCT Mid-Band Frequency Embedding: Guarantees survival against lossy JPEG compression.
  2. Use Reversible Watermarking for Medical Scans: Preserves diagnostic accuracy with 100% lossless restoration.
  3. Deploy Dynamic Traitor Tracing: Inject unique employee IDs when downloading sensitive cloud documents.
  4. Enforce Indian Copyright Act Section 65B: Log watermark verification certificates for court admissibility.

* COMMON BEGINNER WATERMARKING PITFALLS:
  - Relying on Spatial LSB for copyright protection (Destroyed instantly by standard JPEG compression).
  - Modifying DC low-frequency DCT coefficients (Causes visible, objectionable blocky image distortion).
  - Modifying high-frequency DCT coefficients (Completely wiped out by lossy JPEG quantization).
  - Over-embedding (Setting alpha too high, resulting in ruined PSNR < 30 dB).
