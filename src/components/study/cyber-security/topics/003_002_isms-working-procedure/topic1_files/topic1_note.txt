1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & EVOLUTION OF ISO/IEC 27001
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ISO/IEC 27001 is the world's most widely recognized international standard specifying requirements 
for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an ISMS:

  * THE EVOLUTION : BS 7799 (1995) ➔ ISO/IEC 17799 ➔ ISO 27001:2005 (133 controls) 
                    ➔ ISO 27001:2013 (114 controls in 14 domains) 
                    ➔ ISO 27001:2022 (93 controls in 4 themes + 11 NEW controls).
  * THE PARADIGM  : A unified two-part standard: Mandatory Management Clauses (4-10) and the 
                    Reference Control Catalog (Annex A).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MANDATORY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM CLAUSES (CLAUSES 4 TO 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

+--------------------+-------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| STANDARD CLAUSE    | REQUIREMENT TITLE       | MANDATORY OPERATIONAL DELIVERABLES             |
+--------------------+-------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Clause 4           | Context of Organization | Internal/External Issues, Stakeholders, Scope  |
| Clause 5           | Leadership              | Board Policy, Budget, CISO Appointment (5.3)   |
| Clause 6           | Planning (Risk Engine)  | Risk Assessment (6.1.2), Treatment & SoA (6.1.3|
| Clause 7           | Support                 | Resources, Competence, Awareness, Documentation|
| Clause 8           | Operation               | Operational Controls & Risk Treatment Execution|
| Clause 9           | Performance Evaluation  | Security KPIs, Internal Audit (9.2), Mgt Review|
| Clause 10          | Improvement             | Nonconformity, Root Cause (5-Whys), CAPA (10.1)|
+--------------------+-------------------------+------------------------------------------------+

3. THE 4 ANNEX A CONTROL THEMES (93 CONTROLS TOTAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * 1. ORGANIZATIONAL (Clause A.5 - 37 Controls) : Policies, asset management, vendor risk, 
                                                  incident response, privacy governance.
  * 2. PEOPLE         (Clause A.6 - 8 Controls)  : Background screening, JML lifecycle, awareness.
  * 3. PHYSICAL       (Clause A.7 - 14 Controls) : Physical perimeters, clear desk, monitoring.
  * 4. TECHNOLOGICAL  (Clause A.8 - 34 Controls) : Cryptography, IAM, network security, DLP, 
                                                  secure coding, configuration management.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE 11 BRAND-NEW CONTROLS INTRODUCED IN ISO 27001:2022
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * A.5.7  Threat Intelligence        : Actionable analysis of external threat actor TTPs.
  * A.5.23 Cloud Services Security    : Security requirements for AWS/Azure/GCP workloads.
  * A.5.30 ICT Readiness for BCP      : Redundant architectures for business continuity.
  * A.7.4  Physical Security Monitor  : CCTV, perimeter alarms, and intruder detection.
  * A.8.9  Configuration Management   : Hardening baselines and preventing configuration drift.
  * A.8.10 Information Deletion       : Automated data purging (DPDP Storage Limitation).
  * A.8.11 Data Masking               : Dynamic masking of sensitive PAN/Aadhaar fields.
  * A.8.12 Data Leakage Prevention    : Endpoint, network, and cloud DLP filtering.
  * A.8.16 Monitoring Activities      : User behavior analytics (UBA) and SIEM correlation.
  * A.8.23 Web Filtering              : Blocking malicious domains and phishing websites.
  * A.8.28 Secure Coding              : DevSecOps secure development lifecycle (OWASP Top 10).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE 5 CONTROL ATTRIBUTES TAXONOMY (ISO/IEC 27002:2022)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every Annex A control is classified across 5 multidimensional attributes:
  1. Control Type              : #Preventive, #Detective, #Corrective
  2. InfoSec Properties        : #Confidentiality, #Integrity, #Availability
  3. Cybersecurity Concepts    : #Identify, #Protect, #Detect, #Respond, #Recover (NIST CSF)
  4. Operational Capabilities  : #Governance, #Asset_management, #IAM, #Cryptography, #Physical
  5. Security Domains          : #Governance_and_Ecosystem, #Protection, #Defense, #Resilience

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. STATUTORY & REGULATORY SAFE HARBOR UNDER INDIAN LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * IT ACT 2000 SECTION 43A (SPDI RULES 2011):
    - ISO/IEC 27001 certification constitutes conclusive proof of "Reasonable Security Practices".
  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8(5) & SECTION 33:
    - Annex A controls (A.8.10, A.8.11, A.8.12, A.8.24) fulfill statutory technical safeguards, 
      immunizing the enterprise from ₹250 Crore fines!

8. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL ISO 27001 HABITS:
  1. Leverage the 5 Attributes Taxonomy: Filter controls by NIST concepts and CIA properties.
  2. Implement All 11 New 2022 Controls: Prioritize Cloud Security (A.5.23) and Data Masking (A.8.11).
  3. Keep the SoA Updated: Review the Statement of Applicability before every change management cycle.
  4. Integrate Multi-Standards via Harmonized Structure: Combine ISO 27001 with ISO 22301 and ISO 27701.

* COMMON BEGINNER ISO 27001 PITFALLS:
  - Confusing Clauses 4-10 (Mandatory) with Annex A (Selective based on Risk Assessment).
  - Failing to justify excluded controls in the Statement of Applicability.
  - Ignoring the 11 new 2022 controls during recertification audits.
  - Treating ISO 27001 as a purely technical checklist without executive governance.
