1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE 12-MONTH ISMS IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implementing an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management System is a comprehensive 
organizational transformation spanning executive governance, risk engineering, technical hardening, 
and independent accredited auditing over a structured 12-month lifecycle:

  * THE GOAL      : Transform ad-hoc security practices into a certifiable, resilient ISMS.
  * THE TIMELINE  : 6 structured phases spanning Initiation (Month 1) to External Certification (Month 12).
  * THE OUTCOME   : ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certificate + Total Safe Harbor under Indian Cyber Law!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EXTERNAL AUDIT ARCHITECTURE : STAGE 1 VS STAGE 2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * STAGE 1 AUDIT (Readiness & Documentation Review):
    - Assesses ISMS Scope (4.3), Board Policy (5.2), Statement of Applicability (6.1.3), 
      completed Internal Audit (9.2), and Management Review (9.3). Missing docs block Stage 2!
  * STAGE 2 AUDIT (Operational & Technical Control Testing):
    - Auditors conduct on-site/remote inspections, developer interviews, and Evidence Triangulation 
      (Document + Interview + System Log) across all 93 Annex A controls in live production.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EVIDENCE TRIANGULATION AUDIT TECHNIQUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Auditors verify controls using 3 independent pillars:
  1. Document  : Written Standard Operating Procedure (e.g. SOP-IAM-02 requires FIDO2 MFA).
  2. Interview : Staff answers: "How do you log in to the production Kubernetes console?"
  3. Telemetry : Auditor verifies live Okta/CloudTrail logs showing hardware FIDO2 tokens used.

6. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL IMPLEMENTATION HABITS:
  1. Secure Board Commitment Early: Present business ROI and DPDP legal risk to the Board in Month 1.
  2. Populate 100% of the SoA: Document technical justification for every included and excluded control.
  3. Execute Full Internal Audit before Stage 1: Never face external auditors without a mock audit (9.2).
  4. Practice Evidence Triangulation: Ensure written SOPs match actual developer habits and live cloud logs.

* COMMON BEGINNER IMPLEMENTATION PITFALLS:
  - "Scope Slicing" Failure (Attempting to exclude core production databases from ISMS scope).
  - Unjustified Excluded Controls in SoA (Excluding controls without documented technical rationale).
  - Skipping Management Review (Facing Stage 1 audit without signed Board minutes under Clause 9.3).
  - Leaving Major NCs Unaddressed (Missing the 90-day CAPA remediation window after Stage 2).
