1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & ANNEX A CONTROL RESTRUCTURING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Annex A of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the universal reference benchmark of 93 information security 
controls. In the 2022 revision, controls were modernized and streamlined from 114 controls across 
14 domains into 93 controls across 4 logical, operational themes:

  * THE THEMES    : Organizational (37), People (8), Physical (14), and Technological (34).
  * THE ATTRIBUTES: Every control is tagged across 5 metadata attributes (Type, CIA, NIST, 
                    Operational Capability, Domain) enabling dynamic filtering and compliance mapping.

3. THE 5 CONTROL ATTRIBUTES TAXONOMY (ISO/IEC 27002:2022)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every Annex A control is categorized by 5 multidimensional tags:
  1. Control Type              : #Preventive, #Detective, #Corrective
  2. InfoSec Properties        : #Confidentiality, #Integrity, #Availability
  3. Cybersecurity Concepts    : #Identify, #Protect, #Detect, #Respond, #Recover (NIST CSF)
  4. Operational Capabilities  : #IAM, #Information_protection, #Cryptography, #Physical_security
  5. Security Domains          : #Governance_and_Ecosystem, #Protection, #Defense, #Resilience

4. STATUTORY INDIAN CYBER LAW MAPPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023:
    - Section 8(5) Technical Safeguards : A.8.11 (Masking), A.8.12 (DLP), A.8.24 (Crypto).
    - Section 8(7) Storage Limitation   : A.8.10 (Information Deletion / Crypto-Shredding).
    - Section 33 Penalties              : Full Annex A shields enterprise from ₹250 Cr fines!

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 (CERT-In DIRECTIONS 2022):
    - Section 70B 180-Day Log Storage   : A.8.15 (Logging) & A.8.16 (Monitoring Activities).
    - Section 70B NTP Clock Sync        : A.8.17 (Clock Synchronization with NPL IST Servers).
    - Section 70B 6-Hour Escalation SLA : A.5.24 & A.5.28 (Information Security Incident Management).

7. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL ANNEX A HABITS:
  1. Filter Controls by 5 Attributes: Query by #Preventive and #Confidentiality for DPDP mapping.
  2. Enforce Maker-Checker (A.5.3): Separate software development, QA approval, and deployment roles.
  3. Automate Information Deletion (A.8.10): Deploy TTL partition drops and cloud KMS Crypto-Shredding.
  4. Retain 180-Day Indian Logs (A.8.16): Archive immutable SIEM telemetry under IT Act Section 70B.

* COMMON BEGINNER ANNEX A PITFALLS:
  - Focusing only on Technology while ignoring People (8 controls) and Physical (14 controls).
  - Leaving default vendor configurations unchanged (Violates Control A.8.9 Configuration Management).
  - Allowing developers to approve their own pull requests (Violates Control A.5.3 Segregation of Duties).
  - Failing to synchronize server clocks with Indian Standard Time (Violates Control A.8.17).
