1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE 4-TIER DOCUMENTATION PYRAMID
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An Information Security Management System cannot exist solely as verbal instructions or ad-hoc 
scripts. ISO/IEC 27001 mandates a structured 4-Tier Documentation Hierarchy that establishes 
governance authority, technical parameters, operational workflows, and advisory best practices:

  * TIER 1 : POLICIES   ➔ Executive Strategy & Intent (Mandatory | Board Signed | Why & What).
  * TIER 2 : STANDARDS  ➔ Measurable Technical Baselines (Mandatory | Numbers/Ciphers | Criteria).
  * TIER 3 : PROCEDURES ➔ Step-by-Step SOP Runbooks (Mandatory | Chronological | How & Who).
  * TIER 4 : GUIDELINES ➔ Advisory Best Practices (Discretionary | Tips | How to Optimize).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. CONTROL OF DOCUMENTED INFORMATION (CLAUSE 7.5)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every ISMS document must satisfy the mandatory requirements of ISO 27001 Clause 7.5:
  1. Unique Identification : Document ID (e.g. POL-SEC-08), Title, Release Date, and Semantic Version.
  2. Formal Approval       : Explicitly signed by designated executive authority before release.
  3. Controlled Access     : Published on a central Intranet portal with read-only permissions for staff.
  4. Change History        : Complete revision table documenting author, date, and exact changes made.
  5. Retirement Protocol   : Obsolete policies marked 'SUPERSEDED' and archived in encrypted vaults.

4. STATUTORY SAFE HARBOR UNDER INDIAN CYBER LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * IT ACT 2000 SECTION 43A (SPDI RULES 2011):
    - Producing an audited 4-tier documentation suite provides conclusive judicial proof of 
      "Reasonable Security Practices", immunizing corporate directors in civil compensation suits.
  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8 & SECTION 33:
    - Documented itemized consent registers, DPO assessment logs, and crypto-shredding deletion 
      manifests (A.8.10) provide total protection against ₹250 CRORE statutory fines!
  * CERT-In DIRECTIONS 2022:
    - Enforces documented 6-hour incident escalation playbooks and 180-day log retention SOPs.

6. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL DOCUMENTATION HABITS:
  1. Keep Policies High-Level: Avoid hardcoding software version numbers in Tier 1 board policies.
  2. Put Technical Specs in Standards: Define exact cipher suites (AES-256-GCM) in Tier 2 Standards.
  3. Write Numbered Chronological SOPs: Ensure Tier 3 runbooks can be executed during crises without ambiguity.
  4. Control Document Versions (Clause 7.5): Watermark and archive obsolete policies immediately.

* COMMON BEGINNER DOCUMENTATION PITFALLS:
  - "Paper Tiger" Syndrome (Writing elaborate policies that staff never read or execute in practice).
  - Mixing Tiers (Putting technical CLI commands inside board-level policies).
  - Un-approved Draft Documents (Distributing draft Word documents lacking CISO/Board approval logs).
  - Missing Review Dates (Failing to conduct mandatory annual reviews of security policies).
