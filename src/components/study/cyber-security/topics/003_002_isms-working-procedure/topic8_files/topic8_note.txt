1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE CONTINUAL IMPROVEMENT ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Achieving initial ISO/IEC 27001 certification is only the starting line. An Information Security 
Management System must continually improve its suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness under 
Clause 10 to combat entropy, architectural drift, personnel turnover, and emerging cyber exploits:

  * THE REALITY   : A static ISMS rapidly decays within 6 months.
  * THE PARADIGM  : Continuous maintenance through Security KPIs (9.1), Internal Audits (9.2), 
                    Management Reviews (9.3), and 5-Whys CAPA Remediation (10.1) ensures defenses 
                    remain unassailable over multi-year cycles.

3. THE 5 CORE SECURITY METRICS & KPIs (CLAUSE 9.1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Incident Velocity        : Mean Time to Detect (MTTD < 15s) & Mean Time to Remediate (MTTR < 45s).
  2. Patching Velocity        : 100% of Critical CVSS >= 9.0 vulnerabilities patched within 48 Hours.
  3. Phish-Prone Percentage   : Employee phishing simulation failure rate kept below 2.0%.
  4. Access Review Compliance : 100% quarterly certification of privileged cloud & database accounts.
  5. CAPA Closure SLA         : 100% of internal/external audit non-conformities closed within 90 days.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EXECUTIVE MANAGEMENT REVIEW (CLAUSE 9.3) INPUTS & OUTPUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * MANDATORY INPUTS  : Audit results, KPI telemetry, status of past CAPAs, emerging cyber threats, 
                        DPDP Act compliance status, and resource needs.
  * MANDATORY OUTPUTS : Executive decisions on policy updates, improvements, and board capital 
                        budget allocations (e.g. allocating ₹25 Lakhs for automated DLP tooling).

7. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL ISMS MAINTENANCE HABITS:
  1. Automate IaC Drift Detection: Use AWS Config to catch unauthorized infrastructure changes in seconds.
  2. Enforce Auditor Independence (Clause 9.2): Never allow engineers to audit their own codebase.
  3. Track Critical Patch Velocity: Enforce a strict 48-hour patch SLA for vulnerabilities with CVSS >= 9.0.
  4. Perform Quarterly Access Reviews: Certify privileged credentials quarterly to eliminate orphaned accounts.

* COMMON BEGINNER MAINTENANCE PITFALLS:
  - "Certificate on the Wall" Syndrome (Ignoring the ISMS after initial certification until the next audit).
  - Self-Auditing Non-Conformity (Allowing DevOps leads to audit their own Kubernetes clusters).
  - Confusing Corrections with Corrective Actions (Fixing the symptom without eliminating the root cause).
  - Stale Statements of Applicability (Failing to update the SoA when cloud architecture evolves).
