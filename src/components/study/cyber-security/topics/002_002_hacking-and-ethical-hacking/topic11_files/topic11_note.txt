3. LANDMARK HISTORICAL CASE STUDIES ANALYZED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1. 2016 BANGLADESH BANK SWIFT HEIST (₹650 CRORES / $81M)]
  * Root Cause: Unsegmented $10 office switches connected to SWIFT gateway.
  * Malware Tradecraft: Lazarus Group injected `evtdt.exe`, hooking a PDF printer DLL (`fxservice.dll`) 
    to prevent paper receipts of fraudulent MT103 wire transfers from printing.
  * Ethical Remediation: Mandatory Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) & dedicated air-gapped SWIFT VLANs.

[2. 2021 COLONIAL PIPELINE RANSOMWARE CRISIS ($4.4M / ₹35 CRORES)]
  * Root Cause: Single-factor legacy VPN account with no Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  * Impact: Paralyzed 5,500 miles of fuel pipelines across US East Coast for 6 days.
  * Ethical Remediation: Universal FIDO2 MFA enforcement & automated dark-web password audit.

[3. 2022 AIIMS NEW DELHI RANSOMWARE OUTAGE]
  * Root Cause: Ransomware compromised core EHR database and laboratory billing servers.
  * Impact: Paralyzed online services for 14 days; doctors reverted to manual paper registers.
  * Ethical Remediation: Immutable AWS S3 WORM storage & strict hospital clinical micro-segmentation.

[4. 2015 UKRAINE POWER GRID BLACKOUT (BLACKENERGY 3 / INDUSTROYER)]
  * Root Cause: Spear-phishing macro gave remote HMI mouse control to Russian Sandworm APT.
  * Attack Sequence: Opened 220kV circuit breakers across 30 substations and wiped bridge firmware (KillDisk).
  * Ethical Remediation: Hardware optical data diodes & cryptographically signed IEC 61850 commands.

[5. 2020 SOLARWINDS ORION SUPPLY CHAIN ATTACK (SUNBURST / UNC2452)]
  * Root Cause: Build environment injected backdoor code during official DLL compilation.
  * Impact: 18,000 global enterprise and government clients installed trojanized updates.
  * Ethical Remediation: Multi-party build verification & Software Bill of Materials (CycloneDX SBOM).

6. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL ARCHITECTURAL HABITS:
  1. Enforce Optical Air-Gaps on SCADA: Never trust software firewalls alone for power grids.
  2. Implement Immutable WORM Backups: Ensure ransomware cannot delete backups even with admin keys.
  3. Deploy Hardware Security Modules: Never store master cryptographic keys in plaintext server RAM.

* COMMON BEGINNER INDUSTRY MISTAKES:
  - Assuming internal networks are safe once past the perimeter firewall (Castle-and-Moat fallacy).
  - Mixing hospital patient Wi-Fi on the same subnet as smart infusion pumps and MRI machines.
  - Storing credit card CVVs or cleartext passwords in database tables (Violates PCI-DSS & DPDP Act).
  - Forgetting to conduct live tabletop disaster drills with executive leadership.
