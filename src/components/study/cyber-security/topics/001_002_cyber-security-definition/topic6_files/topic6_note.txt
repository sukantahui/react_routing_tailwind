1. THE CYBER THREAT LANDSCAPE & ACTOR TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Cyber Threat Landscape represents the dynamic, global ecosystem of all active threats,
threat actors, attack vectors, vulnerabilities, and exploitation methodologies.

The 5 Major Threat Actor Categories:
1. Script Kiddies:
   Unskilled amateurs utilizing automated, pre-compiled tools without understanding root mechanics.
2. Hacktivists:
   Ideologically, politically, or socially motivated groups launching DDoS and website defacements (e.g. Anonymous).
3. Cyber Criminal Syndicates (e-Crime):
   Financially motivated organized cartels running Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) and banking fraud.
4. Insider Threats:
   Malicious, negligent, or compromised employees with valid credentials and internal access.
5. Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) / Nation-States:
   Military-grade, state-sponsored intelligence units conducting multi-year espionage and sabotage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CYBER THREAT INTELLIGENCE (CTI) & THE PYRAMID OF PAIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Pyramid of Pain (David Bianco) measures the defensive impact of blocking adversary indicators:
• Level 1 - Hash Values (Trivial to change - MD5/SHA256).
• Level 2 - IP Addresses (Easy to change - proxy VPNs/Tor).
• Level 3 - Domain Names (Simple to change - fast-flux/DGA).
• Level 4 - Network/Host Artifacts (Annoying to change - URI patterns/C2 protocols).
• Level 5 - Tools (Challenging to change - rebuilding custom trojans/compilers).
• Level 6 - TTPs (Tactics, Techniques & Procedures - TOUGH! Forces adversary to reinvent tradecraft).

The MITRE ATT&CK Matrix:
Structures 14 tactical adversary objectives from Initial Access and Execution to Exfiltration and Impact.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. STEALTH TACTICS: LIVING-OFF-THE-LAND (LotL) & DWELL TIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Living-off-the-Land Binaries (LOLBins):
  Adversaries utilize legitimate built-in operating system tools (PowerShell, WMI, certutil)
  to evade traditional signature-based antivirus detection.
• Dwell Time:
  The duration an attacker remains undetected inside a network (global average: 10-16 days).
  Defended using proactive threat hunting and behavioral Indicators of Attack (IoAs).

4. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Precision Foundry MITRE ATT&CK TTP Behavioral Mapping (Debangshu - Barrackpore)
Debangshu mapped foundry network telemetry to the MITRE ATT&CK matrix in Barrackpore for ₹3,80,000.
When an adversary attempted LOLBin execution via `certutil.exe`, automated EDR behavioral rules
intercepted the technique (T1105 Ingress Tool Transfer) in 8 seconds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Diagnostic Clinic Ransomware RaaS Behavioral Defense (Mahima - Ichapur)
Mahima deployed an advanced EDR and behavioral sandbox solution in Ichapur for ₹4,20,000.
When an employee opened a weaponized invoice, the system detected Volume Shadow Copy deletion
commands (`vssadmin delete shadows`), terminating the ransomware process before encryption began.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: State Research University STIX/TAXII Threat Feed Ingestion (Mamata - Kolkata)
Mamata integrated automated STIX/TAXII threat intelligence feeds into university firewalls in Kolkata
for ₹5,20,000. The firewall autonomously blocked 14,000 newly active botnet command-and-control IPs
streamed in real time from international CERT advisories.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: Cyber Security Lab APT Attribution & Reverse Engineering Sandbox (Abhronila - Jadavpur)
Abhronila established a malware reverse engineering laboratory in Jadavpur for ₹2,40,000.
Students dissect live nation-state APT samples in isolated Ghidra sandboxes, extracting custom C2

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never rely exclusively on static hash blocklists (Level 1); focus on detecting adversary TTPs (Level 6).
- Monitor built-in administrative tools (PowerShell, WMI) for anomalous Living-off-the-Land activity.
- Proactively hunt for stealth adversaries to reduce dwell time from weeks down to under 24 hours.
- Formulate all enterprise CTI feeds, EDR sandboxes, and hunting platform budgets in Indian Rupees (₹).
