1. DEFINITION & CLASSIFICATION OF CYBER CRIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cyber Crime refers to any unlawful activity wherein a computer, digital device, or network
is either the target of the crime, the instrument/tool used, or the digital scene of evidence.

Primary Classifications:
1. Computer as a Target:
   Ransomware, trojans, DDoS attacks, zero-day exploitation, website defacements, buffer overflows.
2. Computer as a Tool:
   Financial wire fraud, UPI scams, Business Email Compromise (BEC), SIM swapping, identity theft,
   cyberstalking, predatory loan app extortion, child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INDIAN LEGAL FRAMEWORK (IT ACT 2000 & PENALTIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Section 65: Tampering with computer source documents (Up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹2 Lakh fine).
• Section 66: Computer hacking and system damage (Up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹5 Lakh fine).
• Section 66C: Identity theft / stolen passwords (Up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹1 Lakh fine).
• Section 66D: Cheating by personation using computer resources (Up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹1 Lakh fine).
• Section 66E: Privacy violation / non-consensual image capture (Up to 3 years imprisonment + ₹2 Lakh fine).
• Section 66F: Cyber Terrorism against national security/infrastructure (Punishable with Life Imprisonment).
• Section 67/67A: Transmitting obscene or sexually explicit content (Up to 5 years imprisonment + ₹10 Lakh fine).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CITIZEN REPORTING & DIGITAL FORENSICS PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• National Cyber Crime Helpline '1930' & CFCFRMS:
  Immediate reporting within the 'Golden Hour' (1-2 hours) allows automated freezing of stolen
  funds across multi-layer banking mule networks via the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C).
• Digital Forensics & Chain of Custody:
  - Cryptographic Hashing (SHA-256): Proves evidence integrity from crime scene to courtroom.
  - Hardware Write-Blockers: Permits read-only disk imaging without modifying timestamps.
  - Volatile Memory Forensics: Extracts live in-memory encryption keys and malware payloads before reboot.

4. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Precision Foundry BEC Invoice Fraud Interception & FIR Filing (Debangshu - Barrackpore)
Debangshu discovered a spoofed vendor email attempting to divert a ₹28,00,000 casting supplier payment
in Barrackpore. Dialing 1930 and providing email header metadata enabled cyber police to freeze the
fraudulent mule account within 45 minutes, prosecuting the criminal under IT Act Section 66D.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Diagnostic Clinic SIM-Swap & OTP Bypass Investigation (Mahima - Ichapur)
Mahima investigated a SIM-swap credential theft targeting an Ichapur clinic's corporate account for ₹1,80,000.
Forensic logs proved telecom store negligence, enabling the clinic to recover ₹4,50,000 in diverted funds
and mandate FIDO2 hardware tokens, eliminating SMS OTP dependencies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: State Research University Web Magecart Skimmer Removal (Mamata - Kolkata)
Mamata led the digital forensic investigation of a malicious Magecart payment skimmer in Kolkata for
₹3,20,000. Identifying injected JavaScript on the university merchandise portal, the forensic team purged
the backdoor, rotated payment API keys, and filed an evidentiary cybercrime report with Kolkata Cyber Police.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: Cyber Security Lab Digital Evidence Chain-of-Custody Testbed (Abhronila - Jadavpur)
Abhronila established a court-admissible digital forensics laboratory in Jadavpur for ₹2,10,000.

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never enter your UPI PIN to "receive" money; entering a PIN always deducts funds from your account.
- In financial fraud emergencies, immediately dial National Helpline '1930' within the Golden Hour.
- Never power down a compromised system before forensic RAM triage, as in-memory evidence will be lost.
- Formulate all enterprise anti-fraud, brand protection, and DFIR retainers in Indian Rupees (₹).
