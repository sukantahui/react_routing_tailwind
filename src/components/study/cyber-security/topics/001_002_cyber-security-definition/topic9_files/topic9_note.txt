1. DEFINITION & NATURE OF CYBER TERRORISM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cyber Terrorism is the premeditated, politically, religiously, or ideologically motivated
use of cyber attacks against computers, networks, and information systems to generate mass public fear,
physical destruction, economic paralysis, or loss of human life to coerce governments or societies.

Distinct Motivations:
• Cyber Crime: Motivated by financial profit and monetary extortion.
• Cyber Warfare: Conducted by sovereign state militaries for national geopolitical dominance.
• Cyber Terrorism: Motivated by ideological extremism to create mass civilian terror and panic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TARGETS & MODALITIES OF CYBER TERRORISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Municipal Water Treatment:
   Altering chemical dosing SCADA parameters (e.g. increasing sodium hydroxide/chlorine to lethal levels).
2. Aviation & Air Traffic Control (ATC):
   Transmitting spoofed ADS-B radio signals to create "ghost aircraft" on radar or jamming primary radars.
3. Emergency Broadcast Systems (EBS) & Civil Defense Sirens:
   Broadcasting fabricated ballistic missile strikes or toxic chemical leaks to trigger fatal stampedes.
4. Hospital ICU Networks:
   Manipulating bedside infusion pump dosages or silencing centralized cardiac arrest alarms.
5. Darknet Terrorist Financing:
   Crypto-crowdfunding via Monero and Bitcoin tumblers to fund weapons and lone-wolf operations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. STATUTORY LEGAL FRAMEWORK (INDIAN IT ACT SECTION 66F)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Section 66F of the Information Technology Act 2000:
  - Specifically criminalizes Cyber Terrorism against the unity, integrity, security, or sovereignty of India.
  - Punishable with MANDATORY LIFE IMPRISONMENT (Non-Bailable).
• National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre (NCIIPC):
  - Designated national agency under Section 70A protecting 5 critical sectors:
    Power & Energy, Banking & FinTech, Telecom, Transport & Aviation, and Strategic/Nuclear Enterprises.
• Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA):
  - Empowers the National Investigation Agency (NIA) to seize servers and prosecute digital terror conspiracies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE DEFENSE: ANALOG MECHANICAL INTERLOCKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Redundant Multi-Sensor Physical Telemetry:
  Real-time chemical sensors operating with hardwired physical shutoff valves that dump water
  automatically if chemical concentrations exceed safe thresholds (independent of PLC software).
• Emergency Air-Gaps & Secondary Primary Radar Fallbacks:
  Ensuring air traffic controllers can fall back to primary skin-echo radar during ADS-B spoofing attacks.
