1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE BOTNET MULTIPLIER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
While single-source DoS attacks are constrained by the attacker's single network link, Distributed 
Denial of Service (DDoS) weaponizes thousands or millions of globally compromised zombie hosts:

  * THE BOTNET MULTIPLIER ARCHITECTURE :
    1. BOTMASTER      : The adversary issuing attack commands via Command and Control (C2).
    2. ZOMBIE BOTS    : Compromised IoT cameras, routers, and servers executing the flood.
    3. C2 TOPOLOGIES  : Centralized IRC/HTTP vs Decentralized P2P DHT vs Fast-Flux DNS & DGAs.
    4. MULTI-VECTOR   : Simultaneous 1+ Tbps Volumetric, 50 Mpps Protocol, and 200k RPS App floods.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADVANCED BOTNET MECHANICS & EVASION TECHNIQUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. DOMAIN GENERATION ALGORITHMS (DGA) & FAST-FLUX DNS :
   - DGAs generate 500 random C2 domains daily; Fast-Flux shifts C2 IPs every 60s (defeats static blacklists).

B. THE MIRAI IOT PARADIGM (2016) :
   - Automated Telnet brute-forcing with 62 factory default passwords assembled 400,000 IoT bots (1.2 Tbps).

C. ASYMMETRICAL DDoS ECONOMICS :
   - Attackers spend ₹1,500 on booter subscriptions to inflict ₹2.5+ Crores in enterprise downtime.

4. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 66F   : DDoS on Critical National Infrastructure is CYBER TERRORISM (LIFE IMPRISONMENT).
    - Section 70    : DDoS attacks on PROTECTED SYSTEMS (Up to 10 YEARS IMPRISONMENT + Fine).
    - Section 43(c) : Civil compensation up to ₹1 CRORE for distributing botnet contaminants.
    - Section 66    : Criminal hacking & operating C2 infrastructure (Up to 3 Years Prison + Fine).

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 420   : Cheating & Fraudulent Ransom DDoS Extortion (Up to 7 Years Prison).
    - Section 427   : Mischief & Service Disruption (Up to 2 Years Prison).

  * CERT-In MANDATORY INCIDENT REPORTING DIRECTIVE :
    - All distributed denial of service outages must be reported to CERT-In within 6 HOURS.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5) & 33: Mandatory availability safeguards; failure triggers fines up to ₹250 CRORES.
