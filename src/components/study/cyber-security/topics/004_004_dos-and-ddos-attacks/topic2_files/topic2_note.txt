1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & VOLUMETRIC SATURATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Volumetric DDoS attacks are designed to saturate the physical bandwidth capacity (Gbps/Tbps) of 
network links, edge routers, and firewalls, causing upstream ISPs to drop legitimate packets:

  * PRIMARY VOLUMETRIC ATTACK VECTORS :
    1. UDP FLOODS          : Random high-port connectionless floods forcing ICMP error generation.
    2. ICMP PING FLOODS    : Echo requests (Type 8) saturating inbound & outbound symmetrical links.
    3. FRAGMENTATION FLOODS: Incomplete IP fragments exhausting kernel reassembly buffers.
    4. JUNK PAYLOAD PADDING: 1400-byte payloads maximizing bandwidth consumption per packet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HARDWARE & KERNEL SATURATION DYNAMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. NIC RING BUFFER OVERFLOW & SOFTIRQ SATURATION (`ksoftirqd`) :
   - At 30+ Million PPS, hardware interrupt storms lock CPU Core 0, dropping all network frames.

B. ASYMMETRICAL BANDWIDTH CHOKE :
   - Inbound requests saturate downlinks; automated ICMP/UDP error replies choke smaller uplinks.

================================================================================

    - Section 66F   : Volumetric floods on Critical Infrastructure is CYBER TERRORISM (LIFE IMPRISONMENT).
    - Section 70    : Attacks on PROTECTED SYSTEMS (Up to 10 YEARS IMPRISONMENT + Fine).
    - Section 43(f) : Civil compensation up to ₹1 CRORE for causing Denial of Access.

    - Section 420   : Cheating & Fraudulent Volumetric DDoS Extortion (Up to 7 Years Prison).
