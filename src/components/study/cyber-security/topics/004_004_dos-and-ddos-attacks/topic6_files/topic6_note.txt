1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE PHYSICS OF REFLECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DDoS Reflection and Amplification attacks weaponize misconfigured third-party servers to multiply 
an attacker's bandwidth by $50\times$ to $50,000\times$, requiring two fundamental prerequisites:

  * THE TWO CORE PREREQUISITES :
    1. STATELESS PROTOCOL (UDP): Allows forging the victim's IP address in the Source IP field.
    2. ASYMMETRIC RESPONSE PAYLOAD: A small query generates a response packet 10x-50,000x larger!
