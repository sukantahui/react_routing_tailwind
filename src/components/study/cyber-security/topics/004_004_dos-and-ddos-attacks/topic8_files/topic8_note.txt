1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PHYSICAL-CYBER CONVERGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DDoS attacks on Critical National Information Infrastructure (CNII) and cloud environments 
transcend digital disruption, threatening physical grid stability, economic collapse, and human lives:

  * THE 6 CRITICAL CNII SECTORS IN INDIA (NCIIPC / SECTION 70A IT ACT) :
    1. POWER & ENERGY    : SCADA 220kV protection relays, IEC 61850 trip signals, Smart Meter HES.
    2. BANKING & BFSI    : RTGS, NEFT, UPI, Core Banking Switches, National Settlement Hubs.
    3. TELECOM & INTERNET: Submarine Cable Landing Stations (Mumbai/Chennai), Tier-1 IXPs, `.in` ccTLD.
    4. TRANSPORTATION    : Indian Railways PRS (Tatkal booking), Air Traffic Telemetry (ADS-B).
    5. HEALTHCARE & ICU  : Real-time ICU patient telemetry, Oncology registers, Emergency 112 dispatch.
    6. GOV & CITIZEN     : Aadhaar UIDAI authentication (80M daily requests), PDS food grain networks.

2. CLOUD MULTI-TENANCY & ECONOMIC DENIAL OF SUSTAINABILITY (EDoS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. ECONOMIC DENIAL OF SUSTAINABILITY (EDoS) :
   - Exploits auto-scaling cloud groups (AWS EC2 / Lambda) by sending continuous Layer 7 traffic;
     the cluster auto-scales to hundreds of instances, bankrupting the victim with ₹45 Lakh monthly bills!

B. MULTI-TENANT NOISY NEIGHBOR CONTAMINATION :
   - An 800 Gbps flood targeting Tenant A saturates shared Top-of-Rack (ToR) switches and NAT gateways,
     inflicting collateral packet drops on innocent medical and municipal tenants in the same cloud zone.

4. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 66F   : Attacks on Critical Infrastructure is CYBER TERRORISM (LIFE IMPRISONMENT).
    - Section 70    : Attacks on PROTECTED SYSTEMS (Up to 10 YEARS IMPRISONMENT + Fine).
    - Section 70A   : NCIIPC National Critical Infrastructure Framework and 24/7 Monitoring.
    - Section 43(f) : Civil compensation up to ₹1 CRORE for causing Denial of Access.
    - Section 66    : Criminal hacking & service disruption (Up to 3 Years Prison + Fine).

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 420   : Cheating & Fraudulent Critical Infrastructure Extortion (Up to 7 Years Prison).
    - Section 427   : Mischief & Public Utility Disruption (Up to 2 Years Prison).

  * CERT-In MANDATORY INCIDENT REPORTING DIRECTIVE :
    - All critical infrastructure and cloud DDoS outages must be reported to CERT-In within 6 HOURS.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5) & 33: Mandatory availability safeguards; failure triggers fines up to ₹250 CRORES.
