1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & ADVERSARY MODELING PARADIGMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Adversary modeling frameworks provide standardized languages for understanding, analyzing, 
and neutralizing cyber attacks.

Two dominant frameworks govern modern enterprise defense:
  1. LOCKHEED MARTIN CYBER KILL CHAIN : A 7-stage linear sequential model focused on perimeter 
     prevention and early-stage disruption.
  2. MITRE ATT&CK FRAMEWORK            : A 14-tactic non-linear matrix of real-world adversary 
     behaviors, techniques, and procedures focused on post-compromise detection and threat hunting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE 7 STAGES OF THE CYBER KILL CHAIN (LOCKHEED MARTIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1. RECONNAISSANCE]   --> Researching target infrastructure, employee OSINT, and exposed ports.
[2. WEAPONIZATION]    --> Pairing an exploit with a malicious payload (e.g. MS Office macro + C2 beacon).
[3. DELIVERY]         --> Transmitting weaponized payload to victim (Spear-phishing email, USB drive).
[4. EXPLOITATION]     --> Triggering the vulnerability (buffer overflow, macro execution) on endpoint.
[5. INSTALLATION]     --> Installing backdoor, registry run key, or service for persistent access.
[6. COMMAND & CONTROL]--> Establishing encrypted two-way C2 communication channel over HTTPS/DNS.
[7. ACTIONS ON OBJ.]  --> Achieving final goal: data exfiltration, ransomware encryption, disruption.

* BREAKING THE KILL CHAIN:
  - Because the chain is strictly sequential, stopping the attacker at ANY single phase (e.g. blocking 
    email delivery at Stage 3) permanently thwarts the entire cyber attack!

5. THREAT VALIDATION & DETECTION ENGINEERING TOOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Atomic Red Team (Red Canary):
   - Modular scripted tests mapped directly to ATT&CK technique IDs (e.g. `Invoke-AtomicTest T1003.001`).

2. Sigma Generic Detection Rules:
   - Open YAML signature format mapped to ATT&CK IDs, converting to Splunk SPL, Elastic KQL, and Sentinel.

3. MITRE ATT&CK Navigator:
   - Interactive matrix visualizer for defensive coverage heatmapping and threat actor TTP profiling.

7. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL THREAT MODELING HABITS:
  1. Map Every Alert to MITRE ATT&CK IDs: Enables automated metric tracking and threat correlation.
  2. Use Atomic Red Team to Validate Rules: Never assume a detection rule works without testing it.
  3. Combine High-Level and Granular Models: Use Kill Chain for executives and ATT&CK for SOC engineers.

* COMMON THREAT MODELING MISTAKES:
  - Relying exclusively on file hashes (Attackers change hashes in 1 second; focus on TTPs).
  - Assuming the Cyber Kill Chain models modern cloud/API breaches (Cloud attacks bypass delivery/installation).
  - Collecting threat intelligence without mapping it to defensive Sigma detection rules.
  - Failing to recognize that NCIIPC and CERT-In standardize national alerts using MITRE ATT&CK IDs.
