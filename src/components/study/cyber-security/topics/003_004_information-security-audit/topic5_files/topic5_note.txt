1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE 3 PILLARS OF TECHNICAL AUDITING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and international compliance standards, technical security auditing 
validates the operational reality of enterprise defenses across three critical pillars:

  * PILLAR 1 : AUDIT LOG REVIEW & SIEM TELEMETRY (Control A.8.15 & A.8.16) ➔ What occurred & who did it.
  * PILLAR 2 : SYSTEM CONFIGURATION & HARDENING   (Control A.8.9)          ➔ How systems are built & secured.
  * PILLAR 3 : USER ACCESS REVIEWS & PRIVILEGE    (Control A.5.15 & A.5.18)➔ Who holds access & permissions.

3. USER ACCESS REVIEWS (UAR) & CIS HARDENING BASELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * QUARTERLY USER ACCESS REVIEWS (UAR) :
    - Every 90 days, department heads must formally reconcile and re-certify user permissions.
    - Identify and immediately revoke dormant accounts (> 90 days inactive) and offboarded staff.
  * PRINCIPLE OF LEAST PRIVILEGE (PoLP) & SOD :
    - Users receive only the minimum permissions required for their specific role.
    - Separation of Duties ensures developers cannot deploy code to production without peer approval.
  * PRIVILEGED ACCESS MANAGEMENT (PAM) & JIT :
    - Eliminate permanent root/admin access; grant Just-In-Time (JIT) 2-hour elevation with session recording.
  * CIS BENCHMARK CONFIGURATION AUDITS :
    - Evaluate Linux, Windows, Kubernetes, and AWS against Center for Internet Security (CIS) Level 1 rules.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STATUTORY SAFE HARBOR UNDER INDIAN CYBER LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8 & SECTION 10 :
    - Enforcing strict access reviews and logging all queries to personal citizen data provides 
      verifiable proof of "Reasonable Safeguards," shielding companies from ₹250 CRORE fines!
  * RESERVE BANK OF INDIA (RBI) MASTER DIRECTIONS :
    - Mandates quarterly User Access Reviews + daily SOC review of privileged admin actions + maker-checker.
  * CERT-In DIRECTIONS 2022 :
    - Mandatory 180-day ICT system log retention in India + mandatory 6-HOUR incident notification.

6. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL TECHNICAL AUDITING HABITS:
  1. Automate Quarterly UARs: Never rely on manual spreadsheets to track employee permissions.
  2. Embed IaC Linters in CI/CD: Use tfsec and Checkov to catch configuration drift before deployment.
  3. Deploy File Integrity Monitoring (FIM): Alert immediately on changes to `/etc/passwd` or system files.
  4. Enforce Just-In-Time (JIT) PAM: Never give permanent root or admin credentials to human engineers.

* COMMON BEGINNER TECHNICAL AUDIT PITFALLS:
  - The "Dormant Account" Trap (Leaving contractor accounts active months after contract expiration).
  - Unaudited Root SSH Access (Permitting `PermitRootLogin yes` in production server configs).
  - Mutable Log Storage (Allowing admins to modify or delete historical SIEM logs).
  - Single-Admin Privilege Abuse (Operating without Separation of Duties or maker-checker controls).
