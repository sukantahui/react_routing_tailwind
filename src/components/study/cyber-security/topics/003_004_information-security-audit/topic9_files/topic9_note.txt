1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE CAPA TRIAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) is the systematic engineering process of 
investigating security non-conformities, determining underlying root causes, and implementing 
permanent changes to prevent incident recurrence (ISO 27001:2022 Clause 10.1 & 10.2).

  * THE CORE CAPA TRIAD :
    1. CORRECTION (Containment)       ➔ Immediate fix to stop ongoing harm (e.g., isolate infected subnet).
    2. CORRECTIVE ACTION (Recurrence) ➔ Eliminate root cause of detected NC so it never happens again.
    3. PREVENTIVE ACTION (Proactive)  ➔ Eliminate causes of potential NCs before they ever occur.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS (RCA) METHODOLOGIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * THE 5-WHYS DRILLDOWN :
    - Iterative questioning technique to move past superficial symptoms to systemic failures.
    - Stopping at Why 1-2 only fixes human error; reaching Why 4-5 fixes governance & architecture.

  * THE ISHIKAWA / FISHBONE DIAGRAM (4 Ps / 6 Ms) :
    - POLICIES    ➔ Missing or outdated security standards and management guidelines.
    - PROCEDURES  ➔ Defective change control, offboarding, or review workflows.
    - PEOPLE      ➔ Lack of security awareness, fatigue, or missing role certifications.
    - TECHNOLOGY  ➔ Unpatched software, lack of automated linters, or legacy systems.
    - MEASUREMENT ➔ SIEM alerting gaps, missing metrics, or audit blindness.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE 8D (EIGHT DISCIPLINES) CAPA PROBLEM-SOLVING FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

+----+----------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
| D# | DISCIPLINE NAME            | CORE OBJECTIVE & LEAD AUDITOR DELIVERABLE           |
+----+----------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+
| D1 | Establish the Team         | Assemble cross-functional CIRT (SecOps, DevOps, Law)|
| D2 | Describe the Problem       | Formulate precise PLOR Non-Conformity statement     |
| D3 | Implement Containment Fix  | Immediate operational correction (Quarantine/Patch) |
| D4 | Identify True Root Cause   | Execute 5-Whys and Fishbone root cause analysis     |
| D5 | Choose Permanent Fix (PCA) | Select long-term architectural safeguard (e.g. SCIM)|
| D6 | Implement & Validate PCA   | Deploy fix in staging & production with telemetry   |
| D7 | Prevent Recurrence         | Update ISMS policies, Terraform linters, and SOPs   |
| D8 | Verification of VoE & Sign | Lead Auditor re-testing, 90-day VoE, & formal close |
+----+----------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. VERIFICATION OF EFFECTIVENESS (VoE) & 90-DAY CLOSURE SLA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * WHY DEPLOYMENT != CLOSURE :
    - Deploying a software fix only proves implementation, not effectiveness.
    - Under ISO 19011 Clause 6.7, a Lead Auditor requires a 30 to 90-day monitoring 
      window where re-sampling confirms a 0% Root Cause Recurrence Rate.
  * MAJOR NON-CONFORMITY 90-DAY SLA :
    - Accredited certification bodies (BSI, TÜV, DNV) give organizations 90 CALENDAR DAYS 
      to close Major NCs before withholding or cancelling ISO 27001 certificates.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS UNDER INDIAN CYBER LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8(5) & SECTION 33 :
    - Demonstrating a verified, proactive CAPA register with root cause remediation proves to the 
      Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) that the enterprise acted with reasonable safeguards, 
      averting statutory fines of up to ₹250 CRORES.
  * RESERVE BANK OF INDIA (RBI) CYBER SECURITY FRAMEWORK :
    - Mandatory quarterly Board IT Sub-Committee reporting of all open CAPAs and Root Cause Recurrence KPIs.
  * CERT-In DIRECTIONS 2022 :
    - Incident root causes and corrective action plans must be submitted following critical security breaches.

7. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL CAPA HABITS:
  1. Never Stop at Why #1: Dig through at least 5 levels of 'Why' until governance/tooling gaps are revealed.
  2. Separate Containment from Correction: Fix the bleeding immediately (D3), but engineer the permanent fix (D5-D7).
  3. Require Single Named Custodians: Assign every CAPA task to an individual engineer with a hard deadline.
  4. Always Execute VoE: Wait 30-90 days to re-sample before marking any Non-Conformity ticket as 'Closed'.

* COMMON BEGINNER CAPA PITFALLS:
  - Blaming Human Error (Concluding "the developer made a mistake" without fixing lack of automated scanners).
  - Instant Ticket Closure (Closing the CAPA ticket the exact minute code is merged, omitting VoE testing).
  - Vague Acceptance Criteria (Writing "improve security" instead of "0 critical CVEs on SonarQube").
  - Missing Management Review (Failing to present CAPA trends to C-Suite, resulting in zero budget allocation).
