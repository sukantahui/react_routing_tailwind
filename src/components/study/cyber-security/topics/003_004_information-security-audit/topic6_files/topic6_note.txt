1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE 5 MAJOR COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modern enterprise security auditing requires managing overlapping regulatory and contractual frameworks:

  * 1. INDIA DPDP ACT 2023 ➔ Governs personal data in India; up to ₹250 CRORE fine for security failures.
  * 2. EU GDPR             ➔ Governs EU citizen data; up to €20M or 4% global turnover for privacy lapses.
  * 3. US HIPAA            ➔ Governs Protected Health Information (ePHI); Administrative/Physical/Technical rules.
  * 4. PCI-DSS v4.0        ➔ 12 requirements securing Cardholder Data Environment (CDE) and payment tokens.
  * 5. AICPA SOC 2 TYPE 2  ➔ Independent CPA audit testing 5 Trust Services Criteria over 6-12 month windows.

3. UNIFIED COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK (UCF): "MAP ONCE, COMPLY MANY"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * UNIFIED CONTROL PHILOSOPHY :
    - Instead of building 5 separate silos, implement a single master technical control baseline 
      (e.g., ISO/IEC 27001:2022) and map it across all relevant standards.
  * CROSS-WALK MAPPING EXAMPLE (AES-256 ENCRYPTION AT REST) :
    - ISO 27001:2022   ➔ Control A.8.24 (Cryptography)
    - DPDP Act 2023    ➔ Section 8(5) (Reasonable Security Safeguards)
    - EU GDPR          ➔ Article 32(1)(a) (Pseudonymisation and Encryption)
    - US HIPAA         ➔ 45 CFR §164.312(a)(2)(iv) (Encryption and Decryption)
    - PCI-DSS v4.0     ➔ Requirement 3.4 (Protect Cardholder Data)
    - AICPA SOC 2      ➔ Trust Services Criteria CC6.6 (Encryption Controls)

4. STATUTORY SAFE HARBOR UNDER INDIAN CYBER LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8 & SECTION 33 :
    - Implementing reasonable technical safeguards (AES-256, WORM logs, RBAC) establishes verified 
      due diligence, shielding Data Fiduciaries from ₹250 CRORE statutory fines.
  * CERT-In DIRECTIONS 2022 VS GDPR :
    - India mandates reporting cyber security incidents within 6 HOURS of noticing (vs GDPR 72 hours).
  * RBI MASTER DIRECTIONS & PCI-DSS :
    - Scheduled commercial banks must comply with both RBI Cyber Security Directives and PCI-DSS v4.0.

6. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL MULTI-FRAMEWORK HABITS:
  1. Map Once, Comply Many: Harmonize controls to ISO 27001 to satisfy DPDP, GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2.
  2. Reduce PCI Scope: Use network segmentation and tokenization to shrink the Cardholder Data Environment.
  3. Prepare for SOC 2 Type 2: Collect continuous operational evidence across a 6 to 12 month testing window.
  4. Appoint Resident DPO: Enforce India-based Data Protection Officer appointment under DPDP Section 10.

* COMMON BEGINNER COMPLIANCE PITFALLS:
  - The "Siloed Audit" Trap (Conducting 5 separate expensive audits with duplicate testing).
  - Scope Sprawl (Failing to segment payment networks, putting all corporate laptops in PCI scope).
  - Confusing SOC 2 Type 1 with Type 2 (Assuming a point-in-time snapshot proves continuous security).
  - Missing the 6-Hour SLA (Failing to report critical security incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours).
