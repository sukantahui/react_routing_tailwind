1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & TELEPHONY SOCIAL ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Vishing (Voice Phishing) and Smishing (SMS Phishing) exploit the intimacy, urgency, and high 
response rates of mobile telecommunication channels:

  * THE 4 CORE TELEPHONY THREAT VECTORS :
    1. VISHING (VOICE PHISHING) : Live voice manipulation using authority or fake IT support.
    2. SMISHING (SMS PHISHING)  : Urgent SMS messages with shortened links (KYC / Bill Disconnect).
    3. CALLER ID SPOOFING       : SIP header manipulation making calls appear from legitimate banks.
    4. SIM SWAPPING / HIJACKING : Porting victim phone numbers to attacker SIMs to steal SMS 2FA.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADVANCED TELEPHONY ATTACK INNOVATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. REAL-TIME AI VOICE CLONING (DEEPFAKE VISHING) :
   - Neural diffusion models synthesize executive speech in real time (<150ms latency).
   - Impersonates the CEO over live phone calls to demand emergency wire transfers.

B. ADVERSARY-IN-THE-MIDDLE (AiTM) REVERSE PROXYING :
   - Attacker guides victim to Evilginx proxy, capturing session cookies (`ESTSAUTH`).
   - Bypasses SMS OTP multi-factor authentication completely.

C. AUTOMATED IVR PHONE TREES (ROBOCALLING) :
   - Asterisk PBX autodialers instruct victims to type ATM PINs via DTMF keypad tones.

4. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 66D   : Cheating by Personation using computer/mobile resources (Up to 3 Years Prison).
    - Section 43(a) : Civil damages up to ₹1 CRORE for unauthorized access via vishing.
    - Section 66    : Criminal hacking & fraudulent deception (Up to 3 Years Prison + ₹5 Lakh Fine).

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 420   : Cheating & Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property (Up to 7 Years Prison).

  * TRAI TCCCPR REGULATIONS 2018 :
    - Mandatory DLT registration for commercial SMS headers to prevent alphanumeric spoofing.

  * CERT-In MANDATORY INCIDENT REPORTING DIRECTIVE :
    - All telephony compromises & account takeovers must be reported within 6 HOURS.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5) & 33: Mandatory employee voice training; failure triggers fines up to ₹250 CRORES.
