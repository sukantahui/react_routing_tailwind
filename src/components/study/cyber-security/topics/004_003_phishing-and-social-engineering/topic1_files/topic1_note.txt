1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & DR. CIALDINI'S PRINCIPLES OF INFLUENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Social Engineering weaponizes human evolutionary psychology and cognitive heuristics to bypass 
technical security controls:

  * DR. ROBERT CIALDINI'S 6 PRINCIPLES OF INFLUENCE :
    1. AUTHORITY    : Reflexive compliance with perceived institutional rank (CEO Fraud / BEC).
    2. URGENCY/FEAR : Amygdala panic narrowing cognitive bandwidth (15-min account lockout).
    3. SCARCITY     : Fear of missing out (FOMO) on limited bonuses or vouchers.
    4. LIKING/TRUST : Rapport-building, compliments & grooming on LinkedIn over weeks.
    5. RECIPROCITY  : Innate desire to return favors or help a polite colleague (Helpfulness Trap).
    6. SOCIAL PROOF : Herd conformity ("All senior engineers have already submitted tokens").
