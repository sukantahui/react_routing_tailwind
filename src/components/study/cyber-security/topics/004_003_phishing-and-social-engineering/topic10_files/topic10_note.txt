1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & EMAIL AUTHENTICATION ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Email was designed without native sender verification. Technical email authentication establishes 
cryptographic and policy identity verification across three core pillars:

  * THE TRIPLE PILLARS OF EMAIL IDENTITY :
    1. SPF (RFC 7208)  : Authorizes IP addresses permitted to send mail for a domain in DNS TXT records.
    2. DKIM (RFC 6376) : Asymmetric cryptographic signatures (RSA-2048/Ed25519) ensuring message integrity.
    3. DMARC (RFC 7489): Enforces domain alignment between Header From and SPF/DKIM; policies (`none`, `quarantine`, `reject`).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADVANCED PROTOCOLS & COMPLEMENTARY STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. BIMI & VERIFIED MARK CERTIFICATES (VMC) :
   - Displays verified corporate trademark logos in webmail inboxes for domains with DMARC `p=reject`.

B. AUTHENTICATED RECEIVED CHAIN (ARC - RFC 8617) :
   - Preserves authentication results across mailing lists and email forwarders that alter headers.

C. MTA-STS (RFC 8461) & DANE TLSA :
   - Enforces TLS encryption on SMTP transport, stopping man-in-the-middle STARTTLS stripping attacks.

4. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 66D   : Cheating by Personation via email spoofing (Up to 3 Years Prison + Fine).
    - Section 43(a) : Civil compensation up to ₹1 CRORE for unauthorized access via spoofing.
    - Section 66    : Criminal hacking & fraudulent deception (Up to 3 Years Prison + ₹5 Lakh Fine).

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 420   : Cheating & Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property (Up to 7 Years Prison).

  * CERT-In MANDATORY INCIDENT REPORTING DIRECTIVE :
    - All domain spoofing outbreaks & mass phishing campaigns must be reported within 6 HOURS.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5) & 33: Mandatory email security safeguards; failure triggers fines up to ₹250 CRORES.

================================================================================
  [✔] SPF authorizes sending IP addresses; DKIM cryptographically signs email headers and body.
  [✔] DMARC ties SPF and DKIM together, enforcing alignment with the visible `From:` header.
  [✔] The 3 DMARC policies are `p=none` (monitor), `p=quarantine` (spam), and `p=reject` (block).
  [✔] SPF evaluation must not exceed 10 DNS lookups; use SPF flattening to avoid `PermError`.
  [✔] BIMI displays verified trademark logos next to emails for domains with DMARC `p=reject`.
