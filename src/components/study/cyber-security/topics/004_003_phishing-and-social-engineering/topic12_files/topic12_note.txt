1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & HUMAN RISK MANAGEMENT (HRM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Security Awareness Training transforms the workforce from the weakest security link into an active 
Human Firewall through continuous simulated testing, micro-learning, and rapid reporting:

  * THE 4 CORE PILLARS OF HUMAN RISK RESILIENCE :
    1. BASELINE ASSESSMENT: Measuring Phish-Prone Percentage (PPP = Clicks / Sent * 100).
    2. JUST-IN-TIME (JIT) : Instant 500ms interactive micro-coaching delivered on simulated clicks.
    3. RAPID REPORTING    : One-click "Report Phishing" inbox button triggering auto-SOAR mailbox purge.
    4. MICRO-LEARNING     : Monthly 2-minute scenario modules achieving 84% long-term knowledge retention.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADVANCED SIMULATION TESTING & SAFE HARBOR GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. MULTI-CHANNEL SIMULATION TESTING :
   - Testing workforce resilience across Email Spear Phishing, Quishing (QR Codes), Smishing, and USB drops.

B. ETHICAL SAFE HARBOR GUIDELINES :
   - Prohibits cruel/traumatic simulation pretexts (layoffs, pay cuts) to maintain organizational trust.

C. CLICK-TO-REPORT RATIO (C2R) :
   - Measuring active defense; top enterprises achieve C2R > 10:1 (10 reports for every 1 click).

4. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 43A   : Mandatory Reasonable Security Practices (Workforce Training Compensation).
    - Section 66D   : Cheating by Personation via deceptive lures (Up to 3 Years Prison + Fine).
    - Section 66    : Criminal hacking & fraudulent deception using computer systems.

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 420   : Cheating & Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property (Up to 7 Years Prison).
    - Section 419   : Cheating by Personation for Vishing phone scams (Up to 3 Years Prison).

  * CERT-In MANDATORY INCIDENT REPORTING DIRECTIVE :
    - Fast employee reporting enables compliance with the mandatory 6-HOUR incident SLA.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5) & 33: Mandatory organizational workforce training; failure triggers ₹250 CRORE fines.
