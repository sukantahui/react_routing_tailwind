1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THREAT TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Baiting and Pretexting manipulate human greed, curiosity, deference to authority, and social 
helpfulness to compromise digital networks and physical facilities:

  * BAITING TACTICS (PHYSICAL & DIGITAL) :
    - Physical: Dropping infected USB flash drives or BadUSB Rubber Duckys labeled "Salaries 2026".
    - Digital : Distributing trojanized software cracks, pirated tools, or typosquatted PyPI packages.

  * PRETEXTING TACTICS (IDENTITY FABRICATION) :
    - Inventing an elaborate persona (e.g. State Pollution Inspector, Senior Audit Director).
    - 5 Core Principles: Plausibility, Contextual Depth, Authority, Empathy, and Isolation.
    - Elicitation Techniques: Bracketing and deliberate false statements to extract confidential specs.
