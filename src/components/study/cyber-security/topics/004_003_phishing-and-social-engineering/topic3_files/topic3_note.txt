1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & TARGETED PHISHING TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Targeted phishing moves away from mass broadcast spray toward high-context, intelligence-driven 
operations customized against specific individuals, executives, or active business workflows:

  * THE 4 CORE TARGETED ATTACK TYPES :
    1. SPEAR PHISHING : High-context lures targeting specific roles, referencing real projects.
    2. WHALING        : Targeting C-suite executives with legal summons, M&A filings, or tax audits.
    3. CLONE PHISHING : Duplicating a real historical email, replacing attachments with malware.
    4. THREAD HIJACK  : Compromising a vendor mailbox to inject malicious replies into active threads.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ADVANCED DELIVERY VECTORS & EVASION MECHANISMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. REMOTE TEMPLATE INJECTION (DOCX) :
   - Clean `.docx` downloads malicious Word macro template (`.dotm`) dynamically from C2.
   - Bypasses static perimeter email gateway scanners completely.

B. CALLBACK PHISHING / TOAD ATTACKS :
   - Clean email with no links or attachments containing only a fake customer care phone number.
   - Live phone attacker guides the victim to download AnyDesk / Remote Access Trojans.

C. GEOFENCING & COUSIN DOMAINS :
   - C2 serves exploits only to victim enterprise IP ranges in Kolkata, returning 404 to researchers.
   - Registers lookalike domains (`kolkata-fintech-cloud.in`) with valid SPF/DKIM records.

================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 66D   : Cheating by Personation using computer resources (Up to 3 Years Prison).
    - Section 43(a) : Civil damages up to ₹1 CRORE for unauthorized access via spear phishing.
    - Section 66    : Criminal hacking & fraudulent deception (Up to 3 Years Prison + ₹5 Lakh Fine).

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 420   : Cheating & Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property (Up to 7 Years Prison).
