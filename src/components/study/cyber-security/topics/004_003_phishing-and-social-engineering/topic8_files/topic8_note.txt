1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & INSIDER THREAT TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Insider threats originate from individuals with legitimate credentials and authorized access, 
classified into three primary archetypes:

  * THE 3 PRIMARY INSIDER ARCHETYPES :
    1. MALICIOUS INSIDERS   : Intentional data theft, intellectual property sabotage, or espionage.
    2. NEGLIGENT EMPLOYEES  : Accidental data leaks, cloud misconfigurations, and shadow IT bypasses.
    3. COMPROMISED INSIDERS : Legitimate accounts hijacked by external threat actors via infostealers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BEHAVIORAL PATHWAYS & TECHNICAL EXPLOIT MECHANICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. THE CERT CRITICAL PATH TO INSIDER RISK :
   - Personal Predispositions ➔ Workplace Stressor ➔ Concerning Behavior ➔ Technical Precursor ➔ Malicious Act.

B. THE MICE MOTIVATION FRAMEWORK :
   - Money (competitor bribes), Ideology (whistleblowing), Coercion (blackmail), Ego (entitlement).

C. ADVANCED INSIDER EXPLOITS :
   - Notice Period Data Hoarding (mass downloading code/client lists before resigning).
   - Logic Bomb Sabotage (scripts detonating when the admin's account is deleted).
   - Accidental Cloud Misconfigurations (publicly exposed S3 buckets and databases).

4. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 72A   : Breach of Confidentiality under lawful contract (Up to 3 Years Prison + ₹5 Lakh Fine).
    - Section 43(b) : Civil damages up to ₹1 CRORE for copying or extracting corporate data.
    - Section 66    : Criminal hacking, sabotage, and logic bomb deployment (Up to 3 Years Prison).

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 408   : Criminal Breach of Trust by Clerk or Servant (Up to 7 Years Prison + Fine).
    - Section 420   : Cheating & Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property (Up to 7 Years Prison).

  * CERT-In MANDATORY INCIDENT REPORTING DIRECTIVE :
    - All insider data exfiltrations and unauthorized privileged access must be reported within 6 HOURS.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5) & 33: Mandatory employee data access governance; failure triggers fines up to ₹250 CRORES.
