1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Social engineering cannot be solved by technology or awareness alone. Enterprise immunity requires 
a synchronized 3-Tier Defense-in-Depth Matrix combining Policy, Technical, and Human controls:

  * THE 3-TIER SOCIAL ENGINEERING DEFENSE MATRIX :
    1. POLICY TIER    : Mandatory Out-of-Band (OOB) dual verification (> ₹1,00,000) & Two-Person rules.
    2. TECHNICAL TIER : FIDO2 Hardware Keys (YubiKey), DMARC `p=reject`, Number Matching MFA & UEBA ML.
    3. HUMAN TIER     : 500ms Just-in-Time (JIT) coaching, One-Click Report button & Security Champions.
