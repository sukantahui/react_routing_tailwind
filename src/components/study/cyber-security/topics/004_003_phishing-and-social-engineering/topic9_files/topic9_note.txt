1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THREAT TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Privilege Abuse, Data Exfiltration, and Sabotage represent the most destructive internal threat 
actions, weaponizing legitimate administrative authorization against the enterprise:

  * THE 3 PRIMARY INTERNAL THREAT VECTORS :
    1. PRIVILEGE ABUSE   : Misusing authorized access (snooping on executive salaries, mass SQL dumps).
    2. COVERT EXFILTRATION: Stealing data via DNS Tunneling, ICMP payloads, and LSB Image Steganography.
    3. INFRASTRUCTURE SABOTAGE: Volume Shadow Copy deletion, Logic Bombs, and Partition Table wiping.

2. ADVANCED EXPLOIT MECHANICS & COVERT CHANNELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. DNS TUNNELING EXFILTRATION & SHANNON ENTROPY :
   - Encodes stolen data in DNS query subdomains (`base64.c2.attacker.com`) over outbound UDP 53.
   - Detected via Shannon entropy analysis ($H(X) > 4.5$) and query length inspection.

B. LEAST SIGNIFICANT BIT (LSB) STEGANOGRAPHY :
   - Hides confidential text inside the lowest-order bit of RGB image pixels (invisible visually).
   - Detected via Chi-Square ($\chi^2$) statistical analysis of sample pairs of values.

C. LIVING OFF THE LAND (LotL) SABOTAGE :
   - Uses pre-installed native binaries (`vssadmin`, `certutil`, `bitsadmin`, `tar`, `dd`) to evade AV.

4. STATUTORY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE IN INDIA
================================================================================

  * INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT 2000 :
    - Section 70    : Unauthorized access/sabotage of PROTECTED SYSTEMS (Up to 10 YEARS PRISON).
    - Section 43(c) : Civil compensation up to ₹1 CRORE for introducing contaminants/sabotage.
    - Section 66B   : Receiving or retaining stolen computer data (Up to 3 Years Prison + Fine).

  * INDIAN PENAL CODE (IPC) :
    - Section 427   : Mischief & Sabotage causing damage to property (Up to 2 Years Prison).
    - Section 408   : Criminal Breach of Trust by Clerk or Servant (Up to 7 Years Prison + Fine).

  * CERT-In MANDATORY INCIDENT REPORTING DIRECTIVE :
    - All data exfiltration, database sabotage, and privileged abuse must be reported within 6 HOURS.

  * DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 :
    - Section 8(5) & 33: Mandatory organizational data safeguards; failure triggers fines up to ₹250 CRORES.
