1. QUANTUM NETWORKING & QUANTUM KEY DISTRIBUTION (QKD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Quantum Key Distribution (BB84 / E91):
  Transmits cryptographic keys using single photon polarization states. By Heisenberg's
  Uncertainty Principle and the No-Cloning Theorem, any eavesdropping attempt disturbs
  the quantum states, creating a measurable Quantum Bit Error Rate (QBER > 11%) that
  alerts legitimate parties to discard compromised keys.
• Quantum Repeaters & Entanglement Swapping:
  Teleports quantum states across dark fiber spans, establishing multi-thousand-kilometer
  entangled quantum internet connections without measuring or destroying qubits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. POST-QUANTUM CRYPTOGRAPHY (PQC - NIST STANDARDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 'Q-Day' Preparation:
  Shor's algorithm running on future quantum computers will break RSA and ECC cryptography.
• NIST Standardized Post-Quantum Algorithms:
  - ML-KEM (Kyber): Lattice-based Key Encapsulation Mechanism.
  - ML-DSA (Dilithium) & SLH-DSA (SPHINCS+): Quantum-resistant digital signatures.
• Hybrid TLS 1.3:
  Combines classical ECDH (X25519) + ML-KEM (Kyber768) to protect current encrypted
  traffic from 'Harvest Now, Decrypt Later' (HNDL) nation-state adversaries.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. 6G WIRELESS, TERAHERTZ & LEO SATELLITE SPACE MESHES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 6G Terahertz Wireless (100 GHz – 3 THz):
  Delivers 1 Terabit per second (Tbps) peak throughput and sub-100 microsecond latency.
  Integrates Sensing and Communication (ISAC) and Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS).
• LEO Satellite Mega-Constellations (Optical Laser ISLs):
  Satellites communicate via 100G free-space optical lasers in space vacuum, routing packets
  faster than transoceanic undersea fiber cables with direct-to-cell smartphone connectivity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AI-NATIVE INTENT NETWORKING & PROGRAMMABLE SILICON (P4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• AI-Native Intent-Based Networking (IBN):
  Declarative high-level business goals translated by AI into self-healing routing rules.
• Digital Network Twins:
  Mathematical software clones simulating routing changes before hardware deployment.
• P4 Programmable Data Planes (Intel Tofino):
  Custom packet processing and In-Band Network Telemetry (INT) at 25.6 Tbps wire speed.
