1. DEFINITION & PHYSICAL LAYER TAXONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transmission Media is the physical conduit (guided / bounded) or free-space
electromagnetic channel (unguided / unbounded) that transports data bits as physical
signals (electrical voltages, optical laser pulses, or radio waves) between network
devices across OSI Layer 1.

Primary Classifications:
1. GUIDED (WIRED / BOUNDED) MEDIA:
   - Signals are physically contained and guided within solid conductors or glass.
   - Includes: Twisted-Pair Copper (UTP/STP), Coaxial Cables, Optical Fiber (SMF/MMF).
2. UNGUIDED (WIRELESS / UNBOUNDED) MEDIA:
   - Electromagnetic waveforms propagate freely through the atmosphere or space.
   - Includes: Radio Frequency (RF - Wi-Fi/Cellular), Microwaves (LOS dishes), Infrared.

3. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Precision Foundry Inter-Building Fiber Run (Debangshu - Barrackpore)
Debangshu installed a 6-core Single-Mode OS2 armored optical fiber link (650m) between two
manufacturing buildings in Barrackpore for ₹85,000. It provides 10Gbps line rate, complete
immunity to industrial arc-furnace EMI, and zero lightning surge risks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Diagnostic Clinic Cat6a Shielded Cabling (Mahima - Ichapur)
Mahima installed certified Cat6a S/FTP pure copper cabling in an MRI diagnostic wing in
Ichapur for ₹1,40,000. The metallic shielding prevents high-magnetic-field interference
from corrupting patient monitoring data packets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: University Multi-Department Backbone Modernization (Mamata - Kolkata)
Mamata deployed OM4 multi-mode fiber backbones across 8 academic departments in Kolkata for
₹4,50,000, connecting departmental distribution switches to the central server farm with
40Gbps link aggregation and sub-millisecond latency.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: High-Tech Cyber Lab Optical Intrusion Monitoring (Abhronila - Jadavpur)
Abhronila implemented an Optical Fiber Intrusion Detection System (OFIDS) in Jadavpur for
₹75,000, detecting micro-bending light leakage and physical cable tampering to protect
classified research networks from physical wiretapping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never exceed the 100-meter total channel limit for Category 6 copper twisted-pair cables.
- Beware of cheap Copper-Clad Aluminum (CCA) cables; always demand 100% solid bare copper (23AWG).
- Enforce Plenum-rated (CMP) jackets in ceiling air return plenums for fire code compliance.
- Formulate all structured cabling, fiber splicing, and testing budgets in Indian Rupees (₹).
