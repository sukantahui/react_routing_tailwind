1. DEFINITION & PHYSICAL LAYER CAPACITY THEORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In digital computer networking, Bandwidth refers to the maximum theoretical data
transfer capacity of a communication channel, representing the maximum volume of bits
that can travel through the network per second (measured in bps, Mbps, Gbps, or Tbps).

In analog signal processing, Bandwidth ($B$) is the width of the frequency range
($B = f_{max} - f_{min}$) measured in Hertz (Hz).

Fundamental Capacity Theorems:
1. Nyquist Bit Rate Theorem (Noiseless Channels):
   $$C = 2B \log_2(M)$$
   where $M$ is the number of discrete signal voltage levels.
2. Shannon Channel Capacity Theorem (Noisy Real-World Channels):
   $$C = B \log_2(1 + \text{SNR})$$
   where $\text{SNR} = \frac{S}{N}$ (Signal-to-Noise Ratio).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BANDWIDTH VS THROUGHPUT VS GOODPUT VS LATENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Bandwidth: Theoretical maximum capacity of the pipeline (e.g., 1000 Mbps port).
• Throughput: Actual measured data rate successfully transmitted across the wire
  taking into account collisions, congestion, and TCP window scaling (e.g., 850 Mbps).
• Goodput: Useful application payload data received excluding all protocol headers
  (Ethernet, IP, TCP) and retransmissions (e.g., 780 Mbps).
• Latency: Round-trip time (RTT) delay taken for a bit to travel from sender to receiver (ms).

Bandwidth-Delay Product (BDP):
$$\text{BDP (Bits)} = \text{Bandwidth (bps)} \times \text{RTT (seconds)}$$
$$\text{Optimal TCP Receive Window (Bytes)} = \frac{\text{BDP}}{8}$$
If TCP buffers are smaller than BDP, high-speed fiber links stall and underutilize bandwidth.

3. INTERNET LEASED LINE (ILL) VS SHARED BROADBAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Parameter         | Dedicated Leased Line (ILL)   | Shared FTTH Broadband
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bandwidth Ratio   | 1:1 Symmetric (Up = Down)     | Asymmetric (Fast Down, Slow Up)
Contention Ratio  | 1:1 (100% Dedicated Unshared) | 1:8 or 1:10 (Shared with Neighbors)
SLA Guarantee     | 99.9% Uptime with 4-hr MTTR   | Best-Effort (No Financial SLA)
Static Public IPs | Subnet Block (/29 or /28)     | Single Dynamic / CGNAT IP
Est. Cost (₹)     | ₹15,000 – ₹25,000 / mo (100M) | ₹800 – ₹1,500 / mo (300M)

4. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Precision Foundry ILL Leased Line Deployment (Debangshu - Barrackpore)
Debangshu commissioned a 100 Mbps 1:1 symmetric Internet Leased Line in Barrackpore for
₹18,500/month. The dedicated unshared pipe eliminated evening ERP lag, supporting 200
telemetry streams and dual site-to-site IPsec VPN tunnels with 99.95% measured uptime.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Multi-Speciality Clinic QoS Video Telemedicine (Mahima - Ichapur)
Mahima implemented Quality of Service (QoS) bandwidth reservation on a 200 Mbps link in
Ichapur. Reserving 40 Mbps guaranteed bandwidth for doctor HD video consultations (DSCP EF)
ensured zero video stuttering even during heavy staff file backups.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: University Examination Result CDN Edge Absorption (Mamata - Kolkata)
Mamata deployed a Cloudflare Enterprise CDN in Kolkata for ₹60,000 during semester result
announcements. The CDN edge absorbed 94% of the 450 Mbps traffic spike, preventing the
university's central 100 Mbps leased line from saturating and crashing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: High-Tech Cyber Lab BDP Window Tuning & iPerf Benchmarking (Abhronila - Jadavpur)
Abhronila tuned Linux kernel TCP socket buffers (`sysctl net.ipv4.tcp_rmem = 4096 87380 16777216`)
across 10G optical fiber links in Jadavpur for ₹35,000 in testing equipment. Benchmarked via
`iperf3`, single-stream file transfer throughput jumped from 420 Mbps to 9.4 Gbps.

5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never confuse bits per second (Mbps) with bytes per second (MB/s); divide Mbps by 8.
- Tune TCP socket receive windows (RWIN) to match the Bandwidth-Delay Product (BDP).
- Enforce QoS bandwidth prioritization (DSCP) to protect VoIP and video during congestion.
- Formulate all telecom ILL procurement, CDN, and DDoS mitigation budgets in Indian Rupees (₹).
