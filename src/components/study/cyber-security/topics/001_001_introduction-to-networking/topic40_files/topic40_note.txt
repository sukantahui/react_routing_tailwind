1. NATIONAL KNOWLEDGE NETWORK (NKN) & CAMPUS HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• National Knowledge Network (NKN): A multi-gigabit (1G to 10G) low-latency optical
  backbone established by the Government of India connecting universities, IITs, NITs,
  and research institutes nationwide for collaborative supercomputing and research.
• Campus 3-Tier Hierarchy:
  - Access Layer: 24/48-Port PoE+ switches connecting classrooms, labs, and Wi-Fi APs.
  - Distribution Layer: Building-level Layer 3 switches handling inter-VLAN routing.
  - Core Layer: Redundant 40G/100G fiber switches in the Central Computer Center.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CAMPUS WIRELESS & GLOBAL EDUROAM FEDERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Eduroam (Educational Roaming): Global Wi-Fi roaming using 802.1X RADIUS federation.
  Visiting professors/students authenticate using their home institution credentials
  via EAP-TTLS / PEAP with WPA3 Enterprise encryption.
• Multi-SSID Architecture:
  - Faculty/Staff SSID (VLAN 10): 802.1X WPA3, direct intranet and gradebook access.
  - Student SSID (VLAN 20): Captive portal / 802.1X, daily quota management (5GB/day).
  - Eduroam SSID (VLAN 30): Global research federation roaming.
  - Guest SSID (VLAN 90): OTP mobile verification, internet-only isolated access.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SECURE ONLINE COMPUTER-BASED TESTING (CBT) & LMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• CBT Examination Security:
  - Air-gapped or firewall-locked Examination VLAN (VLAN 40).
  - Safe Exam Browser (SEB) client locking out local desktop, USBs, and third-party apps.
  - IP whitelisting restricting testing terminals strictly to the local exam server.
• High-Concurrency LMS (Moodle/Canvas):
  - Kubernetes container auto-scaling with Redis session caching and MySQL read-replicas.
  - Cloud CDN edge caching and virtual waiting rooms during board result publishing.

4. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Engineering College Wi-Fi 6 & Eduroam Deployment (Debangshu - Barrackpore)
Debangshu deployed 45 enterprise Wi-Fi 6 Access Points and an Eduroam RADIUS gateway across an
engineering campus in Barrackpore for ₹3,80,000. Over 2,400 students and visiting IIT faculty
experience seamless high-speed roaming across academic blocks and research laboratories.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Medical College Online CBT Examination Center (Mahima - Ichapur)
Mahima configured a locked 120-seat Computer-Based Testing (CBT) examination center in Ichapur
for ₹1,45,000. Firewall rules restrict examination terminals strictly to the local assessment
server, while Safe Exam Browser blocks unauthorized internet searches during university medical exams.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: University Result Publishing Cloud CDN Architecture (Mamata - Kolkata)
Mamata architected a Cloudflare CDN edge caching and virtual waiting room solution in Kolkata
for ₹85,000. When 450,000 students accessed semester results simultaneously, 99.1% of traffic
was served from regional edge caches, keeping origin database CPU utilization under 15%.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: Cyber Security Lab HPC Research Cluster Isolation (Abhronila - Jadavpur)
Abhronila engineered an isolated 100G RoCE supercomputing research cluster in Jadavpur for ₹4,20,000.
The network provides sub-microsecond latency for AI training and molecular simulation, while
strict boundary firewalls block unauthorized external access from public campus Wi-Fi networks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never deploy single unmanaged Wi-Fi access points in high-density lecture auditoriums.
- Isolate online examination terminals into locked VLANs with strict outbound egress filtering.
- Implement Layer-7 content filtering to block bandwidth-heavy torrents and crypto miners.
- Budget campus Wi-Fi 6 controllers, CBT exam switches, and NKN hardware in Indian Rupees (₹).
