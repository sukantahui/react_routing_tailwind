1. CORE BANKING SOLUTION (CBS) & FINANCIAL INTERCONNECT NETWORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core Banking Solutions (CBS - e.g. Finacle, BaNCS) centralize financial ledgers in real time:
• Primary Data Center (DC) & Disaster Recovery (DR) Site: Connected via high-speed
  Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing (DWDM) dark fiber for sub-2ms synchronous database writes.
• Branch WAN: Dual-homed MPLS circuits with automated encrypted 4G/5G IPsec failover.
• ATM & Micro-ATM Networks: Cellular IoT / VSAT encrypted IPsec tunnels with ISO 8583 messaging.

Global & National Financial Interconnects:
• SWIFTNet: Dedicated SWIFT Alliance Gateways (SAG) handling ISO 20022 / MT103 wire transfers.
• NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India): Operates high-speed financial switches for
  UPI (Unified Payments Interface), IMPS, RuPay, NACH, and National Financial Switch (NFS).
• RBI INFINET: Indian Financial Network closed user group (CUG) for high-value RTGS and NEFT.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BANKING CYBER SECURITY & HARDWARE CRYPTOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Payment Hardware Security Modules (HSMs - Thales payShield / Entrust nShield):
  Tamper-proof hardware appliances generating and storing Master Keys (ZMK, ZPK, TPK),
  encrypting ISO 9564 PIN blocks, and validating CVV/CVC codes with zero memory exposure.
• PCI-DSS Cardholder Data Environment (CDE) Segmentation:
  Strict firewall isolation ensuring non-cardholder subnets cannot access payment data.
• DUKPT (Derived Unique Key Per Transaction):
  POS terminals derive a brand-new cryptographic key for every single payment swipe.
• Tokenization: Replaces real 16-digit card numbers with surrogate tokens for online shopping.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BANKING CYBER THREATS & OPERATIONAL DEFENSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• ATM Jackpotting / Black-Box Attacks: Defended by cryptographic cash dispenser bus encryption.
• SWIFT Fraud & Credential Theft: Mitigated by SWIFT Customer Security Programme (CSP) controls.
• Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Transaction Tampering: Defended by Mutual TLS (mTLS) and digital HMACs.
• DDoS Attacks on NetBanking: Defended by Cloud DDoS Scrubbing Centers and BGP Anycast.

4. FOUR REAL-WORLD BENGAL CASE STUDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CASE STUDY 1: Precision Regional Bank CBS Branch WAN & 4G Failover (Debangshu - Barrackpore)
Debangshu upgraded 18 bank branch WAN routers in Barrackpore with Cisco SD-WAN and encrypted
4G cellular failover for ₹1,85,000. When primary fiber lines were severed during road construction,
branch teller Finacle sessions failed over to cellular in 1.2 seconds with zero ledger corruption.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 2: Cooperative Credit Society Payment HSM Deployment (Mahima - Ichapur)
Mahima installed a redundant Thales payShield Payment HSM pair for an Ichapur cooperative bank
for ₹16,50,000. The HSM processes 1,200 ATM and Micro-ATM PIN verifications per second under FIPS
140-2 Level 3 tamper-zeroization compliance, enabling full RuPay and AePS card integration.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 3: Commercial Bank NPCI UPI Switch & mTLS Gateway (Mamata - Kolkata)
Mamata deployed a high-capacity NPCI UPI payment switch gateway in Kolkata for ₹22,00,000.
Handling 8,500 transactions per second (TPS) over dedicated mTLS MPLS links, the gateway
settles instant mobile remittances between Kolkata retail merchants and national banks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CASE STUDY 4: Cyber Security Lab SWIFT CSP & ATM Dispenser Audit (Abhronila - Jadavpur)
Abhronila conducted security auditing on SWIFT Alliance terminals and ATM cash dispensers in
Jadavpur for ₹95,000. Enforcing isolated management VLANs and dispenser bus encryption neutralized
potential black-box hardware attacks and unauthorized cross-border wire injections.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. COMMON PITFALLS & PRACTICAL ADVICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never store cleartext PINs or CVVs in application server memory; always process inside HSMs.
- Enforce strict PCI-DSS network segmentation around the Cardholder Data Environment (CDE).
- Implement out-of-band mobile OTP verification for all high-value online transactions.
- Budget enterprise Payment HSMs, SD-WAN routers, and UPI switches in Indian Rupees (₹).
