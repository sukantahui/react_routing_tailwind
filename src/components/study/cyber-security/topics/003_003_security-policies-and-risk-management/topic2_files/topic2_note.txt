1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE TRIAD OF IDENTITY & DATA GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cybersecurity resilience is built upon three foundational operational policies under ISO/IEC 27001:2022:
  * 1. PASSWORD & AUTHENTICATION POLICY (Control A.8.5 / NIST SP 800-63B) : Verifies identity.
  * 2. ACCESS CONTROL POLICY (Controls A.5.15, A.5.18, A.8.2)              : Enforces Least Privilege.
  * 3. DATA RETENTION & DISPOSAL POLICY (Control A.8.10 / DPDP Act Sec 8)  : Enforces Storage Limitation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. MODERN PASSWORD POLICY (NIST SP 800-63B VS LEGACY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

+--------------------+-------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| POLICY DIMENSION   | LEGACY PASSWORD RULES   | MODERN NIST SP 800-63B / ISO 27001 STANDARD    |
+--------------------+-------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| Minimum Length     | 8 Characters (Weak)     | >= 16 Characters (Passphrases favored)         |
| Periodic Expiry    | Mandatory every 90 Days | BANNED (Induces weak predictable variations)   |
| Complexity Rules   | Symbols/Numbers forced  | Discretionary; prioritize passphrase length    |
| Breached Screening | None                    | Mandatory screening against leaked DBs         |
| Authentication     | Single-Factor / SMS OTP | Phishing-Resistant FIDO2 Hardware Tokens       |
+--------------------+-------------------------+------------------------------------------------+

3. ACCESS CONTROL MODELS & THE JML LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * PRINCIPLE OF LEAST PRIVILEGE (PoLP - Control A.5.15) : Grant minimum necessary permissions.
  * ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)  : Assigns permissions to corporate job functions.
  * ATTRIBUTE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (ABAC) : Dynamic Zero Trust rules based on user, device, network, time.
  * JOINER-MOVER-LEAVER (JML - Control A.5.18) :
    - Joiner : Provisions least privilege on Day 1.
    - Mover  : Revokes old departmental roles when transferring.
    - Leaver : Automated de-provisioning (< 15 mins) eliminating orphaned accounts.
  * QUARTERLY PRIVILEGED ACCESS REVIEWS (A.8.2) : Mandatory 90-day manager re-certification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DATA RETENTION, STORAGE LIMITATION & CRYPTO-SHREDDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8(7) STORAGE LIMITATION :
    - Data Fiduciaries must permanently erase personal data once the processing purpose is fulfilled.
  * STATUTORY RETENTION SCHEDULES IN INDIA :
    - Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) : Retain KYC & banking ledgers for 5 YEARS.
    - CERT-In Directions 2022 (Section 70B)     : Retain rolling ICT system logs for 180 DAYS.
    - Indian Income Tax Act                     : Retain financial accounting records for 8 YEARS.
  * NIST SP 800-88 REV 1 SANITIZATION TIERS :
    - Level 1: Clear   ➔ Software overwrite tools (Writing zeros across sectors).
    - Level 2: Purge   ➔ Cryptographic Erasure (Crypto-shredding by deleting master KMS key).
    - Level 3: Destroy ➔ Physical disintegration/shredding of disks to < 2mm particles.

6. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY & DATA HABITS:
  1. Mandate Long Passphrases (>= 16 chars): Prioritize passphrase length over symbol complexity.
  2. Deploy Phishing-Resistant FIDO2: Replace SMS/Email OTPs with domain-bound WebAuthn tokens.
  3. Automate JML Offboarding: Ensure 100% of accounts are suspended in < 15 minutes of resignation.
  4. Use Crypto-Shredding in Cloud: Delete the KMS master key to sanitize petabytes of cloud storage instantly.

* COMMON BEGINNER IDENTITY PITFALLS:
  - 90-Day Forced Password Expiration (Forces users to pick predictable patterns like `Summer2026!`).
  - Relying on SMS OTPs (Vulnerable to SIM-swapping and reverse-proxy phishing kits like Evilginx).
  - Indefinite Data Hoarding (Keeping personal data forever, triggering massive DPDP Act liabilities).
  - Ignoring Orphaned Accounts (Failing to conduct mandatory quarterly privileged access reviews).
