1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & THE ESSENCE OF ASSET GOVERNANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You cannot protect what you do not know you own. Under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Controls A.5.9 (Inventory of 
Information Assets), A.5.12 (Classification of Information), and A.5.13 (Labeling of Information), 
asset identification and valuation form the indispensable prerequisite for all risk assessments:

  * PRIMARY ASSETS    : Core business processes and information (Customer PII, Financial Ledgers).
  * SUPPORTING ASSETS : Tangible IT infrastructure hosting primary assets (Servers, Cloud VPCs, Staff).
  * THE OUTCOME       : Prioritized security budgets, eliminated orphan assets, and statutory defensibility!

3. ASSET VALUATION METHODOLOGIES (QUANTITATIVE VS QUALITATIVE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * QUANTITATIVE VALUATION EQUATION :
    $$Total Asset Value = C_{hardware} + C_{rebuild} + V_{revenue\_daily} + L_{regulatory\_fines} + V_{brand}$$
    - Example : A ₹5 Lakh database server hosting ₹30 Cr/day UPI flows with ₹250 Cr DPDP statutory 
      liability has a true quantitative asset value exceeding ₹280 CRORES!

  * QUALITATIVE CIA TRIAD IMPACT SCORING (1 TO 5 SCALE) :
    - Confidentiality Impact : Damage if leaked publicly.
    - Integrity Impact       : Damage if data is tampered with / corrupted.
    - Availability Impact    : Damage if service suffers downtime.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ASSET OWNERSHIP VS CUSTODIANSHIP (CONTROL A.5.9)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * ASSET OWNER (Business Executive) :
    - Senior department head (e.g. CFO / Head of Compliance) who owns the business data, 
      determines classification, and approves access permissions.
  * ASSET CUSTODIAN (Technical Administrator) :
    - Technical engineer / sysadmin (e.g. Lead DBA Mamata) who implements technical safeguards, 
      configures AES-256 encryption, and manages automated backups.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. STATUTORY SAFE HARBOR UNDER INDIAN CYBER LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * IT ACT 2000 SECTION 70 (PROTECTED SYSTEMS) :
    - Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) declared Protected Systems by NCIIPC; 
      unauthorized access is punishable with up to 10 YEARS RIGOROUS IMPRISONMENT!
  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8 & SECTION 33 :
    - Comprehensive asset registers proving identification of personal data repositories 
      protect corporations against ₹250 CRORE statutory fines!

7. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL ASSET MANAGEMENT HABITS:
  1. Automate Cloud Discovery: Ingest dynamic cloud API telemetry into central CMDBs continuously.
  2. Include Legal Liabilities in Valuation: Factor in DPDP Act ₹250 Cr penalty exposure.
  3. Enforce Strict Classification Banners: Label all documents and S3 buckets under Control A.5.13.
  4. Separate Owner from Custodian: Ensure business owners define access while custodians manage technical locks.

* COMMON BEGINNER ASSET PITFALLS:
  - Orphan Assets (Leaving abandoned test servers running without an assigned asset owner).
  - Hardware-Only Valuation (Valuing a server at its ₹2 Lakh purchase price rather than its ₹50 Cr data value).
  - Manual Excel Registers (Using static spreadsheets that become obsolete within days in dynamic cloud setups).
  - Over-Classification (Classifying 100% of documents as 'Restricted', creating massive operational friction).
