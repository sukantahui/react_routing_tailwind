4. STATUTORY SAFE HARBOR UNDER INDIAN CYBER LAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * DPDP ACT 2023 SECTION 8 & SECTION 33 :
    - Maintaining an actively managed Risk Register, Privacy-by-Design threat models, and 
      rigorous vendor DPAs establishes statutory Safe Harbor, immunizing against ₹250 Cr fines!
  * RESERVE BANK OF INDIA (RBI) MASTER DIRECTIONS :
    - Commercial banks must present an automated, quantified Risk Register directly to the 
      Board Risk Management Committee (BRMC) on a quarterly basis.
  * CERT-In DIRECTIONS 2022 (IT ACT SEC 70B) :
    - Mandatory 6-hour incident reporting and strict 48-hour Critical CVE remediation SLAs.

5. PROFESSIONAL TIPS & COMMON PITFALLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* PROFESSIONAL ERM HABITS:
  1. Speak the Language of Finance: Always present Rupee ALE and ROSI percentages when pitching to CFOs.
  2. Maintain Continuous GRC Sync: Connect vulnerability scanners directly to the enterprise risk register.
  3. Enforce 100% SBOM Coverage: Eliminate unmonitored transitive dependencies across software supply chains.
  4. Benchmark Against Appetite: Never deploy a software release if residual risk exceeds board appetite.

* COMMON BEGINNER ERM PITFALLS:
  - The "Paper Compliance" Myth (Assuming having a PDF security policy equals real-world cyber resilience).
  - The "Spreadsheet Trap" (Tracking enterprise risks in static Excel files without audit trails).
  - Ignoring Legal Penalties in Asset Value (Failing to include ₹250 Cr DPDP fines in SLE equations).
  - Unowned Risk Registers (Logging risks without assigning a named engineer and calendar SLA).

6. STUDENT MINI CHECKLIST (POINTS TO REMEMBER FOR EXAMS & INTERVIEWS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] ERM synthesizes Asset Valuation, Threat Modeling, Quantitative Loss, and RTP into one framework.
[ ] Single Loss Expectancy: SLE = Asset Value (AV) x Exposure Factor (EF).
[ ] Annual Loss Expectancy: ALE = SLE x Annual Rate of Occurrence (ARO).
[ ] Return on Security Investment: ROSI = [(ALE Reduction - Control Cost) / Control Cost] x 100.
[ ] 4 Treatment Options: Mitigate (Reduce), Transfer (Share), Avoid (Stop), Accept (Retain).
[ ] Risk Appetite is the board's non-negotiable maximum residual risk threshold (e.g. <= 3.0).
[ ] STRIDE maps threats to Authenticity, Integrity, Non-Repudiation, Confidentiality, Availability, Authorization.
[ ] DPDP Act Section 8 and Section 33 govern data breach liability and ₹250 Crore statutory fine safe harbor.
[ ] RBI Master Directions mandate quarterly Risk Register presentation to the Board Committee (BRMC).
