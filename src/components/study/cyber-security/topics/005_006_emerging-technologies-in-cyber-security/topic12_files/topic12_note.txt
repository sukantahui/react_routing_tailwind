2. QUANTUM RESILIENCE & CRYPTOGRAPHIC BILL OF MATERIALS (CBOM)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM - CycloneDX / SPDX):
   - A machine-readable inventory of all cryptographic algorithms, certificate chains, key lengths, and cipher suites 
     embedded across an enterprise's software repositories, microservices, and network hardware.

2. Mosca's Theorem of Quantum Risk ($X + Y > Z$):
   - $X$ = Security Shelf Life (Time in years data must remain secret; e.g., 20 years for military/banking).
   - $Y$ = Migration Time (Time in years to inventory, upgrade, and deploy PQC across enterprise infrastructure).
   - $Z$ = CRQC Arrival Time (Time in years until a Cryptanalytically Relevant Quantum Computer exists).
   - If $X + Y > Z$, data is ALREADY in critical peril due to "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) attacks!
