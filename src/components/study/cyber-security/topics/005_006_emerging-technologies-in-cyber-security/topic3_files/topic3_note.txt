1. THE ADVERSARIAL MACHINE LEARNING TAXONOMY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Adversaries target the machine learning pipeline at three distinct phases:

1. Training-Time Attacks (Data Poisoning / Backdoors):
   - Data Poisoning: Injecting malicious or mislabeled data into the training corpus to degrade overall accuracy.
   - Backdoor / Trojan Attacks (BadNets): Embedding a secret trigger pattern (e.g., a specific byte sequence 
     or 4-pixel watermark). The model performs with 99%+ accuracy on normal data, but misclassifies any input 
     containing the trigger into the attacker's chosen class (e.g., classifying ransomware as benign system driver).

2. Inference-Time Attacks (Evasion / Adversarial Perturbations):
   - Fast Gradient Sign Method (FGSM): Adding calculated mathematical noise $\epsilon \cdot \text{sign}(\nabla_x L)$ 
     to shift an input across the decision boundary while remaining imperceptible to human analysts.
   - Black-Box vs White-Box: White-box attacks exploit known model gradients; Black-box attacks query 
     APIs repeatedly to reconstruct surrogate gradient approximations.

3. Model Inversion & Extraction:
   - Stealing model architecture and weights or reverse-engineering proprietary training datasets.


2. GENERATIVE AI ATTACK VECTORS: DEEPFAKES & AI-CRAFTED PHISHING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Deepfake Video Puppetry & Voice Cloning (CEO Fraud / BEC):
   - Adversaries clone executive voices from public speeches using diffusion models.
   - Video calls use real-time GAN face-swapping to trick corporate treasury clerks into wiring funds.

2. Deepfake Forensic Detection Metrics:
   - Involuntary Biological Signals: Human eyes blink 12–20 times per minute; early GANs fail to blink naturally.
   - Viseme-Phoneme Desynchronization: Micro-delays between acoustic phonemes and visual mouth shapes ($> 80\text{ms}$).
   - Remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG): Real skin reflects subtle cardiac pulse micro-color shifts; deepfakes have flat rPPG spectrums.
   - Acoustic Phase Coherence: Synthesized AI voices lack natural high-frequency vocal tract resonance.

3. AI-Crafted Spear-Phishing:
   - LLMs generate culturally tailored, grammatically flawless phishing emails mimicking executive writing styles.


3. MITIGATION & DEFENSE STRATEGIES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Adversarial Training (Data Augmentation):
   - Retraining models with generated FGSM adversarial samples to harden decision boundaries.

2. Cryptographic Digital Signatures & C2PA Content Credentials:
   - Cryptographically signing media at the hardware camera sensor level (Coalition for Content Provenance 
     and Authenticity - C2PA) to verify authentic origin.

3. Multi-Channel Out-of-Band Verification:
   - Enforcing that all financial transfers exceeding ₹1,00,000 require secondary cryptographic FIDO2 approval 
     regardless of video/voice instructions.
