1. CORE MODALITIES OF PHYSIOLOGICAL BIOMETRICS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Biometrics authenticates users based on distinct, measurable biological characteristics ("Something You ARE"):

1. Fingerprint Recognition:
   - Friction ridge skin patterns on fingertips.
   - Sensor Technologies: Optical (TFT/CMOS camera), Capacitive (silicon semiconductor array), 
     Ultrasonic (high-frequency sound waves penetrating epidermal layers).
   - Feature Extraction: Minutiae points (Ridge Endings, Bifurcations, Core, Delta).
   - Mathematical Representation: $(x_i, y_i, \theta_i, \text{type}_i)$.

2. Facial Recognition:
   - Geometry and texture of the human face.
   - Sensor Technologies: 2D RGB cameras, 3D Structured Light Infrared Projectors (30,000 dots), 
     Time-of-Flight (ToF) sensors.
   - Feature Extraction: Deep Neural Network embeddings mapping facial landmarks into a 128-D or 
     512-D normalized hypersphere vector space.
   - Matching Metric: Cosine Similarity or Euclidean Distance between probe and gallery vectors.

3. Iris Scanning (Daugman's Algorithm):
   - Epigenetic trabecular meshwork pattern of the human iris muscle.
   - Sensor Technology: Near-Infrared (NIR 700–900 nm) illumination to illuminate dark brown irises.
   - Feature Extraction: Multi-scale 2D Gabor wavelets transform the unwrapped polar coordinate iris 
     into a 2048-bit binary "IrisCode" with a 2048-bit noise mask (eyelids/eyelashes).
   - Matching Metric: Fractional Hamming Distance ($HD = \frac{\sum (A \oplus B)}{N}$). Match declared if $HD < 0.32$.


2. THE BIOMETRIC TEMPLATE NON-REVOCABILITY DILEMMA
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike passwords or cryptographic keys, biological biometric traits are permanently non-revocable and public:
- If an adversary breaches a raw biometric database, the victim CANNOT change their face, fingerprint, or iris.
- Architectural Mandate: Raw biometric images must NEVER be stored in central databases.
- Storage Solutions:
  1. Secure Enclave / TPM: Storing templates strictly inside hardware-isolated security chips on the client device.
  2. Cancellable Biometrics (BioHashing): Applying parameterized, non-invertible mathematical transforms. 
     If compromised, a new transform key is issued to produce a completely distinct template from the same finger.


3. PRESENTATION ATTACK DETECTION (PAD / LIVENESS VERIFICATION - ISO/IEC 30107-3)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Presentation Attack occurs when an adversary presents a synthetic replica (spoof artifact) to a sensor:
- Spoof Types: 2D printed photographs, digital screen playback, 3D silicon/latex masks, gelatin fake fingerprints (gummy fingers), prosthetic eyes.

Liveness Detection Techniques:
1. Hardware-Based (Active):
   - Infrared depth sensing (verifies 3D facial curvature).
   - Thermal infrared imaging (verifies $36.5^\circ\text{C} - 37.5^\circ\text{C}$ biological heat signature).
   - Pulse oximetry & optical coherence tomography (detects capillary blood pulsation).
2. Software-Based (Passive):
   - Involuntary micro-blinking and pupil dilation analysis.
   - Micro-texture reflection analysis (detecting screen pixel moire patterns or printed paper glare).
   - Challenge-Response (asking user to turn head or smile).
