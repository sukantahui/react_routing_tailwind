1. STANDARD COLLECTIONS HAZARDS:
   - HashMap & ArrayList have zero internal synchronization.
   - Race conditions cause lost updates and node pointer corruption.
   - 'size++' is non-atomic (read-modify-write race).
   - Iterators throw 'ConcurrentModificationException'.
   - NEVER share plain HashMap/ArrayList across threads without synchronization.
