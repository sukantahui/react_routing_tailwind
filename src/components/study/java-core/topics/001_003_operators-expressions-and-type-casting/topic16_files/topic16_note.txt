1. THE COMPLETE 15-TIER OPERATOR PRECEDENCE HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Level | Category          | Operators                            | Associativity
------+-------------------+--------------------------------------+--------------
 15   | Postfix           | expr++  expr--                       | Left-to-Right
 14   | Primary / Access  | ( )  [ ]  .                          | Left-to-Right
 13   | Unary / Prefix    | ++expr  --expr  +  -  !  ~  (type)   | RIGHT-TO-LEFT
 12   | Multiplicative    | *  /  %                              | Left-to-Right
 11   | Additive          | +  -                                 | Left-to-Right
 10   | Shift             | <<  >>  >>>                          | Left-to-Right
  9   | Relational        | <  >  <=  >=  instanceof             | Left-to-Right
  8   | Equality          | ==  !=                               | Left-to-Right
  7   | Bitwise AND       | &                                    | Left-to-Right
  6   | Bitwise XOR       | ^                                    | Left-to-Right
  5   | Bitwise OR        | |                                    | Left-to-Right
  4   | Logical AND       | &&                                   | Left-to-Right
  3   | Logical OR        | ||                                   | Left-to-Right
  2   | Ternary / Cond    | ? :                                  | RIGHT-TO-LEFT
  1   | Assignment        | =  +=  -=  *=  /=  %=  &=  |=  ^= ...| RIGHT-TO-LEFT
------+-------------------+--------------------------------------+--------------

2. CRITICAL PRECEDENCE TRAPS TO MEMORIZE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Additive beats Shift:
   1 + 2 << 2  ==> (1 + 2) << 2 = 12  (NOT 1 + 8 = 9!)

2. Relational beats Bitwise AND:
   flags & MASK != 0  ==> flags & (MASK != 0) -> COMPILATION ERROR!
   Fix: ((flags & MASK) != 0)

3. Logical AND beats Logical OR:
   a || b && c  ==> a || (b && c)

3. RIGHT-TO-LEFT ASSOCIATIVITY MEMORY HOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only 3 categories associate Right-to-Left:
[1] Unary operators   : - - -50  ==> -(-(-50)) = -50
[2] Ternary operator  : a ? b : c ? d : e  ==> a ? b : (c ? d : e)
[3] Assignment ops    : a = b = c = 100   ==> a = (b = (c = 100))

Everything else is strictly Left-to-Right!
