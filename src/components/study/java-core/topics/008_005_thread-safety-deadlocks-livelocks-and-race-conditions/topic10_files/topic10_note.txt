1. DINING PHILOSOPHERS:
   - 5 philosophers need 2 chopsticks to eat.
   - Naive algorithm: All grab left chopstick &rarr; Deadlock!
   - Dijkstra's Solution: Resource Hierarchy (Global Ordering).
   - Always acquire smaller indexed chopstick first: 'min(left, right)'.
   - Breaks Circular Wait condition.
