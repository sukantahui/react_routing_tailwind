1. COFFMAN 3: NO PREEMPTION:
   - Locks cannot be stolen by force from the holding thread.
   - Intrinsic 'synchronized' has NO timeout and cannot be preempted.
   - Broken by: 'ReentrantLock.tryLock(timeout)' with voluntary back-off.
