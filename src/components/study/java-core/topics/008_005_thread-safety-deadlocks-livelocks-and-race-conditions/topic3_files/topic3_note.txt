1. THE 4 COFFMAN CONDITIONS:
   - 1. Mutual Exclusion: Exclusive lock access.
   - 2. Hold and Wait: Holding Lock 1 while waiting for Lock 2.
   - 3. No Preemption: Locks cannot be confiscated by force.
   - 4. Circular Wait: Thread A waits for B; Thread B waits for A.
   - BREAK ANY 1 CONDITION &rarr; DEADLOCK IS PREVENTED!
