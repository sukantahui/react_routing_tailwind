1. LIVELOCK:
   - Threads actively change states in lockstep without progress.
   - RUNNABLE state (100% CPU burn, unlike Deadlock's 0%).
   - Analogy: Two polite people dodging back and forth in a hallway.
   - Solution: Randomized Back-Off Jitter before retrying.
