1. BACKREFERENCE RULES:
   - In Pattern: '\1', '\2' matches content of Group 1, Group 2.
   - In Replacement: '$1', '$2' substitutes Group 1, Group 2 text.
   - Ideal for grammar linters, duplicate word detection, and XML tag matching.
