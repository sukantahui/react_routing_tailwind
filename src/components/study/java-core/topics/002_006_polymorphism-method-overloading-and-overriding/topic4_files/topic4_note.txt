1. @Override ANNOTATION POWERS:
   - Compile-Time Safety Guard: Compiler verifies matching parent method exists.
   - Prevents Silent Bug: Without @Override, a typo creates an unused overload.
   - Best Practice: Always annotate overridden methods with @Override.
