1. NULL-SAFE COMPARATORS:
   - Standard sorting crashes on 'null' with NPE.
   - 'Comparator.nullsFirst(c)': places all nulls at the beginning.
   - 'Comparator.nullsLast(c)': places all nulls at the end.
   - Essential for real-world enterprise databases with nullable columns.
