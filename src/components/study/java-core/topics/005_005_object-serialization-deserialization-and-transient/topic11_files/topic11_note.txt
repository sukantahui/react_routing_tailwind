1. EXTERNALIZABLE VS SERIALIZABLE:
   - 'Externalizable' extends 'Serializable'.
   - 2 methods: 'writeExternal(out)' & 'readExternal(in)'.
   - Mandatory: 'public MyClass()' no-arg constructor.
   - Ignores 'transient' keyword (programmer has total manual control).
   - Higher performance, zero reflection overhead.
