1. THE 4 CONSTRUCTORS:
   - 'public MyEx()'
   - 'public MyEx(String message)'
   - 'public MyEx(Throwable cause)'
   - 'public MyEx(String message, Throwable cause)'
   - Always forward arguments to 'super(...)'.
