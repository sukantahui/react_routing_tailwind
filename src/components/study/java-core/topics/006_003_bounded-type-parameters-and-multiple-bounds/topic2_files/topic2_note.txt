1. 'extends' FOR INTERFACES:
   - In generics, 'extends' = 'is a subtype of'.
   - Applies to classes AND interfaces.
   - '<T extends List>' and '<T extends Runnable>' are correct.
   - '<T implements Runnable>' is INVALID and will not compile.
