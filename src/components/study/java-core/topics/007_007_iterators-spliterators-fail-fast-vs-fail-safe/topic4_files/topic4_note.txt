1. THE MODCOUNT FIELD:
   - 'protected transient int modCount = 0;'.
   - Incremented on structural changes: 'add()', 'remove()', 'clear()'.
   - NOT incremented on 'set(index, val)' or 'get(index)'.
   - Serves as the version counter for Fail-Fast iterators.
